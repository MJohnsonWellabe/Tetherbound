# Meadows Wild Roster — Canon

## Scope
This document defines the canonical **Meadows wild roster** only.

There are:
- **12 wild species**
- **1 evolution**
- **1 total evolutionary line in the biome**

## Canonical Meadows wild roster

### Ground
1. **Bramblebun** — Ground rabbit  
2. **Mudsnout** — Ground pig / rooting runt  
3. **Trailpup** — Ground canine  
4. **Meadowhart** — Ground rideable mount  
5. **Burrowback** — Ground badger  

### Water
6. **Paddlenewt** — Water newt / amphibian  
7. **Mosshell** — Water turtle  
8. **Brooktail** — Water otter  
9. **Reedwing** — Water/Air waterfowl  

### Air
10. **Pipwing** — Air bird  
11. **Duskhush** — Air owl  
12. **Galecrest** — Air hawk  

### Evolution
13. **Tuskroot** — evolved Ground boar

## Evolution rule
Only one Meadows species evolves:
- **Mudsnout -> Tuskroot**

No other Meadows creature currently evolves.

## Canon species notes

### Bramblebun
A Ground rabbit. Fast, nimble, and alert. Distinct long ears and compact grounded silhouette.

### Mudsnout
A Ground piglet / rooting runt. Youthful, sturdy, earthy, and clearly the pre-evolution of Tuskroot.

### Trailpup
A Ground canine. Lean, prairie/coyote-like, adventurous, and clearly **not** a starter.

### Meadowhart
A rideable Ground mount. A normal meadow deer/antlered mount, friendly and useful, clearly **not** the legendary.

### Burrowback
A broad, low Ground badger. Defensive, burrowing, heavy-forelimb silhouette.

### Paddlenewt
A small Water amphibian/newt. Quick, smooth, and expressive.

### Mosshell
A Water turtle. Tanky, calm, shell-forward silhouette.

### Brooktail
A Water otter. Friendly, resourceful, semi-aquatic mammal.

### Reedwing
A Water/Air waterfowl. Webbed feet, buoyant silhouette, distinct from the smaller Air bird.

### Pipwing
A small Air bird. Round, quick, compact, and much smaller/lighter than Galecrest.

### Duskhush
An Air owl. Silent, watchful, nocturnal-feeling.

### Galecrest
An Air hawk/raptor. A true raptor silhouette, larger and more serious than Pipwing, and clearly **not** the Air starter.

### Tuskroot
A Ground armored boar. Larger, heavier, tusked, stone-plated evolution of Mudsnout.

## Scale canon
Average impression: these Pals live in the same physical scale as the player.

Desired feel:
- some slightly smaller than the player
- some around similar overall presence
- some somewhat larger
- but the roster should average around player-world scale

Suggested interpretation:
- smallest: Pipwing, Bramblebun
- largest: Meadowhart, Galecrest, Tuskroot
- most others: roughly compact-to-medium companion scale

Do not let the roster drift into toy-sized creatures or giant oversized monsters.
