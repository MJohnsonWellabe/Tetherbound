# Meadows Wild Roster — Art Mapping

## Purpose
Map older exploratory art / temporary names to the final canonical Meadows wild roster.

## Canon mapping

| Canon name | Canon role | Older / exploratory source |
|---|---|---|
| Bramblebun | Ground rabbit | older rabbit concepts such as `HOPPI` / `HOPPEROO` |
| Mudsnout | Ground pig / base boar line | exploratory boar/groundbit-style pig concepts |
| Trailpup | Ground canine | earlier canine slot / leaflet-adjacent silhouette inspiration |
| Meadowhart | Ground rideable mount | new normal mount concept, not the legendary |
| Burrowback | Ground badger | new badger concept |
| Paddlenewt | Water amphibian/newt | older `DEWCALE` / `PONDLET`-type amphibian concepts consolidated into one canonical slot |
| Mosshell | Water turtle | new turtle concept |
| Brooktail | Water otter | new otter/beaver-adjacent aquatic mammal concept, now resolved as otter |
| Reedwing | Water/Air waterfowl | waterfowl slot; may inherit some style language from older bird concepts but is its own canonical species |
| Pipwing | Air bird | older small-bird / `SKYTUFT`-like direction |
| Duskhush | Air owl | new owl concept |
| Galecrest | Air hawk | raptor slot, distinct from starter |
| Tuskroot | Mudsnout evolution | formerly boar-like ideas; now explicitly the evolved form of Mudsnout |

## Non-canonical older assumptions to discard
The following are not canonical Meadows wild-creature rules:
- canine evolves in Meadows
- deer can share the legendary's visual language too closely
- the Air hawk can resemble the Air starter too strongly
- any old random concept-board creature automatically belongs in Meadows
- old printed image labels are final canon

## Important distinction map
### Meadowhart vs legendary
Meadowhart is:
- normal wild/rideable Meadows mount
- lighter, friendlier, more practical
- less sacred / less ancient / less legendary
- not visually interchangeable with the legendary

### Trailpup vs Ground starter
Trailpup is:
- a wild Ground canine
- leaner and more coyote-like
- less mascot/starter-coded
- not stone-mantled like the Ground starter
- not a duplicate of the starter's silhouette

### Galecrest vs Air starter
Galecrest is:
- a true meadow raptor / hawk
- stronger predator silhouette
- broader wings / more serious posture
- not a starter-like cute fox-bird hybrid

### Mudsnout vs Tuskroot
Mudsnout:
- younger
- rounder
- lower-intensity
- pre-evolution

Tuskroot:
- larger
- armored
- more tusked
- clearly the evolved form
