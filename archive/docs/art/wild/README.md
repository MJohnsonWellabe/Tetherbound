# Tetherbound — Meadows Wild Canon Pack

This ZIP is the canonical art + instruction pack for the **Meadows wild roster** in Tetherbound.

## Scope
This pack covers **only**:
- the 12 Meadows wild Pals
- the 1 Meadows evolution

This pack explicitly does **not** cover:
- the 3 starters
- the Meadows legendary
- Grandpa
- the Meadows Warden
- the player character

## Canon directive
For the Meadows wild roster, the art and documents in this pack should be treated as **canon** unless the owner later replaces them with a newer canonical pack.

If any older generated boards, temp notes, or exploratory names conflict with this pack:
- this pack wins
- older placeholder labels are non-canonical
- older evolution assumptions are non-canonical
