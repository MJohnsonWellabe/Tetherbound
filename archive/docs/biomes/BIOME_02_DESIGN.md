## 1. Locked Starting Constraints

These constraints are approved before creative selection begins.

- The biome should play like Meadows, but be longer.
- Target creature roster: **10–12 creatures**.
- **No starter** in this biome.
- **Exactly one evolution line**.
- **Exactly one legendary**.
- The creatures should form a cohesive ecosystem and visual set.
- The path should contain mini-bosses.
- The path should contain trainer battles.
- NPCs should teach new things.
- The biome should introduce new items.
- Prefer one or two meaningful new ideas over a large pile of new mechanics.

These constraints may be changed only by the owner/parent.
