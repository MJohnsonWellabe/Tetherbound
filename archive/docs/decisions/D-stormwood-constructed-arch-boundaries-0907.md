# Constructed Stormglass road boundaries

The three-pair limit conservatively includes the player-raised Crown road: two ordinary player pairs may coexist with the Still Grove–Crown pair, or three ordinary pairs may stand until the player dismantles enough to make room for the Crown road. The Crown footing substitutes six Crown-grade Stormglass for ordinary Stormglass in the authored arch recipe; other ingredients and the normal refund remain the same. Story and realm gates still apply in Free Build, which waives costs only. The host saves stable twin building UIDs in one ledger delta and atomically clears the survivor on dismantle. Character inventory uses the existing build-placement preflight and settlement path; this does not claim a new host-owned inventory escrow system.
