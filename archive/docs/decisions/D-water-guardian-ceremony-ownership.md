# Water Guardian ceremony ownership

The freed Deep Watcher follows the existing regional monument model: one world settlement and one personal recipient, with the ordinary five-creature roster choice. The first host-validated interaction beside the freed Guardian or Edda reserves the exact creature for that stable character; a world journal survives reconnect until that character saves the resulting party and receipt. Declining companionship also completes the ceremony. The Guardian is a gift, so this transaction does not award Catching XP. The Tideglass Compass is earned for the world after settlement and is placed at Salt Crown's authored shrine; activation uses each player's existing single-active relic slot. This conservative implementation preserves the five-creature limit and avoids inventing duplicate world legendaries or storage. Client-owned character-save acknowledgments remain a trust boundary and require an adversarial multiplayer audit before final acceptance.
