# Tetherbound — Future Regions Art + World Structure Handoff

## Purpose

Use the attached corrected art boards as the high-level visual and design reference for Regions 2–8 and for the world before/after the Rifts.

This document supersedes the earlier region/world concept boards where they conflict with the rules below.

The boards are **directional references**, not literal instructions to reproduce every detail exactly.

---

# 1. Production Art Target

The environmental target is **Palworld / Fortnite-level stylized open-world quality**, not ultra-detailed dark fantasy concept art.

Required characteristics:
- colorful and readable
- large simple terrain forms
- clean silhouettes
- strong landmarks
- moderate prop density
- stylized vegetation
- achievable with the current Tetherbound pipeline
- performant enough for the intended game

Do NOT design every region like Valheim Ashlands.
Do NOT assume dense handcrafted micro-detail across the whole map.
Do NOT create environments that would require AAA custom environment production.

The world should still look exciting, but it needs to be buildable.

---

# 2. Meadows Is Existing Canon

Region 1, the Meadows, is already substantially built.

Do NOT redesign the Meadows geography, village, pond, Burrow Warrens, Meadows Hall, existing roads, or established creature roster simply to match these concept boards.

Treat the Meadows imagery on the attached boards as broad visual shorthand only.

When future work touches Region 1:
- inspect the current game and repo first
- preserve existing canon
- improve rather than replace
- do not invent conflicting landmarks or geography

---

# 3. The Rifts Physically Broke the World Apart

This is critical.

The world was originally **one continuous landmass**.

Before the Rifts:
- all eight regions physically touched
- roads connected them
- rivers crossed between them
- mountain passes connected them
- settlements and trade routes could move through the whole world

After Team Tether created/held open the Rifts:
- the same continent was physically torn into separated land chunks
- enormous open chasms/Rifts now sit between those chunks
- old roads terminate at broken cliff edges
- bridges were ripped apart
- rivers may pour off severed land edges
- the torn edges should visibly correspond so a player can understand the pieces once fit together
- Team Tether machinery/towers stabilize and maintain the separation

Do NOT represent the before/after state mainly by changing colors or weather.

The key transformation is:
**ONE CONTINENT → PHYSICALLY SHATTERED CONTINENT**

As legendaries are freed, the corresponding Rift collapses and the region physically reconnects to the growing world.

---

# 4. Creature Design Rule

Future region rosters must contain genuinely new species.

Do NOT use the following as the normal creature-design method:
- deer + ice
- deer + lightning
- bird + fire
- bird + ice
- cat + dark
- cat + psychic
- Meadows species with elemental recolors

Rare crossover variants are allowed, but they are exceptions.

The standard roster philosophy should be closer to Pokémon / Palworld:
- recognizable fantasy creatures
- strong silhouettes
- different body plans
- varied sizes
- varied locomotion
- mascot creatures, tanks, predators, strange creatures, bugs, reptiles, amphibians, mammals, flying forms, and non-obvious fantasy forms

### Silhouette test

If all color, glow, and elemental VFX are removed, creatures from different regions should still look like different species.

If not, reject the design.

Each future region should eventually target roughly 10–12 cohesive creature species, subject to later scope decisions.

# 5. Region Order and Systems

## Region 1 — Meadows / Ground
Already substantially built.

Core systems.
First Regional Monument Blessing:
**Extended Sprint**

After Meadows completion:
- player is prompted to return to the village
- legendary talisman is returned to the Meadows monument
- monument blessing system is introduced
- player can activate Extended Sprint

---

## Region 2 — Electric Prairie

Visual:
- rolling prairie
- tall windblown grass
- dark storm clouds
- frequent lightning
- scattered trees and rocks
- charged ground veins
- Team Tether storm towers / machinery
- still recognizably a prairie, NOT a post-apocalyptic wasteland

New systems:
1. Environmental damage to the human
2. Flying unlock

Charged ground:
- electricity travels through specific ground sections
- damages the human
- teaches route planning
- makes healing, food, armor, and potions more important

Flying:
- flying-type mini-boss guards a special stone
- defeating it gives the material needed to build a Flying Saddle
- compatible creatures can then be flown

Creature roster direction:
- electric ferret/marten
- storm bison
- lightning insect/cricket
- electric snake
- strange prairie rodent
- unusual aerial mini-boss that is NOT simply another hawk
- additional original electric fantasy species

## Region 3 — Volcano Mountainside / Fire

Visual:
- broad volcanic mountainside
- black/brown rock
- bright readable lava channels
- sparse scorched vegetation
- smoke
- ash
- orange-red grasses
- Team Tether industrial structures
- clean readable routes rather than endless visual clutter

New systems:
1. Heat resistance / stronger survival preparation
2. Timed volcanic environmental hazards

Hazards:
- running lava damages the player
- lava bubbles
- eruptions can launch hot material
- debris can rain onto paths
- player learns timing and shelter

Climax:
- Team Tether base is inside the volcano
- player must descend a frightening path into the volcano to reach it

Creature roster direction:
- ember salamander
- ash beetle
- magma tortoise
- fire monkey/baboon-like fantasy creature
- smoke serpent
- armored volcanic predator
- other original fire species

# 6. Traversal Progression

Keep the traversal spine simple:

1. Walk / Sprint
2. Ride
3. Swim
4. Fly
5. Teleport

Do not add unnecessary parallel systems such as:
- glider
- grappling hook
- mandatory boats
- elaborate climbing
- digging traversal

Creature traversal remains central.

---

# 7. Environmental Escalation

Meadows:
Mostly safe.

Electric Prairie:
The ground can hurt you.

Volcano:
Terrain and eruptions can hurt you.

Water:
The environment controls movement.

Ice:
Climate punishes poor preparation.

Air:
Wind and terrain demand flight mastery.

Psychic:
Space/navigation becomes unreliable.

Dark:
Perception and light become part of survival.

---

# 8. Art Board Use

The attached boards are intended to communicate:
- world structure
- achievable visual target
- biome identity
- creature diversity expectations
- overall region order

They are NOT final production maps.

Before implementing any future region:
1. read the regional progression document
2. inspect current game systems
3. create a dedicated region design spec
4. create an original creature roster
5. validate the scope against the current production pipeline
6. do not begin implementation until the project's region-gating requirements allow it
