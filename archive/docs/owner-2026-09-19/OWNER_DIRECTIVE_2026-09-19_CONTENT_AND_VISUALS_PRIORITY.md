# Owner directive — add content and visuals as primary focus, alongside combat depth

**Owner call, 2026-09-19, verbatim:** "I think the game plays well as far as
systems go. We should primarily be focused on content throughout the map and
how the game looks." Corrected same day: **combat depth continues.** "Systems
play well" was about the *existing* systems being solid, not a call to stop
finishing the combat depth ladder that's already underway.

This is a priority addition, not a reopening of `docs/owner/OWNER_DIRECTIVE_2026-09-12_FINISH_MEADOWS_FIRST.md`'s
sequencing (Meadows first) or `OWNER_DIRECTIVE_2026-09-19_PARALLEL_MEADOWS_CLOUDREACH_LANES.md`'s
lane structure.

## What this means concretely

**Combat depth continues.** `docs/specs/COMBAT_DEPTH_PLAN.md`'s full ladder,
COMBAT-1 through COMBAT-7 (poise/stagger, creature wind, the telegraph retune
and burst-step dodge, the Y-skill/learnsets, AI reactions, the widened type
chart, the authored early-fight ladder), is not paused by this directive. Work
it in the sequence that plan already lays out in its §8.

**Content and visuals are added as co-equal primary focus, not a
replacement.** Alongside combat depth and ordinary correctness work, both
lanes should be reaching for:

- **Content throughout the map** — `docs/acceptance/MEADOWS_EXIT_CRITERION.md`
  category G (optional activities at 6-10 density, every detour pays into
  preparation, genuine roster temptation per region) and the off-path draws
  named in `OWNER_DIRECTIVE_2026-09-12_FINISH_MEADOWS_FIRST.md` Tier 3 (distant
  villages/glows/creature clusters visible from the path, NPCs who tell you
  where things are), plus its Tier 1 wayfinding items (the beacon system, map
  markers, NPC-driven map reveals) as the delivery mechanism for that content.
- **How the game looks** — categories B (creatures), C (NPCs/human cast), D
  (terrain/world), E (locations), J (one deliberate game, the Palworld/key-art
  bars) in the same exit criterion. This is exactly what the Cloudreach lane
  (`docs/CODEX_GOAL_2026-09-19_CLOUDREACH_VISUAL_PRODUCTION.md`) is already
  scoped to.

**Correctness and verification work is unaffected either way.** Bug fixes, the
Gate F continuous-campaign proof toward Meadows' A0–A11, save/multiplayer
reliability, and CI health continue exactly as already planned.

## For the next session picking up either lane

Don't read this directive as a reason to stop combat-depth work in favor of
content or visuals, or vice versa — all three are in scope now. Use ordinary
judgment on sequencing (finish what's mid-flight before starting something
new), and ask if a genuinely new, unplanned system (not already named in
`COMBAT_DEPTH_PLAN.md` or an existing spec) seems like it's needed.
