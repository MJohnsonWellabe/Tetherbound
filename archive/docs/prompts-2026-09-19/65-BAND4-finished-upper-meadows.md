# BAND 4 — FINISHED UPPER MEADOWS

## Goal
Turn crossing the restored river through defeating the three regional captains and opening the final approach into a finished regional gameplay experience.

## Player purpose
This region should answer:

> **Have I become powerful and versatile enough to challenge the regional stronghold?**

The player should visibly feel stronger here than at the beginning, while still finding meaningful reasons to improve the five.

## Regional identity
Use deliberate contrast inside one Meadows biome:
- broad high pasture/open fields;
- old-growth pockets;
- wind ridges and overlooks;
- ruined watch/occupation traces;
- Team Tether patrol camps;
- stronger resource formations;
- long sightlines that reveal creatures, captains and distant Hall/pylons.

Current Band 4 population cannot remain effectively one Meadowhart cluster.

## Required gameplay package
### Ironwood tier
Introduce the later preparation resource when its recipes matter. It should enable real utility/riding/final-assault preparation rather than become another collectible.

### Wild ecology
Populate upper habitats with stronger appropriate species, Meadowhart and other current canon species. Include at least one special encounter and multiple legitimate roster temptations.

### Riding payoff
Meadowhart + generic saddle progression should become genuinely useful here. Riding must improve revisiting/traversal without turning the creature into a dead combat slot.

### Three regional captains
Field, Ridge and Riverwatch (current canonical identities/data) should each:
- occupy a believable sub-location;
- test something distinguishable;
- reward a physical Sigil;
- contribute to a 0/3 → 3/3 objective;
- feel like preparation for the Warden rather than arbitrary errands.

### Route structure
Avoid one captain line. Use regional loops/branches/reconnects so the player makes route decisions while still understanding the macro goal.

### Resources/rewards
Ironwood and other useful materials/rewards should make exploration worthwhile and support final preparation.

### Camp/home rhythm
Distance and captain attrition should create a plausible reason to use a field camp or return home. Coordinate home progression so a return trip can feel valuable rather than mandatory backtracking.

### Optional content
At least one meaningful optional trainer/special wild/discovery should compete for attention with the captain route.

## Five-creature pressure
By this band, a normal player should already have encountered more than five plausible desirable creatures. Validate roster pressure explicitly.

## Evidence run
Cross restored river → explore Upper Meadows loops → obtain/use Ironwood tier → encounter Meadowhart/riding payoff → challenge captain routes → collect three Sigils → open final approach.

Record:
- team composition/levels before and after;
- which creatures tempted roster changes;
- riding utility;
- captain difficulty/identity;
- resource/reward usefulness;
- camp/home decisions;
- dead travel;
- whether the region feels like one huge field or several connected places.

## Acceptance
- region feels like the player has entered the chapter’s high-level frontier;
- ecology is rich enough to support team choice;
- open and old-growth compositions both work;
- Ironwood matters;
- riding pays off;
- captains are memorable and spatially distinct;
- three-Sigil progression is clear without feeling like a checklist corridor;
- roster pressure is real before the legendary.

## Definition of done
Opening the final approach feels like the result of a strong, experienced five conquering the Upper Meadows—not simply reaching the far end of the map.
