Branch: `ralph/W09-VFX-0904`. Model tier: creating (shader/particle) + visual judgement.

**Why.** Funded by the owner 2026-09-04. Every blind judge has said a fight looks like two models standing near each other; Bar B cannot reach yes without combat that produces a picture, and part of "beating creatures is way too easy" is that winning produces nothing on screen. Scope: (a) a hit spark/impact burst on every landed attack (scaled by damage, tinted by move type — `data/moves/*.json` / `docs/GAMEPLAY_SYSTEMS.md` §Combat), with a brief hit-flash on the struck body; (b) a level-up flourish on the creature that levelled (a rising ring/burst + brief rim glow, ~1.5 s); (c) a KO puff and a catch-success burst if cheap. All inside the installed kit: GPUParticles3D/CPUParticles3D with primitive quads, `shaders/` additions, no new meshes, no Meshy. Compatibility renderer (what ships) — test under `--rendering-driver opengl3`.

**Owns:** new `scripts/vfx/*.gd` (a small `CombatVfx` helper node that other systems call), new `shaders/vfx_*.gdshader`, `data/config/vfx.json` (tunables: sizes, durations, colours by type), ONE hook in `scripts/combat/combat_manager.gd` where damage is applied and ONE where a KO/catch resolves (keep each hook to a few lines that call your helper; another lane is editing `_flee_pressed()` in the same file — stay away from it), ONE hook for level-up: poll `Game.party` creature levels by `party.revision` in your helper (a progression feed is being built by another lane; do not build one; design your helper so it can later consume `Game.progression_feed` events of kind `level_up`), `tests/test_combat_vfx.gd` (real: the helper spawns, lives, and frees its node on a real damage event in a headless fixture), `tools/_capture_vfx_*.gd`, `ralph/reports/W09-VFX-0904/`. Do not touch `creature_body.gd` beyond reading its mesh/material for the flash (if the flash needs a material hook there, write the patch in the report instead of applying it).

**Verify.** `smoke_combat`, `smoke_boss`, `smoke_trainer_battle`, `smoke_catching`, `smoke_combat_camera` green; the headless test seen red with the helper disconnected. Capture under xvfb: a fight frame at the moment of impact and a level-up frame, using `tools/_capture_combat_moments.gd` as the model (it already stages a fight); one contact sheet; code-blind judge asks "does the fight read as an event / does the level-up read as a reward" and looks for artefacts (particles through terrain, wrong scale against the 1.80 m trainer). Two rounds max. Measure draw-call delta at `band1_open` with a fight running.

**Acceptance.** The fight frame contains a visible impact; the level-up frame contains a visible reward; smokes green; perf delta small and stated. Update `docs/CURRENT_STATE.md` CL-A2.
