Why the backlog looks the way it does

It's ordered by priority, not by date or lane — and that's load-bearing. The file's own first line is "Work top-down. This file is the state of the project," and every firing's actual instruction is to take the topmost unblocked item. Sort it by date instead and the loop loses the exact signal it reads to decide what to do next.

"Lane" isn't a category anything belongs to. Three lanes rotate through whatever's topmost and unclaimed — they aren't tied to subject matter, so grouping by lane would scatter related work at random. (The By Feature tab groups by subject instead.)

Closed items stay inline in the real file, but this view collapses them to a count per phase so the open work is what you see first.
