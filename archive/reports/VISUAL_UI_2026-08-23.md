# D2 HUD and menus — blind pass, round 1

One Fable critic, 23 frames, blind, judged at 40% downscale as the seven-inch
handheld proxy the owner actually plays on.
**(a) belongs to the keyart world — no. (b) reads as a finished game — no.**

This completes the sweep: **all eight domains are now judged.**

## A hard-rule violation

CLAUDE.md's hard rules include **"Controller first."** The interface is not.

- `02-starter-picker` — the **first interactive screen in the game** prompts
  with arrow keycaps and an Enter keycap.
- `04-dialogue-panel` — "Continue" is bound to an **E keycap**.
- `10-combat-hud` — a **mouse icon** (Pebble Toss), an **F keycap** (Throw) and
  **`[C]`** (Switch), in real-time combat.
- `12`-`18` — every menu footer is keyboard-first: *"Enter / A Select · Escape /
  B Close · Q / LB Prev tab"*.
- `14-menu-map` — *"[Minus] Zoom Out [Equal] Zoom In Right Stick Pan"*: two
  keyboard keys and a stick in one sentence.
- `19`-`23` — footers reverse the order (*"Craft: A / Enter"*), so the dual-prompt
  convention contradicts itself.

`06-hud-input-glyphs` proves controller glyphs EXIST, so this is a default and a
device-detection problem, not missing art. The critic's own prescription: *"show
the device you're holding, applied everywhere, in one order, in one glyph
style."*

## Two content defects that are not layout

**The Day-1 quest log spoils the chapter** (`15-menu-quest-log`). Twenty-plus
future main-story steps — through *"Defeat the Meadows Warden"* — print as a
flat, equally-weighted checklist on the first day, burying the one actionable
line second in a wall of identical rows, with no scroll affordance and the last
line clipped against the panel edge.

**Developer toggles lead the shipping settings menu** (`18-menu-settings`).
"Free build" and "Debug teleport", each with a paragraph explaining that they
are development settings, are the first two items a player sees, above Controls.
The keybinding table also lists Keyboard/mouse before Gamepad.

## Layering and overflow — the critic's #1 separating gap

> *"A shipped interface never prints one panel through another."*

- `10-combat-hud`: the exploration panel, a second TEAM roster and the health
  meter overprint each other — "TEAM 5/5" reads across half-visible "...apup",
  "120 / 120" ghosts through the combat roster. **Two team rosters on screen at
  once plus a third creature plate — the same creature named in three places.**
  At 40% the left third of the screen is illegible.
- `14-menu-map`: the HUD **bleeds through** the pause menu, and the minimap
  **draws on top of** it. The layering contract is broken in both directions in
  one frame.
- `19-craft-panel`: every row's ingredients spill into the next row's title,
  orphaned ghost strings sit behind the rows, lists truncate on a hanging comma,
  and the last row prints over the "Leave: B / Esc" footer.
- The quickbar clips "40/40" to half-glyphs in **all four** frames it appears in.
- `13-menu-creatures`: HP bars poke out from behind the portrait viewport.
- A stray unlabelled horizontal bar floats bottom-centre of every gameplay frame.

## Empty instruments

> *"Shipped interfaces earn their screen space by putting information inside the
> chrome; these panels are chrome around vacancy."*

The minimap is a frame around sky with no terrain or road (`05`, `07`); the world
map is a black column with one oversized village label at 0% surveyed (`14`); the
creature portrait is an unidentifiable close-up of fur on the screen whose job is
looking at your creatures (`13`); the roster identifies creatures by flat colour
swatches while the HUD uses real sprites — same data, two screens, two identity
systems; and `11-capture-reticle` **has no reticle**.

## Five visual languages

The panel system (dark card, teal accent, one sans) is *"a real, coherent
system"*. Against it sit the title screen's flat vector poster art, an anime
portrait pasted on an uncropped light-grey square, a sci-fi stencil display font
on the map and bond meter that *"fights the friendly meadow fiction"*, and the
swatch-vs-sprite split above.

On (a): *"Nothing in the panel language says 'meadow', 'cozy', or 'hand-made';
it says 'dark-mode tool'."* The bones are judged compatible with a reskin.

## Two frames this survey failed to produce

`08-party-strip` and `09-stamina-arc` are solid near-black. Either the harness
failed or the elements do not draw — *"both are failures someone must own before
this set can be called a survey of every HUD element."* Under investigation;
this sweep's harnesses have already photographed the wrong thing five times, so
the harness is the likelier cause and will be ruled out first.

## What works

`12-menu-backpack` is *"the best screen in the set"*. `21-storage-panel` and
`22-swap-panel` are *"small, legible, single-purpose dialogs that a shipped game
would not be ashamed of structurally"*. The title screen has correct
controller-only prompts. Teal-for-focus and red-for-KO discipline is mostly held.
