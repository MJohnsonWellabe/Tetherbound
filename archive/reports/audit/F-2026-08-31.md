## F2 — Team Tether escalates rumor → evidence → confrontation → operation → hierarchy → Warden, never as unrelated fights

**Test.** No single script asserts the six-stage shape by name. The
strongest machine-checked instrument that exists is
`tests/test_dialogue_runner.gd::test_no_dialogue_before_the_stronghold_names_the_legendary`
+ `test_the_exemption_is_exactly_one_file` (run live for this report as part
of a 151-test/0-failed batch, see below) — these enforce that no line spoken
anywhere in the game before the stronghold names the legendary, the
Veridian Stag, or "power source," so the reveal cannot leak early and
collapse the ladder into "you already knew." Beyond that, the escalation
itself is a **data/authorship** claim, corroborated by cross-referenced
design comments, not by a single continuous played run — see the caveat
below.

**Verdict: PASSES on authored structure and its one enforced invariant;
CANNOT BE FULLY DETERMINED whether a real continuous player experiences it
as escalating rather than disjointed**, because no post-`LAND-0830J`
continuous run has reached past the game's opening two segments (see
"Gate F chain status" below) — that confirmation is Lane 12
(`GATE-F-FULL`)'s deliverable, not reproducible from a data audit alone.

**Evidence for the authored ladder:**
- **Rumor:** Grandpa's opening line, `data/dialogue/opening.json:12-13`
  ("Team Tether's back. They've taken Meadows Hall...").
- **Evidence:** the Quarry/Warrens band is designed to "discover evidence of
  Team Tether activity" (`docs/TETHERBOUND_GAME_VISION.md:128`), and Sela's
  testimony at the relay (`data/dialogue/relay.json::relay_captive_freed`)
  delivers exactly spec §32's rung — the Rifts are artificial, the
  separation is actively maintained, power flows toward the Warden's
  stronghold, and she explicitly does **not** know the legendary is the
  source ("What's at the far end of it, I genuinely do not know").
- **Confrontation:** the Relay band (`data/config/bands/band3_the_river_lock/trainers.json`)
  is ranked `grunt, grunt, officer, captain, captain` — a visible escalation
  in one location, not a scattered set of fights.
- **Operation:** victory frees the captive, hands over the mill gear, and
  (per `data/config/meadow_healing.json`/`stronghold_climax.gd`) disables

  (`data/config/bands/band4_upper_meadows_ironwood/trainers.json`, two
  `captain`-rank entries plus a third band's worth) each carry a Sigil;

- **Hand-off is written, not just ranked:** the trainer ladder Dorn → Pell →

  character — Kest's own line, "not like I'm the last one you'll run into
  between here and the river," is the region pointing the player at Band 3.
  Confirmed by direct file read for this report, matching
  `ralph/reports/handover-T5-STORY-2-2026-08-30.md` §6's independent audit
  of all 27 trainers finding no gaps in the challenge/defeated pairing.

## F4 — The Warden is a character and an exam, not an HP pool

**Test.** `tests/smoke_gate_e_finale.gd` — a continuous, real in-engine run
that arrives at Meadows Hall with a full five-creature team at chapter-final
levels and fights the actual gauntlet through to the Warden using the
production `encounter_director.begin_trainer_battle()` path (fights are won
via the same HP-floor allowance `smoke_boss.gd`/`smoke_trainer_battle.gd`
use — this is a wiring test, not a balance test).

**Verdict: PASSES, both halves, run live for this report.**

```
$ godot --headless --path . --script tests/smoke_gate_e_finale.gd
...
beat the Warden; tracked objective now 'Shut down what Team Tether built under the Hall.'
...
gate E finale smoke test passed
```

- **Character.** `data/dialogue/stronghold.json`'s Warden lines
  (`stronghold_warden_challenge`, `stronghold_warden_defeated`) give him a
  sincere, non-cartoonish worldview: he confirms the tether readout rather
  than denying it, argues the human cost is worth paying, never says "you
  cannot stop me," and — on losing — concedes the fight but not his
  position, while still honoring his own wager and letting the player pass.
  Independently praised in `ralph/reports/handover-T5-STORY-2-2026-08-30.md`
  §6 as "worth leaving alone."
- **Exam, not an HP pool.**
  `data/config/bands/band5_stronghold_approach/trainers.json:140-199` — the
  Warden fields the chapter's **only** full team of five, spanning three
  types (2 Ground/1 Water/1 Air plus a Ground ace) at levels 16–20, a clear
  step above the Upper Meadows captains' 10–16. His own comment states
  plainly "THERE IS NO BOSS MODE AND NO BOSS SCRIPT" — same team schema,
  same `begin_trainer_battle()`, same defeat-flag mechanism as every other
  trainer, so no separate, untested "boss path" could quietly diverge from
  what the rest of the game exercises. Confirmed live: after the fight,
  `_director.call("can_challenge", ...)` on the Warden returns false — the
  chapter's last fight is not farmable/re-grindable.

**Caveat, honestly stated.** What is verified is the **wiring** — the fight
is reachable, uses a genuinely different team, and cannot be re-fought.
What is **not** verified by anything in this audit is whether the fight
*feels* like an exam of the player's own five under real (not HP-floored)
play — that is a difficulty/fun judgment only a human playtest can make,
and belongs on the owner's list.

**Cross-cutting note (audio, see also the coordinator's specific ask
below).** `scripts/audio/world_audio.gd::_wanted_track()` (read in full for
this report) returns only `"combat"`, `"village"`, `"exploration"`, or
`""`. The dedicated `warden` and `release` music tracks that
`T1-AUDIO` generated and configured are **never selected by anything**,
confirmed by reading the function's complete body — the climactic fight
plays under the same generic combat cue as a wild Bramblebun scrap. This
does not fail F4 (the fight is still a real exam of the team), but it is a
gap in how the climax is *delivered*, and is cheap to close (a hook call
from `stronghold_climax.gd`'s own stage transitions into `WorldAudio`,
per `ralph/reports/handover-T1-AUDIO-2026-08-30.md` §6 item 4, which
flagged this and declined to guess at the climax owner's state machine).

**Closing cost.** Narrative/mechanism: none outstanding. Owner-only: a
ROG Ally playtest of the Warden fight under real (non-floored) conditions,
~15 minutes. Optional, cheap: wire the warden/release music cues.

## F5 — The legendary volunteers, and the release ceremony carries enough history to hurt

**Test.** The same `tests/smoke_gate_e_finale.gd` run above,
`_pull_the_lever()` / `_the_full_belt_takes_the_decision()` steps — a real
UI-driven release ceremony: focuses a belt row, presses `ui_accept`, reads
the farewell confirmation text, presses through to resolution, and checks
the party afterward.

**Verdict: PASSES, run live for this report.**

- **Volunteers, not caught or forced.** `data/dialogue/stronghold.json::stronghold_legendary_joins`
  is explicit: "There is no ring to stop it and no reason left to be in
  this building, and it comes over anyway... Whatever this is, it is being
  asked, not taken — you have not so much as reached for an orb." The code
  path (`scripts/world/stronghold_climax.gd::_hand_over_the_legendary()`,
  read for this report) never uses a catch mechanism; it only ever routes
  through `Game.pending_catch` and the ordinary release-ceremony UI when the
  belt is full.
- **Carries real history.** `scripts/ui/tab_creatures.gd::_history_line()`
  composes battles fought, day caught, and levels earned since — and
  returns an empty string rather than a fabricated zero when there is
  nothing honest to say. The finale test **asserts on this directly**: the
  farewell body label must contain both `"Lv "` and `"bond"`
  (`tests/smoke_gate_e_finale.gd:526`), and the live run for this report
  passed that assertion and printed `the decision resolved: 'Kettle'
  released, the legendary on a belt of five` — a real creature, named and
  leveled by the test's own finale-team roster, actually given up on a full
  belt.
- **The decision is genuinely recorded, not merely cosmetic.** The test
  confirms the released creature is off the belt, the legendary (species id
  `veridian`) is on it, the party still holds exactly five afterward (the
  hard cap survives the ceremony), and the tracked objective moves on.

**Cross-cutting note.** Same audio gap as F4: the release-ceremony's own
generated music cue is never selected by `world_audio.gd`, so the ceremony
that is supposed to "hurt" currently resolves in silence or ambient
exploration audio rather than under a dedicated cue. Not a narrative-text
defect; a delivery gap, cheap to close, flagged in the same place as F4's.

**Closing cost.** None outstanding on mechanism or writing. Owner-only: a
human playtest to confirm the ceremony's emotional weight actually lands —
inherently a subjective judgment the audit cannot make. Optional, cheap:
the same music-hook wiring named under F4.

## F6 — The ending shows the world healed rather than stating it, and points at the next biome without entering it

**Test.** The same `tests/smoke_gate_e_finale.gd` run,
`_the_region_answers()` / `_the_meadows_acknowledges_it()` steps, plus
direct reads of `data/config/meadow_healing.json` and
`data/dialogue/meadows_freed.json`.

**Verdict: PASSES, run live for this report.**

```
[meadow] the tether let go: 1916 plants back, 15 tether lights out, 0 barriers open, 4 beaten patrols withdrawn
...
the region answered: 1916 plants back, 15 tether lights out, 0 barriers open, 4 patrols withdrawn
the Meadows acknowledged the victory and the objective chain terminated
```

- **Shows, not states.** Confirmed as a *world-state* change, not a text
  box: `data/config/meadow_healing.json` restores the exact scatter
  instances the drain thinned (same models/positions/scales), fades the
  relay's dead-ground overlay over ~12 real seconds (long enough to be seen
  if the player is standing there, short enough to have visibly changed by
  the walk home), and turns every lit Team Tether pylon/conduit dark. The
  live run measured 1,916 plants regrown and 15 tether lights extinguished
  as real, counted world objects, not an asserted flag.
  - **"0 barriers open" in the log is not a defect**: this test's own setup
    (`ARRIVED_AT_THE_HALL`) pre-sets `road_gate_open` and
    `hall_approach_open` as flags a player would already have earned by
    ordinary means before reaching the Hall, so by the time healing runs
    there is nothing left for it to newly open — confirmed by reading
    `meadow_healing.json`'s own comment that barriers are opened "for the
    ordinary reason" a save already carries, not a second mechanism. Noted
    here explicitly so a future reader does not mistake it for a healing
    failure.
  - The one deliberate, honest exception: the quarry floor's baked
    discoloration does **not** heal (D45's own recorded decision — the
    damage was baked into terrain textures and re-baking is out of scope),
    and the Quarry Foreman says so to the player's face
    (`data/dialogue/meadows_freed.json::village_quarry_foreman_freed`,
    "Floor's still grey down there... I'll take the rim.") rather than the
    game silently pretending everything healed.
- **Points at what's next without entering it.** Kell, a **post-victory-only**
  NPC (`place_when: {if_flag: legendary_freed}`, so she does not exist in
  the world until the ending), stands at the Storm Road's dead end and
  speaks of "seven roads... six to go" — explicitly framed as a question,
  not a plan, and the road past her is exactly as impassable as it was in
  hour one (confirmed by the same `severed_spokes.gd` mechanism, unchanged).
  This matches `CLAUDE.md`'s hard rule (no Biome 2 implementation; any
  reconnection view is distant/non-enterable) and
  `docs/TETHERBOUND_GAME_VISION.md`'s own description of the intended
  effect (distant landmass, a storm wall dissipating, etc., never an
  enterable neighbor).
- **The chapter actually ends.** The finale run confirms the objective
  chain terminates cleanly (`_quest_line()` returns `""` after a
  post-victory villager is greeted) rather than leaving a stale tracked
  line — itself a callback to the exact class of defect T5-STORY-2 found
  and fixed under F3 (a stuck tracked objective).

**Closing cost.** None outstanding.
