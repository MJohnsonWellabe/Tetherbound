# Band 2 (Stone & Root) — round 1

**What changed since the last round:** nothing yet — this is the baseline. Band 2
now has its first authored content (Old Quarry evidence site, Burrow Warrens
required dungeon, a ranger camp prop cluster, ridge harvest/spawn fill) and,
as of this round, real vegetation scatter across the whole corridor
(`VEG-CORRIDOR`) for the first time. These seven frames are what that looks
like before any critique-driven fixes.

**Critic's verdict, in its own words.** Bar A (belongs to the keyart world):
**No.** Bar B (reads as trying to be Palworld): **No.**

Top three gaps it named, ranked: (1) world density/emptiness in frames 01, 05,
03 — "frame 05 here is a bare hill with nothing but a barely-visible smudge on
the horizon"; (2) named locations don't deliver their content — "'ranger camp'
has no tent, fire, or crate of any kind... indistinguishable from any other
empty hillside," and the quarry overlook "has no excavation, no stone
stockpile, nothing quarry-like"; (3) the two night frames are "crushed to
near-total black... failing the keyart's explicit 'day and night create
different moods' direction by simply not rendering a scene."

Also named: an unexplained "kite-shaped shadow" intruding bottom-centre in
frames 02/03/04; a "cyan wireframe/line-only structure" on the quarry hill
that "looks like debug/gizmo geometry"; a floating icon in frame 01 and a
"cyan sparkle" in frame 03 with no visible source; oxblood-red tree foliage
at the Warrens reading as the reserved danger colour leaking onto ordinary
scenery; and that no creature in the set reads clearly as a creature.

Full verdict kept out of git (it's long); see the round's own investigation
notes on `ralph-status`/`ralph/NOTES.md` for the complete text and what round
2 does about each finding.
