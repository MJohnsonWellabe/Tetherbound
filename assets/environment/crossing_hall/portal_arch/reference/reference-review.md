# Portal arch reference review

**Verdict: HOLD both drafts.** This independent review inspected only `portal-arch-v1.png` and `portal-arch-v2.png`. No mesh, engine, generation service, or physical measurements were inspected.

The warm tan stone, chipped block edges, and legible arch silhouette fit the proposed Hall material family. Both drafts clearly reserve one circular emblem socket and one blank name plaque. The broad cap, stepped shoulders, heavy bases, and repeated carved pilaster motifs make the asset feel monumental and formal; the amount of ornament competes with its role as a reusable compact arch.

The visible aperture is the blocking issue. In both drafts it reads tall and narrow, occupying roughly half to three-fifths of the outer width. The brief requires an opening of at least 2.4 m within a total width of at most 3.2 m, which requires an opening-to-total-width ratio of at least 75%. The drawings do not visibly support that relationship. This is a comparison of image proportions, not certification of any 3D dimension. The opening also reads narrower relative to its height than the intended 2.4 m by 3.6 m minimum envelope.

| Draft | Reference suitability |
| --- | --- |
| v1 | Clear front-facing structure and stone treatment, but bulky piers and projecting ornament constrict the opening. Very tight framing at the crest and base reduces confidence in the complete outline. HOLD. |
| v2 | Better whole-asset framing, with the crest and bases more comfortably visible. The aperture remains narrow and the ornament remains heavy. Better starting reference, but HOLD. |

Before using either as an image-to-3D reference, widen the aperture relative to the complete silhouette, slim the piers and projections, and simplify the repeated carvings while retaining the single plaque and circular emblem socket. Clean the conspicuous colored fringe around portions of the cutout edge. A clear reference should show the entire asset with margin and provide enough depth information to resolve side thickness; these frontal drafts leave the back, thickness, and opening depth ambiguous.

Actual opening clearance, total width, the relationship to a 1.8 m trainer, topology, collision, emblem/plaque modularity, three material-state variants, and in-engine appearance remain **OPEN**. Neither draft establishes 3D scale or full art acceptance.
