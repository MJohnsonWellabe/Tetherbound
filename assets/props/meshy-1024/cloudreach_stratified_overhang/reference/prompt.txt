A single game environment prop: a sculpted cantilevered rock ledge that juts out from a cliff, about 40 m wide along the wall and reaching about 42 m outward, about 20 m thick at the wall and tapering to a broken lip (side profile like the attached section). Pale warm-grey stratified limestone (#c8beaa to #a89c88) in rounded, stacked horizontal bands with moss on each band edge, a thin green grassy top lip, and an underside that steps and tapers in organic overhanging tiers down to a broken irregular edge, like the underside of the attached floating islet. No flat bottom, no straight extrusion, no box shape. The back face is flat and open where it meets a cliff. Stylised realism, painterly fantasy sky-island rock. No buildings, no towers, no banners, no people, no text, no red. ONE three-quarter view from slightly below so the tiered underside shows, whole object in frame, plain light-grey background, even studio light.
