Concept (image-to-image from board_exterior_crop.png, board_falls_entrance_crop.png, board_palette_crop.png):
Tidewake hero landmark piece: ONE modular tier of the Veilfall cascade cliff, about 18 m wide, 10 m deep and 12 m tall. Pale grey stratified limestone in clear horizontal bedding courses with weathered, slightly overhanging lips; a wide notch at the top edge where a broad white waterfall pours over and down the face; the stone in the pour line wet and darker; moss, small ferns and a few stunted pines on the dry ledges and shoulders; a shallow turquoise catch basin cut into the top surface behind the notch. Flat, level base and top so three or four tiers can be stacked and offset into the board's terraced mountain. Stylised realism in the Palworld / Tetherbound key-art style: chunky readable silhouette, hand-painted materials with real depth and wear, no photographic noise. ONE isolated object, whole object in frame, three-quarter view, plain light-grey background, even studio light, no text, no labels, no people, no creatures, no surrounding terrain. No red or oxblood anywhere. For image-to-3D.

Placement: replaces the faceted near-black terrain faces at the Veilfall approach (F39-look.json veilfall-near stand, ~(384, 2.2, 3805)), stacked on the existing mountain. Judged in vf2 against vf1's limestone material candidate (4.20) and shipping (3.35). Lands flag-OFF.
