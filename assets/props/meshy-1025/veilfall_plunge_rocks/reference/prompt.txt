Concept (image-to-image from board_approach_crop.png, board_palette_crop.png):
Tidewake: the base of a waterfall. A cluster of water-worn pale grey limestone boulders, about 9 m across, 7 m deep and 3 m tall, ringed around a shallow turquoise plunge basin. Boulders polished and darker where falling water strikes them, white mineral crust at the waterline, a few ferns and moss in the crevices, foam-white spray lines carved into the near face. Stylised realism in the Palworld / Tetherbound key-art style: chunky readable silhouette, hand-painted materials with real depth and wear, no photographic noise. ONE isolated object, whole object in frame, three-quarter view, plain light-grey background, even studio light, no text, no labels, no people, no creatures, no surrounding terrain. No red or oxblood anywhere. For image-to-3D.

Placement: at the foot of the Veilfall falls, where the judges say "the waterfall stops partway down with no plunge pool or spray". Lands flag-OFF.
