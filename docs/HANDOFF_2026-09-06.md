## 5. Non-art work still open

### 5.1 Cloudreach grass continuity (owner, 2026-09-06: "in grass areas it should be continuous, not abrupt stops")

Diagnosis from the code, not yet fixed:

- Both grass layers are **patch-bounded**. `cloudreach_world.gd` registers `_cover_patches`
  (ellipses of fixed half-sizes at regions, landmarks, pads, settlements, shelves; segments
  along routes) and `cloudreach_ground_cover.gd::build` fills only those shapes. The
  finish layer in `cloudreach_look.gd::_dress_ground_cover_finish` raycasts, but again only
  inside the same ellipses. Turf outside any registered ellipse gets nothing, which is the
  hard edge in the renders.
- The main layer's `visibility_range_m` is 260 m and the far tier is 0.06 blades/m² at
  900 m, so there is also a **distance cliff**: dense blades to ~260 m, then almost bare.
- Blade scale differs between layers (0.30–0.64 vs `far_scale_m` 1.3), which the judge read
  as "07 spikes vs 01 blades".

Fix direction (one pass, `cloudreach_look.json.ground_cover_finish` stays the tunable home):
sample a regular grid over the whole realm footprint, raycast down, keep every hit whose
collider is turf-topped (`_is_turf_top` exists) and outside paths/yards/exclusions, and
distribute the instance budget by distance bands so density fades smoothly to the horizon
instead of stopping; unify scale; keep `region_grass_patch_cap`-style budgets as totals.
Acceptance: the 12-stand sheet shows no bare green plane anywhere between camera and
horizon, and `probe_cloudreach_wild_performance.gd` frame time holds.

## 6. Traps this session paid for (read before rendering or tuning lights)

1. **Compatibility renderer light caps.** `rendering/limits/opengl/max_lights_per_object`
   was 8 and `max_renderable_lights` 32. A Hall floor is one slab per chamber inside ~13
   omni ranges, so torches were silently dropped on exactly the floor; the Hall stands ~50
   omnis in one frustum. Both are raised (16 / 64) in `project.godot`; `smoke_stronghold`
   reads the project value. Two blind rounds were spent before this was found.
2. **ACES toe at night.** `art.json::_comment_night_light` already records that small
   energies vanish under the night exposure; the 6.5-energy torches were under the toe.
   Order-of-magnitude changes move night frames; 20 % nudges do not.
3. **Flames behind pilasters.** A sconce 0.15 m off a wall whose bays project 0.36 m is
   invisible from every grazing stand. Wall torches now stand 0.6 m proud.
4. **Render cost under software GL.** `capture_cloudreach_environment_correction.gd`
   supersamples to 1920×1200 and samples 42 frames per stand: one stand ate 9 minutes.
   `tools/_capture_cloudreach_cliff_options.gd` (native size, 9 frames, `--out=`,
   `--only=`) renders three stands in ~5 minutes. Use it, or the same settings.
5. **Memory.** `~/godot-bin/godot` is a 3-slot flock wrapper; a Meadows boot is ~3.3 GB,
   a render ~7 GB. Never run two renders at once. Fixture smokes (`smoke_warrens_fixture`,
   Cloudreach scene boots) are seconds; boot the Meadows once at the end.
6. **`json.dump` reformats whole files.** Twice this session a one-key edit rewrote every
   array in `stronghold.json`/`burrow_warrens.json`. Edit lines with `sed`/targeted python,
   or accept the churn knowingly.
7. **Source-string test pins.** `test_cloudreach_continuous_harness.gd` pinned literal
   code strings and went red on the lane merge; it now pins behaviour-bearing names. Do not
   add new `world_source.contains(...)` assertions.
8. **CI run badge lies.** The known-red pair cancels itself, making the run "cancelled".
   Read job conclusions (`actions_list list_workflow_jobs`).
9. **Blind judge reads exposure off the PNG.** Measure Rec.709 medians and floor-band
   medians yourself before spawning a judge; the judge will, and a round costs 15 minutes.
10. **Lane check-ins.** `send_later` self-check-ins every 30–45 minutes kept two remote lanes
    merging without a human; one fired after the work was already done. Always re-arm
    until the lane is idle, then archive the session only when the owner says so.
