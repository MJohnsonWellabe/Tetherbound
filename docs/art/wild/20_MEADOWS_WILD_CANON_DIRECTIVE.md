# Meadows Wild Roster — Canon Directive For Claude

## Instruction
Make this Meadows wild roster **canon** for Tetherbound.

For the Meadows biome wild-creature roster, treat the art and documents in this folder as the current authoritative source of truth.

## This pack canonizes
- the final Meadows wild-species list
- the final Meadows wild-species names
- the Meadows evolution rule
- the Meadows scale rule
- the Meadows visual distinctions between species
- the Meadows palette and silhouette guidance

## Superseded assumptions
The following older assumptions are no longer canonical for Meadows:
- that the canine evolves in Meadows
- that multiple Meadows species evolve
- that the deer should resemble the legendary
- that the canine should resemble the Ground starter
- that the hawk/eagle should resemble the Air starter
- that old exploratory board names are the final roster

## Current canon
### Evolution
Only **one** Meadows creature evolves:
- **Mudsnout -> Tuskroot**

No other Meadows wild creature currently evolves in canon.

### Distinction rules
- **Meadowhart** must remain clearly distinct from the Meadows legendary.
- **Trailpup** must remain clearly distinct from the Ground starter.
- **Galecrest** must remain clearly distinct from the Air starter.
- Starters are excluded from the Meadows wild roster.

### Scale rule
All Meadows wild Pals should feel broadly in the player's scale world:
- some smaller
- some a little larger
- but on average visually around the player-character scale

Do not design the roster as tiny critters plus one giant mount.  
Keep the roster feeling physically present, rideable where appropriate, and readable at third-person gameplay distance.
