# Water (Biome 4) — Start Here

**Status: design only. Do not implement yet.**

This directory holds the design contract for Biome 4, the Water
Archipelago:

- `TETHERBOUND_BIOME4_WATER_ARCHIPELAGO_DESIGN_DIRECTIVE.md` — the
  high-level creative/gameplay contract: world fantasy, swimming and
  drowning rules, dock-gated progression, the Water Dragon Alpha and Swim
  Saddle, currents, Skill Candy, Team Tether escalation, the final
  waterfall stronghold, and the completion bar. Read this in full before
  any Water design or implementation work begins.

Water is **Stage B** in `docs/DEVELOPMENT_ROADMAP.md`, executed after
Stormwood (Stage A) and before the four-biome product audit (Stage C).
Before writing implementation code, complete the directive's §23 "Later
Design Deliverables" — the island/dock map, full creature roster, Water
Dragon Alpha production board, Swim Saddle board, Skill Candy board,
NPC/Team Tether visual additions, final-island/waterfall stronghold
concept board, legendary/Warden design, and the numeric tuning and
multiplayer-authority specs — at the same depth Stormwood received in
`docs/biomes/stormwood/BUILD_STORMWOOD_TO_COMPLETION.md` before Codex
started building it.

Water must be built on the multiplayer-capable architecture from the
first implementation, per the directive's §18 and `docs/DEVELOPMENT_ROADMAP.md`'s
standing rule that all post-multiplayer work is multiplayer-native.
