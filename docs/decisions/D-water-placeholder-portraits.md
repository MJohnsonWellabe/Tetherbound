# Water placeholder portraits

Water's twelve namespaced creatures temporarily reuse the portrait of the installed species explicitly recorded in `water_roster.json` at `species/<board_id>/placeholder/source_species`, matching their borrowed bodies. The HUD and Creatures menu share one resolver; a dedicated `assets/ui/portraits/creatures/water_<board_id>.png` takes priority when delivered. These borrowed portraits are temporary presentation, not accepted Water reference art. Replace them alongside the owner-authorized creature mesh sequence and judge the resulting screens; no image generation or duplicated final-art files are part of this change. The exact five Water swimmers supplement the unchanged five Meadows mounts, while Tidecoil, Abyssal Guardian and the remaining Water species cannot inherit rideability from their borrowed bodies.
