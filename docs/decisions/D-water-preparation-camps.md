# Water preparation camps

Each of the eight main islands receives an authored camp seven metres beside its first graded inland approach. The installed tent, bedroll, fire, workbench and creature bed make its services visible; existing RestPoint, NightRest sleep voting, CraftPanel and creature-bed recovery own the behavior. Water reserves creature-bed indices -41 through -48, distinct from installed earlier chapters, and creates no player-building achievement while loading these camps. Mara now greets the First Shore arrival, Pell stands beside the swimming lesson, and Elin stands beside its camp, shortening the opening's unnecessary journey to the island centre. These are starting compositions requiring real interaction and blind visual evidence, not a visual completion claim.
