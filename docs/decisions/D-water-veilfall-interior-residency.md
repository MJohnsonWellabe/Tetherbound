# Veilfall interior residency

The waterfall entrance remains at the end of the authored Veilfall hike. Behind its curtain, a short entrance interaction moves the player into a concealed cave interior in the same Water realm. The interior occupies a separate collision area at the edge of the realm rather than removing mountain terrain needed by another player outside. Its geometry exists on every occupied Water shell, and only its local interior presentation is shown to a player inside; entering does not replace the realm or move another player. The normal player position save and owner replication therefore retain independent exterior/interior residency. Floor and swimming queries must recognize the interior's built surfaces. This is an implementation choice for the board's mountain-hidden Heart Chamber, not a substitute for its waterfall, hike, pumps, sluices, bridge, captain or release sequence; those remain required runtime and visual evidence.
