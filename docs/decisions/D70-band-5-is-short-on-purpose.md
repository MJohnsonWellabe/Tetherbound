# D70 — Band 5 is short on purpose; the payoff is inside the Hall

**Decided:** 2026-08-30, by the owner, in session.
**Status:** Settled. Do not reopen without newer owner direction.

## The measurement that raised the question

A corridor census (`tools/_probe_gate_f_corridor.gd`, run 2026-08-30) measured
the chapter at 11,519 m and found band 5 to be **651 m — 2.7 minutes of walking,
5.6% of the chapter's length**, against band 4's 3,436 m.

| Band | Length | Walk | Things met | Worst gap |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Lower Meadows | 2403 m | 10.0 min | 122 | 106 m |
| 2 Stone & Root | 2653 m | 11.1 min | 101 | 165 m |
| 3 River Lock | 2375 m | 9.9 min | 107 | 163 m |
| 4 Upper Meadows | 3436 m | 14.3 min | 206 | 156 m |
| 5 Stronghold Approach | **651 m** | **2.7 min** | 53 | 64 m |

The coordinator raised this as a possible structural gap, on the reading that
`TETHERBOUND_MEADOWS_MIDGAME_FUN_REBUILD.md` §15 asks the Stronghold Approach to
deliver seven distinct things — the Hall becoming visually dominant, increased
Team Tether hardware, more drained land, stronger wild creatures, a final roster
temptation, a clear final camp, and an optional challenge — and that 2.7 minutes
of travel is short runway for all seven.

## The decision

**Band 5 does not need to be longer.** The owner's words: *"I don't think it
needs to be super long. It's the payoff inside the stronghold or Hall. It should
be all about combat and freeing the legendary."*

So band 5's job is **arrival, not journey**. Its length is correct.

## What this means in practice

- **Do not pad band 5's corridor** to raise its metre count, its POI count or its
  gather density. A future cadence pass that flags band 5 as thin is measuring
  against the wrong intent; this file is the answer.
- Band 5's own worst gap is already the best in the chapter at **64 m**, so the
  §12 cadence target is met on the approach as it stands.
- **§15's beats still have to land** — they just land compressed, or at the end of
  band 4 where the climb toward the Hall actually begins. "Are these the five I
  want to take in?" is a real beat and it needs somewhere to happen; the runway
  for it is the tail of band 4, not an extended band 5.
- The weight belongs **inside** the Hall: the gauntlet, the elite, the Warden, the
  machine, the legendary release, and the five-creature ceremony. That content is
  interior and is not measured by a corridor probe at all, which is part of why
  the corridor number looked alarming and was not.

## What was NOT decided here

This says nothing about band 5's **visual** treatment. The Hall rebuild
(`ralph/reports/HALL_DESIGN_2026-08-30.md`) and the band-5 skyline's untextured
horizon masses are live Track 1 work and remain in scope — a short approach makes
the composition along it *more* load-bearing, not less, because the player spends
those 2.7 minutes looking at the thing they are about to fight.

Nor does it lower the bar on band 5's roster temptation. §15's "at least one final
tempting roster opportunity" still applies; the creature expansion places
Stormtrail and Ashtusk on Team Tether hardware and industrial sites, which is
band 5's own terrain.
