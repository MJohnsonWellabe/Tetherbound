# D74 — Band 5 gets findables, off the road; D70's shape and P-5.3's road are kept

**Decided:** 2026-09-04, by the W18-DENSITY-B4-B5 lane, under the common lane rule
("make the smallest defensible call, record it, continue").
**Status:** accepted unless newer owner direction says otherwise.

## The conflict

Two settled documents disagree about the Stronghold Approach, and the density
pass had to author into both:

- `docs/specs/GATE3_ENCOUNTER_CONTRACTS.md` **P-5.3**: *"Nothing else is added to
  the road. Not a trainer, not a cache, not a cluster. Fails if the band's entry
  count rises for any reason other than P-5.2 and §7."* And **D70**: band 5 is
  short on purpose; do not pad its corridor to raise its counts.
- `docs/owner/OWNER_DIRECTIVES_2026-09-04-C.md` §2–§3 (newer, and owner):
  candy and useful findables across the *full* chapter, placed by world logic —
  *"dangerous spaces: stronger supplies; secrets: high-value pickups; deep travel
  and difficult regions: recovery resources where useful"* — and
  `docs/FINISH_THE_MEADOWS_ADDENDUM_2026-09-04.md` §B: *"the distribution should
  become more rewarding as the player moves into harder regions."* The W18 brief
  applies that to band 5 explicitly.

Precedence (`CLAUDE.md`) puts the owner directive first. The question is only
how to honour it without breaking what P-5.3 and D70 protect.

## The decision

1. **Band 5 gets its share of the chapter's findables — 15 pickups (7 Good, 3 Great,
   1 Rare, 4 recovery) — and every one of them sits at a place the band already
   names, off the road's own line:** the seam's west shoulder, the outer watch,
   the danger beat (the alpha Galecrest pack), the scorched pocket P-5.2 announces,
   the Sigil gate's near-side wing, the dell behind the waystop, and the doorstep
   alpha of §7 R-3. The Rare is at the doorstep alpha, the band's hardest optional
   encounter.
2. **Three basic supplies are the only on-road entries** (a Large Potion at the
   mouth before Corr, a Small Potion before Warder Ness, and the road watch's own
   dropped Good Candy), per the directive's *"main routes: occasional basic
   supplies"*. No Great or Rare is on the road.
3. **The road's beat list is unchanged.** No trainer, no prop cache and no cluster
   is added on the road. The two harvest nodes and the one wild cluster this pass
   adds are all off-road (an ironwood and burrowback under the outer watch's own
   stonework; stone on the west shoulder), which is the "crescendo of occupation"
   D70 asks for rather than a repeat of band 1.
4. **The waystop clearing stays untouched** (§7 R-1: three things and nothing
   else; R-5: nothing at the waystop heals for free). The nearest pickup is 55 m
   outside it.
5. **Recovery arrives before the attrition it supports**, never after: before Corr,
   before the alpha pack, before Ness. Nothing is placed between the Sigil gate and
   the works except the Rare at the doorstep alpha, which is a temptation, not a
   heal.

## What this does not decide

The chapter-wide candy budget (~60/30/10) and its progression-safety tests belong
to the candy/items lane; band 5's 11 candies are its share of the addendum's
"more rewarding as the player moves into harder regions", not a separate budget.
P-5.1 (the Hall must grow) and P-5.2 (the pocket visible from the spine) are
world-lane items and are not touched by this pass.
