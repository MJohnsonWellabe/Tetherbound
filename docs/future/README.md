# Future content vision — Regions 2–8 and the world structure

**Status: VISION ONLY. Nothing in this directory is authorized for implementation.**

Owner-supplied direction, committed 2026-08-30 so the intended progression spine
survives until production reaches it. The binding rule is the one both documents
carry and `CLAUDE.md` enforces:

> Do not begin building Regions 2–8 until the Meadows passes its exit criteria
> (`ralph/MEADOWS_EXIT_CRITERION.md`). Any reconnection view stays distant and
> non-enterable.

## What is here

- **`TETHERBOUND_REGIONAL_PROGRESSION_PLAN.md`** — the high-level region order
  (Meadows → Electric Prairie → Volcano → Wetlands → Frozen Mountain → Sky
  Highlands → Psychic Wilderness → Dark Forest), the 1–2-new-systems-per-region
  rule, the Regional Monument Blessing system (Meadows blessing: Extended
  Sprint), the traversal ladder (walk → ride → swim → fly → teleport), the
  environmental-escalation spine, and the explicit list of systems NOT to build.
- **`FUTURE_REGIONS_ART_AND_WORLD_HANDOFF.md`** — the corrected art/world
  handoff: one continent physically shattered by the Rifts into separated
  chunks that visibly fit back together; Palworld/Fortnite-level stylized
  environmental target (not ultra-detailed dark fantasy); Meadows is existing
  canon and is not redesigned from concept art; future rosters need genuinely
  new species and silhouettes, with elemental refits as rare exceptions.
- **`boards/`** — the corrected visual reference boards the handoff refers to.
  Directional references, not literal build orders.

## Precedence notes

- These are owner-supplied directives and sit at precedence level 1 of
  `CLAUDE.md`'s canon ordering for what they cover (future regions and world
  structure). They do **not** override anything about the Meadows itself —
  Meadows canon stays with `docs/MEADOWS_PROGRESSION_SPEC.md` and the current
  game plan.
- The one-blessing-active-at-a-time restriction is explicitly **not locked**
  in the plan; treat it as an open design question for the owner when Region 2
  planning actually starts.
- The Meadows hard rules (five creatures, no storage, no Biome 2 until the
  exit gate, Meshy discipline) are unchanged by anything here.
