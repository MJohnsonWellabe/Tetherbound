# Tetherbound — Regional Progression & System Introduction Plan

## Purpose

This document captures the high-level region order, biome themes, traversal progression, environmental hazards, and major systems to introduce across Tetherbound after the Meadows.

The goal is to keep each region mechanically distinct without turning every biome into a completely new game.

A strong rule for the full game:

> **Each region should introduce only 1–2 major new systems.**

The player should feel more capable and more prepared with every region, while the world becomes more dangerous and mechanically demanding.

---

# Core Region Order

## 1. Ground — Meadows

### Theme
Rolling meadows, prairie, oak groves, streams, ponds, villages, old roads, Burrow Warrens, and Meadows Hall.

### Main purpose
Introduce the complete core Tetherbound loop.

### Systems introduced
- movement
- stamina
- gathering
- crafting
- building
- food
- creature catching
- five-creature ownership limit
- creature bonding
- real-time combat
- trainer battles
- riding
- basic survival preparation
- Team Tether
- legendary stronghold progression

### Post-region progression

After defeating the Meadows Warden and freeing the Meadows legendary:

1. The player is prompted to return to the village.
2. The player brings the legendary talisman back to the Meadows monument.
3. The talisman is placed into the monument.
4. This introduces the **Regional Monument Blessing system**.
5. The Meadows blessing becomes available.

### Meadows Monument Blessing
**Extended Sprint**

Effect:
- meaningfully increases how long the player can sprint before stamina exhaustion

Purpose:
- immediately useful everywhere
- improves traversal without trivializing the game
- establishes why returning talismans to regional monuments matters

---

# Regional Monument Blessing System

Each major region has:
- a legendary
- a legendary talisman
- a corresponding regional monument

After completing a region, the player returns the talisman to that monument.

This grants a regional blessing.

### Design direction

Blessings should:
- improve the player across the entire world
- reflect the identity of the completed region
- feel meaningful without becoming mandatory power creep
- create a reason to return to settlements / monuments after major victories

### Possible restriction

Consider allowing only **one regional blessing to be active at a time**, especially early in the game.

This turns blessings into strategic choices rather than eight passive stat increases.

That decision is not locked yet.

# 2. Electric — Storm Prairie

## Theme
A wide electric prairie under constant or frequent storms.

Visual identity:
- dark storm clouds
- windblown grass
- lightning
- scorched trees
- exposed ridges
- crackling energy
- old electrical / Team Tether machinery
- patches of electrically charged ground

This region should still feel open like the Meadows, but much more dangerous.

## Major new systems

### 1. Environmental damage to the human

This is the first region where the land itself can directly injure the player outside combat.

Electricity can travel through certain sections of the ground.

The player must:
- identify unsafe zones
- route around them
- time movement
- use food, potions, armor, and preparation more seriously

This is the first major step in making the **human survival layer** matter.

### 2. Flying

Flying is introduced in this region.

The flying unlock comes from defeating the region's mini-boss.

### Flying unlock structure

The mini-boss is:
- Flying type
- guarding a special stone

Defeating it grants:
**Flying Stone** / equivalent named item

The stone allows the player to craft:
**Flying Saddle**

The player can then use compatible creatures for aerial traversal.

Flying should feel like a major freedom increase.

## Environmental purpose

The electrical hazards should make these systems more important:
- healing items
- human HP
- food buffs
- armor
- preparation
- route planning

---

# 3. Fire — Volcano Mountainside

## Theme

A volcanic mountainside built around dangerous vertical descent.

Visual identity:
- black rock
- ash
- lava channels
- smoke
- heat shimmer
- scorched vegetation
- glowing cracks
- cliff paths
- industrial Team Tether structures bolted into volcanic ruins

## Major new systems

### 1. Heat resistance / preparation

The Fire region should make resistance preparation matter more strongly.

Useful tools may include:
- heat-resistant food
- potions
- armor
- temporary buffs
- safe rest zones

Do not overcomplicate this into a constant survival meter unless playtesting demands it.

### 2. Timed environmental hazards

Running lava damages the player.

Lava pools and channels may:
- bubble
- erupt
- throw molten debris into the air
- rain hazardous material onto nearby paths

The player should learn to:
- watch the environment
- time movement
- use shelter
- choose safer routes

## Stronghold climax

The regional Team Tether base is **inside the volcano**.

The climax begins with a dangerous descent.

The player should see where they are going before they reach it.

The final approach should feel:
- intimidating
- deep
- hot
- industrial
- increasingly hostile

The descent itself should be memorable before the stronghold battle begins.

---

# 4. Water — Wetlands / Flooded Coast

## Theme

A wetland and flooded coastal region.

Possible geography:
- marshes
- winding rivers
- partially submerged ruins
- islands
- reed fields
- flooded villages
- old bridges
- coastal cliffs
- waterlogged Team Tether structures

## Major new systems

### 1. Swimming becomes required traversal

Swimming may exist before this region, but this biome is where it becomes essential.

The player should need a compatible swimming creature to reach:
- islands
- flooded ruins
- hidden routes
- stronghold approaches
- shortcuts

Swimming should become a genuine traversal choice rather than simply crossing a pond.

### 2. Water movement hazards

Potential mechanics:
- currents
- deep water
- stamina pressure
- dangerous channels
- underwater / partially submerged paths

Avoid adding too many meters or simulation systems.

The emphasis should remain on readable traversal and creature utility.

---

# 5. Ice — Frozen Mountain

## Theme

A high frozen mountain region.

Visual identity:
- snow
- ice
- exposed rock
- frozen forests
- steep ridges
- blowing snow
- frozen lakes
- old mountain roads
- distant peaks

## Major new systems

### 1. Cold exposure

Cold should make preparation important.

Possible tools:
- cold-resistant armor
- warm food
- potions
- fire
- shelter
- temporary resistance effects

Again, avoid turning this into a constantly annoying meter unless playtesting proves it useful.

### 2. Expedition preparation

This region should make long trips away from home feel more serious.

Players should think about:
- supplies
- healing
- food
- resistance
- creature condition
- safe locations

The biome should reinforce the survival side of Tetherbound without becoming tedious.

---

# 6. Air — Cliffs / Sky Highlands

## Theme

A highly vertical region based around cliffs and exposed highlands.

Visual identity:
- huge ravines
- natural stone arches
- wind-carved cliffs
- isolated plateaus
- high towers
- waterfalls
- clouds below some routes
- giant open sky
- distant structures visible across gaps

## Major system

### Advanced flying

Flying was unlocked in the Electric region.

This region should teach the player to **master flying**.

Electric region:
> "I can fly now."

Air region:
> "Now I need to be good at flying."

Possible mechanics:
- updrafts
- downdrafts
- wind pushing the player
- altitude management
- narrow landing locations
- aerial routes
- flying through arches
- airborne creature encounters
- inaccessible cliffs reachable only through flight mastery

Do NOT introduce a separate glider system.

Flying creatures should remain the traversal solution.

---

# 7. Psychic — Ancient Strange Wilderness

## Theme

The strangest region in the game.

It should feel ancient and subtly wrong rather than simply being "purple magic land."

Possible visual identity:
- impossible stone formations
- tilted ruins
- floating rocks
- unusual light
- warped paths
- ancient observatories
- strange vegetation
- creatures behaving oddly
- geography that seems to contradict itself

## Major new system

### Teleport

Psychic progression introduces teleportation.

Teleport should have two possible layers.

### Layer 1 — Creature teleport

Short-range exploration ability.

Possible uses:
- blink across gaps
- reach isolated platforms
- bypass certain blocked paths
- access strange ruins
- escape specific hazards
- solve spatial traversal puzzles

### Layer 2 — World teleport

Later in the region or after defeating its major boss:

Unlock fast travel between:
- activated monuments
- major settlements
- home
- other approved travel points

Psychic should therefore provide one of the biggest quality-of-life progression rewards in the game.

---

# 8. Dark — Blighted Forest / Endgame Region

## Theme

A hostile, blighted forest and endgame region.

Visual identity:
- dense dark canopy
- fog
- dead or corrupted vegetation
- twisted roots
- dim paths
- ruined Team Tether infrastructure
- strong contrast between darkness and artificial light
- dangerous creatures stalking through cover

This region should feel oppressive without becoming unreadable.

## Major new system

### Darkness / light pressure

Darkness finally becomes mechanically meaningful.

Potential mechanics:
- reduced visibility
- harder-to-spot creatures
- paths obscured by fog/shadow
- some threats revealed by light
- lanterns / torches / buildable lights become genuinely useful
- certain creatures may avoid or react to light
- light can reveal environmental clues

Do NOT make this a horror game.

The goal is pressure and uncertainty, not preventing the player from seeing.

---

# Traversal Progression

Keep traversal progression simple and readable.

## Core traversal ladder

1. Walk / Sprint
2. Ride
3. Swim
4. Fly
5. Teleport

Do not add unnecessary parallel traversal systems unless playtesting clearly demands them.

Avoid:
- grappling hook
- dedicated climbing system
- glider
- boats as a required core system
- digging traversal
- elaborate stealth traversal

The creature-based traversal identity should remain central.

---

# Environmental Escalation

The regions should progressively increase environmental danger.

## Escalation spine

### Meadows
Mostly safe natural environment.

### Electric Prairie
The **ground can hurt you**.

### Volcano
The **terrain and eruption timing can hurt you**.

### Water Region
The **environment controls movement** through currents and deep water.

### Ice Mountain
The **climate punishes poor preparation**.

### Air Highlands
The **terrain and wind demand traversal mastery**.

### Psychic Wilderness
The **world itself becomes spatially unreliable**.

### Dark Forest
The **player's ability to perceive threats is pressured**.

This progression should make the world feel increasingly dangerous without relying only on stronger enemy levels.

---

# Human Survival Progression

Human survival systems should become more important gradually rather than all at once.

## Meadows
Food and healing are useful.

## Electric
Environmental damage makes healing and protection more important.

## Fire
Heat protection and preparation become meaningful.

## Water
Stamina and route planning matter.

## Ice
Resistance, shelter, food, and expedition planning become important.

## Air
Travel preparation matters because mistakes can strand the player far from safe terrain.

## Psychic
Navigation and planning matter more than raw resistance.

## Dark
Light, supplies, and awareness become central.

---

# Systems We Should Avoid Overbuilding

The game already has enough major systems.

Do NOT automatically add:

- universal temperature meters
- oxygen meters
- hunger meters that constantly punish the player
- thirst
- elaborate stealth systems
- separate climbing
- grappling hooks
- gliders
- boats as mandatory progression
- digging
- excessive environmental status effects

Add mechanics only when they strengthen:
- creature usefulness
- exploration
- preparation
- bonding
- combat
- building
- world progression

---

# High-Level Design Principle

Every region should answer three questions:

## 1. What makes this place visually unmistakable?
Example:
Electric Prairie = storm-dark grasslands with lightning and charged ground.

## 2. What new thing does the player learn or unlock here?
Example:
Electric Prairie = environmental danger + flying.

## 3. What existing system becomes more important here?
Example:
Electric Prairie = human HP, food, potions, and armor.

If a biome cannot answer all three clearly, it needs more design work before production.

# Current Recommended Region Sequence

1. **Ground — Meadows**
   - Core systems
   - Riding
   - Monument blessing system
   - Meadows blessing: Extended Sprint

2. **Electric — Storm Prairie**
   - Human environmental damage
   - Flying unlock

3. **Fire — Volcano Mountainside**
   - Heat resistance / preparation
   - Timed lava and eruption hazards
   - Stronghold inside volcano

4. **Water — Wetlands / Flooded Coast**
   - Required swimming traversal
   - Currents / deep-water route challenges

5. **Ice — Frozen Mountain**
   - Cold exposure
   - Expedition preparation

6. **Air — Cliffs / Sky Highlands**
   - Advanced flying mastery
   - Wind / altitude / landing challenges

7. **Psychic — Ancient Strange Wilderness**
   - Short-range teleport traversal
   - Eventual world fast travel

8. **Dark — Blighted Forest**
   - Darkness / light pressure
   - Endgame survival atmosphere

---

# Status

This is a high-level future-region design document.

Do not begin building Regions 2–8 until the Meadows vertical slice satisfies its required completion / exit criteria.

The purpose of this document is to preserve the intended progression spine for later production.
