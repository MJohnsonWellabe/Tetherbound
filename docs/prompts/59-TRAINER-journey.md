# TRAINER-JOURNEY — Author one escalating human challenge ladder through Meadows

## Goal
Turn the chapter’s trainer battles into a coherent progression ladder rather than isolated NPC fights.

Reuse the existing trainer NPC system, ranks, palettes, battle system, rewards and defeat flags. No new human mesh program is required.

## Chapter target
The progression spec targets roughly 12–17 trainer battles spread across meaningful locations. Treat that as a tuning target, not a quota to fill mechanically.

## Required escalation
Author and verify a readable ladder such as:

1. local beginner/enthusiast trainers around the village/Lower Meadows;
2. tournament opponents;
3. stronger route trainers with clearer team-building lessons;
4. first Team Tether grunts/patrols;
5. officer-level opposition;
6. Relay Captain Vance;
7. Upper Meadows regional captains;
8. stronghold patrol/elite;
9. Warden.

Use current canonical names/data where they already exist. Do not rename established characters casually.

## For every trainer define
- identity/name;
- faction/rank;
- world location and why they are there;
- required or optional;
- team composition;
- levels;
- intended combat lesson/test;
- reward;
- defeat/progression flag;
- whether rechallenge is allowed;
- how the player recognizes the trainer’s importance before battle.

## Siting philosophy
Do not evenly space trainers every N metres.

Good contexts include:
- village square/tournament space;
- farm road;
- bridge/work site;
- quarry overlook;
- camp;
- path fork;
- river crossing;
- Team Tether checkpoint/patrol;
- relay approach;
- ridge/high pasture;
- stronghold approach/interior.

A trainer should look like they belong where they stand.

## Combat variety
Trainer identity should come from team composition, type/role pressure, switching demands, arena context and current mechanics—not from bespoke one-off systems for every fight.

Major captains/elite/Warden should each have at least one memorable identity beyond “more HP.” Coordinate the quality plan’s signature-encounter rule.

## Difficulty
Coordinate `57-TEAM-progression-curve.md`. Trainer strength is authored and not player-scaled.

Local trainers should establish confidence. Later trainers should expose weaknesses in a poorly balanced five without making one canonical team mandatory.

## Rewards
Coordinate `58-REWARD-resource-economy.md`. Required fights should produce useful progress; optional trainers need enough value to justify engagement.

## Dialogue
Keep concise and characterful. Team Tether hierarchy should communicate its doctrine/occupation without turning every grunt into a lore dump. Existing nuance rules remain.

## Deliverable
Create/update a chapter trainer map/table, then implement through current band-local trainer data.

The table should make it obvious:
- where fights occur;
- which are required;
- what difficulty progression they establish;
- what each rewards/teaches;
- whether any long region lacks human opposition entirely.

## Verification
Play each regional gate and record:
- team levels entering trainer battles;
- losses/retries;
- whether the fight taught/tested something distinct;
- reward usefulness;
- whether trainers feel naturally sited;
- whether stretches between trainers feel barren or, conversely, like a trainer tunnel.

## Acceptance
- trainer battles recur often enough to be a normal progression source;
- escalation from locals to Warden is obvious;
- Team Tether ranks read clearly;
- required fights govern meaningful world progression;
- optional trainers feel worth finding;
- no major region is simply wild traversal followed by one boss;
- no sequence becomes a monotonous trainer hallway.

## Definition of done
The player remembers several trainers as milestones on the journey and can feel their five being tested by progressively more capable human opposition.
