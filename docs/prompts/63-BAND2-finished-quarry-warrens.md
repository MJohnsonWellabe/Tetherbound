# BAND 2 — FINISHED QUARRY / WARRENS

## Goal
Turn the South Bridge crossing through completion of the Burrow Warrens and the handoff toward the river into a finished regional gameplay experience.

## Player purpose
This region should answer:

> **What do I gain by exploring dangerous places instead of just following the road?**

The answer is new capability: Rootstone, better preparation, stronger creatures, meaningful rewards and evidence that Team Tether is operating deeper in the region.

## Regional identity
Use:
- more exposed stone/rock;
- quarry cuts and worked remains;
- ridge trails;
- deeper woodland pockets;
- abandoned/rundown human traces;
- a memorable Warrens entrance and underground chamber language.

It must still read as Meadows, not a separate biome.

## Required gameplay package
### Rootstone
Introduce it clearly, place real deposits where its fiction belongs, and ensure the player immediately or soon understands what useful upgrades it enables.

### Wild ecology
Ground-oriented stronger populations around quarry/warrens, with meaningful alternatives to early-team members. Do not rely only on the dungeon guardian.

### Burrow Warrens
Required compact dungeon/nest with:
- readable navigation;
- aggressive creatures;
- Rootstone;
- meaningful chamber progression;
- memorable guardian;
- optional harder branch/rare reward;
- evidence of Team Tether activity.

### Special encounter
At least one optional strong/rare encounter in the region or deep branch that is worth seeing even if not caught.

### Rewards
Rootstone cache/upgrade progression plus a useful reward such as TM/evolution lead/rare trait opportunity consistent with current systems.

### Camp/rest
Provide a believable staging/recovery location outside or near the dangerous area, without making the dungeon itself a free hotel.

### Navigation
Quarry and Warrens must be findable through geography/paths/landmarks. The player should recognize optional vs critical-path routes.

## Evolution connection
Coordinate Mudsnout → Tuskroot. This region is an appropriate place for the evolution requirement/item/lead to become meaningful, following current canon/tuning.

## Evidence run
South Bridge → quarry discovery → first Rootstone → meaningful use/unlock → Warrens entry → guardian → optional branch/special encounter if discovered → exit with clear next objective.

Record:
- whether Rootstone had an understood purpose;
- team changes/levels;
- dungeon duration/readability;
- guardian identity/difficulty;
- optional temptation;
- camp/rest use;
- dead travel;
- reward usefulness.

## Acceptance
- region feels mechanically different through exploration/material/dungeon play;
- Rootstone creates real capability;
- wild ecology remains present outside the dungeon;
- Warrens guardian is memorable, not standard fight + HP;
- optional exploration has worthwhile payoff;
- Team Tether evidence advances curiosity;
- player exits stronger and understands why.

## Definition of done
The player leaves Band 2 thinking: **going off the easy path made my team and preparation better, and now I know something larger is happening.**
