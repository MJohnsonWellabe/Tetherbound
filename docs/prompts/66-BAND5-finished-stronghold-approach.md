# BAND 5 — FINISHED STRONGHOLD APPROACH

## Goal
Turn opening the final approach through entering Meadows Hall into a finished escalation segment that makes the player feel they are crossing into Team Tether-controlled ground.

## Player purpose
This region should answer:

> **Am I ready to commit my five to the final assault?**

The stronghold should feel increasingly close, dangerous and consequential.

## Regional identity
Use current authored spine/pylons/stronghold geography and preserve correct existing work.

Increase:
- visible Team Tether occupation;
- drained/damaged ground where current canon supports it;
- pylon/tether machinery readability;
- patrol presence;
- defensive/controlled crossings;
- stronghold silhouette dominance;
- risk in wild encounters.

The approach must still contain Meadows ecology and resource decisions. It should not become an empty faction corridor.

## Required gameplay package
### Navigation spine
Pylons/landmarks should create readable world-space direction without becoming magical GPS. Fix stale coordinate drift only from current main evidence.

### Storm/gated crossings
Use current canonical STORM-GATE placement if it belongs on this route. A physical gorge/barrier must actually constrain travel; guarded bridge progression should read as a real occupied checkpoint.

### Wild ecology
Populate the approach with stronger believable wild presence. Current empty Band 5 spawn data is not acceptable. Use habitat/Team Tether damage to shape placement rather than simply increasing density.

### Trainers/patrols
Use ordinary faction patrols/checkpoints to build pressure before the interior. Do not consume every major combat beat before the stronghold.

### Special encounter
Consider one final optional strong wild or discovery that tempts a prepared player to risk resources before entering the Hall.

### Final preparation point
Provide a memorable place where the player can reasonably stop, adjust the five, rest/camp or return home before the stronghold. Do not create an automatic free heal unless current canon already provides it.

### Visual cleanup
Reproduce-first and fix only if still present:
- stronghold materials;
- sky-plane artifacts;
- white billboard artifacts;
- torch/night readability on the final route.

## Environmental storytelling
The player should increasingly understand that the land is being affected by the tether system before entering the Hall. Use existing machinery, drained-ground/healing systems and faction language rather than inventing unrelated lore.

## Evidence run
Start when the final/Sigil approach opens and play continuously to Hall entry.

Record:
- time/distance;
- wild/trainer/resource opportunities;
- route readability;
- whether player chooses to rest/prepare;
- whether stronghold grows in visual dominance;
- whether faction occupation escalates;
- longest dead-travel interval;
- collision/gate failures;
- visual artifacts.

## Acceptance
- route feels like final controlled territory;
- stronghold/pylons provide strong world-space motivation;
- wild ecology remains present;
- faction encounters escalate pressure;
- physical gates cannot be trivially bypassed;
- player gets a meaningful final preparation opportunity;
- no long empty victory lap precedes the climax;
- Hall entry feels like a commitment.

## Definition of done
The player reaches Meadows Hall already feeling the tension of the final assault, with a prepared five and a clear sense that the land itself is under Team Tether control.
