# STRONGHOLD-FINALE — Make Meadows Hall the payoff for the entire chapter

## Goal
Turn Hall entry through defeating the Warden, freeing the legendary, resolving the five-creature choice and seeing the world heal into a memorable continuous finale.

Do not rebuild the established stronghold from scratch if current geometry/data is correct. Use the existing five-space structure and current Warden/tether/world-state systems.

## Player purpose
This is the answer to the chapter’s central question:

> **Was the team I built strong and prepared enough to break Team Tether’s hold on the Meadows?**

## Required sequence
Preserve current canonical order:
1. Outer Works.
2. Courtyard / Hall Approach.
3. Tether Chamber Approach.
4. Warden Arena.
5. Legendary Chamber.
6. Disable/free tether system.
7. Legendary offers to join.
8. If five owned, run release ceremony.
9. Post-Warden region/world-state change and acknowledgment.

## Stronghold pacing
Target a compact finale, not a giant puzzle dungeon.

The player should feel escalating commitment:
- exterior patrol/occupation;
- trainer encounter(s);
- increasing machinery;
- elite/final pre-Warden test;
- a recovery/preparation opportunity at the appropriate authored point;
- Warden;
- emotional/story release.

Avoid repetitive rooms whose only content is another identical trainer.

## Environmental storytelling
Use existing architecture/material language:
- old stone/natural Hall;
- Team Tether hardware bolted onto it;
- reserved faction colors;
- conduits leading toward the source;
- deeper rooms becoming more machine-dominated;
- captive legendary clearly functioning as the power source when the reveal lands.

Reproduce/fix current material/sky-plane defects only where they still exist.

## Major encounters
### Stronghold trainers/elite
Need a role in the escalation and should test a prepared team without exhausting every resource before the Warden.

### Warden
Must be the hardest meaningful trainer battle in Meadows and have a recognizable combat identity, not simply the largest numbers.

Use current canonical team/story/personality. Preserve nuance: the Warden can believe in Team Tether’s doctrine even while opposing the player.

Difficulty should assume the natural progression curve, not scale to whatever team enters.

## Legendary release
The freeing sequence must be physically/emotionally legible. The player should understand that disabling the machinery changes the creature’s state and the region’s tether system.

The legendary offers to join voluntarily.

## Five-creature decision
Coordinate prompt 67 and release ceremony prompt 46.

If full:
- present all five with meaningful identity/history;
- make the choice explicit and permanent;
- do not silently discard;
- complete release ceremony before finalizing the legendary roster change.

If not full, joining should still receive a meaningful moment.

## World healing
After Warden/legendary resolution:
- drained ground around tether infrastructure heals according to current systems;
- relevant barriers/patrols/occupation states change;
- rescued/settlement NPC acknowledgment updates where current story requires;
- stronghold/tether effects change;
- the world points toward later regions without entering/building Biome 2.

The Meadows must not look identical after victory.

## Persistence
Every finale step must save/load safely:
- Warden defeated;
- mechanism disabled;
- legendary freed/join state;
- release result;
- post-Warden healing/world flags.

No duplicate legendary/repeat ceremony after reload.

## Evidence run
Start at Hall entry with a realistic fresh-run team and play continuously through post-win acknowledgment.

Record:
- team composition/levels/condition entering;
- trainer/elite difficulty;
- use of switching/resources/recovery;
- Warden retries/time;
- whether environment communicates the tether reveal;
- whether release decision feels meaningful;
- world-state changes seen after victory;
- save/reload at key finale states.

## Acceptance
- stronghold feels like a climax, not a late content room set;
- Warden tests the chapter’s team-building skills;
- reveal is understandable in the world;
- legendary freeing has ceremony;
- five-slot decision can be genuinely difficult;
- world visibly responds;
- no progression/save duplication;
- final sequence leaves a strong next-chapter question without entering Biome 2.

## Definition of done
The finale pays off the hours spent catching, choosing, training, gathering, caring and preparing, and the player leaves the Meadows with a team that feels earned.
