# BAND 1 — FINISHED LOWER MEADOWS

## Goal
Turn tournament victory through opening the South Bridge into a finished regional gameplay experience.

This is not a new world-layout system. Reuse current village, routes, pond/groves, terrain, map, trainer, wild, gathering, objective and gate infrastructure.

## Player purpose
The player has proven basic competence in the village. Now the question becomes:

> **Can my team handle the Meadows beyond home?**

South Bridge is the visible progression payoff. The region should teach the normal chapter cadence before deeper material/dungeon systems appear.

## Regional identity
Still one Meadows biome, but this section should combine:
- broad rolling/open fields with long sightlines;
- farm/local roads and trail hierarchy;
- selected tree lines/groves;
- the approved lush pond-side pocket;
- water and small landmarks;
- readable distant pulls toward the bridge/deeper route.

Open spaces must not be bare. Use visible creatures, trainers, resources, camps, side trails, terrain silhouettes and landmarks.

## Required gameplay package
### Wild ecology
Enough common/early creatures to train and still reconsider the five. Habitats should be readable. Include at least one less-common/special temptation.

### Trainers
Use the chapter trainer map. Roughly the local Lower Meadows trainer circuit belongs here, naturally sited at meaningful places.

### Resources
Wood/Stone/Fiber/Berries and any early progression material access should support normal camp/home needs and upcoming unlocks.

### Optional content
At least one meaningful detour/discovery beyond ordinary fights/resources. Coordinate CONTENT-ACTIVITIES rather than inventing a separate quest architecture.

### Camp/rest
At least one plausible memorable clearing where stopping could make sense after a combat stretch. Do not force use.

### Navigation
Trails visible in world and map. Player can form a mental map. South Bridge should read as progress before reaching it.

### Gate
Bridge progression must be physical/story/trainer based, never arbitrary level-lock UI.

## Positive constraints
- Preserve current basic structure placement improvements.
- Preserve minimap movement-up if current verification still passes.
- Preserve pond vegetation quality.
- Fix pond water/usable doors/trails only where still broken; do not redesign correct content.

## Evidence run
Start immediately after tournament victory and play continuously until South Bridge opens/crosses.

Record:
- elapsed time;
- encounters/trainer fights;
- team level/composition changes;
- useful resources gathered;
- optional detours noticed/taken;
- longest dead-travel interval;
- whether player considered camp/rest;
- objective clarity;
- route/map readability.

## Acceptance
- player knows why they are leaving the village;
- region provides regular but non-uniform reasons to stop;
- wilds/trainers/resources all contribute to team preparation;
- at least one optional thing tempts the player away from direct progress;
- lush and open compositions both look intentional;
- no long stretch feels like purposeless running;
- South Bridge feels like a meaningful first regional threshold.

## Definition of done
The player crosses South Bridge feeling that the world has opened up and that their five are already more personal/capable than they were at the tournament.
