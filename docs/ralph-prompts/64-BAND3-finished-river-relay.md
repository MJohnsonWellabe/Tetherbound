# BAND 3 — FINISHED RIVER / TETHER RELAY

## Goal
Turn the Warrens exit through defeating Captain Vance, rescuing the captive and restoring the major crossing into a finished regional gameplay experience.

## Player purpose
This region should answer:

> **What is Team Tether actually doing here, and can my team beat them directly?**

The river creates the physical problem. The Relay creates the first serious faction confrontation.

## Regional identity
The river must be a real dominant landmark, not decorative terrain.

Use:
- readable banks and approaches;
- water-edge vegetation;
- open views across/along the river;
- mill/crossing structures;
- Team Tether infrastructure increasing toward the relay;
- a clear transition from natural river region into industrial intrusion.

## Required gameplay package
### Real water and crossing logic
The player sees the river, understands why it blocks progression and can identify the controlled/disabled crossing.

### Wild ecology
Populate the region with appropriate Water/Air and bank species plus other sensible Meadows inhabitants. Current empty Band 3 spawn data is not acceptable for completion.

### Trainers before relay
The route should not jump from wilderness directly into the four-person relay gauntlet. Use the trainer journey as appropriate so the player has normal progression opportunities and sees Team Tether presence building.

### Tether Relay escalation
Preserve/reuse existing authored trainer ladder where current data is valid:
- outer grunts/pickets;
- officer;
- Captain Vance.

The site should visually and mechanically escalate inward.

### Captain Vance
Must feel like the end of a mini-stronghold. Use team composition, arena/site context, rank and pacing to distinguish him from ordinary trainers.

### Captive/rescue
Victory must physically advance the world:
- captive rescued;
- relevant machinery/control disabled;
- crossing restored/provided access;
- objective changes;
- story acknowledges Team Tether as a direct threat.

### Resources/rewards
River/relay route needs useful gathering and rewards consistent with current tier. Do not let faction content erase the survival/crafting loop.

### Camp/rest
A reasonable recovery/staging point before or around the relay approach should exist if attrition warrants it. Avoid free full-service healing inside the gauntlet unless canon already provides a recovery opportunity.

### Optional content
At least one detour/special wild/discovery associated with river ecology or Team Tether occupation.

## Evidence run
Start after Warrens completion and play continuously through:
river discovery → crossing problem → preparation/exploration → relay approach → Hess/Orrin/Dell/Vance or current equivalent → rescue → restored crossing.

Record:
- wild/trainer/resource cadence;
- team strength entering relay;
- losses/retries and recovery decisions;
- whether the river is understandable as geography;
- whether Team Tether escalation reads clearly;
- reward usefulness;
- dead travel;
- objective/story clarity.

## Acceptance
- river feels like a major regional landmark;
- wild ecology is real and findable;
- Team Tether presence builds before the captain;
- relay is a memorable compact assault, not four NPCs standing together;
- Vance is a real milestone;
- rescue/crossing restoration visibly changes what the player can do;
- player leaves understanding Team Tether through personal experience, not exposition.

## Definition of done
Crossing the restored river feels earned because the player has defeated a real local Team Tether operation and the world physically responded.
