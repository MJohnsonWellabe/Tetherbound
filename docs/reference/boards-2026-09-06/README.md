reference art" that Meshy generations and new meshes require, and per
`docs/biomes/stormwood/BUILD_STORMWOOD_TO_COMPLETION.md` §0.8 an owner board wins over
the written visual language for the thing it depicts. Judge builds against the
composition, materials, palette and silhouettes here, not per-pixel fidelity
(see `../README.md`, "How NOT to use these").

**This closes the creature-roster gap `BUILD_STORMWOOD_TO_COMPLETION.md` deliberately left open.** §33 "Intentionally deferred" names "the final Stormwood creature roster" and "the legendary's final body" as missing, and the Cloudreach directive shipped with Meadows creatures standing in as placeholders in every encounter slot. Both are now named and drawn. Voltarach is Stormwood's Alpha catch and Fulgocobra is its legendary per §0.4/§17.5; the equivalent Cloudreach roles are Tempestwing (Alpha) and Solmane (legendary). Replacing the Meadows placeholders with these species (kitbashed first, Meshy/Tripo generation only after the chapter is broadly playable, per the goal prompts' existing Meshy-spend ordering) is real follow-up work, not yet done.

Reconciliation with the Stormwood directive: the directive's Region 6 and §18 call the
stronghold **the Dynamo** (a storm-harvesting station with a turbine-floor core arena
around the captive's containment pillar). The board calls it **the Stormheart Tree**.
Read them together: the Stormheart Tree is the Dynamo. Team Tether has built the Dynamo
into the lightning-split trunk; the Outer Works are the lower platforms and walkways, the
ascent through the hollow trunk is the approach, the Dynamo Core arena sits at the top
around the split, and the legendary's chamber is the crown. Keep the directive's arena
mechanics (capacitor banks, rod plates, three phases) and give them the board's look.
