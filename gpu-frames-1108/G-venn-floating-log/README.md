# Venn fight "floating log" (for LANE-LAND-MT, 13 Nov)

Tidewake Venn fight (phase2_capture_tidewake_fights.gd --trainer=water_trainer_venn, --fixed-fps 20, High, main content c673d5285 + crag files flag-off).
The fight starts at one of two cameras per run:

- camera A: position (376.89, 10.56, 3788.08), yaw 0.66 rad. Faces the dune. **The log is visible here.**
- camera B: position (356.86, 9.54, 3817.28), yaw 8.75. Faces the sea. The log is out of shot.

camA-t-000.00.jpg is the full 1920x1080 t-000 frame at camera A. The log is the mossy driftwood shape at the top left, about x 440-660, y 15-45. It sits above the dune top and reads as hanging in the air.
venn-floating-log-sheet.jpg: the camera B frame with the same box marked (empty), then that box enlarged for camera B (sky) and for camera A (the log).
Source runs: G-land-mt-cragback base-High (camera A), G-land-mt-cragcut base-High (camera B).
