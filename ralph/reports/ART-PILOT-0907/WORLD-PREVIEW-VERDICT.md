# ART-PILOT world-preview verdict

**REJECT_PREVIEW_1**

An independent code-blind judge compared the accepted reference candidate `b.png`
with the four neutral Godot renders in `world-preview/`. The preview retains the
broad crested-raptor identity, open wings, chest mass, taloned legs and trailing
tail, but it does not clear the visual bar.

## Severity-ranked findings

1. **Critical — palette and material readability:** the render is nearly all white
   with pale-blue shading and clipped highlights. The navy body and primaries,
   cream face and chest, tan secondary feathers, dark beak and talons, and amber eye
   are not demonstrated.
2. **High — face readability:** the hooked beak, eye socket, brow and cheek mask do
   not remain readable at gameplay scale.
3. **High — authored feather hierarchy:** the chest lacks its cream-and-blue
   chevron stack and the wings read as broad slabs instead of nested feather tiers.
4. **Medium — transitions:** profile and three-quarter views expose weak separation
   around the head, shoulder and near wing, plus lumpy torso-to-leg transitions.

The judge considered a focused material/lighting and selective facial/feather-form
pass more appropriate than a wholesale remodel. This remains a neutral asset
render, so it cannot prove in-world scale or fit beside the shipped roster.

## Spend decision

No refine or rig is authorised from this rejected preview. The pilot remains at 20
credits spent. The game-facing creature lane will first pursue the owner's preferred
colour/material and scale improvements on the installed roster; any later paid
Galecrest attempt still requires fresh evidence and must remain within the stated
goal ceiling.
