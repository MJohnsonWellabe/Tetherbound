# The Broken Tower — R1 production review

## Final disposition: POLISH

Retain the structural upgrade, but do not count this location as a hard pass.

All six expected 1280x720 PNGs exist and `manifest.json` reports complete with
no capture failures. The three day frames show a material improvement: the
installed masonry arch widens the former thin-slab silhouette, gives the road a
clear entrance, and connects the upright ruin to the threshold stones. The
broken crown, unequal surviving leaves and fallen section remain readable, so
the addition does not repair the ruin into an intact symmetric tower.

The night result remains below the strict bar. In all three night frames the
front and tall surviving masonry are still nearly black. The teal source makes
the arch opening and west remnant legible, but it does not reveal enough facade
surface, stone course or watch-remnant depth to read the same authored structure
without relying on its silhouette. The nearby glowing pickup also competes with
the smaller ward source at standing distance.

The capture itself is complete and honestly recorded. One limitation is now
explicit in the manifest: `01-route-arrival-day` records a 44.804m
camera-to-player separation after settle and contains no visible trainer; the
other five frames retain 5.756–5.937m ordinary third-person separation. That
first frame remains useful as a daylight establishing view, not as player-scale
proof.

Verdict: **POLISH — keep the arch, route threshold and former-watch structure;
night facade readability is still the hard-pass gap.**
