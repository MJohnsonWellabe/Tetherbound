## Image judgment

The day route frame is a good landmark read: tower, trail, trainer, broken continuation, open countryside, and distant context form a clean hierarchy. The threshold frame is still the strongest composition, while the close frame clearly proves scale and material. Night navigation is usable and the cyan arch/route cues do not wash out the whole field.

Strict PASS is withheld because the close evidence makes the ruin's broad flat cut face and straight fallen-wall bars unavoidable, while the night wash leaves one major facade plane nearly featureless black. A final pass should break or dress those planar terminations and add a restrained grazing contribution to that dark face without weakening the stepped silhouette.
