# Cloudreach High Perches R1 — static candidate, 2026-09-11

Status: static candidate only; production capture and independent visual verdict are required before any ledger promotion.

## Acceptance defect

The current formal FAIL is specific and repeated across the Cloudreach baseline and blind reviews: the ordinary view presents enormous uninterrupted brown shafts over an empty green lawn. The six needles establish altitude, but do not communicate a maintained perch function, intermediate human/creature scale, an arrival, or a lived-in destination. The prior collar/rigging and low-log experiments remained visually insufficient; this candidate does not count those held approaches as a win.
