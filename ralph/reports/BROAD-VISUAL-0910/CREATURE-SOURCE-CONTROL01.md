(neutral X) over the vivid repaint (Y). Skyrill regained clearer blue/cream/warm
facial and body regions. Torrentoad lost harsh orange/teal boundaries. Neither
judgment found a substantial advance toward the genre reference or commercial
acceptance; both still have poorly resolved eyes and surface design. These are
isolated fixtures and do not establish habitat or gameplay readability.
