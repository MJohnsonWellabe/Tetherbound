# Grandpa's Village R3 — static correction

R2 remained POLISH because four complete `Stairs_Exterior_Platform` models were
crossed at one origin to fake a curb; the north production view exposed them as
three repeated shrine-scale wells. R3 removes those four inappropriate modules
and replaces them with one low twelve-stone curb and recessed water surface while
retaining the authored timber posts, canopy, bucket, paved apron and collider.

The actual opening farmhouse gains one visual-only green-and-gold
`GRANDPA'S HOME` plaque beside its east-facing door, giving the village a specific
family/home focal cue instead of relying on a generic civic label. The capture set
adds a third ordinary square-to-house view. No route, NPC, harvest, encounter,
progression, door, interior, footprint or collision changes.

Production render and independent blind review are required before PASS.
