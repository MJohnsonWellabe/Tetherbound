# The Highfield hero identity R6 — independent visual judgment

## Verdict: POLISH — retain, not a strict named-location PASS

R6 preserves the Highfield as a readable summer pasture and improves the strongest
composition enough that herd, drove gate, stock camp, fire, and the warm landmark
tree can all be found in one frame. It does not clear R5's strict blocker. The
working structures remain small mid-ground marks inside a broad, largely generic
field, while the east pair is substantially occluded by foreground trees and leaves.
The location has identity and is not a FAIL, but it does not yet deliver one
unambiguous commercial hero read.

### Evidence integrity: PASS, with a framing defect

- `manifest.json` reports `complete: true`, six frame records, and zero failures.
- All six native PNGs are present, unique, non-empty 1280x720 files. The three
  camera positions each have a visibly distinct day/night frame.
- The manifest identifies `res://scenes/world/meadows_playground.tscn` and discloses
  an ordinary player, production Terrain3D/scatter/props/encounters, a hidden HUD,
  pinned clear weather/time, and a fixed 70-degree third-person camera at 5.2 m.
  It states that progress and encounters were not injected. Player position,
  displacement, ground clearance, camera distance, and subject distances are
  retained per frame.
- The receipt does not carry a source SHA or per-source hashes, so it establishes
  production-scene/capture provenance but not commit identity on its own.
- Frames `03` and `04` are technically valid captures, but not clean visual proof:
  the close foreground trunk and dense leaf masses cover much of the player-to-camp
  axis and partially hide the intended large creature. This is a framing defect,
  not a missing-file failure.

### Strict visual findings

- **Pasture/ecology: PASS.** Continuous mixed ground cover, flowers, rolling grade,
  a bounded woodland edge, open grazing space, and multiple ordinary herd bodies
  read as an inhabited high-summer pasture rather than a bald debug clearing. Day
  and night preserve that ecological contrast.
- **Herd read: PASS at the location level.** Several pale herd bodies are clear in
  `01`, `02`, `05`, and `06`, with useful distribution across the slope. The left
  hero creature is cropped in the wide pair and foliage-obscured in the east pair,
  so its distinct bull/hero role is not consistently legible across the proof.
- **Gate and stock camp: POLISH blocker.** The fence rhythm, gate, wagon/stock
  structure, and campfire are present and the night fire gives a useful warm cue.
  At the recorded 36-58 m gate distances they remain low, pale, and visually close
  to the surrounding fence segments. They do not yet create a strong working-land
  silhouette or destination threshold.
- **Hero composition: improved, still POLISH.** `05`/`06` are the strongest pair:
  herd on the right, gate/camp near centre, woodland route on the left, and the warm
  central tree all coexist without losing the player-height context. Even there,
  the large tree owns the vertical focal hierarchy while the actual Highfield work
  complex is a small cluster below it. `01`/`02` retain a wide undifferentiated
  middle plane; `03`/`04` sacrifice the composition to foreground occlusion.
- **Day/night readability: PASS with limits.** The night set keeps player, herd,
  campfire, lit tree, woodland edge, and terrain contours separable. Gate/fence
  construction nearly disappears into the dark grass, and the occlusion in `04`
  becomes especially severe, but the location itself remains navigable and legible.
- **Named-location identity: POLISH.** The combination of drove fencing, herd,
  campfire, and pasture distinguishes Highfield from an ordinary road segment. The
  authored idea is present; its key built elements are not yet strong enough in
  scale, value, or composition to make the name inevitable from the image alone.

## Promotion condition

Retain R6 as the current Highfield baseline. A strict PASS needs fresh production
day/night evidence from an unobstructed player-height stand where the ordinary herd,
distinct hero bull, drove gate, and stock camp resolve immediately as one hierarchy.
The built threshold needs more vertical/value authority than the surrounding fence
rhythm while preserving the accepted pasture clearing, ground cover, route, herd,
camp function, and night fire.
