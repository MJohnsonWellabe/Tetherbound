# Village Inn common room R7 — production disposition

## Disposition: strong POLISH — retain

The public room now has a bordered central aisle textile, occupied contrasting table
runners and place settings, a stocked bar with Bram facing the room, and a lower
warm-cloth lodging screen that separates the visible guest bed from the taproom. The
R7 reduction fixes R6's tall cyan-divider read and preserves every gameplay collider
and route.

This is a clear composition improvement but not a strict commercial pass. The broad
plaster walls and simple box-built bar still dominate the room, the common-room
palette remains very clean and evenly lit, and the bed is intentionally still partly
visible. Future work should target architectural material depth and lived wear, not
additional freestanding clutter.
