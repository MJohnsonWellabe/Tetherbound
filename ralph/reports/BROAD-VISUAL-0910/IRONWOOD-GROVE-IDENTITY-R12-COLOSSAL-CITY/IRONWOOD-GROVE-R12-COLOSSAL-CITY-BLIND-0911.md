# Ironwood Grove R12 colossal-city blind verdict — 2026-09-11

## Evidence reviewed

All eight completed production frames in `IRONWOOD-GROVE-IDENTITY-R12-COLOSSAL-CITY` (four matched day/night views). This verdict is based only on the rendered frames.

## Verdict

**RETAIN R12. The requested scale correction passes; the location remains POLISH overall.**

The Ironwood now reads decisively bigger and wider. In the long-road view it owns the skyline, its crown spans several ordinary trees, and in the root-district view the trainer and nearby trees establish a trunk volume large enough to plausibly contain multiple rooms or a small settlement. This is no longer an incremental enlargement or an ordinary hero tree.

Relocation successfully protects the workyard: its platform, gate, route, pickups, and surrounding ground remain open and readable, with no visible root or canopy intrusion. The tradeoff is that the workyard frame shows no visual relationship to the Ironwood at all, so the landmark and gameplay space currently read as two adjacent scenes rather than one authored district.

## Remaining defects

- The renders communicate **city-scale volume**, but not yet **a city in the tree**: the close root view shows no readable entrances, windows, galleries, bridges, or inhabited silhouette.
- The southwest view is heavily occluded by ordinary trees; the landmark becomes a pale background mass instead of the intended focal point.
- The night set is too dark for terrain and route readability, and the Ironwood shifts toward a cold blue silhouette without warm habitation cues.
- The giant scale exposes the asset's repeated, faceted leaf clusters and stretched trunk topology. The pale canopy/trunk are distinguishable from the Meadows family, but currently look washed and stone-like in daylight.
- The long approach is spatially clear but visually sparse between the player and landmark; there is little authored transition drawing the player from road to root district.

## Promotion call

Promote the **size/width issue** to PASS and retain this placement/scale direction. Keep **Ironwood Grove overall at POLISH**, pending a city-cue/readability pass and a composition pass that reconnects the protected workyard to the colossal tree without obstructing gameplay.
