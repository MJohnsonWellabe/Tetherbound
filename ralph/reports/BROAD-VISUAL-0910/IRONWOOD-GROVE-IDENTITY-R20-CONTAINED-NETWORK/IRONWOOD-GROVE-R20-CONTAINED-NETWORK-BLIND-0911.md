# Ironwood Grove R20 contained-network blind verdict — 2026-09-11

## Evidence reviewed

All eight R20 PNGs and `manifest.json`, compared image-to-image with the eight-frame
R18 prominent-fissures set and its recorded visual gaps. No production source or
implementation report was inspected.

The R20 manifest is internally complete: eight 1280x720 frames, four matched
day/night views, `complete=true`, and no capture failures. It discloses the production
Meadows scene, ordinary player and live world content, hidden HUD, and fixed
third-person stands. The workyard pair does not contain the hero tree and therefore
cannot prove electrical treatment from that angle.

## Verdict

**POLISH — RETAIN R20.**

R20 is a clear improvement over R18 and should replace it. The enormous white decal-
like bolts have become a restrained network of narrower cyan fissures, allowing brown
bark planes and the internal trunk split to remain visible in day and night. The
colossal crown, root mass, trainer scale and warm-bark/green-canopy identity remain
intact. It does not reach strict PASS: the electrical system is still concentrated in
the central trunk, several short luminous slivers read detached or pasted at local
distance, the southwest evidence is heavily screened by ordinary trees, and the
workyard remains a separate generic timber rig rather than part of one unmistakable
city-tree district.

## R18 gap comparison

- **Bark contact: improved to strong POLISH.** R20's thinner channels follow and are
  interrupted by trunk planes far more convincingly than R18's broad white ribbons.
  In `03-root-district-day`, however, several short cyan strokes terminate abruptly
  on otherwise unbroken faces and still resemble emissive overlays rather than light
  emerging from deep fissures.
- **Floating geometry / foliage contamination: improved, not closed.** The obvious
  R18 crown shards are greatly reduced. Small isolated cyan flecks remain among upper
  leaves and on the right branch/crown edge in both long-road frames. At night these
  detached points are easier to see against the dark canopy.
- **Lightning morphology: improved to POLISH.** R20 has useful thick-to-thin variation
  and more branching. The network still reads as several disconnected local strokes,
  not one continuous root-to-crown system wrapping the ancient tree.
- **Day/night prominence: PASS.** The treatment is immediately readable at 56 m and
  remains legible at the 96 m long-road stand without R18's clipped white dominance.
  R20 preserves bark information at night materially better than R18.
- **Root-to-crown and alternate-angle coverage: POLISH.** The front/root view shows a
  credible vertical spread, but exposed outer roots and the major lateral boughs are
  comparatively quiet. The southwest pair proves some alternate-angle visibility,
  yet ordinary trees obscure most of the lower network and prevent a strong around-
  tree read.
- **Central trapped-source hierarchy: POLISH.** The cool internal glow now competes
  less with surface bolts and reads as the brightest source in the trunk split.
  Nothing visible distinctly communicates a trapped living legendary rather than a
  magical lightning heart.
- **Scale and palette retention: PASS.** Long-road and root-district frames retain a
  city-scale ancient silhouette, readable trainer comparison, warm bark and green
  crown. No obvious new geometry collapse or import corruption appears.
- **Workyard integration: POLISH.** `04-ironwood-workyard-{day,night}` is readable at
  trainer scale, but the simple rail/frame assembly, featureless timber and absence
  of the world tree from the composition make it feel like a detached rural saw rig,
  not an inhabited root-city production district.
- **Night: POLISH.** The frontal tree survives well, but the southwest foreground and
  route fall close to black. The workyard has readable player/floor values but weak
  task lighting and no visual connection to the electrical landmark.

## Remaining strict-PASS blockers

Remove the remaining detached crown flecks; connect the current narrow fissures into
a visibly continuous but restrained root/trunk/major-bough network; give the trapped
source a distinct living silhouette or containment cue; and compose at least one
ordinary route/workyard view where the functioning district and colossal tree read as
one place. Do not return to R18's broad clipped-white bolts.
