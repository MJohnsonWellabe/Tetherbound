## Independent image-only verdict

A non-author graded the set **POLISH, not PASS**. The inside age-ladder and
crafting-glade views establish a deliberate grove and usable destination, but
the arrival and crown views still resemble generic open meadow. PASS remains
blocked by the lack of an unmistakable Ironwood hero landmark, grey A-frames
that read as primitive scaffolds, mottled ground and thin grass, broad empty
hills, crushed night trunks/terrain, an oversized flat moon, edge-cropped
creatures, detached pickup glow blobs, and overly lime daylight foliage.

The next round must solve those visible composition defects, not add another
small prop layer or rely on the static role census.
