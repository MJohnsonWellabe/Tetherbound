## Full-frame criteria

1. **Silhouette and small-size readability.** In all four frames, the trainer and right-hand masonry wall remain clear when mentally reduced to thumbnail size. The grass becomes a narrow dark fringe; small distant trees dissolve into specks. F01/F03's far cliff line has repeated rounded uprights instead of a distinctive crown or summit. The diagonal road at right is visible but minor; it does not organize the otherwise empty central plateau. F02/F04 lose most interior cliff shape to the pale distance treatment.

2. **Colour and value structure.** F01/F03 have a natural green/stone/blue foundation, but a large uninterrupted yellow-green ground field dominates the image. The close stone wall has much stronger material definition than the landscape. F02/F04 exchange this for dark teal ground against an unusually light grey-blue sky and cliff band. The split reads as dark foreground under a bright overcast sky more readily than 23:00. No visible oxblood misuse; these frames do not provide a danger-color comparison.

3. **Intentionality.** All frames contain a broad, nearly featureless middle plateau, tiny scattered trees and a narrow rim of similar grass clumps. The far wall repeats column-like rock forms across most of the horizon. Distinct clusters, erosion breaks, major buttresses and clear rock-to-soil transitions are insufficient to make that wall feel geologically authored. F03/F04's small left-edge rock addition does not alter this reading.

4. **Lighting.** F01/F03's strong cast shadow across the foreground places the grass and wall in sunlight, and the trainer is grounded. The distant cliffs have much less directional modeling. F02/F04 darken the ground and wall while keeping grass edges and the trainer conspicuously bright and distance very pale. Night is distinguishable from day, but lacks the key art's coherent deep-blue landscape and selective warm light hierarchy.

5. **Horizon and depth.** F01/F03 establish foreground, middle plateau and background wall, but the continuous cliffs are more backdrop than navigable layered terrain. F02/F04 compress their internal detail into a light silhouette, weakening perceived depth. The bright void-like opening under the far-right cliff termination is poorly explained spatially in every frame. No LOD transition can be established from stills.

6. **Interface.** Text panels fit the image and are readable at full size. The top tutorial strip and large right mission panel command more attention than the road or any landscape destination. F03 adds another centered title above the tutorial, increasing top-center competition. The five lower-right slots mostly present tiny number markers rather than immediately understandable content. Lower-left food text has weak contrast. The minimap conveys the player arrow clearly but little readable terrain. These are internal hierarchy observations, not comparisons to Palworld UI.

7. **Artefacts.** F01/F03's close left slope has conspicuously broad banded shading and sharp triangular plane changes; the distant cliffs repeat similar horizontal bands. All four frames show small detached dark line fragments in the upper-left sky. Their cause is not identifiable from these images. The dark tiny upright on the distant plateau in F02/F04 reads as an isolated speck rather than a meaningful object. Still images cannot prove popping, z-fighting or motion artifacts.

8. **Scale agreement.** The trainer provides a clear person-scale anchor. Close grass reaches a plausible shin-to-knee range, though its sparse individual blades look oversized against the ground texture. The wall's large blocks are plausible for a substantial gate. The extremely small trees and broad empty plateau give weak intermediate scale cues between the trainer and tall cliffs. ## Three largest reference gaps

1. **Landscape construction and composition — all frames, clearest F01/F03.** Palworld-04 combines irregular rock masses, terraces, trees and a distant landmark into several depth layers. The key art likewise gives hills and landmarks a clear hierarchy. These frames present a repeated cliff curtain behind a flat green expanse, with the road relegated to the right. Improve large and medium landform variation and the path's relationship to a destination before adding small scatter.

2. **Ground coverage and material integration — all frames.** Palworld-02/04 blend grass, exposed ground and rocky slopes with broad patches and convincing transitions. Here the near field is mostly a green texture with a thin line of tall blades, while the plateau is almost blank. Ground coverage, clustering, rock-foot transitions and less uniform terrain color are scene/material work. The pictures cannot establish whether suitable assets already exist.

3. **Night value structure and depth — F02/F04.** The key art's night scene retains deep blue land layers and localized inviting light. Here pale distant cliffs and sky overpower a uniformly dark ground plane. Rebalance distance, sky, ambient illumination and local accents so night retains terrain form and a focal hierarchy. This is a scene/lighting gap, not a demand for painterly fidelity.
