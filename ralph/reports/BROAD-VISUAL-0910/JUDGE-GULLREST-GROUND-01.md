## Full rubric

1. **Silhouette and small-size readability.** In both frames the trainer, hill crest and rectangular timber cage/tower remain identifiable when considering the image at roughly 30% size. The trainer's dark outline separates from the yellow-green ground. The red figure remains visible against the sky but loses species and pose. There are no nearby trees, bushes or rocks to form a readable ecology. At thumbnail size both frames become essentially the same bare hill, cage, player and large UI blocks; F02's texture improvement becomes minor. The cage is the destination by isolation, not by a rich or distinctive landmark silhouette.

2. **Colour and value structure.** Both frames offer useful dark player/shadow accents, but the environment is dominated by continuous yellow-green ground and a pale blue-grey sky/water band. F01's high-frequency yellow marks and F02's blurred yellow-green patches do not create the key art's broader division into warm paths, darker groves, clear blue water and flower accents. The red creature is a strong isolated accent. Its allegiance is not visible, so oxblood misuse on friendly elements cannot be established. The two frames agree in palette and read as the same place.

3. **Intentionality.** F01 and F02 have a clearly placed crest structure, but its surroundings read as an unpopulated terrain surface. There is no worn approach, entrance clearing, shore vegetation cluster, working area or accompanying prop group explaining why it stands there. The uninterrupted ground treatment crosses slopes and crest alike. Palworld-02 and -05 turn open ground into purposeful paths and clearings with vegetated edges; this hill lacks that relationship. A few deliberately grouped coastal rocks, tufts and a worn route would matter more than spreading equal detail across the whole surface.

4. **Lighting.** F01 and F02 clearly show directional daylight: long shadows anchor the trainer and structure and describe the hill. The structure's large dark shadow is stronger than most terrain modelling, while the sea is nearly white and the sky subdued. The result feels dry and stark rather than the inviting, balanced sunny atmosphere of the key art. The visible morning label is compatible with long shadows, but exact sun/time accuracy is not judged. F02 makes the trainer shadow easier to distinguish from ground marks; it does not solve the lighting imbalance.

5. **Horizon and depth.** The crest occludes the middle ground in both frames, and low distant islands provide limited depth cues. The right island has a visibly angular, stepped shoreline; the pale water and sky nearly join into one band. The coastal openness is understandable, but there is no sequence of near shore detail, middle-distance destination and distinct far silhouette like the spatial layering in Palworld-04. The cloud field is visually broad and streaked, attracting attention above a sparse landscape. No definite LOD/chunk seam is identifiable from these stills.

The five mostly empty dark cards consume considerable lower-right space while offering only tiny white key labels. The lower-left food text has weak contrast over terrain. The minimap is an almost uniform green square, communicating the player marker much better than the coastal shape or a route. At thumbnail size the objective and empty cards become dominant shapes, while their content largely disappears.

F02 reduces their sharpness but exposes broad smearing and stretching on the near slope. Thin broken horizontal marks are visible across the bright water on the left in both frames; their cause cannot be determined here. The distant right shoreline is conspicuously faceted. No definite z-fighting, floating trainer, or geometry penetration can be asserted. Motion popping, temporal shimmer and animation are unobservable.

## Ranked three largest gaps

1. **An environment with volume and a purposeful approach — F01 and F02.** Palworld-02/-04/-05 give paths and clearings edges made from grass, rocks, trees and architecture. Here essentially all traversable space is one textured hill. Add an authored approach to the cage and clustered coastal ground/shore layers with clear gaps; retain open traversal space. This is a scene-composition gap, subject to suitable art being available.
2. **A compelling character/creature presentation — F01 and F02.** Palworld-01/-03/-04 feature readable, distinctive bodies and expressions; these frames give a generic rear trainer silhouette and a distant partly concealed red shape. A closer, fully visible creature staging is a scene change that can establish what the art actually supports. Distinctive character silhouettes, readable anatomy and coherent material treatment, if absent from the assets, require art work rather than a ground-texture adjustment. These images cannot establish the unseen asset inventory.
3. **Coherent ground, shoreline and distance treatment — F01 and F02.** The references separate natural surfaces into readable material areas and spatial layers. F01 substitutes sharp all-over pattern; F02 substitutes softer but muddy pattern, while both have near-white water and angular distant banks. Establish calmer broad ground values, local worn/rocky areas, a legible water colour and a more deliberate shore silhouette. Palette/material tuning and scene shaping can address much of this; convincing surface and shoreline assets may require authored art.
