Inspected the actual Meadows key art, all five `palworld-0*.jpg` images, and the supplied three Enshrouded images and Once Human settlement image. No implementation, history or other verdicts consulted. This verdict concerns only this Rodline Post view; the visible location caption says The Conductor Run.

**Pair comparison: no meaningful overall preference between F01/F02 and F03/F04.** The scene composition, ground coverage, large tree forms and creature presentation are effectively the same. F01/F02 have a brown patch behind the small furniture in the cottage entrance; F03/F04 have a pale opening. The brown version gives that small area more material definition, but does not solve the scene's dominant shortcomings. In F04 the trainer is darker than in F02, which better fits the night surroundings but reduces separation. Neither difference establishes a material advance toward the references. No chronology is assumed.

The two large-eared creatures are recognizable and appealing at this distance. They have clearer faces and silhouettes than the surrounding ground has structure. However, these repeated creatures and the trainer do not yet demonstrate Palworld's strong, varied hero presentation: their material detail looks brittle, their poses are quiet and similar, and the lower foreground character is obscured by interface. A close character view would be needed to distinguish intrinsic asset limitations from this presentation.

## Eight criteria

1. **Silhouette and small-size readability.** F01–F04 retain a readable trainer, two cream creatures, a well and a cottage when mentally reduced to roughly a third size. Tall ears and dark face markings work well. Thin grass and tiny flowers collapse into noise. Tree trunks become immense repeating vertical bands. The HUD covers most of the lower foreground character and part of the cottage, so those subjects cannot be evaluated cleanly.

2. **Colour and value structure.** F01/F03 combine yellow-green ground, highly saturated red shrubs, grey trunks and pale golden architecture without a strong dominant palette hierarchy. The red shrubs are ordinary vegetation yet compete for the danger accent. F02/F04 clearly move to a blue sky and darker terrain, but retain conspicuously bright green grass and pale creatures. The key art instead organizes natural greens into broad light and shadow groups and reserves stronger accents for focal subjects. Both pairs read as the same location, but the muddy ground dominates their colour impression.

3. **Intentionality.** F01–F04 put the well and cottage across an open space, which is a useful start for a social place. Yet there is no clearly worn connection between their entrances, the trainer and the wider landscape. Sparse, similarly sized grass sprigs appear across the entire mottled substrate; ornamental shrubs are sprinkled between repeated monumental trees. This reads as placed assets on a common surface rather than a used outpost. Palworld's base image organizes activity around bare working ground and useful object clusters; the key-art village uses paths, fences and grouped buildings to express habitation.

4. **Lighting.** The sky distinguishes day in F01/F03 from night in F02/F04. Cottage brightness provides a possible night focal point, but the light does not convincingly spread from the entrance onto its immediate surroundings. Bright fine grass detail competes with it. The well and cottage have insufficiently persuasive contact and directional shadow structure for their large forms. The trainer is better separated in F02 and more naturally subdued in F04; neither transforms the lighting of the scene. Once Human's supplied night view shows a coherent direction and ground shadow pattern, while the key-art night image groups settlement warmth against cool distance.

5. **Horizon and depth.** F01–F04 terminate in a nearly level green horizon with similar sharp tree trunks on successive planes. A small blue arch at the far left is visible but disconnected from a readable route. There is little middle-distance landform or distant landmark hierarchy. Palworld's plateau image uses ledges, overlapping vegetation and a distant tower to establish travel depth; the Enshrouded vista similarly separates near, middle and far landforms. No clear chunk seam can be established from these stills.

6. **Interface.** F01–F04 keep text inside the frame and make the interaction prompt legible. However, the wide lower control strip and empty-looking five-slot panel occupy a large share of the play view, and the large story panel hides the cottage. The interaction prompt masks the foreground NPC instead of supporting an unobstructed conversation subject. The minimap is predominantly an undifferentiated green square, offering little visible environmental information. Tiny keyboard glyphs are much less readable than their labels. These are direct usability observations, not comparisons against Palworld UI.

7. **Artefacts.** F01–F04 show harsh, speckled timber/roof surface detail and very thin bright grass edges against a blurred, blotchy ground texture; the mismatch reads visually unstable even in a still. Cottage entrance furniture is hard to parse, especially against the pale opening in F03/F04. F02/F04 contain nearly black, ragged sky-cloud patches. These deserve closer capture inspection; a still cannot establish shimmer, popping or z-fighting. No definite geometry intersection is established here.

8. **Scale agreement.** Using the central 1.80 m trainer as the ruler, the cottage doorway is broadly plausible at its distance and the well is recognizable at human scale. The enormous, similarly thick tree trunks dwarf both structures and suggest giant-tree woodland more than the key art's varied oak grove. The two creatures stand farther away than the trainer, so their smaller screen heights cannot establish an incorrect world scale. The obscured lower character likewise cannot be measured reliably. No species-role size judgment is supportable from these images alone.

## Three ranked gaps

1. **Ground and habitation structure — F01–F04.** A muddy, blurry substrate plus separated green needles fills most of the scene. Palworld 02/05 and the key-art village distinguish worn paths, usable clearings and fuller vegetation margins. Build a connected pedestrian surface between the well and cottage, with purposeful bare areas and denser grouped vegetation at boundaries; organize props around a visible use. The first remedy is scene layout, coverage and material blending. The noisy timber and weak ground surfaces may also require material/texture art work if this is their best presentation.

2. **Depth and authored tree composition — F01–F04.** Similar giant trunks stand over a flat horizon, offering little destination or topographic rhythm. The key art and Palworld 04 provide overlapping landforms, diverse tree groupings and a landmark with a route toward it. Change tree grouping/scale variation, compose a middle distance and establish a visible approach. These are scene and landscape fixes; more assets are not demonstrated to be necessary by this view.

3. **Lighting and material cohesion — especially F02/F04, also F01/F03.** Creatures, sparse grass, textured wood and broad terrain do not share a convincing light hierarchy. Palworld's subjects stand out through coherent value grouping; the supplied night references have legible warm/cool or directional relationships. Strengthen contact and light-source relationships, reduce competing bright grass detail, and unify the surface response. Scene lighting/material tuning can address much of this. If roof and timber remain granular after that, their texture art needs revision; changing density alone cannot fix it.

## Bar answers

**A — Meadows key-art world: No.** The timber cottage, well, trees and friendly creatures supply compatible nouns, but the flat, patchy giant-tree clearing lacks the key art's rolling, connected, inviting settlement composition and controlled natural palette. Most of this failure is scene-fixable through ground structure, tree composition, depth and lighting. The surface-art shortcomings are a separate texture/material concern; these frames do not prove a need for replacement character or creature meshes.

**B — Same kind of game as Palworld: Yes.** A third-person backpacked trainer, conspicuous stylized companion creatures and rustic settlement objects in an explorable outdoor area clearly signal that family of game. This is a genre-recognition answer, not a quality pass.

**Commercial visual quality against the supplied shipped-game images: Not met by either pair.** The principal deficits are environment integration, depth and lighting/material coherence. The supplementary Enshrouded and Once Human views reinforce those observations without changing the project's palette target. Combat spectacle, animation and performance cannot be judged from these quiet static frames.
