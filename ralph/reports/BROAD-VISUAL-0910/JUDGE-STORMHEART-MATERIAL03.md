# Blind visual verdict — Stormheart material03

Evidence: six neutral stills, paired F01/F04, F02/F05, F03/F06. Reviewed the Tetherbound Meadows key art and all five supplied Palworld screenshots. The supplied Enshrouded image was used only as supplementary architecture/material context. No source, history, other reports, or provenance was inspected; no Godot run was made. No scores.

**Group preference: F04–F06, narrowly.** Their foreground floor reads more clearly as timber, and the warmer surface separates the standing plane from the enclosing gray walls. F01–F03 have a smoother reddish floor that reads like an indistinct coated surface. The preference is a local improvement in material legibility. F04–F06 still show very large, repetitive grain, and their larger visible cyan shapes are more distracting. Effect shapes differ between the stills; these images do not establish whether that difference persists in motion.

The trainer is the most resolved asset in every frame: hair, backpack, sleeves, trousers, and boots produce a recognizable adventurer silhouette. However, the rear view and neutral stance cannot establish expressive character quality beside Palworld's posed characters. **No creature is visible in any frame**, so these images supply no evidence that the game's central creature art meets the reference bar. That is an evidence limit, not a pass for unseen art.

## Largest reference gaps, ranked

1. **Construction and material scale — F01–F06.** The key art's stronghold has readable masonry courses, openings, buttresses, and accumulated detail. Palworld's plateau building and base have distinct assembled parts. Here, immense grain-covered planes and dark spiral strips dominate without enough joints or structural hierarchy. The supplementary Enshrouded image also demonstrates large architecture retaining smaller construction cues and a convincing connection to rock and terrain; it does not set an additional acceptance gate.
2. **Grounding and coherent lighting — especially F02/F05 and F03/F06.** Palworld's open path and base use shadows, grass boundaries, and surface variation to place characters and objects in space. These interiors juxtapose a bright trainer, dark ramps, uniformly green leaves, and a flat cyan feature with little visible interaction between them. Changing the floor colour does not establish contact or depth.
3. **An inhabited creature-adventure scene — all six.** Palworld's references make creatures, people, activity, and the environment a coherent composition. These frames show one rear-facing trainer inside sparse monumental geometry, with no visible creature or visible officer anchoring the challenge prompt in F01/F04. The location may have a useful fantasy premise, but its player-facing identity remains weak in these views.

## Separate bar answers

**A — Key-art world: No, for both groups.** Natural wood, green leaves, and a mysterious cyan feature provide a loose thematic connection. Oversized repetitive grain, severe geometric ribbons, and flat local lighting prevent the space from sharing the board's authored natural-material language and mood. This does not demand matching painted per-leaf fidelity.

**B — Same kind of game as Palworld: Yes, narrowly, for both groups.** The stylized third-person adventurer, explorable fantasy structure, quest, and five-slot companion interface convey an adventure with companions. This is a statement of apparent genre intent. With no creatures or combat visible, the creature-adventure promise is supported only indirectly in these frames.

**Actual commercial quality beside Palworld: No, for both groups.** F04–F06's clearer floor does not close the architectural, material, lighting, or scene-composition gaps. The resolved trainer does not compensate for the surrounding construction reading as unfinished geometry.

**Scene-fixable work:** correct texture scale and stretching; distinguish structural wood, wall surface, and walking surface; improve contact and light direction; make supports, landings, and route entry readable; reduce the cyan feature's flat geometric appearance; vary and ground foliage; frame the named interaction and a companion when judging the inhabited scene.

**Art requirements that scene dressing alone cannot resolve:** the visible spiral needs convincing structural detailing and material definition if its present large smooth surfaces are the intended final assets. Hero architecture may require authored geometry and appropriately scaled material maps. These stills cannot establish what suitable assets already exist elsewhere in the build, so they cannot justify claiming that a purchase or new generation is necessary. Creature art remains unassessed because it is absent; the trainer needs a closer posed/front view to assess expression and consistency against the creature cast.
