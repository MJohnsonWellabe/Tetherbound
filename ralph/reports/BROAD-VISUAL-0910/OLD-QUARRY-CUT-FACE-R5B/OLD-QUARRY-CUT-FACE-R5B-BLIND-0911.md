# The Old Quarry R5B — blind visual verdict, 2026-09-11

## Evidence

Reviewed all eight completed production frames: four matched day/night views covering arrival, worked floor, conduit head, and cut face. The manifest is complete with zero failures. Grade is based only on the rendered evidence, compared with R4's recorded gaps.

## Verdict: POLISH — retain

R5B materially improves the quarry hierarchy. The pale exposed-stone face gives the site a mid-height extraction silhouette that R4 lacked, and the worked floor, retaining walls, equipment, spoil rocks, dead growth, and energized pylons now read as one authored worksite from several views. The player route remains visibly open and no new obstruction defeats the arrival.

It is not a strict PASS. The new face reads as a short row of separate upright boulders rather than a convincingly cut or terraced excavation wall, and frame 04 composes it apart from most of the worksite. The wide arrival still gives more screen area to ordinary forest and foreground meadow than to the quarry. At night the floor, cart, retaining walls, and dark spoil mass lose most of their separation; the cyan conduit is much clearer than the excavation itself.

Retain R5B over R4. The remaining closure is structural/material integration of the cut face with the worked floor plus restrained warm work-light or reflected fill, not another isolated prop or larger cyan source.
