# T2 device judge: 7-inch readability

Judged from `_sheet_7inch_1..3.jpg` at 100%, as if each cell were the handheld screen. I opened full frames F01, F07–F14 and F19 only to confirm what a detail was.

## 1. Currents (F01–F06): WEAK
At 7 inches, every crossing shows a band of white dash streaks beside the dock (F01/F02 on the left of the dock, F03/F04 left of the dock, F05/F06 fanning out from the dock). They clearly mark a lane of moving water, and you can see its axis. You cannot see which way it flows. The dashes are symmetric strokes with no head or taper you can read at this size, and they recede along the view axis in perspective. Toward the far island or toward the camera reads equally well. Each pair shows no change between its two views that gives the direction away. So you can find the current, but not its flow direction.

## 2. Docks (F07–F13): YES (F12 WEAK)
People per frame: F07 2, F08 3, F09 2, F10 2, F11 2, F12 2, F13 2. In every frame the villagers stand in a cluster with a crate or stone prop at the dock head, just right of the player's back, so the eye lands on them within a second. Each figure is roughly 5 mm tall on the panel, small but dark against bright sand. F12 is weaker: the placeable cube and the player overlap that area, one figure sits against the cube's edge, and the two large creatures on the left pull the eye first. The people are still findable there.

## 3. Distance read (F14–F16): YES
- **F14:** the waterfall peak is findable as a small grey cone at the centre horizon. Its falls are only faint white slivers.
- **F15:** the peak is larger at left-centre, and two white falls read clearly.
- **F16:** the peak is large at the top centre, with a distinct central fall and mist.

Detail clearly builds from far to near. F14 is the marginal one, but it is still findable because it is centred and is the only grey peak.

## 4. Restoration (F17/F18 vs F19/F20): YES
Before restoration (F17/F18), a dense sheaf of bright white streaks fills the lower right third of the water around the swimmer. After restoration (F19/F20), only a few faint thin streaks remain. At a glance the water goes from "rushing" to "calm", and each half-second pair is consistent.

## 5. HUD: NO (illegible elements present)
- **"Day 1 · 08:00" (top centre, all HUD frames):** small, thin, low-contrast text over a bright sky. You cannot read it at 7 inches. In F12 it also sits on top of the waterfall, the landmark subject.
- **Hotbar slot numbers 1–5 and the M/I/B key glyphs on the Map/Satchel/Build strip (all HUD frames):** these glyphs are about 1 mm on the panel and cannot be read. The words Map, Satchel and Build are readable.
- **"FOOD" label (bottom left):** dim orange on a translucent panel over grass or sand. It is barely legible, and the percentage is borderline.
- **Readable elements:** the Main Story panel, the health "100 / 100" readout and the F12 "Open the eastern sluice" prompt are legible.
- **Coverage:** the Main Story panel and the hotbar together take up much of the right third of the screen. In F14 a creature at right sits just under the quest panel, and in F16 the hotbar crowds the fence and dock area. None fully hides a judged subject, apart from the clock over the waterfall in F12.
- **Not HUD:** the world sign in F03/F04/F14 ("Reedhaven Channel Open") cannot be read at this size.

## Overall
**7-inch readability: FAIL.** Q5 is a NO: the clock, hotbar numbers and key glyphs are illegible at handheld size. Q1 is WEAK on the very thing asked: the current is findable, but its flow direction is not.
