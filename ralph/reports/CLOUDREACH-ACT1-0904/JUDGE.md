Here, exposed slabs, suspended grass, bare planar chasms, and interior openings dominate. Repair geometry, scatter support, floor joins, and route composition before adding decorative detail.

Fix interaction framing, portrait preparation, prop visibility, and HUD coverage. This is not a finding that new humanoid meshes are required.
