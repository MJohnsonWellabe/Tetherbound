# Cloudreach blind visual judgment

Reviewed the supplied contact sheet, all ten supplied PNG frames at full size, the Tetherbound Meadows key art, and all five supplied Palworld screenshots. No source, implementation notes, prior judgments, or other reports were consulted. Creature absence is explicitly excluded. This judgment covers visible presentation; still images cannot establish motion quality, performance, or interaction reliability.

## Verdict

**A. Does this look like the provided Tetherbound key art? No.** The green vegetation, warm roofs, blue sky, and medieval silhouettes share some ingredients, but the compositions are dominated by bare geometric platforms and walls. The reference's connected landscape, layered vegetation, grounded settlement spaces, and inviting routes are missing from most views. This is a palette and composition judgment, not a demand for the painting's per-leaf fidelity.

**B. Does this look commercially comparable to the supplied Palworld references? No.** The terrain silhouettes, surface treatment, landmark construction, and environmental dressing fall visibly below those real-time screenshots. The broad adventure-game intent is recognizable, but that alone does not establish commercial visual comparability. Creature absence does not contribute to this failure.

## The three largest gaps, ranked

1. **The cliffs and distant landforms read as geometric enclosure pieces rather than an authored landscape.** In `03-windscar-ravine.png`, the entire background is a nearly level wall with repeated narrow vertical projections; at sheet scale it reads like a perimeter fence. `05-upper-cloudreach-cliffhold.png` is dominated by one enormous brown prism with a sharply separated tan cap, with little to establish geological structure or a destination. `02-lower-cliffs-galefoot.png` repeats the flat rim behind the houses. The pale formations in `04-high-roost-before-fly.png` and `08-upper-cliffhold-east-arrival.png` have huge blank planes and very few silhouette changes. Palworld's plateau reference establishes different landform scales, irregular outcrops, vegetated ledges, and overlapping depths; the key art does the same compositionally. Break the level rims and continuous vertical faces into differently sized masses, recesses, shelves, slopes, and selective vegetation clusters. Preserve the high-cliff identity, but give the large surfaces readable structure and the eye a sequence of near, middle, and distant forms.

2. **The ground reads as a textured platform with dressing placed along its edges.** The foreground of `02-lower-cliffs-galefoot.png` is a broad, pale, mottled plane; the village has little visible ground wear, planting, or domestic organization linking its buildings. `08-upper-cliffhold-east-arrival.png` has a perfectly edged, constant-width brown strip leading into a similarly bare settlement. The path in `04-high-roost-before-fly.png` consumes most of the lower image without verges, erosion, embedded stones, or enough surface variation to sell it as a traveled route. `01-arrival-first-reveal.png` has more useful tree and shrub framing, but its foreground still relies heavily on the flat ground texture. Palworld's open-field and base screenshots distinguish bare footfall areas from dimensional grass, shadows, and clustered objects; the key-art settlements have yards, fences, and connected worn routes. Add irregular path edges and transitions, localized ground cover, exposed rock, and coherent building-side clusters. Do not simply scatter objects uniformly over the empty space.

3. **The destination structures lack convincing material and architectural hierarchy.** In `06-summit-final-approach.png`, the fortress reads as two square towers and a flat central gate with uniformly patterned surfaces; the bright trim is a flat accent rather than a developed entrance treatment. In `09-final-arena-space.png`, giant dark walls fill nearly the whole frame with one coarse repeating stone pattern and little base, course, buttress, or doorway articulation. In `07-fly-only-destination.png`, a plain cyan oval sits on a rectangular pillar frame; it reads as an undeveloped marker, not a distinct mysterious artifact. The key-art stronghold has an unmistakable entrance sequence, layered masonry, banners, and occupation details; Palworld's plateau landmark has a distinct silhouette embedded in its environment. Develop the approach, bases, openings, supports, trim, and material scale with a clear focal hierarchy. Give the cyan feature a designed frame, surface behavior, and relationship to the supporting structure.

## Additional visible defects

- **Summit foreground obstruction:** `06-summit-final-approach.png` places the camera inside coarse, very long grass blades. Opaque diagonal strips cross the trainer, doorway, and roofline, obscuring the intended reveal even at sheet scale. Clear or shorten the vegetation in the camera/approach corridor and check this actual framing again. This frame cannot establish that the grass is universally oversized: proximity also enlarges it.
- **Material mismatch:** The timber-frame cottages in `02-lower-cliffs-galefoot.png` and `08-upper-cliffhold-east-arrival.png` have much finer structural detail than the nearby towers and cliffs. The disparity makes the latter appear unfinished. Match the readable detail scale and material language across the structures; adding equally fine noise to everything would not solve it.
- **Lighting and depth flattening:** `02-lower-cliffs-galefoot.png` and `08-upper-cliffhold-east-arrival.png` have broad pale ground with little modeled form, while the distant pale monoliths remain large blank shapes. `09-final-arena-space.png` goes to the opposite extreme: the walls become an almost undifferentiated dark mass. Build useful light/shadow grouping and depth separation without losing the fortress's weight. The existing tree shadows in `01-arrival-first-reveal.png` do help place objects on the ground.
- **Sky competes with the landmarks:** `04-high-roost-before-fly.png` and `05-upper-cloudreach-cliffhold.png` devote much of the image to clouds with soft luminous outlines around large flat gray interiors. The conspicuous cloud pattern pulls attention from already simple landmarks. Improve cloud shape/value variation and keep the landmark silhouette dominant in these views.
- **Limited character evidence:** The trainer is identifiable in most small views, with a coherent backpack and clothing silhouette. `06-summit-final-approach.png` loses that readability behind grass, and `09-final-arena-space.png` places the dark legs against dark ground. These rear-only views are insufficient for a defensible verdict on face, expression, or bespoke character appeal. No creature-art conclusion is made.

## What is actionable from this evidence

**Scene/material/composition work:** All three ranked gaps have substantial actionable portions: vary and layer landforms, adjust surface scale and transitions, cluster vegetation and settlement props, articulate buildings with reusable pieces, control approach framing, and improve light/value grouping. The summit grass obstruction is a direct scene fix.

**Art requirements:** The large exposed cliffs and fortress surfaces need more resolved shapes and material definition than the visible versions provide; the cyan destination feature needs a designed presentation. Those are visible art-quality requirements, but these images cannot establish whether suitable meshes, materials, or modular pieces already exist elsewhere in the build. A blind review cannot honestly claim that new purchases or generations are necessary. First demonstrate how existing assets resolve these specific visible gaps; if they cannot, the remaining need is for authored cliff/material and landmark detail, not additional creatures.
