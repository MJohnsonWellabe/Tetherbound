## Overall F08#3: FAIL
The rim crowding is fixed. Height still does not read in the primary frames, and the landmark is not yet coherent art.

## Fixes
1. **Capture:** move the arrival camera lower and farther out so the crown sits clearly above the plateau top line, with the pillar shaft and something far below both in frame.
2. **Capture:** frame the departure so the perch appears behind or beside the flyer. Keep the halo cloud out from behind the trainer.
3. **Scene/art:** add a visible world floor far below (valley, river, forest canopy or cloud sea with depth fade) as a height ruler. The void is currently flat green-grey fog.
4. **Scene/art:** lower the adjoining left plateau or the drum wall, or raise the perch, so the perch is the local high point.
5. **Scene/art:** the "needles" are uniform cylinders with plank caps and read as smokestacks or columns. Make them tapered, weathered stone spires of varied girth, and remove the table-like caps.
6. **Scene/art:** the cliffs are pale, flat and blotchy-tiled with no strata or warm/cool separation (against ART_DIRECTION's Cloudreach row). The drum wall is featureless.
7. **Scene/art:** replace the flat disc and puff placeholder clouds. Remove the stray bench, stray planks and the loose curved posts at the rim.
