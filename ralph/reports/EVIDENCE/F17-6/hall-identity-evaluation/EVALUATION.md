The revised `hall-waymark-v2-overlay.svg` is a transparent512x1420 cloth-space graphic, proportioned to the measured asset. It uses one bold home doorway and seven simple route marks above it, with a quiet lower field. The primary doorway must read at normal camera distance; counting small marks is close-view detail, not a navigation requirement. Cream over existing village teal follows the installed palette and avoids Team Tether red. The symbol asserts neither newly invented biome identity nor unlocked state. Flat clean vector edges remain a draft limitation: a final textile should retain weave, normal response and restrained edge wear; no glow, floating label or bright UI-white panel.
