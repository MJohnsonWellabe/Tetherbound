# Blind visual verdict: giant creature fit

The green antlered figure is the bound creature. The brown and black mass is the Tether Machine and its foreground dais. Pair 1 is an extremely close view of that structure; it does not establish physical penetration by the creature.

## Per-frame defect verdicts

- **pair-1-A — FAIL.** The machine structure fills the view and completely occludes the green bound creature. This frame does not show that the creature fits the chamber or establish a readable scale relationship.
- **pair-1-B — FAIL.** More of the masonry is visible, but the machine still hides almost all of the green creature; only a small pale-green fragment is visible near the upper right. Fit and scale remain unreadable. The close camera view alone does not prove physical intersection.
- **pair-2-A — WEAK.** The green creature is fully visible within the tall chamber and appears to meet the central platform. The surrounding machine mass is so tangled and bulky that its relation to the chamber corners does not read naturally.
- **pair-2-B — FAIL.** The antlers are cut by the top frame edge, and the large foreground ramp hides the creature’s relation to the platform. The frame does not communicate how it fits the machine.
- **pair-3-A — WEAK.** The creature fits within the visible chamber without clear body-machine penetration, but foreground spires and the large platform crowd the view and obscure the machine boundaries.
- **pair-3-B — WEAK.** The wider view shows the chamber corners and the creature fitting in the room without obvious penetration. Angular spires still overwhelm the central platform, so the machine’s mass does not resolve coherently.

## Pair preferences

- **Pair 1: B preferred.** It exposes more chamber context, though both frames leave the creature almost entirely occluded.
- **Pair 2: A preferred.** The creature and its relation to the platform are more completely framed.
- **Pair 3: B preferred.** The wider view makes the chamber corners and creature-to-room scale easier to read.
