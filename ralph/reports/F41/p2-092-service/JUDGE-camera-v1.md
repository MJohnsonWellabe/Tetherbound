## Full visual bar

**A. Does this read as belonging to the world in `tetherbound-meadows-keyart.png`? No.** The reference promises bright, inviting meadows, varied green ground cover, flowers, streams, and layered hills. These frames are a dim, rainy, mostly bare brown clearing with scattered dark trunks and little depth. The amber-and-black boss has a strong silhouette, but the scene’s palette and mood do not carry the reference world.

**B. Beside `palworld-0*.jpg`, would someone say this is trying to be the same kind of game? Yes.** The large creature encounter, trainer, companion, health display, and active fight read as the same creature-battling adventure genre. The frames fall well short of the references in combat staging and the amount of lush, readable world around the fight.

## Three largest gaps from the references

1. **Combat staging still hides the people and companions.** In 01-B and 02-B the trainer merges into the boss’s legs; 03-A lets the boss consume the frame; in 04-A and 06-A the trainer is nearly lost beneath it, and the companion is still obscured in 04-B and 06-B. Palworld 01 and 03 keep the human and boss in separate, readable parts of the composition while preserving a large boss. Improve camera framing and subject placement so both trainer and ally have clear ground around their silhouettes without changing creature world scale.
2. **The environment lacks the reference’s color and depth.** Across all twelve frames, the flat brown ground, dark trees, and sparse low plants replace the references’ vivid grass, varied foliage, flowers, open paths, and distant landmarks. This is addressable through scene palette, lighting, vegetation density and clustering, and clearer background layers.
3. **Supporting character and companion presence is too weak at encounter distance.** In 01-A, 03-B, 04-B, and 06-B the trainer or companion becomes a tiny shape beside the boss; the green companions also read as small shrub-like forms at this distance. Palworld 01 and 03 make the human and nearby creatures easy to distinguish through stronger silhouettes and more expressive, readable forms. Camera composition can help, but stronger trainer and companion art and animation are needed for the full gap.

## Limits of the evidence

These stills show composition only. They prove neither physical collision nor a world-scale change, and they provide no evidence about performance. This judgment makes no claims on those points.
