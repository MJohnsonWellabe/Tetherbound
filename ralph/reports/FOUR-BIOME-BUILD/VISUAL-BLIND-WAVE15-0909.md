## Ranked gaps

1. **Architecture and value structure — apparatus, approach, standing.** The references separate structural planes, entrances and focal objects with readable light/value changes. Here the massive platform/arch becomes a dark block and the machine mounting lacks a persuasive visual hierarchy. Scene-fixable: lighting, mounting composition, material scale and silhouette articulation. More distinctive structural parts may require additional authored art, but these images cannot establish what assets already exist.
2. **Ground composition — road and approach.** The references organize worn paths, grass edges and clustered activity areas. Here isolated clumps sit evenly across broad brown space, with small debris/crates offering little sense of purpose. Primarily scene-fixable through ground transitions and deliberate grouping; adding more uniform scatter alone would not solve it.
3. **Cast material consistency and framing — road and standing.** The references preserve faces, hair volume and clear clothing masses. The bright white hair patch, mottled dark outfits and cropped foreground bodies weaken that clarity. Framing/value fixes are scene work; stronger character material/identity treatment is an art problem if existing assets cannot provide it.
