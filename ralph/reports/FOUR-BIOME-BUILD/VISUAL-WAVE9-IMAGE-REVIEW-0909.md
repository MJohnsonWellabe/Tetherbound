# Wave 9: craftsperson paired pixel review

**This is not a blind review.** It reuses a reviewer with operational naming exposure. Before inspection, an unsolicited author message disclosed that the left uses a self-lit full atlas and the right retains a held albedo derivative with emission disabled. The image labels also disclose source/current rendering intent. Root explicitly authorized a pixel review with this contamination recorded. No implementation files or author reports were read. This evidence is weaker than an independent blind verdict and does not automatically authorize shipping.

Only input: `shots/character-craftsperson-emission/craftsperson-source-vs-pbr-1280x800.png`, left versus right, against the previously inspected key art and five Palworld references. Both complete bodies and shadows are present. Judged at displayed size as well as full frame.

## Visible comparison

The right is meaningfully more restrained in its highlights: the face, shirt front and toe caps no longer form the conspicuous pale yellow-white spots seen on the left. Brown leather, green cloth and dark vest are easier to group as coherent surfaces. Both figures retain the same immediately legible stocky, bearded, goggle-wearing artisan identity and tool-belt silhouette. The right is the stronger material presentation in this particular fixture.

The tradeoff is visible: the right loses some separation across the dark beard, vest, gloves and belt, and its trousers/tools become less readable at small size. The left communicates those small details more brightly but also looks more uniformly shiny and harshly lit. Neither presents a new identity or a major silhouette improvement. Both feet appear planted; neither shows an obvious floating gap. The different shadow extents are visible, but the still does not establish their cause or whether either is correct in a world scene.

## Ranked gaps

1. **Busy and generic character identity (both figures).** Goggles, beard and densely equipped belt convey an ordinary fantasy artisan effectively, but lack the unusually clear, bespoke identity of the reference cast. Material adjustment cannot create that distinctiveness; resolving it fully may require character art/design work.
2. **Face and garment value grouping (right especially).** Dark beard and torso merge, while the face has hard light/dark patches. Scene lighting and material tuning may improve separation; the image alone cannot distinguish baked detail from lighting contributions.
3. **Small-detail noise (both figures).** Gold belt fittings, glove edges and layered clothing compete in a compact silhouette. Simplifying or regrouping accents is partly material work; silhouette changes would need art work. The right reduces glare without fully resolving this density.

## Bars and limits

**A — Yes, provisionally for this character.** Warm natural browns/greens, approachable proportions and readable occupational cues can belong to the key-art world. The right's calmer finish supports that reading.

**B — Yes.** Both are recognizable stylized third-person adventure characters of the same broad game category as the Palworld references. This is not a claim of equal bespoke character quality.

Narrow pixel preference: right over left for material restraint, with reduced dark-detail legibility as the regression to retain. The bare static stage cannot establish nighttime/world integration, animation, gameplay readability under movement, performance or whole-biome acceptance. A shipping decision should not treat this contaminated review as the missing independent blind gate.
