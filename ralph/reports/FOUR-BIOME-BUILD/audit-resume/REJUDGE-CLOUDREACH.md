# Cloudreach: independent blind visual judgment

Evidence: 24 canonical individual PNGs in `shots/catalogue/cloudreach/round-pylon-20260909T015726Z`, the cloudreach contact sheet, combined-116.png and combined-page-01 through combined-page-04.png in `shots/catalogue/audit-pylon-sheets/`. All canonical individuals were opened, including blocked views. The full, unmodified `.claude/skills/visual-judge/SKILL.md` was used. Primary references inspected: `docs/reference/tetherbound-meadows-keyart.png` and all five `docs/reference/palworld-0*.jpg`. No code, implementation reports, or history informed this judgment. Sheet thumbnails were judged at small size; full frames supplied detail.

## Creatures and characters first

The fox, bird and white-blue wolves in `cloudreach__gate_lower_cliffs__02__galefoot_waycamp__day.png` and `cloudreach__windscar_ravine__06__windscar_flight_aerie__day.png` clearly advertise creature companionship. Their silhouettes are appealing enough to recognize, but they do not yet hold up as a cohesive cast beside Palworld. The fox is almost flat luminous yellow, the bird has a much more layered mottled feather treatment, and the wolves have harsh white/blue patches. Their surfaces are more visually agitated than the trainer's relatively restrained clothing. Several of the best subjects sit behind the quest panel or are cut off at the right edge. These are not successful showcase compositions.

## Full rubric

1. **Silhouette and readability at small size.** The pale-shirted trainer and bridge are clear in `cloudreach__broken_causeways__03__three_bells_bridge__day.png`. The wolf remains legible in `cloudreach__windscar_ravine__06__windscar_flight_aerie__day.png`, but the bird in `cloudreach__gate_lower_cliffs__02__galefoot_waycamp__day.png` merges into the UI and its companion. `cloudreach__windscar_ravine__05__windscar_beacon__day.png` and `cloudreach__windscar_ravine__05__windscar_beacon__night.png` offer no readable player or destination: a close surface fills almost everything. `cloudreach__high_roost_sky_shrine__07__sky_shrine__day.png` substitutes a large stone cylinder for the player and shrine overview.
2. **Colour and value structure.** Green grass, grey cliffs and blue sky form an intelligible region in `cloudreach__gate_lower_cliffs__01__realm_gate_crag__day.png`. However, `cloudreach__upper_cloudreach__10__old_wind_observatory__day.png` has pure-white cloud masses that overwhelm the mid-distance. `cloudreach__gate_lower_cliffs__02__galefoot_waycamp__night.png` renders creatures much brighter than their setting while the trainer darkens. The giant brown columns in `cloudreach__high_roost_sky_shrine__08__the_high_perches__day.png` introduce a large plain material block unlike the nearby worked stone. No clear friendly oxblood misuse is visible.
3. **Intentionality.** The bridge in `cloudreach__broken_causeways__03__three_bells_bridge__day.png` is a useful compositional route, and huts plus bed/camp ring in `cloudreach__gate_lower_cliffs__02__galefoot_waycamp__day.png` describe a stop. But `cloudreach__upper_cloudreach__09__cliffhold__day.png` has sharply edged polygonal path fragments, tiny isolated bushes and identical-looking huts, while `cloudreach__high_roost_sky_shrine__08__the_high_perches__day.png` places giant plain columns over a mostly empty grass floor. These read assembled rather than like a lived-in sky civilization.
4. **Lighting.** Direct shadows ground the trainer and bridge in `cloudreach__broken_causeways__03__three_bells_bridge__day.png`. The time change is visible, yet the very bright grey-blue night sky in `cloudreach__gate_lower_cliffs__01__realm_gate_crag__night.png` conflicts with the nearly black foreground. `cloudreach__high_roost_sky_shrine__07__sky_shrine__night.png` shows well-lit pale distance and dark local structure without a convincing local night focal point. This sky is also much brighter than Meadows' night sky on the combined sheet.
5. **Horizon and depth.** The high cliffs do convey altitude and large scale in `cloudreach__upper_cloudreach__10__old_wind_observatory__day.png`. Their repeated vertical bands, table-like green tops and overlapping white ellipses read as constructed tiers, not the reference's naturally eroded landforms and atmospheric layers. `cloudreach__upper_cloudreach__10__old_wind_observatory__night.png` washes the cliffs toward the sky value. The foreground-to-far landscape connection remains weak.
6. **Interface.** Text and margins are usable; the upper-right objective and lower-right empty slots occupy valuable creature space in `cloudreach__gate_lower_cliffs__02__galefoot_waycamp__day.png`. The minimap in `cloudreach__gate_lower_cliffs__01__realm_gate_crag__day.png` is too visually soft to communicate much beyond a pointer and a few symbols. Large blank slots with minute keycap numbers create a poor information-to-area ratio. UI is judged on its own readability, not Palworld styling.
7. **Artefacts.** The fully obstructed beacon pair `cloudreach__windscar_ravine__05__windscar_beacon__day.png` / `cloudreach__windscar_ravine__05__windscar_beacon__night.png`, player-hidden shrine pair `cloudreach__high_roost_sky_shrine__07__sky_shrine__day.png` / `cloudreach__high_roost_sky_shrine__07__sky_shrine__night.png`, and player-hidden overlook pair `cloudreach__summit_final_stronghold__12__waterward_overlook__day.png` / `cloudreach__summit_final_stronghold__12__waterward_overlook__night.png` are concrete failures of scene presentation. The grass/path boundary in `cloudreach__upper_cloudreach__09__cliffhold__day.png` is visibly angular and patchworked. Cloud shapes in `cloudreach__upper_cloudreach__10__old_wind_observatory__day.png` have hard, flat-looking overlaps. None of these stills identifies the responsible implementation.
8. **Scale agreement.** With the trainer at 1.80 m, the bed in `cloudreach__gate_lower_cliffs__02__galefoot_waycamp__day.png` has a plausible person-sized relationship; the fox and bird are substantially larger companions. Wolves in `cloudreach__windscar_ravine__06__windscar_flight_aerie__day.png` are also clearly larger than the trainer. This establishes that large creatures can read large here; it does not identify their design roles. Door proportions in `cloudreach__upper_cloudreach__09__cliffhold__day.png` appear small against the human, and the immense undecorated columns in `cloudreach__high_roost_sky_shrine__08__the_high_perches__day.png` lack intermediate details that would make their monumental scale believable.

## Product reading and evidence limits

Cohesion: common trainer, huts and stonework connect this region to the others, but the primitive-looking columns/clouds and highly bright creatures undermine a common art finish. Authoredness: a bridge/camp sequence reads intentionally; several other destinations read as large props on blank grass. Biome distinction: altitude, cliffs and cloud layer are immediately different from Meadows, though local ground dressing remains similar. Cast appeal/readability: some appealing silhouettes, poorly staged and inconsistently surfaced. Navigable landmarks: bridge and distant spire in `cloudreach__broken_causeways__03__three_bells_bridge__day.png` suggest direction; the beacon and shrine captures do not. Visual readiness: **not ready at the shipping-art reference bar**.

Scene-fixable: framing/occlusion, creature spacing relative to HUD, grass clustering, path transitions, sky/local lighting balance, and better cliff/cloud compositions. Art-needed: believable monumental architecture detail, a coherent cast surface finish, and cloud/rock forms that do not look like simple stacked primitives. This is a requirement for the visible look; screenshots cannot prove whether suitable unused assets already exist. Motion, combat, performance and actual reachability are outside this evidence.

## Three ranked reference gaps

1. **Cast unity and display.** Palworld 04 separates rider, mount and companion into strong silhouettes with controlled material detail. `cloudreach__gate_lower_cliffs__02__galefoot_waycamp__day.png` jams differently treated fox/bird art behind HUD, and `cloudreach__windscar_ravine__06__windscar_flight_aerie__day.png` clips a wolf. Re-stage immediately; unify creature surface/shape treatment as art work.
2. **Believable landmark and terrain language.** The key art and Palworld 04 use broken, layered terrain and detailed recognizable structures. `cloudreach__high_roost_sky_shrine__08__the_high_perches__day.png` has plain giant columns, while `cloudreach__upper_cloudreach__10__old_wind_observatory__day.png` has banded cliffs and flat white cloud discs. Some composition can improve this, but these visible forms need art attention.
3. **Finished, readable inhabited spaces.** Palworld 05 gives an open base specific activities and coherent ground. `cloudreach__upper_cloudreach__09__cliffhold__day.png` has pasted path polygons and sparse isolated props; `cloudreach__windscar_ravine__05__windscar_beacon__day.png` offers only a wall. Scene dressing and camera clearance address much of this.

**A.** Do these frames read as belonging to the world in `docs/reference/tetherbound-meadows-keyart.png` — the project's own art direction?

**No.** Green uplands and medieval huts are compatible ingredients, but the dominant plain pillars, tiered cliffs, hard cloud discs and sparse sky sites do not yet feel like an extension of the key art's natural, inhabited fantasy world. Scene composition/density and lighting can close part of the gap; finished monumental/environment art and cast material direction are also needed.

**B.** Shown these frames beside `docs/reference/palworld-0*.jpg`, would someone say these are trying to be the same kind of game?

**Yes.** The trainer, conspicuous fantasy companions, outdoor camp, routes and distant fantasy landmark make the same broad creature-adventure aspiration unmistakable. This does not mean these images match Palworld's shipping quality.
