# Four biomes: independent blind visual judgment

Evidence: all **116 canonical individual PNGs** were opened and visually inspected: Cloudreach 24 in `shots/catalogue/cloudreach/round-pylon-20260909T015726Z`; Meadows 20 in `shots/catalogue/meadows/round-camera-20260909T011407Z`; Stormwood 24 in `shots/catalogue/stormwood/round-pylon-20260909T020124Z`; Water 48 in `shots/catalogue/water/round-camera-20260909T013251Z`. Also inspected all four per-biome sheets, combined-116.png and combined-page-01 through combined-page-04.png in `shots/catalogue/audit-pylon-sheets/`. Sheets and validation previews are not additional canonical frames. The identity-only frame-map.csv was used to retain exact filenames. References inspected: the Meadows key-art board and all five Palworld reference screenshots. Judgment uses the full, unmodified visual-judge skill; no source, reports, history or implementation explanation informed it.

## Creatures and characters first

The cast does not yet meet the project's stated visual bar. There are appealing individual ideas: the white round creature in `meadows__band4_upper_meadows_ironwood__07__the_ironwood_grove__day.png`, the foxes in `stormwood__conductor_run__05__rodline_post__day.png`, and the pink aquatic animal in `water__drowned_garden__22__drowned_garden_beach__day.png` all communicate approachable fantasy animals. The trainer is consistently recognizable by silhouette and costume.

The roster as presented is not cohesive enough to be the central attraction. `cloudreach__gate_lower_cliffs__02__galefoot_waycamp__day.png` combines an almost flat yellow fox with densely mottled feathered bird art; `stormwood__conductor_run__06__the_capacitor_grove__day.png` combines acid green/purple and magenta/black bodies; `stormwood__deepwood__10__the_fallen_giant__night.png` makes the giant cyan frog's patches and spikes stronger than its face; `water__tidal_cradle__09__aquaryn_tidal_basin__night.png` puts an extremely ornate blue/gold creature into an almost bare terrain trough. Palworld's screenshots establish much more controlled face, body and material hierarchy. Several creature shots are blocked, clipped or entangled with UI. This is a visible art-direction and presentation criticism, not a claim about asset provenance.

## Full rubric

1. **Silhouette and readability at small size.** The sheets establish four broad environment types, but human/creature recognition varies dramatically. `meadows__band4_upper_meadows_ironwood__07__the_ironwood_grove__day.png` preserves the white creature against greenery; `water__drowned_garden__22__drowned_garden_beach__day.png` preserves the pink creature against sand. `meadows__band4_upper_meadows_ironwood__07__the_ironwood_grove__night.png` loses the trainer's lower body at night. `cloudreach__windscar_ravine__05__windscar_beacon__day.png`, `stormwood__cinder_verge__01__the_struck_sentinel__day.png`, `stormwood__dynamo__11__the_glass_field__day.png` and `water__veilfall__16__veilfall_mountain_crown__day.png` are close surfaces rather than readable destination scenes. These blocked tiles remain in the judgment.
2. **Colour and value structure.** Meadows' golden green and warm stone in `meadows__band1_lower_meadows__01__grandpas_village__day.png` is the most convincing natural palette. Cloudreach `cloudreach__upper_cloudreach__10__old_wind_observatory__day.png` uses stark white cloud discs; Stormwood `stormwood__conductor_run__06__the_capacitor_grove__day.png` saturates both decorative vegetation and creatures; Water `water__drowned_garden__21__drowned_garden_terraces__day.png` compresses land and sea into pale values. The same trainer and interface connect the images, but regional lighting/material treatments do not yet feel like a single controlled visual system. Oxblood faction banners work in `meadows__band5_stronghold_approach__10__meadows_hall__day.png`; widespread red ornamental vegetation in `stormwood__glowmoss_hollows__03__the_lantern_pools__day.png` weakens reserved danger colour.
3. **Intentionality.** `meadows__band1_lower_meadows__01__grandpas_village__day.png` and `meadows__band5_stronghold_approach__10__meadows_hall__day.png` give credible authored centers. `cloudreach__broken_causeways__03__three_bells_bridge__day.png` gives an obvious bridge route. Much of `stormwood__glowmoss_hollows__04__crown_overlook__day.png` looks like repeated tree/plant scattering, and `water__reedhaven__03__reedhaven_woven_hall__day.png` is a named settlement represented by empty grass and a creature. Distinct labels are not a substitute for visible distinct places. The whole catalogue is more convincing as a collection of biome ingredients than as a finished inhabited world.
4. **Lighting.** Objects often cast grounding shadows and day/night is recognizable. The regional contrast is inconsistent: Meadows night `meadows__band2_stone_and_root__03__the_old_quarry__night.png` is deeply crushed, Cloudreach night `cloudreach__gate_lower_cliffs__01__realm_gate_crag__night.png` has a very pale bright sky, and Stormwood night `stormwood__glowmoss_hollows__03__the_lantern_pools__night.png` leaves props broadly bright against dark sky. Water `water__veilfall__15__the_veilfall_cascade__night.png` becomes almost white. The key art uses darkness to frame useful pools of light; the catalogue frequently loses the focal subject or lights it without a coherent surrounding value structure.
5. **Horizon and depth.** Cloudreach `cloudreach__upper_cloudreach__10__old_wind_observatory__day.png` and Water `water__first_shore__02__first_shore_horizon_stones__day.png` communicate scale through cliffs and separated landforms. Yet banded cliffs, flat cloud ellipses, smooth capped islands and repetitive empty horizons advertise construction more than a natural place. Stormwood `stormwood__glowmoss_hollows__04__crown_overlook__day.png` opens straight into pale vacant distance. Meadows `meadows__band3_the_river_lock__06__old_mill_crossing__day.png` layers path/river/land better, although its tree masks the tower. Static evidence cannot establish LOD transitions or popping.
6. **Interface.** Safe margins and basic quest/interaction legibility are sound. Empty five-slot panels and a broad command strip consume a great deal of useful scene area in every region, especially `cloudreach__gate_lower_cliffs__02__galefoot_waycamp__day.png`, `meadows__band2_stone_and_root__04__the_burrow_warrens__day.png` and `water__tidal_cradle__09__aquaryn_tidal_basin__day.png`. Tiny white keycap numbers float inside large otherwise empty boxes. The minimaps vary from recognizable route hints to soft colour fields. These are internal hierarchy/readability findings; no Palworld UI comparison is being used.
7. **Artefacts.** Multiple destination captures have severe foreground occlusion: `cloudreach__windscar_ravine__05__windscar_beacon__day.png`, `meadows__band5_stronghold_approach__09__stronghold_approach__day.png`, `stormwood__cinder_verge__01__the_struck_sentinel__day.png`, `stormwood__dynamo__11__the_glass_field__day.png`, `stormwood__dynamo__12__the_stormheart_tree__day.png`, `water__veilfall__16__veilfall_mountain_crown__day.png`. Plain pale arch/circle structures in `stormwood__hollow_crown__07__the_crown_arch__day.png` and `water__salt_crown__11__salt_crown_tide_shrine__day.png`, blank beach walls in `water__brine_steps__06__brine_steps_east_beach__day.png`, and cut polygonal path edges in `cloudreach__upper_cloudreach__09__cliffhold__day.png` look unfinished beside more detailed assets. `water__sluice_isle__14__sluice_channel_bridge__day.png` shows straight water-value boundaries. The visible symptom is established; an exact runtime or authoring cause is not.
8. **Scale agreement.** Using the trainer as 1.80 m, this catalogue does show meaningful creature size variation: small white animal `meadows__band4_upper_meadows_ironwood__07__the_ironwood_grove__day.png`, small foxes `stormwood__conductor_run__05__rodline_post__day.png`, much larger wolf `cloudreach__windscar_ravine__06__windscar_flight_aerie__day.png`, enormous frog `stormwood__deepwood__10__the_fallen_giant__night.png`, larger ornate aquatic creature `water__tidal_cradle__09__aquaryn_tidal_basin__night.png`. There is no basis to say the whole roster is uniformly sized. There is also no basis to certify practice-versus-companion hierarchy without visible role labels and shared ground-plane views. Low cottage openings in `stormwood__conductor_run__05__rodline_post__day.png` and immense undetailed columns in `cloudreach__high_roost_sky_shrine__08__the_high_perches__day.png` weaken environmental scale agreement. Several near-body frames cannot support exact relative measurements.

## Visually supportable product answers

- **Cohesion:** Partial. The shared trainer, medieval building family, flora vocabulary and interface connect the regions. Different creature surface treatments, plain primitive-looking props and inconsistent night values prevent a convincing unified finish.
- **Authoredness:** Uneven and insufficient overall. Meadows village/hall and Cloudreach bridge/camp are believable local compositions; repeated woodland scatter and many undressed Water sites are not.
- **Biome distinction:** Yes at the broad level: pastoral village/hills, high cliffs/clouds, grey-trunk dark woodland, coastal islands. Internal landmark/site distinction is much weaker, particularly in Water and Stormwood.
- **Cast appeal and readability:** Some genuine visual appeal is present, especially small simple silhouettes. The roster as a whole is not yet consistently appealing/readable at the reference bar; noise, mismatched treatment and occlusion are recurring defects.
- **Navigable landmarks:** Some visual direction exists through bridges, roads, gates and distant peaks. Many named destinations are hidden or not visually apparent, so the catalogue does not establish a dependable landmark network. Actual navigability needs play.
- **Readiness:** **No, these images are not ready at the Palworld shipping-art bar.** This is a still-image product-art verdict, not a statement that the game cannot be played.

No inference is made about combat quality, progression completeness, frame rate, traversal feel, creature animation, swimming, multiplayer or motion artefacts. No prior frame set was provided for a controlled before/after comparison, so this review does not claim improvement or regression.

## Scene-fixable versus art-needed

**Scene-fixable evidence:** clear foreground obstructions and preserve player/target sightlines; stage creatures away from UI; unify regional night values; reduce competing saturation; author routes, clearings and foliage clusters; improve ground/path transitions; compose landmarks into their approaches; add scene depth around empty islands/forest horizons. These are addressable visual needs, not prescribed source changes.

**Art-needed evidence:** the displayed cast needs a common shape/material finish and stronger face/body hierarchy; blank walls and pale primitive-looking landmark pieces need finished treatment; Cloudreach needs convincing monumental/cloud/rock forms; Water needs demonstrated coastal habitation/landmark and shoreline material vocabulary. A scene pass cannot make every plain shape or inconsistent creature surface into finished art. However, the screenshots cannot prove whether suitable unused assets already exist in the build. “Needs art in the visible result” is supported; “must buy a particular asset because none exists” is not.

## Three ranked reference gaps

1. **A coherent, expressive creature cast integrated with its trainer and world.** Palworld 01/03/04 give creatures clean dominant masses, expressive faces and deliberate staging. `cloudreach__gate_lower_cliffs__02__galefoot_waycamp__day.png`, `stormwood__conductor_run__06__the_capacitor_grove__day.png`, `stormwood__deepwood__10__the_fallen_giant__night.png` and `water__tidal_cradle__09__aquaryn_tidal_basin__night.png` instead combine conflicting surface treatments, busy internal detail and weak environmental integration. Staging/value is scene work; consistent cast finish is art work.
2. **Authored, inhabited landscapes with clear destinations.** The key art and Palworld 02/04/05 connect routes, vegetation, settlements and landmarks. `stormwood__glowmoss_hollows__04__crown_overlook__day.png` and `water__reedhaven__03__reedhaven_woven_hall__day.png` read as scatter/empty terrain, while `cloudreach__upper_cloudreach__09__cliffhold__day.png` exposes assembled ground pieces. Layout and density can address much; finished landmark/prop art must also appear.
3. **Unobstructed presentation with coherent depth and lighting.** The references make subjects and terrain layers legible. `cloudreach__windscar_ravine__05__windscar_beacon__day.png` and `water__veilfall__16__veilfall_mountain_crown__day.png` hide the world, `meadows__band2_stone_and_root__03__the_old_quarry__night.png` loses it to black, `water__veilfall__15__the_veilfall_cascade__night.png` loses it to pale wash, and `cloudreach__upper_cloudreach__10__old_wind_observatory__day.png` substitutes hard white cloud discs for atmosphere. Camera/staging/value repairs are immediate scene work, with environment art needed where crude visible forms remain.

**A.** Do these frames read as belonging to the world in `docs/reference/tetherbound-meadows-keyart.png` — the project's own art direction?

**No.** Meadows contains clear yes examples, especially its village and hall. Across all four biomes, however, bare Water sites, repeated open woodland, primitive-looking landmarks and inconsistent cast/lighting treatments prevent the set from reading as one convincing extension of the key art's natural, inhabited, inviting world. Scene density, palette, lighting and composition can close substantial gaps; coherent cast and finished environment/landmark art are also required in the visible result.

**B.** Shown these frames beside `docs/reference/palworld-0*.jpg`, would someone say these are trying to be the same kind of game?

**Yes.** The third-person adventurer, small appealing creatures, large elemental animals, camps, settlements and broad outdoor exploration plainly communicate an attempt at the same kind of creature-adventure game. A viewer can recognize that ambition while also seeing a large gap in execution. This yes must not be used as a shipping-quality acceptance.
