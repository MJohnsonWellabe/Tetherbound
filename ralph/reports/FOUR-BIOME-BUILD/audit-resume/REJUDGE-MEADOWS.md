# Meadows: independent blind visual judgment

Evidence: 20 canonical individual PNGs in `shots/catalogue/meadows/round-camera-20260909T011407Z`, the meadows contact sheet, combined-116.png and combined-page-01 through combined-page-04.png in `shots/catalogue/audit-pylon-sheets/`. All canonical individuals were opened, including blocked views. The full, unmodified `.claude/skills/visual-judge/SKILL.md` was used. Primary references inspected: `docs/reference/tetherbound-meadows-keyart.png` and all five `docs/reference/palworld-0*.jpg`. No code, implementation reports, or history informed this judgment. Sheet thumbnails were judged at small size; full frames supplied detail.

## Creatures and characters first

The white, round creature has immediate appeal and reads cleanly in `meadows__band4_upper_meadows_ironwood__07__the_ironwood_grove__day.png`. The boar in `meadows__band2_stone_and_root__04__the_burrow_warrens__day.png` has a recognizable face, but blotchy high-contrast surface markings compete with its anatomy. The trainer's backpack, pale collar and blue jacket make an identifiable adventurer; the assembled human cast in `meadows__band3_the_river_lock__05__the_tether_relay__day.png` still looks generic and uneven, with faces, proportions and costume treatment less unified than the character/creature partnership in Palworld 04. These are recognizable game characters, but these pictures do not yet establish a distinctive, polished cast at the reference bar. An attractive creature silhouette does not excuse the visible surface and presentation problems.

## Full rubric

1. **Silhouette and readability at small size.** Village roofs, trees, fences and player remain identifiable in `meadows__band1_lower_meadows__01__grandpas_village__day.png`. The white creature remains identifiable in `meadows__band4_upper_meadows_ironwood__07__the_ironwood_grove__day.png`. At night, the player's dark head and legs disappear into the ground in `meadows__band4_upper_meadows_ironwood__07__the_ironwood_grove__night.png`; the white collar becomes the main player marker. In `meadows__band2_stone_and_root__03__the_old_quarry__day.png`, dead trunks, sign, fences and rocks interrupt the line toward the large creature; its rocky back resembles another prop.
2. **Colour and value structure.** The ochre paths, green hills, warm roofs and stone village in `meadows__band1_lower_meadows__01__grandpas_village__day.png` belong together. The grass in `meadows__band4_upper_meadows_ironwood__08__the_ridgeline_watch__day.png` is heavily yellow-green and mottled, with competing pale flowers at near-uniform visual importance. `meadows__band2_stone_and_root__03__the_old_quarry__night.png` crushes many useful distinctions into black. Oxblood banners at the fortified gate in `meadows__band1_lower_meadows__02__the_south_bridge__day.png` and hall in `meadows__band5_stronghold_approach__10__meadows_hall__day.png` read as a coherent faction/danger language; no clear friendly oxblood leakage is established here.
3. **Intentionality.** `meadows__band1_lower_meadows__01__grandpas_village__day.png` has an actual settlement composition: roof group, well, fences and people. `meadows__band5_stronghold_approach__10__meadows_hall__day.png` arranges banners and braziers around a clear entrance. Conversely `meadows__band4_upper_meadows_ironwood__08__the_ridgeline_watch__day.png` repeats similarly sized V-shaped flower shrubs across exposed ground, and the blocky relay enclosure in `meadows__band3_the_river_lock__05__the_tether_relay__day.png` places isolated crates and people across a broad bare floor. Local authorship is present, but the intervening landscape reads scattered.
4. **Lighting.** Day/night are unambiguous. Shadows anchor buildings and give village paths form in `meadows__band1_lower_meadows__01__grandpas_village__day.png`; warm braziers make `meadows__band5_stronghold_approach__10__meadows_hall__night.png` more legible and purposeful than most exterior nights. `meadows__band1_lower_meadows__02__the_south_bridge__night.png` and `meadows__band3_the_river_lock__06__old_mill_crossing__night.png` lose the walking surface and player separation under deep dark foregrounds. The enormous white moon draws more attention than the destination.
5. **Horizon and depth.** `meadows__band3_the_river_lock__06__old_mill_crossing__day.png` offers foreground path, adjacent river and distant terrain, but the central tree hides much of the tower. `meadows__band4_upper_meadows_ironwood__08__the_ridgeline_watch__day.png` has broad undifferentiated far hills instead of the key art's layered silhouettes and destination hierarchy. The faint vertical forms along the horizon in `meadows__band4_upper_meadows_ironwood__08__the_ridgeline_watch__night.png` read suspiciously repetitive, but their cause cannot be determined from a still. No temporal LOD claim is possible.
6. **Interface.** Safe margins and readable quest text are present. In `meadows__band2_stone_and_root__04__the_burrow_warrens__day.png` the large empty five-slot panel and command strip cover the boar's face/body and lower scene; the slot keycaps are tiny relative to the blank panels. The lower-left food label has weak contrast. The quest instruction is identical across these distant locations; this establishes visual repetition, not a progression defect.
7. **Artefacts.** `meadows__band2_stone_and_root__04__the_burrow_warrens__day.png` and `meadows__band2_stone_and_root__04__the_burrow_warrens__night.png` are cramped between a near character, near creature and dark geometry, preventing a readable view of the warrens. `meadows__band5_stronghold_approach__09__stronghold_approach__day.png` and `meadows__band5_stronghold_approach__09__stronghold_approach__night.png` hide the player behind the pylon. The relay's abruptly edged tan ground patches in `meadows__band3_the_river_lock__05__the_tether_relay__day.png` look pasted onto its grey surface. These are visible presentation defects; camera/source causes are unproven.
8. **Scale agreement.** Taking the trainer as 1.80 m, village doors, fences and benches in `meadows__band1_lower_meadows__01__grandpas_village__day.png` are broadly plausible. The white round creature in `meadows__band4_upper_meadows_ironwood__07__the_ironwood_grove__day.png` reads smaller than the trainer, while the rocky animal in `meadows__band2_stone_and_root__03__the_old_quarry__day.png` reads materially larger, though distance makes exact ratios unsafe. The boar close-up in `meadows__band2_stone_and_root__04__the_burrow_warrens__day.png` cannot establish a reliable full-body scale. No visible basis identifies practice-versus-companion roles, so that hierarchy cannot be certified.

## Product reading and evidence limits

Cohesion: strongest of these four sets, especially settlement and hall. Authoredness: convincing at those two destinations, inconsistent between them. Biome distinction: readable as a pastoral starting region. Cast appeal/readability: a promising white creature and legible trainer, with inconsistent surface treatment and weak night separation. Navigable landmarks: paths and gates give visual direction, but some named destinations are hidden by framing. Visual readiness: **not ready at the Palworld shipping-art bar**; the strong local compositions do not cancel the blocked captures and weak exterior nights. Stills do not establish combat, animation, traversal, progression or performance.

Scene-fixable evidence: night values, destination sightlines, prop/foliage clustering, removal of HUD obstruction, camera clearance, and ground-patch transitions. Art-needed evidence: the displayed cast needs a consistent material/shape finish and stronger character identity; foliage needs more coherent massing if current assets cannot supply it. These images do not prove which replacements already exist elsewhere in the build, nor that every defect requires a new purchase.

## Three ranked reference gaps

1. **Cast finish and presentation.** Palworld 01 and 04 preserve expressive, deliberately separated character forms. `meadows__band2_stone_and_root__04__the_burrow_warrens__day.png` crowds a mottled boar against the trainer and HUD; `meadows__band3_the_river_lock__05__the_tether_relay__day.png` offers an indistinct lineup. Scene spacing helps, but a unified character/creature finish is art work.
2. **Natural, composed ground cover.** Palworld 02 and 03 use connected grass masses and readable clearings; `meadows__band4_upper_meadows_ironwood__08__the_ridgeline_watch__day.png` repeats individual flowers over noisy ground. Cluster, vary scale and reserve quiet ground around subjects; improved foliage assets may be needed after that.
3. **Useful night values and landmark staging.** The key art keeps destination light and terrain layers legible. `meadows__band3_the_river_lock__06__old_mill_crossing__night.png` loses the route and partly hides the tower; `meadows__band5_stronghold_approach__09__stronghold_approach__night.png` replaces the scene with a dark pylon. Lighting and composition can address this.

**A.** Do these frames read as belonging to the world in `docs/reference/tetherbound-meadows-keyart.png` — the project's own art direction?

**Yes.** The warm village, rolling green/ochre landscape, river, familiar adventurer and oxblood stone stronghold clearly belong to the board's pastoral fantasy world. This is a palette/mood/landmark-language judgment, not a claim of equal finish.

**B.** Shown these frames beside `docs/reference/palworld-0*.jpg`, would someone say these are trying to be the same kind of game?

**Yes.** A third-person trainer among appealing fantastical creatures, gatherable landscape, settlements and hostile-looking strongholds reads as trying to be the same kind of game. It remains visibly below the reference's shipping presentation.
