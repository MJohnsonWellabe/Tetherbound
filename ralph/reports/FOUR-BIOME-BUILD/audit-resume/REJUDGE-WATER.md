# Water: independent blind visual judgment

Evidence: 48 canonical individual PNGs in `shots/catalogue/water/round-camera-20260909T013251Z`, the water contact sheet, combined-116.png and combined-page-01 through combined-page-04.png in `shots/catalogue/audit-pylon-sheets/`. All canonical individuals were opened, including blocked views. The full, unmodified `.claude/skills/visual-judge/SKILL.md` was used. Primary references inspected: `docs/reference/tetherbound-meadows-keyart.png` and all five `docs/reference/palworld-0*.jpg`. No code, implementation reports, or history informed this judgment. Sheet thumbnails were judged at small size; full frames supplied detail.

## Creatures and characters first

The trainer is recognizable, and the pink aquatic creature in `water__drowned_garden__22__drowned_garden_beach__day.png` has a readable head, finned silhouette and appealing colour. The ornate blue creature in `water__tidal_cradle__09__aquaryn_tidal_basin__night.png` supplies a clear fantasy-companion aspiration. But these subjects are isolated exceptions within a very empty landscape. The blue creature's many thin fins and gold-edged details are visually noisy beside the plain hills; the green creature in `water__reedhaven__03__reedhaven_woven_hall__day.png` partially merges with the same yellow-green ground. In `water__veilfall__16__veilfall_mountain_crown__day.png` a creature body hides almost the whole view. This cast is not presented coherently or consistently enough to carry the game at the reference bar.

## Full rubric

1. **Silhouette and readability at small size.** Open beaches make the trainer readable by day in `water__deep_watch__24__deep_watch_beach__day.png`. The pink animal in `water__drowned_garden__22__drowned_garden_beach__day.png` is one of the cleanest creature silhouettes in the set. The trainer largely becomes a black figure at night in `water__drowned_garden__22__drowned_garden_beach__night.png`. The creature in `water__reedhaven__03__reedhaven_woven_hall__day.png` needs better value separation from the ground. `water__veilfall__15__the_veilfall_cascade__day.png` and `water__veilfall__16__veilfall_mountain_crown__day.png` lose useful scene silhouettes under pale haze/foreground anatomy.
2. **Colour and value structure.** Pale sand, yellow-green grass, chalk-grey cliffs and cyan water consistently say coast. They are excessively close in value by day in `water__drowned_garden__21__drowned_garden_terraces__day.png`: pale water, pale islands and pale shore flatten the depth. `water__veilfall__15__the_veilfall_cascade__day.png` is almost white with a hard magenta head accent. Night changes the whole region to blue, but the foreground player can be much darker than the water and cliffs in `water__brine_steps__05__the_tidal_stairstones__night.png`. No reliable friendly oxblood leakage can be established from this set.
3. **Intentionality.** The visible terrain has repetitive smooth grass caps and exposed cliff bands, with almost no intermediate dressing. `water__reedhaven__03__reedhaven_woven_hall__day.png` labels a settlement but visibly shows a creature, a couple of remote figures and empty grass. `water__gull_rest__19__gull_rest_signal_spire__day.png` labels a signal-spire location yet presents a bare hill and a red creature. `water__shellwatch__07__shellwatch_rescue_jetty__day.png` shows an undressed slope and distant animal. These captures cannot prove the named landmarks are absent elsewhere; they do prove that this catalogue fails to present authored destinations. `water__salt_crown__11__salt_crown_tide_shrine__day.png` has an intentional circular prop grouping, but it looks unfinished.
4. **Lighting.** Day/night are clear and the trainer casts a grounding shadow in `water__deep_watch__24__deep_watch_beach__day.png`. Broad white daylight in `water__drowned_garden__21__drowned_garden_terraces__day.png` removes local material distinctions. `water__deep_watch__24__deep_watch_beach__night.png` retains some beach form at night, but large smooth blue patches on the water have hard changes of value rather than convincing local coastal variation. `water__veilfall__15__the_veilfall_cascade__day.png` / `water__veilfall__15__the_veilfall_cascade__night.png` are extremely washed out; a waterfall effect may be involved, but its implementation is unknown.
5. **Horizon and depth.** Separated islands and a pointed mountain in `water__first_shore__02__first_shore_horizon_stones__day.png` provide broad orientation. However, the repeated cap-and-band landforms look manufactured; the near to far world lacks vegetation/rock masses to give scale and depth. `water__sluice_isle__14__sluice_channel_bridge__day.png` has obvious straight diagonal changes across the water and cut-like vertical notches in the mountain. `water__lantern_cove__17__lantern_cove_drift_arch__day.png` shows a perfectly level distant strip that flattens the ocean horizon. Still images cannot establish chunking or LOD behavior.
6. **Interface.** Objectives and prompts are readable with safe margins. Large blank slots dominate otherwise empty views such as `water__deep_watch__24__deep_watch_beach__day.png`, and become an obstruction when creatures appear in `water__tidal_cradle__09__aquaryn_tidal_basin__day.png`. `water__salt_crown__11__salt_crown_tide_shrine__day.png` has a long interaction/status label that is readable but does not make the plain grey shrine's purpose visually evident. A small, mostly featureless minimap is weak support for these near-identical islands.
7. **Artefacts.** `water__brine_steps__06__brine_steps_east_beach__day.png` / `water__brine_steps__06__brine_steps_east_beach__night.png` and `water__shellwatch__08__shellwatch_cradle_beach__day.png` / `water__shellwatch__08__shellwatch_cradle_beach__night.png` contain plain rectangular beach walls without a convincing finished material or purpose. `water__salt_crown__11__salt_crown_tide_shrine__day.png` / `water__salt_crown__11__salt_crown_tide_shrine__night.png` show plainly faceted circular/post structures. The cliff-surface transition in `water__salt_crown__11__salt_crown_tide_shrine__day.png` changes abruptly between differently scaled polygon patterns. `water__veilfall__15__the_veilfall_cascade__day.png` / `water__veilfall__15__the_veilfall_cascade__night.png` are dominated by pale overlapping creature/waterfall surfaces; `water__veilfall__16__veilfall_mountain_crown__day.png` / `water__veilfall__16__veilfall_mountain_crown__night.png` are blocked by creature anatomy. These are visible unfinished or obstructed results, not proof of specific code defects.
8. **Scale agreement.** At 1.80 m, the trainer gives the pink aquatic creature in `water__drowned_garden__22__drowned_garden_beach__day.png` a plausible larger-than-human presence, and the blue creature in `water__tidal_cradle__09__aquaryn_tidal_basin__night.png` reads substantially larger again, though depth and pose prevent precise ratios. The plain post circles in `water__salt_crown__11__salt_crown_tide_shrine__day.png` are person-sized rather than grand shrines. `water__tidal_cradle__09__aquaryn_tidal_basin__day.png` has several overlapping creatures in a narrow passage and the player appears on top of anatomy; spatial overlap prevents confident role-scale comparison. Bare islands lack normal-size environmental objects to reinforce distance.

## Product reading and evidence limits

Cohesion: colours repeat consistently, but the environment has much less visual finish and density than Meadows, while detailed creatures look pasted into it. Authoredness: weak; many named sites show indistinguishable empty hills/beaches. Biome distinction: unmistakably a coastal archipelago, insufficient internal site distinction. Cast appeal/readability: the pink swimmer has promise; ornate blue art and near-body obstructions undermine presentation. Navigable landmarks: the pointed distant mountain is recognizable, but nearby routes and named destinations rarely have a visual anchor. Visual readiness: **not ready at the shipping-art reference bar**.

Scene-fixable: camera clearance, landmark-facing views, shoreline/ground dressing, controlled vegetation and rock clusters, value separation, water/haze balance and creature placement. Art-needed: the shown plain walls and circular structures need finished art treatment; coast-specific landmark/inhabitation vocabulary and cohesive terrain/water surfaces are not demonstrated. Screenshots cannot determine whether those assets already exist unshown. Empty frames are evidence about the visible product, not a license to invent missing systems. No combat, traversal, swimming quality, progression or performance conclusion follows.

## Three ranked reference gaps

1. **An inhabited, authored environment.** Palworld 04/05 and the key art give space recognizable structures, routes, vegetation masses and human purpose. `water__reedhaven__03__reedhaven_woven_hall__day.png`, `water__gull_rest__19__gull_rest_signal_spire__day.png` and `water__shellwatch__07__shellwatch_rescue_jetty__day.png` offer empty terrain under destination names. Scene dressing and landmark framing are essential; finished coastal art may also be required.
2. **Coherent creature/environment presentation.** Palworld 04 integrates clearly silhouetted creatures into a finished landscape. `water__reedhaven__03__reedhaven_woven_hall__day.png` camouflages the green animal; `water__tidal_cradle__09__aquaryn_tidal_basin__night.png` puts ornate art in a bare trough; `water__veilfall__16__veilfall_mountain_crown__day.png` gives a body close-up. Composition/value fixes help, while consistent cast and environment art direction remains necessary.
3. **Finished land/water transitions and materials.** The key art uses shore rocks, depth and convincing bank shapes; Palworld 04 layers readable natural terrain. `water__brine_steps__06__brine_steps_east_beach__day.png` has a blank wall, `water__salt_crown__11__salt_crown_tide_shrine__day.png` primitive-looking circles and abrupt cliff texturing, and `water__sluice_isle__14__sluice_channel_bridge__day.png` straight water boundaries. Scene/material repair and finished prop art are needed.

**A.** Do these frames read as belonging to the world in `docs/reference/tetherbound-meadows-keyart.png` — the project's own art direction?

**No.** A coast can belong to the Meadows board's larger world, but these pale empty islands, plain walls and undressed named sites do not yet show its natural richness, inhabited landmarks or inviting sense of place. Scene dressing, value and composition are fixable; finished coastal/prop/material art must also be demonstrated.

**B.** Shown these frames beside `docs/reference/palworld-0*.jpg`, would someone say these are trying to be the same kind of game?

**Yes.** Across the whole set, the trainer, fantastical aquatic creatures and close encounter presentation still make the broad creature-adventure intention legible beside Palworld. Most individual empty landscape shots would not communicate that alone. This yes is about the kind of game being attempted, not visual readiness.
