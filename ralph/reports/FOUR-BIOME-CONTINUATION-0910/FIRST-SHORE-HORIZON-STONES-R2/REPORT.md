**Disposition:** POLISH — retain.

centre stone as a readable aperture toward the distant island and peak. Trainer scale
is clear, all three views preserve the silhouette, and the paired practicals retain

grass and the centre stone partially plugs the aperture. Water and distant terrain also
remain flat/angular, while night lighting leaves the stone faces nearly black.
