# Meadows Broken Tower visual pass — 2026-09-11

## Scope

Bounded structural-depth pass on **The Broken Tower** after the fresh named-
location regrade retained it at `POLISH`. Production evidence showed a
recognizable crenellated ruin, fallen wall and ward, but its long-range massing
was one thin vertical slab, the installed walls met grass without believable
foundation volume, and the practical only exposed a narrow interior patch at
night.

## Candidate

- Added four offset, textured foundation rafts under the three surviving wall
  leaves, deliberately leaving the route-facing mouth open.
- Added three unequal installed-brick footing/buttress remnants and three more
  textured rubble fragments to widen and weight the base silhouette.
- Added a displaced high masonry spur so the crown breaks asymmetrically against
  the sky instead of ending at one regular kit height.
- Added a bounded route-facing spot from the existing modeled teal ward lens.
  It complements the existing chamber omni without changing global exposure.
- The first production candidate proved the new foundation but was rejected:
  its new base volume still compressed behind the tall leaf on the long route,
  and the interior ward cone did not lift enough exterior masonry. The final
  candidate enlarges and turns the surviving west/rear footing remnants off
  axis, moves the crown fracture to the missing east side, and adds a visibly
  modeled outer ward remnant with a bounded 16.5m fill.
- No progression, encounter, pickup, terrain, entrance-route or authored wall
  collider was moved. New low-remnant collision stays outside the open mouth;
  foundation rafts and the high crown are presentation-only.
