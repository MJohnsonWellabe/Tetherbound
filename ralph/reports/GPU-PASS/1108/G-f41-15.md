## Correction to `ITEM-F41-15/CAUSE.md`
That note blamed the speaker key light plus Forward+ glow, and called `npc_vis_look_captain_a` "OFF, so it is not the cause". This round shows the reverse:
- Moving and dimming the key light (arm A) changes nothing visible.
- Grading the captain's albedo (arm B) removes the blowout.
- So the bright albedo on Marrow's head and hair is what blows out under the High lights. The key light is at most a contributor.
- The same albedo check is worth trying on Fenn, Nalia and Nerissa, the other ghost-white NPCs in F-tidewake and F41#20.

## Method
- **Frames:** `tb/gpu-frames-1108`, on an NVIDIA GTX 1060, Forward+ High 1920x1080, JPEG q85, main ba6778f45.
  - `manifest-G-f41-15-arm-a.json` (278c29ae): 16 frames, 8 live and 8 arm A. They cover F41#15 (Marrow, Marrow dynamo-core and Nysa), F41#7 (Marrow dynamo-core), F39#29 (Nalia, Nerissa and trainer Nerissa) and F41#20 (Fenn).
  - `manifest-G-f41-15-arm-b.json` (b5a5aabb): 4 arm B frames. They are compared against the matching live frames from arm-a.
- **Pairs:** each pair puts live and arm frames of the same stands side by side. Up to 4 frames go in a 2x2 grid at 640x360; a single frame is shown at 1280x720.
- **Judging:** `anchor_tiles.py` shuffles the sides and keeps the key outside the judge folder. Three fresh code-blind judges scored with the standard JUDGE_PROMPT and 3 pinned anchor pairs (craft, f10_2_r3_capacitor, f04_6_r22).
- **Fit:** `calibrate.py` linear, slope 1.4374, intercept −1.6568, r 0.918, anchor RMSE 0.457. It is a confirming batch (top anchor 7.35), with no warnings.
- **Scale:** these mapped scores are on this batch's own scale. Live F41#7 maps to 6.11 here against 7.23 in `F-stormwood.md`, which used a different batch and tree (be95950b). Read gains within this file.
- **Judged by:** LANE-SCOUT, 9 Nov 2026 ~04:00 UTC. Judge folders and replies stayed local.
