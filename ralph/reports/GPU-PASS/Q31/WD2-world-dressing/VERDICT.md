**What the judges saw, per item**, and the path to 7.5 from their notes:
- **P2-046 harvest nodes (6.57):**
  - Control: stormglass nodes are brown boulders with a few flat shards, and glowmoss is a flat green blob.
  - Candidate: blue crystal clusters and glowing moss, so each node carries its material.
  - **Path:** make the nodes bigger, and give stormglass and stormglass_crown different silhouettes (they share one now). Add a grounding decal and a small clearing in the uniform moss carpet around each node.
- **P2-070 Glass Sink (4.63):**
  - Control: a hard grass edge over a flat blue-grey plane. Nothing marks the place.
  - Candidate: a stone ring of glass spikes and dead trees on the rim. It marks the spot, but the judges read it as a planter, not a sunken glass basin. The plane below is unchanged.
  - **Path:** a real basin: a sunken bowl with a glass floor or black pool, a marked rim, and spikes rooted in cracked rock (new art).
- **P2-045 candy tiers (5.92):**
  - Candidate tiers are larger and coloured green, blue and gold, and Rare gets a gold ring.
  - Good and Great still share one blob form and scale, so they read mainly by glow colour.
  - **Path:** a different wrapper silhouette per tier, a contact shadow and a rim light, at gameplay scale.
- **P2-043 Glass Field (5.75):**
  - Candidate: tall pale glass spikes stand where control has only flat shards on a purple slab.
  - The base is still flat and dark, sits behind the mushrooms, and is not rooted in the rock.
  - **Note:** one judge marked control FIXED only because control has no rising spikes at all.
  - **Path:** seat the spikes into cracked rock, with the base visible and no slab.
- **P2-033 Meadows pickups (6.34):**
  - Candidate pickups are larger, and the wrapped candy and pink flask now read.
  - Rare candy is a brown bundle on a cloth that doesn't read as candy. There are no contact shadows and no rim light against the green carpet.
  - **Path:** a candy-shaped rare candy, plus a contact shadow and rim light on all three pickups.
- **P2-116 workbench (6.08):**
  - Candidate adds a rack, tool shapes and a lantern post.
  - The station is still small in frame, and the trainer covers it in views 1 and 2.
  - One judge saw a stray translucent cyan outline structure in view 1 on both sides. It is worth checking whether that is a build ghost left visible.
  - **Path:** a larger work surface with clear tools, and camera stands that keep the trainer off the subject.
- **P2-041 Crown Arch (4.78):**
  - Control: a small dark brick block on a pale gridded square slab.
  - Candidate: a single tall blue pillar, a "totem", on a dark square slab.
  - **Neither side reads as an arch**, and the square slab is still exposed in both.
  - **Path:** an authored arch, with two pillars and a spanning crown, seated into the moss with no slab (new art). For comparison, the SW041 arm earlier today moved this item +0.33.
- **P2-115 creature bed (5.23):**
  - Candidate: a wooden-trimmed ring with a dark filled interior. It reads as a fire pit or well rather than a bed.
  - Control: a pale stone ring with sand inside that melts into the dunes.
  - Both still shrink to a thin curb edge-on, and a foreground creature half-blocks view 1.
  - **Path:** a raised nest or cushion shape with bedding, and a clear view 1.
- **P2-054 mushrooms (6.92):**
  - Speed and Wild mushrooms now differ clearly in shape and colour, and the candidate's are larger. The defect is FIXED 3.
  - **Path:** the remaining 0.58 is finish rather than identity: contact shadows and a rim light on the pickups, and a less flat midday grade.
- **P2-035 camp props (5.68, below the qualifying gain):**
  - The candidate's bare dirt pad separates the props a little, and the camp_bed frame shows a readable bedroll.
  - Barrels, crates, the fire, a fox, a rabbit and villagers still pile into one cluster, and a large shell rock clips the bonfire frame.
  - **Path:** spaced prop groups with creatures and villagers kept out of the props' footprint, and a camera stand that frames one prop at a time.
