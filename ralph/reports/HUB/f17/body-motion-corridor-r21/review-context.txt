RECOVERY COMPLETENESS AND INTERNAL VERSUS EXTERNAL CAPS
Each recovery iteration traverses all enabled queried-body shapes and all candidates that survived the hidden2048 cull and original filters. There is no extra8-candidate early stop. However, recovery's collision callback stores at most32 point pairs, independent of MotionParameters.max_collisions=8. When full, _shape_col_cbk keeps deeper pairs by replacing the least-deep slot and discards others; it exposes no saturation/completeness flag to MotionResult. Raw duplicate points may consume that32-pair capacity. Its callback result is not an uncapped initial collider set, and a result containing one contact does not bound those internal pairs.

The solver normalizes collision-priority weights, applies40percent of depth minus margin*.05 for each stored pair, accumulates virtual recovery, and repeats at most4 times. It stops on no reported collision, a zero accumulated correction or the iteration limit. It does not perform a final all-candidate zero-margin disjointness proof. Corrections require depth>margin*.05+CMP_EPSILON. With requested0 the enforced margin.0001m gives min_contact_depth5micrometers and recovery decision tolerance15micrometers. Opposing planes, cancellation, concave geometry, pair capacity, nonuniform priorities and finite relaxation can leave unresolved contacts. Source assigns recovery pair priorities only when callback amount grows; at full32 a pair replacement does not separately update that slot's priority. That is a conditional source hazard for saturated mixed-priority sets, not a diagnosis of this single-box sample.

Step2 tests distances AFTER recovery at the virtual pose; its zero requested motion still does not restore the original initial pose. Step3 extracts rest contacts at the recovered/unsafe virtual pose. max_collisions8 bounds those returned contact results, not all recovery candidates/pairs. Rest callback ranks/reuses/discards contacts; the final count is not a cardinality certificate for initial colliders. Returned depth is a recovered-pose contact quantity. Returned travel includes accumulated virtual recovery even in the !collided branch; false boolean alone is not proof no correction or no initial overlap. Zero requested margin still has.1mm inflation, so a second result can continue reporting/recovering margin contact even after the full nominal body is physically disjoint from a primitive.
