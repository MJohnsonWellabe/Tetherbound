Judged from the 10 PNGs in this folder only. I did not read any source, config or docs.

## 2. Can a player tell the six apart?

They are separate objects with distinct silhouettes, and no two stations look alike. Most can also be identified without help:

- **Clear in day and night:** Workbench, Forge and Kitchen. The night fire glow is a real readability win for the Forge and Kitchen.
- **Clear with a small caveat:** Altar. The candles, plinth and white material read as sacred, but the cross-shaped post leans toward a gravestone or memorial.
- **Distinct but mis-identified:** Den. It reads as a player camping tent with bedrolls, not as a place where creature companions rest. The second tent near the campfire in the background makes this worse.
- **Readable only up close:** Farm. It is clear in the farm and altar views. In the 8 m row views it is low and small, sits at the frame edge, and is covered by the HUD and the teal plane, so it does not read from that distance.

## 3. Occlusion and fitness as evidence

- **Row views:** the MAIN STORY objective panel covers the right third of the frame. That hides the Farm and part of the Den in both row views. The row frames are therefore **unfit as evidence for the Farm**, and weak for the Den.
- **Teal translucent plane:** a vertical teal plane (it looks like a route or quest marker) sits over the farm area in the row, altar, den and farm views.
- **Signpost:** in the row views, the "Grandpa's House" signpost stands in front of the gap between the Altar and the Den. It does not hide either one, but it clutters that space.
- **Den views:** the signpost boards cover the lower-left corner. This does not affect the stations.
- **Frame quality:** otherwise all frames are clean, sharp, at the normal camera, and correctly lit for day and night.
- **Art note (outside this criterion):** the forge furnace and the altar are flat, untextured primitive shapes, which look like placeholders next to the textured bench, cauldron and planter.

## 4. Verdict: **PARTIAL**

| Station | Result | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Workbench | PASS | A wooden bench with vises reads at once, day and night. |
| Forge | PASS | The glowing furnace, chimney and anvil read strongly, especially at night. |
| Kitchen | PASS | The cauldron over a fire plus a prep table reads clearly as cooking. |
| Altar | PASS | The candle-flanked white plinth reads as a shrine; the cross-shaped post slightly suggests a grave. |
| Den | **FAIL (identity)** | It is a distinct object, but it reads as the player's camping tent, not a creature shelter, and a second background tent competes with it. |
| Farm | PASS up close / **FAIL in row view** | The seedling planter reads clearly in the close views. At the 8 m row camera it is tiny, sits at the edge, and is hidden by the HUD panel and teal plane. |

To reach PASS:
- Give the Den a creature-specific cue, such as a nest or straw-bed look, a creature-scale opening or a resting creature, and set the background tent apart from it.
- Recapture the row view so the Farm sits clear of the objective panel and the teal plane, or make the farm plot taller or larger.
