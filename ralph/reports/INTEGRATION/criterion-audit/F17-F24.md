## Integration risks the table can't express

1. **Old play order.** With `redesign_portal_runtime_enabled=false`, travel between biomes uses the old physical chain: Warden→`realm_key_cloudreach`, cloudreach_world.json/stormward handoff→Stormwood, and stormwood_chapter.json grants `realm_key_water`. The live redesign level curve (`chapter_curve.json`, the F19#1 bands) then has the player reach Tidewake (20–33) after Stormwood (42–55). Unknown whether any playthrough has checked this pacing.
2. **No reachable credits (likely).** `redesign_ending_runtime_enabled=true` stops the Tidewake dock exchange from rolling credits. The Stormwood homecoming needs a Home Key home-return receipt, and Home Key use is refused while portals are off. The shipped build likely has no reachable credits or homecoming. A solo end-to-end run should confirm this.
3. **Ungated tests.** No F17–F24 engine smoke is in ci.yml. Every non-unit proof above therefore depends on manual or render.yml runs, not the gate.
