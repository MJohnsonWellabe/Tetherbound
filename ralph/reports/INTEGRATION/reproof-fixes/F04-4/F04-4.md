## Classification: stale witness (no product defect)

The re-proof read Warden rows such as `gap_strike=10.91` against `reach=8.00` as a hit beyond reach. Three measurements showed otherwise.

1. **`gap_strike` is read after the hit.** The witness samples it on `hit_landed`, after the blow's knockback has already moved the struck creature. The witness now also logs `gap_pre` (the last tick before the outcome), `centre_gap_pre`, the reach at resolution and the opponent's own travel (`F04-4-warden-centregap.txt`).
2. **The Warden's strikes are F22 role patterns.** A temporary `combat_manager` print showed that the resolve path for every Warden strike has no link and no travel (`F04-4-diagnostic-resolve.txt`). Even so, hits landed at a centre distance of 7.50 and 8.26 m against range 7.47. That rules out `in_hit_cone`. These strikes carry `pattern_attack_id` and resolve through `combat_ai.gd::pattern_contains`, which connects while the target's centre is within **range + the target's own radius** (l.286-299). That matches the rule COMBAT §5 and `combat.json` `charger_lunge` state for lanes ("any part of my creature's footprint"). This is specified behaviour.
3. **The witness could not dodge these strikes.**
   - After its first dodge, the creature sat on the arena boundary for every later strike (`arena_off=11.00`), about 7 m from a body whose clearance distance is about 8.3-8.8 m.
   - The witness only walked. COMBAT §12.2 answers a WALL ring with a burst, and the combat burst is pad A (`jump`) in a fight (`combat_manager.gd::_read_player_input`, COMBAT-3). The witness header's "there is no dodge button" was out of date.

## Witness changes (test only)

- Between tells, before a DODGE strike, the witness walks back toward the arena centre when it has less than 4 m of room behind it.
- On a **pattern** strike only, the witness taps the burst once, with the stick held away.
  - A first version burst on every dodge. Its mutation check **passed** the mutant (`F04-4-mutation-burst-unconditional.txt`, `captain_field` 1/4), so it no longer detected reach being ignored.

- Rows now log `gap_pre`, `foe_moved`, `centre_gap_pre`, `reach_at_strike`, `pattern` and `shape`.
- The pass rule is unchanged: one landed player hit, one landed STAND strike and one avoided DODGE strike per fight.

## For the coordinator (not changed here)

- **Role patterns.** The Warden lead's logged pattern shapes are `fan` (0.9 s, rock_throw) and `field` (1.1 s, tremor_roll), which are CURRENT-role shapes in COMBAT §12.2. BOSSES l.441 lists the Warden as WALL→DIVER→CURRENT→CHARGER→ACE, with Burrowback as the WALL. Either `combat_config()` at tell time reports another member's pattern table, or the lead is assigned CURRENT patterns. This belongs to the F22 pattern owners.
- **Face lock.** For non-pattern opponents, the charged-move fallback (`wild_creature.gd` l.920) takes `face_lock_fraction` only from `charged_face_lock_fraction`, which defaults to 0. Those strikes therefore track their target for the whole tell. COMBAT §5's **Target** is "facing tracks during the first half of windup, then locks". Changing the fallback would alter every charged-fallback enemy, so it is left for the COMBAT owner.
