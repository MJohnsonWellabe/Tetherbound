# F06-4 — Cloudreach remount, no gate bypass: fix and re-run

**Verdict: PASS.** `godot --headless --path . --script tests/smoke_cloudreach_saddle_remount.gd` reported `CLOUDREACH SADDLE REMOUNT OK checks=331 failures=0`, exit 0, 398 s, started 2026-10-04T19:20:49Z. Code under test: `030e40be` (`tb/reproof-fixes`).

## Root cause (a product defect)

The re-proof guessed that the placer computes `in_geometry` but never rejects on it. That is not quite right. `in_geometry` is the smoke's own measurement. The placer (`scripts/world/cloudreach_riding_controller.gd`) does reject on world overlap, through `_capsule_fits`, but it tested the capsule at `spot + SETTLE_LIFT_M`. `spot` already carried that lift, and `dismount()` sets the trainer at `spot` itself. So the check ran 0.05 m above the real placement.

On the sloped floor below the causeway ledge (`CarvedCrown` collider, normal y ≈ 0.85, about 32°), a 0.4 m capsule's rounded base sitting at floor + 0.05 clips the slope. The placer passed it, and the placed trainer overlapped world collision. That is the re-proof's single failure: `PLACEMENT … "at": (-58.93, 384.91, 1576.59) … "in_geometry": true`.

## Fix, in three commits (each run is evidence for the next)

1. `2790285e`: test the capsule exactly where the trainer is set down. **Try 1** (`F06-4-try1-strict.txt`, 18:51:55Z, exit 1, 13 failures): the overlap is gone, but no candidate fits on that slope at floor + 0.05. The dismount is deferred, the rider stays seated, and the later legs cascade.
2. `3e9c5d21`: `_settled_spot` lowers the trainer's capsule from 0.5 m above the floor hit (`cast_motion`) and places it at its resting height plus the settle lift. **Try 2** (`F06-4-try2-settled.txt`, 18:59:03Z, exit 1, 4 failures): the ledge passes (same spot, now 0.07 m higher, `in_geometry: false`). "ladder mid-hop prefers ground" fails, though: a ring candidate wins at 0.44 m above the floor (`floor_ok: false`) instead of the remembered spot P.
   - **Diagnostic** (`F06-4-diagnostic.txt`, a temporary print that was never committed): on the causeway the capsule rests 0.01–0.07 m above the floor hit, as intended. In the ladder fixture it came to rest on top of a knee-high `TestPost`, and the smoke's own comment says a post top must not count as a floor.
3. `030e40be`: a settled capsule may rise above the floor point under it by at most r(1/cos a − 1), the height of a radius-r capsule on the steepest walkable slope a (`floor_max_angle`), plus cast_motion's 0.01 m step. Anything higher means it rests on something else, and the spot is refused. **Pass** (`F06-4-pass.txt`): 331 checks, 0 failures. The ledge dismount lands at (-58.93, 384.98, 1576.59), feet on the collider top, and the ladder takes its remembered ground again.

No smoke check or tolerance was changed. The fix is product code only.
