## What the re-proof failure was

The re-proof (`origin/tb/reproof-retest:ralph/reports/INTEGRATION/reproof/retest/F07-1.md`) guessed that one cause cascaded into the rest. There were three independent causes.

1. **Stale expectation: when the thanks is offered.** `data/config/cloudreach_physical_runtime.json` `activity_rewards` gates the thanks on `side_courier_medicine_delivered`. Its comment cites coordinator ruling (a), #356 13:20: the thanks waits at the Windscar shelter once the medicine is delivered. The solo `tests/smoke_cloudreach_activity_rewards.gd:47-54` already follows that ruling. The two-peer smoke seeded the delivery flag and then asserted "not offered before Neri hears the report". The guest passed that check only because its reward node did not exist at that moment (`claims_paid: -1`), which is cause 2. The flag is not the per-character scope problem the re-proof suspected: the offer reads the world unlock plus this character's own player-scoped claimed flag, and both scopes are right. **Fix (test, `6d0e6f18`):** the smoke now checks "not offered" before the delivery flag and "offered" after it, on both peers.
2. **Product bug: placements rebuilt in place on every guest world delta.** `ledger_rpc.gd::_restore_progression` runs `cloudreach_physical_runtime.gd::restore_progression_from_game` on each host world delta. That function `queue_free`'d every placement and rebuilt them in the same frame. The freed node was still in the tree, so Godot auto-renamed the replacement, and every lookup by id lost it. A guest claim in flight was also torn down by its own delivery delta. Evidence: try 1 (`F07-1-try1.txt`, after the test fix): the guest's thanks existed and was offered after the delivery, then vanished after Neri's report ("no couriers' thanks node or player here"). **Fix (product, `8808c6b1`):** disposable placements leave the tree before they are freed. Personal rewards, whose `offered()` reads the flags live, are kept and refreshed.
3. **Fixture timing: the guest's first claim was refused `too_far`.** `world_ledger.gd::client_grant_refusal` judges a guest's claim against the host's own `remote_trainer` copy of that guest, with an 8 m reach. The fixture teleports the guest 3 km and presses 30 frames later. Try 2 (`F07-1-try2.txt`) recorded `refusals: [{code: too_far, kind: reward_grant}]`; the repeat press paid. **Fix (test, `a02aba67`):** after the stand, the press waits (bounded, 900 frames) until the host's copy reaches the thanks, and logs the wait. Passing run: `HOST VIEW: guest proxy at [0, 105, -260] after 0 frames`, then at the bag after 10 frames. The host's copy snaps (`remote_trainer.gd` SNAP_M) within under a second of the teleport. A walking player keeps that copy current, so this was a fixture artifact, not a product defect.

No tolerance was widened. The 8 m reach and the 900-frame receipt budget are unchanged, and every check is still present.
