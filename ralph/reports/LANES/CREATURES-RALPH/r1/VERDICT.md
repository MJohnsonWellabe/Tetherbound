## What changed

- **Ashtusk** (on the Tuskroot remake, aspect variant):
  - New remake-only recipe in `data/creatures/aspect_variants.json` (`ashtusk.remake`): a desaturated ash-brown coat that keeps its value, dark basalt dorsal plates, moss turned to ash, ember cracks only between the plates, and an ember crack glow on the ivory tusks. The old recipe crushed the coat to 0.55 value.
  - Tusks are bound rigidly to the head bone in the GLB (`tools/art_pipeline/rigid_bind_region.py`). This removes the stretched-tusk sheet in hit-050, which both arms showed before.
  - The textures are regenerated by `tools/generate_aspect_variant_textures.py --remake ashtusk`.
  - **It ships only when `remake_owner1101_tuskroot` leaves HELD.** That is Task 2, and it needs Tuskroot itself judged.
- **Cliffspike:**
  - Skin weights relaxed in the GLB (`tools/art_pipeline/relax_stretched_weights.py`, two passes). Over hit, faint, attack and charged, the worst triangle stretch goes from 0.66 m to 0.19 m. The judges mark the hit-react sheet FIXED ×3.
  - Banded quills (`tools/art_pipeline/grade_quill_bands.py`): a dark warm body and quill roots, pale tips, and a warm-grey face. Selected by the coat flag `creatures_ralph_cliffspike_bands` (ON).
- **Aquaryn** (dropped): hit ×2.0 and faint ×1.4 clip exaggeration plus glow_read at 2.4. The judges still see the same small, thin coil, and the faint keeps the head raised. Next approach: a silhouette-level change (scale, and a faint that lays the column on the ground).
- **Duskhush** (dropped): a regional grade to a brown owl with a pale breast and disc. The judges preferred the shipped violet tint for separation, and the eyes were still not visible. Next approach: keep the violet coat and add an eye-only glow plus faint pose work.
