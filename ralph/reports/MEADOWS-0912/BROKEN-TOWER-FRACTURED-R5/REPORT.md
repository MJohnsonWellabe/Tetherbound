# Broken Tower fractured R5 — independent visual judgment

## Verdict: PASS

R5 clears the two strict R4 blockers without weakening the accepted route, arch,
or asymmetric ruin silhouette. The former broad pale paper-cut is now a deep,
dark, course-textured surviving leaf with layered horizontal breaks, and the night

into unequal standing walls, an arched threshold, and fallen remnants.

## Residual polish, not a closure blocker

The tallest crown still has a fairly straight top run, and portions of the fallen
right-hand wall retain rectangular masonry-module edges at close range. Those are
visible stylization limits, but they are subordinate to the now-convincing layered
fracture, do not recreate R4's paper-thin pale plane, and do not impair navigation
or night identity. R5 therefore supersedes R4 and meets the named-location PASS bar.
