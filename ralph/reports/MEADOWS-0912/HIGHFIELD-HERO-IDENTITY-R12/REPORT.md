# The Highfield hero identity R12 — independent visual judgment

## Verdict: POLISH — retain the evidence, do not promote to strict PASS

R12 improves the wide Highfield read: it is plainly an inhabited summer pasture with
a herd, working camp, fenced drove threshold, road, and strong day/night continuity.
It still does not provide an unambiguous, ordinary-gameplay visual comparison between
the real alpha and an ordinary Meadowhart. The manifest resolves the bodies and records
a 1.55× height ratio, but telemetry cannot substitute for visible subject proof.

## Evidence integrity — PASS with composition caveat

- `manifest.json` is complete with no failures, six declared 1280×720 PNGs, and three
  materially matched day/night pairs. All six files are present and readable.
- The disclosure retains the production Meadows scene, Terrain3D, scatter, props,
  player, and EncounterDirector population. It says neither creature was injected,
  moved, or frozen; the deterministic seed and disabled player are disclosed controls.
- Player displacement is zero and the camera remains at an ordinary roughly 6 m
  third-person distance. The package is therefore admissible as production evidence.
- Admissibility does not make every frame probative. In `03`/`04`, the nearby alpha is
  almost entirely outside the camera: only a huge clipped muzzle intrudes at the upper
  left. That pair cannot prove the alpha silhouette or a fair alpha/ordinary comparison.

## Promotion condition

Retain R12’s camp/gate consolidation and day/night framing. Strict PASS needs one fresh
ordinary player-height day/night pair with the full real alpha and at least one full
ordinary Meadowhart simultaneously visible, separated rather than overlapping or
cropped, so the alpha’s larger silhouette/treatment is self-evident in the pixels while
the drove gate and stock camp remain in the same composition.
