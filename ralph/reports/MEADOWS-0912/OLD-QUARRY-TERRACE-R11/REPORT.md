# The Old Quarry R11 — independent named-location verdict

## Verdict: POLISH — retain, not a strict named-location PASS

I inspected `manifest.json` and all eight 1280×800 PNGs at native resolution,
without inspecting implementation source. The evidence is admissible: all four
matched day/night views are present, the manifest is complete with no failures,
the production Meadows scene and ordinary quarry/player/world systems are disclosed,
and every stand reports the player on floor with no material support error.

R11 clearly establishes a dressed worksite. In `02-worked-floor-*` and
`03-conduit-head-*`, the excavated dirt floor, retaining walls, sign, barrel, crate,
sack, spoil, dead growth, and three linked conduit pylons form a coherent activity
cluster and preserve an open route. The cyan conduit gives the machinery a readable
hierarchy in both lighting states.

It does not yet establish a convincing worked quarry. In `01-arrival-*` and most
plainly `04-cut-face-*`, the advertised extraction face reads as three separate,
rounded pale boulders laid across an otherwise grassy meadow. The low connecting
pieces do not create a laterally continuous vertical cut, exposed strata, or stepped
benches, and the wide composition gives much more visual weight to unworked grass and
forest than to excavation. The face is also spatially separated from nearly all of
the floor's gear and conduit grammar, so the frames do not show one legible process
from cut face to worked floor to haul route.

Night is coherent with day, but not strong enough for promotion: the pylons, trainer,
and warm retaining-wall pocket remain readable, while the floor, spoil, and supposed
face collapse into broad dark shapes. The cyan cable also reads as a thick floating
tube, and the isolated straight grey slabs terminate abruptly in dirt; both weaken
the authored industrial read.

The three strongest remaining gaps are:

1. `04-cut-face-*`: replace the boulder-row silhouette with an unmistakable connected
   excavated landform—vertical cut, strata, or stepped benching visible above grass.
2. `01-arrival-*` and `04-cut-face-*`: compose the face and active floor together so
   extraction, equipment, and haul route read as one site from ordinary arrival range.
3. `03-conduit-head-*`: resolve the floating cable and abruptly ending slab language,
   and preserve more floor/face separation at night.

R11 is valid evidence and a credible POLISH worksite, but the defining landform still
reads as detached boulders and generic clearing clutter. The Old Quarry ledger row
must remain **POLISH**.
