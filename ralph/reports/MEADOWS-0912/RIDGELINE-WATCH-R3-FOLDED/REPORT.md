# The Ridgeline Watch R3 folded shelter — independent visual judgment

## Verdict: FAIL — do not promote or replace the retained R2 POLISH baseline

R3 does not meet the strict named-location bar and is visually worse than the R2
disposition it was meant to close. Four of six frames fail to present the watch or
service shelter as a judgeable subject. The close canonical pair reveals a regular
two-bay X-braced box surrounded by visibly detached elements: a thin sloping canopy
plane at left, a separate vertical panel, a freestanding ladder at right, and a tiny
hanging object in open sky. The proposed folded shelter does not assemble into a
believable supported structure.

### Evidence integrity

- **Manifest/file integrity: PASS.** `manifest.json` reports `complete: true`, six
  frame records, and zero failures. All six native PNGs exist, are unique, non-empty
  1280x720 files, and form three visibly distinct day/night pairs.
- **Production disclosure: PASS.** The receipt identifies
  `res://scenes/world/meadows_playground.tscn` and runtime node `RidgelineWatch`.
  It discloses the production player, hidden HUD/modal overlays, clear weather/time
  pin, production-equivalent 70-degree third-person camera, and paused player
  processing/velocity solely to hold coordinates. It states that progress and
  encounters were not injected.
- **Acceptance coverage: FAIL.** File completeness does not make the planned subjects
  visible. `01`/`02` are dense-tree views with no readable watch silhouette, and
  `05`/`06`, explicitly named service-shelter frames, show trunks and meadow with
  only a narrow distant scaffold fragment. The shelter cannot be judged there.
- The manifest has per-frame player/camera/watch-distance receipts but no source SHA
  or per-source hashes, so it does not independently bind the pixels to a commit.

### Native-frame findings

- **`01-southwest-arrival-day` / `02-southwest-arrival-night`: FAIL.** At the
  recorded 27.29 m watch distance, the foreground and middle ground are dominated by
  trunks and dense crowns. There is no recognizable lookout, signal mast, shelter,
  lantern, or route-to-watch read. Night makes the same view nearly black. This is
  woodland arrival evidence, not named-location evidence.
- **`03-canonical-watch-day`: FAIL.** The watch finally fills the frame at 7.62 m,
  but its main construction remains two nearly identical rectangular X-braced bays.
  The service treatment is visibly broken apart: the left canopy is a long thin
  grey-green plane extending from trees rather than a supported roof; a narrow dark
  panel floats edge-on above/behind it; and the right ladder hangs in open space,
  detached from the deck and tower. The tiny sky-hung element right of the mast has
  no readable support or function.
- **`04-canonical-watch-night`: FAIL.** Night preserves the tower silhouette and
  oxblood mast cloth but also preserves the detached ladder/panel/canopy read. A
  small warm pool at the player's feet is not a strong deck beacon and does not
  explain or unify the structure.
- **`05-service-shelter-day` / `06-service-shelter-night`: FAIL.** The named service
  subject is absent behind the tree wall. Only a thin scaffold/ladder-like strip is
  visible through a central gap. Neither frame can prove canopy folds, thickness,
  supports, supplies, work use, attachment, or night practicals.

### Strict named-location criteria

- **Watch/overlook silhouette: FAIL across the set.** It exists only in the close
  canonical pair; it disappears from both arrival and service views.
- **Construction quality: FAIL.** The repeated scaffold box remains prototype-like,
  and the surrounding pieces visibly lack coherent attachment/support.
- **Service/lived occupation: FAIL.** A barrel/crate cluster and small light appear
  near the left edge of the canonical frames, but there is no judgeable attached
  shelter or working watch-post arrangement.
- **Route and overlook context: POLISH in isolation.** Open pasture, a woodland edge,
  distant creatures, and a hilltop horizon support the idea of a ridgeline lookout.
  The road is visible at the side of several frames, but it does not lead the eye to
  a visible watch in the arrival proof.
- **Night readability: FAIL.** The canonical silhouette survives, but arrival and
  service evidence collapse into dark trunks; the service layer and deck use do not
  read.

## Required correction

Do not promote R3. Retain R2's POLISH status until a fresh production repair makes
every shelter component physically supported and attached, gives the main tower
less repeated-box construction, and captures unobstructed ordinary player-height
arrival, canonical, and service views. The capture must fail closed when the watch
or named service shelter is not visibly present; distance receipts alone are not
visual proof.
