## Frame-level findings

- `01-long-road-world-tree-day/night`: strong approach pair; scale, silhouette,
  repaired bark colour, and restrained night fissures all read.
- `02-southwest-world-tree-day/night`: cleanest distant silhouette comparison;
  night retains a bright inner source but no longer lets it replace the tree.
- `03-root-district-day/night`: foreground ordinary tree masks the central lower
  hero and route; insufficient as named root-district evidence.
- `04-ironwood-workyard-day/night`: process furniture is readable, but material
  differentiation and Ironwood-specific craft identity remain weak.
- `05-workyard-to-first-ironwood-day/night`: closes the former detached-workyard
  composition at the broad level and proves night coexistence, but crops and
  miniaturizes the actual process chain enough that its stages cannot be judged.

## Minimum follow-up evidence

No further change is justified to the accepted hero scale, day colour/texture, or
night fissure balance. To close the remaining visual hold, provide one unobstructed
root-district day/night pair from an ordinary production stand and one lower,
mid-distance workyard-to-root day/night pair in which the worn haul trace and the
three differently finished stock stages are simultaneously distinguishable. If
those stages cannot be made legible through capture composition alone, their
material/value/shape hierarchy needs a narrow visual pass before recapture.
