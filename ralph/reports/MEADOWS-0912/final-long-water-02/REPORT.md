# The Long Water final-02 — independent visual review

## Verdict: POLISH — retain, not a strict named-location PASS

Final-02 resolves the oversized pale near-bank rock blocker and preserves the
accepted long water axis, eastward bend, tower destination, and day/night
readability. It does not resolve the other final-01 blocker: the opposite bank
still presents as a long, near-constant-height sheer cut. The Long Water remains
**POLISH** until that dominant canal-like profile is materially broken.

## Evidence integrity: PASS

- `manifest.json` reports `complete: true`, eight records, and no failures.
- All eight corresponding native 1280×800 PNGs are present and non-empty as
  four coherent day/night pairs.
- Each record retains the production player exactly 0.35m above its sampled
  live ground and receipts camera/player and named-reach distances.
- The disclosure identifies the production Meadows scene, ordinary player,
  Terrain3D, scatter, props, encounters, authored clocks, clear weather, and
  hidden HUD/submersion overlays. It discloses no progression or encounter
  injection. Native inspection finds no camera inside terrain or a solid.

## Strict findings

- **Long axis, route adjacency, bend, and tower: PASS.** `01` still establishes
  the road and water running together. `05` remains the strongest identity
  view: water owns the frame, bends through unequal wooded banks, and terminates
  on the distant tower. The same hierarchy survives at night with a readable
  reflective corridor.
- **Near-bank geology: PASS.** The final-01 chest-high pale trio has been scaled
  down and spread into modest bank stones. In `03` and `07` the trainer and
  water now remain dominant; the stones no longer read as a placed hero-prop row
  or obscure the shoreline.
- **Far-bank profile: POLISH blocker.** In `01`, `03`, and most decisively `07`,
  the opposite bank still runs for a large fraction of the frame as one sheer,
  nearly level-topped wall. The added green/tan vertical streaks improve color
  variation, but repeat across the same uninterrupted face and do not create
  believable height intervals, shelves, slumps, roots, or material breaks. In
  the night pairs it collapses further into a single dark retaining edge. The
  water therefore still reads partly as an engineered canal rather than a
  naturally worn named reach.
- **Bank wander and night usability: PASS.** The near edge wanders, vegetation
  and tree masses differ between sides, the water remains seated in its bed,
  and every route/shore composition stays navigable at both clocks.

## Acceptance boundary

Retain final-02's rock reduction. Promotion requires breaking the far-bank
silhouette itself—not only recoloring it—into a few visible height/material
intervals while preserving the accepted east-bend/tower composition.
