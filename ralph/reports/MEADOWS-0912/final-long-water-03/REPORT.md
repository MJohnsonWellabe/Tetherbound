## Three strongest gaps from the references

1. **Far-bank landform language — `03-overlook-west-*`, `07-bank-wander-*`.**
   The Meadows key art uses layered, rolling banks with rocks, vegetation, and
   broad height changes to make water feel naturally seated in the landscape.
   R3's narrow vertical cuts do not break the long level crest or create visible
   horizontal terraces, so the reach still reads partly as an engineered canal.
2. **Named-subject hierarchy at arrival — `01-axis-arrival-*`.** Palworld's
   traversal frames establish route, destination, and surrounding layers at a
   glance. Here foreground trunks and canopy dominate; neither the Long Water nor
   its parallel route survives clearly at small size.
3. **Night value separation — `02-axis-arrival-night`,
   `04-overlook-west-night`, `08-bank-wander-night`.** The references preserve
   traversable ground and major landform silhouettes after dark. These frames
   merge far bank, tree line, and near-bank vegetation into dark masses while the
   water becomes an unusually saturated blue strip.

## Binding bar questions

**A. Does this belong to the world in the Meadows key art? No.** The sky, oak
palette, wildflower ground, and river corridor point toward the same world, but the
dominant far-bank geometry lacks the key art's layered, naturally eroded shoreline
and the arrival lacks its clear foreground/mid-ground/distance composition.

**B. Beside the Palworld references, would someone say this is trying to be the
same kind of game? No.** The colorful third-person landscape and broad water route
are directionally compatible, but the canal-like cut, obstructed arrival, repeated
tree forms, and crushed night landform remain below the references' authored,
shipping-quality destination read.

The Meadows key art and Palworld comparisons are the binding bars. BOTW's stylized
water-and-bank integration is supplementary only. The remaining defects are
fixable in-engine: replace the narrow groove treatment with a few broad, separated
height intervals and planted/material shelves; preserve an uneven crest; and restore
an unobstructed ordinary-player arrival that shows water and road together. No new
asset family is implied.

R3 remains **POLISH**. The authoritative named-location ledger and its aggregate
count are unchanged.
