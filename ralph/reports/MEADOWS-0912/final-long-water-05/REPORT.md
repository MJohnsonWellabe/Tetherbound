## Residual polish, not a closure blocker

- `03-overlook-west-*` and `07-bank-wander-*` retain a few abrupt, smooth-faced cuts
  with repeated terrain texture; rock, root, talus, and planted transition detail is
  still sparse compared with the references.
- The waterline in `05-*` and `07-*` is geometrically clean and lightly dressed, so
  reeds, stones, and shallow-edge variation do not yet provide the key art's rich
  shoreline finish.
- In `04` and `08`, the far-bank surface compresses into dark green-grey masses. Its
  new silhouette remains readable, but internal night modelling is limited.

## Three strongest remaining gaps from the references

1. **Bank surface finish — `03-*` and `07-*`.** The key art layers exposed stone,
   plants, rocks, and soft transitions through its riverbanks; these broad R5 forms
   still use large stretches of one smooth terrain treatment.
2. **Life and authored incident — all frames.** Palworld's traversal views use
   creatures, encounters, and prop clusters to make open ground feel active. This
   evidence proves the Long Water's landform and route identity, but the visible scene
   contains only the trainer and sparse shoreline dressing.
3. **Night value structure — `04` and `08`.** The key art's night panel separates
   foreground, middle terrain, and destination with clearer value and local-light
   hierarchy; R5 relies mainly on the bright water plane and silhouette breakup.
