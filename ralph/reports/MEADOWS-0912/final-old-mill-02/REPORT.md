# Old Mill Crossing final-02 independent review

**Verdict: FAIL**

## Evidence integrity

- `manifest.json` is complete with eight distinct 1280x800 production-scene frames, four day/night pairs, and no recorded capture failures.
- The disclosure is appropriate: ordinary player and live terrain/scatter/props/encounters, with only clock/weather and presentation overlays controlled. The nominal capture receipt is valid.
- The visual evidence itself is not fully judgeable: both `03-millrace-three-quarter` frames are severely obstructed by live wildlife. Two enormous bird bodies cover both sides in daylight; at night the right bird covers roughly half the frame and much of the mill. This reproduces, and worsens, final-01's obstructed-frame blocker.

## Strict location bar

| Criterion | Verdict | Native-frame evidence |

| Landmark and route readability | PASS | `01` and `04` clearly establish a tall mill on the travelled crossing axis in both lighting states. The bridge remains legible and usable. |

| Working-mill occupation | POLISH | Barrels, an anvil, bench/rack and a few crates add modest activity, but they remain sparse roadside dressing. The frames do not establish grain/loading flow, millrace hardware, timber work, sacks/cart logistics, or wear produced by an operating water mill. |

| Day/night value | POLISH | The silhouette survives at night, but the facade and work area are subdued enough that the operational story becomes less readable rather than gaining a deliberate night hierarchy. |

## Conclusion

Final-02 preserves the accepted approach, bridge route and basic mill landmark, but it does not close either decisive final-01 blocker: the wheel still lacks a visually continuous water-power mechanism, and the three-quarter evidence remains obstructed. The added props are not yet sufficient to sell an active working mill. Do not promote this location to PASS from this evidence set.
