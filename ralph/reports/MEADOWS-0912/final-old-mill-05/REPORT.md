# Old Mill Crossing `final-old-mill-05` independent review

## Verdict: FAIL

### Evidence integrity

I inspected all eight 1280x720 production frames and `manifest.json`. The four
day/night pairs are distinct, the manifest is complete, and no capture failures are
reported. The disclosed wildlife home reset/freeze does not invalidate this scoped
location review. I did not run Godot, recapture evidence, or inspect implementation
source.

### Strict named-location judgment

| Criterion | Verdict | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Built approach and crossing identity | **PASS** | `01` and `04` retain a clear road, bridge axis, tall mill silhouette and river setting. The destination is immediately identifiable. |
| Structural credibility | **FAIL** | The posts in `02`/`04` improve the distant support read, but `03-day/night` is still dominated by a huge smooth rectangular lower foundation. Its near-unbroken pale face and hard box edges do not read as stepped masonry. The dark headrace bed also projects left beyond the visible structure without a convincing load path. |
| Wheel exposure | **FAIL** | `02` shows only a dark partial wheel shape at the mill base; `03` reduces the wheel to narrow fragments behind the building/foundation edge. The wheel is not readable as the visual focus of a water-powered mill at ordinary size. |
| Source -> contact -> tailrace continuity | **FAIL** | `03-day/night` shows a short bright-blue source trough at far left, but the dark bed conceals its continuation and no visually traceable stream reaches the wheel. No readable falling/contact water or discharge connects the wheel to the broad river surface below. Manifest projection measurements prove named nodes were in frame, not that the hydraulic chain communicates in the pixels. |
| Day/night hierarchy | **POLISH** | The mill and crossing remain navigable at night, but `01-night` loses most of the road foreground and `03-night` spotlights the smooth foundation while the hydraulic mechanism stays obscure. |

### Three largest reference gaps

1. `03-day/night`: the key art's buildings meet land and water through layered,
   materially broken edges; this mill meets the river through a single smooth box face.
2. `02` and `03`: both references establish focal silhouettes cleanly; the wheel and
   its water contact remain fragmented or hidden instead of forming the landmark's
   focal read.
3. `03-day/night`: reference scenes explain their authored spaces through visible
   depth and connected elements; the blue source, dark slab, wheel fragments and river
   read as separate pieces rather than one functioning hydraulic system.

### Bar questions

- **A — Meadows key-art world: No.** The timbered mill, green river valley and road
  share the intended vocabulary, but the exposed smooth foundation, slab underside
  and disconnected water mechanism break the authored, grounded pastoral read.
- **B — Same kind of game as the Palworld references: No.** The landmark lacks the
  finished structural articulation and immediate focal clarity of those real-time
  scenes. These gaps are scene-fixable: break the lower mass into visibly stepped
  masonry, support the full headrace span, expose the wheel, and make one continuous
  source-to-contact-to-discharge path unmistakable in the ordinary three-quarter view.

R5 does not close the R4 blockers. Do not promote Old Mill Crossing or change the
authoritative Meadows ledger/counts from this evidence.
