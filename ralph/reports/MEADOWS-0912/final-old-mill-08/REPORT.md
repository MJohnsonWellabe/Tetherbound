# Old Mill Crossing `final-old-mill-08` independent review

## Verdict: FAIL

### Evidence integrity

I inspected `manifest.json` and all eight 1280x800 PNGs at native resolution. The
four required day/night pairs are present, the manifest reports `complete: true`
and zero automatic failures, and the disclosed wildlife reset does not invalidate
this location review. The projection metrics prove that named production nodes
were in frame; they do not substitute for a readable hydraulic sequence.

### Strict named-location judgment

| Criterion | Verdict | Visible evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Arrival and crossing identity | **PASS** | `01` establishes a traveled road and `04` clearly joins that road, bridge and tall timber mill. The named destination remains identifiable. |
| Grounded mill structure | **FAIL** | `02` and `04` still show pale rectangular channel/foundation blocks with hard unsupported-looking edges. In `03`, the large smooth lower wall hangs over the water while an enormous boulder cluster and flat trough underside dominate the frame; the assembly does not communicate a credible supported mill mechanism. |
| Wheel as focal mechanism | **FAIL** | `02-day/night` contains a partially screened dark wheel-like structure among braces, but its rim, paddles and axle do not resolve cleanly. In the dedicated `03-day/night` proof, the wheel/contact area is almost completely enveloped by the boulder mass and building edge. The manifest's wheel projection size and overlap share are not visible clarity. |
| Headpond -> headrace -> contact -> discharge -> tailrace | **FAIL** | Neither `02` nor `03` presents an unmistakable connected water path. `03` looks upward at pale boxes, a broad dark slab, hanging rocks and isolated brown pieces; no obvious water surface descends from a headpond through a headrace onto the wheel. No readable falling contact water or discharge leads into a shaped tailrace, and the level river across the foreground reads as unrelated background water. |
| Day/night hierarchy | **FAIL** | Night preserves the mill silhouette in `02`/`04`, but `03-night` turns the central rocks and trough into a near-black occluder. The hydraulic story becomes less legible precisely in the frame intended to prove it. |

### Largest remaining gaps from the R5/R6 blockers

1. The dedicated hydraulic view proves node presence but visually hides the wheel
   and flow chain behind oversized rocks and a flat overhead trough.
2. The headpond, headrace water, wheel contact, discharge and tailrace do not read
   as one continuous descending system in any ordinary production frame.
3. The pale foundation/channel blocks and abrupt seams still look assembled and
   partly suspended rather than seated, stepped masonry carrying a working mill.

### Bar questions

- **A — Meadows key-art world: No.** The pastoral road, woodland, bridge and timber
  mill fit the palette, but the exposed box masses, floating-looking channel and
  unreadable hydraulics fall below the authored, grounded landmark bar.
- **B — Same kind of game as the Palworld references: No.** The setting has the
  right broad category, but the landmark lacks the immediate mechanical silhouette,
  material integration and source-to-action readability expected of a finished
  real-time environment.

R8 is complete evidence, but it does not visibly close the R5 blocker or the
missing R6 hydraulic proof. Do not promote Old Mill Crossing or update the
authoritative Meadows ledger from this evidence.
