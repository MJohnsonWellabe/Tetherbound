# Old Mill Crossing `final-old-mill-12` independent visual review

## Verdict: FAIL

### Evidence integrity

I inspected `manifest.json` and all eight 1280x800 PNGs at native resolution,
alongside the Meadows key art and five binding Palworld references. The four required
day/night pairs are present. The manifest reports `complete: true`, no automatic
failures, a grounded unchanged production fixture, and extensive projection and
downhill receipts. Those receipts prove that the named parts exist and project into
frame; the pixels remain authoritative for whether they read as one believable mill.

### Strict named-location judgment

### Three largest gaps from the references

1. **A single readable hydraulic cause-and-effect silhouette — `03-day/night`.** The
   references separate focal actions through continuous shapes and clear value
   hierarchy. R12 fragments its process into cyan wedges, flat shelves and a long
   ramp, so the eye cannot trace water from headpond through wheel to river.
2. **Grounded authored construction — `03-day/night`, `04-day/night`.** The key art
   seats buildings and waterfront structures into terrain; the Palworld base image
   groups construction into a coherent worksite. R12's stacked box piers, thin
   cantilevers and blunt unsupported terminations still look unfinished.
3. **Destination-first arrival — `01-day/night`.** Both reference sets retain a clear
   route-to-landmark read through dense scenery. Here the foreground tree hides most
   of the mill and the fence, not the destination, terminates the road composition.

### Bar questions

- **A — Does this belong to the Meadows key-art world? No.** The woodland palette,
  river, timber mill and bridge use the right vocabulary, but the obstructed arrival,
  oversized primitive supports and unresolved water handoffs fall below the key
  art's grounded, inviting, authored-place standard. These are fixable scene,
  geometry, material and composition problems.
- **B — Beside the Palworld references, is this trying to be the same kind of game?
  No.** The stylized third-person landscape is genre-adjacent, and the broadside
  wheel is a credible focal asset. The location lacks the references' immediate
  action read, coherent construction hierarchy, clean contacts and finish. The
  current blocker is not unavailable creature art: it is the scene's own mill
  assembly, water continuity and arrival framing.

R12 is not eligible for promotion from this evidence. Automatic completeness and
projected-node receipts do not close the visible hydraulic, support, arrival or
finish gates.
