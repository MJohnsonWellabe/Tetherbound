# Old Mill Crossing `final-old-mill-13` independent visual review

## Verdict: FAIL

### Evidence integrity

I inspected `manifest.json` and all eight 1280x800 PNGs individually at native
resolution against the binding Meadows key art and Palworld reference bar. The four
required day/night pairs are present. The manifest reports `complete: true`, no
automatic failures, a grounded fixed production stand, and valid projection,
broadside, downhill and continuity receipts. Those receipts establish that the
named nodes exist and are visible; the pixels decide whether they form a believable
working mill.

R13 materially changes R12's foundation and extends the descending sequence. It
does not close the arrival, support, hydraulic-read or finish gates.

### Three largest gaps from the references

1. **Readable hydraulic causality — `03-day/night`.** The references organize focal
   action with continuous silhouettes and separated values. R13 still presents
   water as scattered cyan patches laid over unrelated shelves and a giant grey
   ramp, so source, wheel contact, discharge and river do not read as one process.
2. **Grounded authored construction — `03-day/night`, `04-day/night`.** The key art
   integrates buildings and waterfronts into terrain; the Palworld base frame makes
   supports belong to a coherent worksite. R13's needle-thin stilts, tiny feet and
   unsupported race endpoints read as temporary blockout scaffolding.
3. **Destination-first arrival — `01-day/night`.** Both references keep the path and
   landmark legible through foliage. R13 hides most of its named location behind a
   foreground tree and gives the fence stronger route termination than the mill.

### Bar questions

- **A — Does this belong to the Meadows key-art world? No.** The woodland, river,
  bridge and half-timbered mill share the broad palette and vocabulary, but the
  obscured arrival and visibly provisional hydraulic/support assembly do not meet
  the key art's grounded, inviting, authored-place standard. These are fixable
  scene, geometry, material and composition defects.
- **B — Beside the Palworld references, is this trying to be the same kind of game?
  No.** The third-person stylized landscape is genre-adjacent and the wheel is a
  credible focal asset. The location lacks the references' immediate action read,
  coherent structural hierarchy, contact finish and destination composition. The
  blocker is the scene assembly, not missing creature art.

R13 is not eligible for promotion. The new nodes and measured downhill chain do not
override the visible scaffold, segmented-ramp and arrival failures.
