# Tether Relay — independent visual judgment

**Verdict: HOLD / substantially improved, but not accepted for OWNER-0912 Tier 2 #12.**

The manifest is complete (`complete: true`, 8/8 expected PNGs captured, no failures),
and I inspected all eight production frames at their native 1280 × 800 resolution.
The scale complaint has been materially addressed: this no longer reads as one small
relay prop dropped in a field. It reads as a broad occupied compound with a gatehouse,
elevated work deck, central machine, and a pylon network extending into the landscape.
The material and secondary-object treatment is not at the same standard, however, and
the close evidence still lets a clean, simplified boiler replace the relay apparatus as
the visual subject. That keeps the owner's combined scale/material item open.

The already-passed real traversal/console smoke is retained as independent mechanics
evidence: the site can be traversed and its console can be used. It does not override
the pixels below, which do not clearly communicate that route or interaction target.

## Acceptance blockers

1. Subordinate the boiler in the actual apparatus composition: move it out of the
   central foreground overlap or reduce its visual weight, connect it visibly into the
   relay with pipes/cables, and weather its body and gold trim into the apparatus family.
2. Bring the supporting compound materials above blockout quality: break the repeated
   dark masonry response and add believable roughness/value variation to rails,
   scaffold, and service structure without repainting the already-successful hero asset.
3. Make the playable console route legible in production pixels. A road/yard view should
   show the circulation cue to the upper work deck, and one ordinary-distance frame
   should identify the console as the interaction destination. Retain the already-passed
   traversal and shutdown behavior unchanged.
4. Replace or thicken the flat Relay standards through the shared banner treatment so
   they show cloth silhouette, folds, and two-sided material response in both day and
   night. Avoid solving only the single left flag while leaving the gate hangings and
   boiler-side panels in the same class.

## Minimum reproof

Recapture the same paired day/night set after the material and hierarchy repair, with
`03-relay-apparatus-*` framed so the full hero apparatus, boiler connection, and their
relative scale are simultaneously visible. Add or revise one road-to-console frame so
the traversable route and console silhouette can be judged without relying on the smoke
test. A positive reproof must retain the current compound scale, field network, bounded
night lights, and functional traversal while clearing the boiler and flat-standard
findings above.
