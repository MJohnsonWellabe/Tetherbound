# Independent visual verdict — `final-stonewater-02`

**Overall: FAIL**

The package is complete: `manifest.json` reports `complete: true`, 8/8 planned 1280×800 production frames, and no capture failures. I inspected every PNG at original resolution. The capture proves a long connected water run and removes some of `final-stonewater-01`'s worst tree/camera occlusion, but it still does not close the owner's “Stonewater Reach looks lame” complaint. The location reads as Meadows terrain with a water sheet, a flat standard, and a plain grey gate rather than a distinctive finished waterworks.

## Delta from `final-stonewater-01`

- **Resolved:** the Springhead arrival is no longer almost entirely blocked by a central tree; `04-*` no longer crops a giant pale wall against the camera; the connected channel and terminal basin remain visible.
- **Improved but not closed:** an arch aperture is now visible in `04-*`, but its untextured crenellated silhouette reads as placeholder geometry rather than installed, grounded waterworks masonry.
- **Still blocking:** the haulage wreck is not readable, the three beats lack distinct visual identities, water overlays grass/dirt/tree bases, Springhead has no clear dry approach or intake, and night water still blows out.
- **Newly explicit in this set:** the red standard itself visibly reads as a flat rectangular plane, directly conflicting with the 09/12 paper-flag complaint.

## Required recapture after production repair

1. **Haulage wreck, ordinary-distance and close-oblique, day/night:** make the damaged wagon/haulage subject unmistakable and show how debris, route, waterworks, and standard relate. Do not let a banner become the primary silhouette.
2. **Road arrival and overlook, day/night:** show a finished engineered landmark before arrival—textured masonry, a clear open aperture/channel, and supporting water-control details—with the road visibly leading to it. Include the standard only after its cloth silhouette/material is repaired.
3. **Run/aperture, both directions, day/night:** frame the complete gate/causeway from ordinary traversal distance, prove that the aperture is open and grounded, and show water/road continuation without boulder, tree, grass, or dirt intersections.
4. **Springhead arrival and basin, day/night:** provide a clear dry approach, an uncontested intake silhouette, conformed banks, and water free of polygonal turf islands and vegetation penetration. Night frames must retain teal depth/ripple detail without the white sheet in `08-*`.

This verdict is limited to the visible production capture. It does not prove collision, swim/wade behavior, or actual traversal through the shown aperture.
