**OWNER-0912 Tier 2 #6: FAIL**

`manifest.json` is complete: 17/17 planned 1280×800 frames, no capture
failures, the production Meadows scene, and the production `StonewaterReach`
runtime node. I inspected every PNG at original resolution. This pass is a real
improvement over `final-stonewater-02`: the wreck is identifiable in one daylight
view, the crest has a readable face, the water is continuous, and the causeway now

than a designed intake/cascade, major rocks and capture-time creatures mask the
composition, and the authored subjects collapse at night.

## Delta from `final-stonewater-02`

- **Resolved:** 17/17 coverage now includes both causeway directions, ordinary and
  oblique wreck views, road arrival, Springhead arrival, and day/night pairs. Water
  remains wide and connected; the prior blown-white night basin is now blue and
  controlled. The crest is visible from its authored face.
- **Improved but not closed:** the causeway now has a stone texture, thickness, and
  open arch, but the boulder masks its approach side and the silhouette remains a
  single capped wall. The daylight wreck reads in `01`, but neither its night view nor
  its closer oblique pair preserves that read.
- **Still blocking:** no legible Springhead cascade/intake, no convincing buttressed
  waterworks mass, vegetation through the pool, rock/water intersection, standard-led
  rather than waterworks-led approach, and failed target hierarchy at night.
- **Evidence defect exposed by the larger set:** `05`/`14` and `15`/`16` are valid
  production frames, but dynamic creatures and foreground trunks obscure the named
  subjects. These angles cannot support acceptance even after the manifest reaches
  17/17.

   wall depth, and any buttresses read together; clear a narrow authored vegetation
   exclusion through the channel and terminal basin rather than changing global
   scatter.
2. Give Springhead one unmistakable intake/cascade beat: a raised headwall/spill lip
   with a visible water drop and grounded side supports. Preserve the connected water
   run.
3. Recompose the wreck so its broken wheel/body remains a clean silhouette from the
   ordinary and oblique approaches; offset the standard and give the wreck/arch modest
   non-emissive night separation.
4. Recapture the same 17-frame plan only after those changes, but seat `05`/`14` and
   `15`/`16` with unobstructed subject lines and no roaming creature across the focal
   area. Acceptance still requires both approach and reverse causeway views, the
   wreck ordinary/oblique day-night pairs, and Springhead arrival/basin day-night.
