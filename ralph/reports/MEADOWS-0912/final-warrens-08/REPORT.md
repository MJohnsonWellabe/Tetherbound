## Three largest gaps from the references

1. **Authored material transition:** `02-mid-oblique-day` separates grey-green bank,
   brown triangular apron, grass, and trees into visibly assembled layers. The key
   art and Palworld references integrate rock, soil, foliage, and wear into a single
   readable landform.
2. **Organic silhouette and construction:** `04-den-arrival-day` uses a precise
   rectangular stone surround and box tunnel as its focal shape. The references use
   irregular terrain cuts, layered edges, and silhouettes that avoid primitive-kit
   construction at hero locations.
3. **Depth and surface richness:** `01-arrival-day` and `03b-threshold-inside-day`
   rely on broad smooth mid-value surfaces with sparse weathering, damp variation,
   nesting/use marks, or embedded stones. The references build depth through varied
   surface scale, clustered detail, and strong foreground/middle/distance separation.

## Bar questions

**A. Does this belong to the world of the Meadows key art? No.** The route, palette,
trees, and creature context point toward the same pastoral-mystery setting, but the
faceted applied brow and box-built den do not meet that board's integrated,
weathered-landform language. Scene-fixable gaps are mound/apron topology, material
transitions, weathering, roots, chamber skins, and opening silhouettes.

**B. Beside the Palworld references, would this be read as the same kind of game? No.**
The large expressive creatures and third-person scale context help, but the hero
location remains visibly primitive-built and materially shallow beside Palworld's
natural terrain cuts and authored environmental density. The principal blockers are
scene construction and surfacing; no new creature art is required to answer this
specific R8 acceptance.

Retain the wider bank direction, reduced loose root bars, rounded immediate throat,
traveled floor, and corrected threshold receipts. Replace the brown triangular apron
with an earth/root transition that buries into both shoulders, remove the remaining
pointed-cone/seam read, and break the den portal and passage into genuinely irregular
non-rectangular silhouettes before recapture.
