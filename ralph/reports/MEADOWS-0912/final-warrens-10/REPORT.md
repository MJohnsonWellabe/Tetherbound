# Burrow Warrens — `final-warrens-10` independent judgment

## Verdict: FAIL

R10's manifest records the intended replacement mechanisms, but the production
pixels retain both R9 blocker classes. The exterior still reads as sharp brown
plates applied over a grey mound, and the den-arrival view still centers a
rectangular stone portal opening into a straight box passage.

## Evidence integrity

- I inspected `manifest.json` and all nine 1280x800 PNGs individually at native
  resolution. The evidence is complete at **9 / 9** with zero reported failures.
- The production-scene, encounter and capture-light disclosures are adequate.
  The grounded `03` -> `03a` -> `03b` sequence establishes traversal honestly.
- The geometry receipt proves that four nodes named as endcaps and a Bank metadata
  marker existed. It does not prove that those mechanisms obscure the rejected
  silhouettes in the rendered view.

## Three largest reference gaps

1. **Integrated landform:** the exterior brown treatment still terminates in long
   triangular points and sits apart from the grey mound instead of dissolving into
   one eroded earth bank.
2. **Organic construction:** `04` remains centered on the same primitive rectangular
   portal and straight tunnel language rejected in R9.
3. **Surface continuity:** the threshold uses very large dark polygonal panels and
   hard seams, producing a visibly faceted shell rather than layered dug earth.

## Bar questions

**A. Does this belong to the world of the Meadows key art? No.** The pastoral palette,
route and creature context fit broadly, but the applied triangular facade and box-built
den do not meet the key art's integrated, grounded landform language.

**B. Beside the Palworld references, would this be read as the same kind of game? No.**
The third-person scale is credible, but the hero location remains visibly primitive,
faceted and materially disconnected beside the references' coherent natural cuts and
finished environmental depth.

R10 does not close R9. Do not promote The Burrow Warrens or update the authoritative
named-location ledger from this evidence.
