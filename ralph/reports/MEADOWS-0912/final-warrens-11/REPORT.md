# Burrow Warrens — `final-warrens-11` independent judgment

## Verdict: FAIL

R11 removes R10's plainly rectangular rendered portal and the separate brown
threshold-fan treatment, but the replacement does not yet read as a finished,
believable den. The exterior remains a stack of steep faceted mounds with an
indistinct entrance, while the immediate throat is dominated by an oversized
smooth ring and the chamber opening terminates in thin pointed jambs. Several
close frames expose black gaps and intersecting shell geometry.

## Evidence integrity

- I inspected `manifest.json` and all nine 1280x800 PNGs individually at native
  resolution. The evidence is complete at **9 / 9** with zero reported failures.
- The `03` -> `03a` -> `03b` sequence and ground receipts establish real
  outside-to-inside staging. The manifest clearly discloses the two mandatory
  residents cleared for the earned interior view and the bounded capture-only
  night evidence lights.
- The geometry receipt supports the claimed carrier hiding and organic surface
  counts, but node counts cannot override visible seams, primitive silhouettes
  or occluded evidence in the pixels.

## Three largest reference gaps

1. **Believable organic construction:** `03-threshold-*`, `03a` and `04` replace
   R10's rectangle with highly regular ring/pointed-arch primitives; the references
   use irregular, grounded natural cuts whose silhouette does not reveal the
   construction method.
2. **Authored exterior legibility:** `01-*` and `02-*` lack a clear mouth hierarchy
   and weathered bank transition. The key art and Palworld references establish
   destinations through readable silhouettes, layered vegetation and material/value
   separation rather than a nearly uniform pile of planar mounds.
3. **Finished interior depth:** `03b` contains black gaps, shell intersections and
   severe subject occlusion, while `04` is sparse and smoothly repetitive. The
   references maintain coherent surfaces and deliberate lived-in detail through
   the full depth of a hero location.

## Bar questions

**A. Does this belong to the world of the Meadows key art? No.** The surrounding
pastoral palette, trees and creature context belong broadly, and R11's exterior
material is less disconnected than R10's. The faceted mound pile, pipe-like throat,
pointed shell opening and black interior discontinuities do not meet the key art's
integrated, intentionally composed landform language. These are scene-fixable
geometry, material-transition, dressing and evidence-composition gaps.

**B. Beside the Palworld references, would this be read as the same kind of game? No.**
The third-person trainer/creature scale and colorful resident suggest the genre, but
the hero environment remains visibly procedural and unfinished beside the references'
coherent natural forms, readable destination hierarchy and dense authored detail.
The environment defects are scene-fixable; greater creature finish would require
stronger bespoke art, though the creatures are not the blocker in this evidence set.

R11 is a material improvement over R10's applied brown plates and rectangular portal,
but it does not close the owner-reopened Warrens approach or the organic-den criteria.
Do not promote The Burrow Warrens or update the authoritative named-location ledger
from this evidence.
