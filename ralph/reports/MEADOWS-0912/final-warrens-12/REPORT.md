# Burrow Warrens — `final-warrens-12` independent judgment

## Verdict: FAIL

R12 does not close the owner-reopened Warrens approach or organic-den gate. It
removes R11's separate smooth ring, but replaces it with an enormous, nearly
perfect half-dome whose construction dominates the threshold. Repeated inner
arches, vertical fins, daylight leaks, black floor gaps and the still-symmetrical
pointed chamber portal make the route read as overlapping shells rather than a
dug, inhabited burrow.

## Evidence integrity

- I inspected `manifest.json` and all nine 1280x800 PNGs individually at native
  resolution. The evidence is complete at **9 / 9** with zero reported failures.
- The three grounded `03` -> `03a` -> `03b` positions honestly establish the
  outside-to-inside route. The manifest discloses the two mandatory cleared
  residents and bounded capture-only night evidence lighting.
- The geometry receipt establishes which carriers are hidden, but counts and
  names cannot outweigh visible shell regularity, gaps and intersections.

## Three largest reference gaps

1. **Organic construction:** `03-threshold-*`, `03a` and `04` use large regular
   domes and nested pointed arches. The references hide construction logic in
   irregular natural silhouettes and grounded transitions.
2. **Destination hierarchy on approach:** `01-*` and `02-*` do not make the mouth
   the compositional focus. The references use value separation, framing and
   authored environmental clustering to make important destinations legible.
3. **Finished interior continuity:** `03a`, `03b` and `04` show daylight leaks,
   straight fins/jambs, black discontinuities and sparse repeated surfaces. The
   references retain coherent material depth and lived-in detail through hero
   spaces.

## Bar questions

**A. Does this belong to the world of the Meadows key art? No.** The pastoral
palette, trees and creature context are broadly compatible, but the faceted mound
assembly, oversized half-dome, shell gaps and pointed portal do not share the key
art's integrated landform language. These are scene-fixable geometry, transition,
dressing and composition defects.

**B. Beside the Palworld references, would this be read as the same kind of game? No.**
The third-person trainer and expressive creatures suggest the genre, but this hero
location remains visibly assembled and unfinished beside the references' readable
destinations, coherent natural forms and dense authored detail. The blocking gaps
are environment work; stronger bespoke creature finish would help overall parity,
but creature art is not what sinks R12.

R12 is not a promotion candidate. Do not update the authoritative named-location
ledger from this evidence.
