# Burrow Warrens — `final-warrens-13` independent judgment

## Verdict: FAIL

R13 removes R12's dominant half-dome and pointed portal, but does not produce a
finished organic den. The threshold and passage are now visibly assembled from
giant smooth capsule/egg masses beneath broad flat slabs. Exposed rectangular
members, daylight leaks, intersections and black floor voids remain visible along
the acceptance route. From the ordinary road, the mouth is still an ambiguous dark
slot rather than a clearly authored destination.

## Evidence integrity

- I inspected `manifest.json` and all nine 1280x800 PNGs individually at native
  resolution. The evidence is complete at **9 / 9**, with no manifest failures.
- The sequential `03` -> `03a` -> `03b` frames provide an honest grounded
  outside-to-inside receipt. The manifest discloses the two cleared residents and
  bounded capture-only night evidence lighting.
- The geometry receipt confirms that rejected carriers are hidden, but node names
  and primitive counts cannot override the visible capsule-and-slab result.

## Three largest reference gaps

1. **Believable organic construction:** `03-*`, `03a` and `04` expose capsule
   primitives, flat slabs and repeated egg supports. The references conceal
   construction logic inside coherent, grounded natural silhouettes.
2. **Destination hierarchy from ordinary arrival:** `01-*` and `02-*` do not make
   the mouth immediately readable. The references use decisive silhouettes, value
   separation and authored prop/terrain clustering to identify destinations.
3. **Finished passage continuity:** daylight leaks, black voids and intersecting
   ceiling/support pieces break the spatial story across `03`, `03a`, `03b` and
   `04`. Reference-quality hero spaces retain coherent surfaces and readable depth.

## Bar questions

**A. Does this belong to the world of the Meadows key art? No.** The pastoral
surroundings and creature context are compatible, but the faceted exterior,
ambiguous mouth and visibly primitive enclosure do not share the key art's
integrated, authored landform language. These are scene-fixable geometry,
transition, dressing and composition defects.

**B. Beside the Palworld references, would this be read as the same kind of game? No.**
The trainer and creatures signal the genre, but the exposed capsule/slab assembly,
gaps, intersections, crushed night approach and sparse interior read as unfinished
environment work beside the references. Creature art is not the blocking issue.

R13 is not a promotion candidate. Do not update the authoritative named-location
ledger from this evidence.
