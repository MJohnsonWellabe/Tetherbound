# Burrow Warrens — `final-warrens-14` independent judgment

## Verdict: FAIL

R14 removes R13's giant capsule corridor and gives the inside route more continuous
earth surfaces, but it does not close the Warrens gate. The entrance is still not a
readable destination from ordinary arrival. At the threshold, a huge pointed dark
shell dominates the frame amid razor-thin facade fins, flat overlapping roof pieces
and open daylight. The route ends at another pointed arch nested inside a square wall,
so the den still reads as assembled tunnel geometry rather than an excavated,
inhabited burrow.

## Evidence integrity

- I inspected `manifest.json` and all nine 1280x800 PNGs individually at native
  resolution, alongside the Meadows key art and five binding Palworld references.
  The evidence is complete at **9 / 9**, with no manifest failures.
- The sequential `03` -> `03a` -> `03b` frames provide a grounded outside-to-inside
  receipt. The manifest discloses the two earned resident clears and bounded
  capture-only night evidence lighting.
- The R14 node receipt says capsule masses and rejected carriers are absent, but the
  pixels—not node names—decide whether the resulting shell reads organically.

## Three largest reference gaps

1. **Organic, coherent construction:** `03-threshold-*`, `03a` and `04` expose
   pointed shells, square recesses, thin fins and layered panels. The references hide
   construction logic inside coherent, grounded natural or built silhouettes.
2. **Immediate destination hierarchy:** `01-*` and `02-*` do not make the den mouth
   readable from the route. The references use clear silhouettes, value separation
   and clustered environmental cues to identify destinations at a glance.
3. **Lived-in finish and spatial continuity:** `03b` and `04` are sparse, box-like
   rooms linked by repeated portals. The references sustain authored detail, clean
   contacts and readable depth through important gameplay spaces.

## Bar questions

**A. Does this belong to the world of the Meadows key art? No.** The meadow palette,
trees and creature context are adjacent to the target, but the faceted mound,
invisible approach mouth, exposed shell seams and repeated pointed portals do not
share the key art's integrated, inviting landform language. These are scene-fixable
geometry, transition, composition, lighting and dressing defects.

**B. Beside the Palworld references, would this be read as the same kind of game? No.**
The third-person trainer and creatures signal the genre, but the hero location still
looks like unfinished environment assembly beside the references' clear destinations,
coherent surfaces, strong value hierarchy and dense authored detail. Creature art is
not what blocks R14.

R14 is not a promotion candidate. Do not update the authoritative named-location
ledger from this evidence.
