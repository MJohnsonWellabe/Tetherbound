# Independent blind visual review — Burrow Warrens R32

**Verdict:** FAIL — playtest visual item and named-location presentation.
**Confidence:** high.

The reviewer inspected all nine production PNGs at their original 1280×720
resolution plus `manifest.json`, without implementation history or prior captures.

## Frame findings

- `01-arrival-day/night`: route and entrance are identifiable, but the steep
  tent-like mound sides, front value change, and attached dark shelves do not read
  as convincing excavated terrain. Night further merges the shelves and entrance.
- `02-mid-oblique-day/night`: angular flanks, flat shelves, and the trapezoidal
  mouth read as assembled shapes; the night silhouette preserves those defects.
- `03-threshold-day/night`: horizontal apron striping and abrupt surface changes
  make the mouth read as a tunnel sleeve beneath a separate grassy opening.
- `03a-threshold-step-day`: grass-covered planes visibly frame a separate brown
  shell, breaking the illusion of entering one solid earth bank.
- `03b-threshold-inside-day`: the route is readable and mushrooms help, but the
  successive centered openings and abrupt floor/wall boundaries read as a corridor.
- `04-den-arrival-day`: the guardian is complete and recognizable, but the passage
  hides most of the den and a rectangular face remains beside the mushrooms.

## Blocking correction

Unify the entrance terrain construction and materials, remove visible apron and
shell joins, and provide a den view that proves room identity rather than only the
guardian. The clean 9/9 manifest establishes capture provenance, not visual PASS.
