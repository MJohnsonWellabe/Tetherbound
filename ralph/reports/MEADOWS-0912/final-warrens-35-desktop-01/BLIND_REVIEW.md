# Independent blind visual review — R35

Reviewed all nine production PNGs at 1280x720 plus `manifest.json`, without
implementation history or prior captures.

## Verdict

- Burrow Warrens visual playtest item: **FAIL** (high confidence)
- Named-location presentation: **FAIL** (high confidence)

## Frame findings

- Arrival day/night: route and glowing mouth are readable and the tree-topped
  mound has a usable silhouette, but the facade still reads as assembled terrain
  wedges. Abrupt material changes, embedded/floating face details, and the
  compressed dark entrance keep the approach unfinished.
- Mid-oblique day/night: hard intersections and seams remain between the mound,
  bank pieces, and portal shell. The entrance is not one continuous geological
  form.
- Threshold day/night and threshold step: the mouth resolves into a blocky
  trapezoidal shell with sharp apron/tunnel transitions rather than naturally dug
  earth.
- Inside threshold: readable route, but sparse and generic with repetitive glossy
  wall treatment.
- Den arrival: strongest frame; the guardian is an effective focal point with
  good scale. The surrounding den remains bare, harshly lit, materially crude,
  and partly box-like.

## Promotion blockers

Re-author the facade and mouth as one continuous excavated landform, remove visible
seams and wedge-like masses, unify the exterior-to-interior material transition,
and carry a distinctive Warrens motif through threshold, passage, and den. The
small threshold marker is unreadable at gameplay distance. Capture-only evidence
lights in the night frames mean those frames do not independently prove shipping
night readability.
