# Burrow Warrens R51 — independent blind visual review

Reviewer inspected all nine 1280×720 production PNGs without implementation or
prior-review context. The manifest is complete 9/9 with no capture failures.

## Meadows production visual playtest bar — FAIL (high confidence)

- Arrival and route are clear, but the pointed intersecting berms still read as
  assembled geometry.
- The threshold is legible, but the mouth sits between artificial wedge walls
  and the ground material changes abruptly at rectangular edges.
- `03b-threshold-inside-day.png` is the decisive blocker: it is nearly black,
  visually empty, uniformly ribbed, and ends in a hard polygonal cutout.
- The guardian is striking, but `04-den-arrival-day.png` leaves too little room
  context and exposes a bare orange enclosure with a severe diagonal light seam.

## Named-location identity — FAIL (high confidence)

The sign and burrow mouth make the arrival unmistakable, but the interior does
not carry that identity: the passage reads as a generic dark tube and the den as
a creature close-up in a generic earthen room. A repeated roots, nesting,
ecology, or rootstone language is not visibly strong enough through the journey.

## Required production correction

Disguise or replace the intersecting façade masses, integrate the ground
transition, make the passage and den readable authored spaces, and carry a
distinct Warrens motif through both interior phases.
