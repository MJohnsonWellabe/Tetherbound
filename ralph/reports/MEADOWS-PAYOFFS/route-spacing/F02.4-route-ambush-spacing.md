# F02.4 — spacing of aggressive ambushes on the earned Upper Meadows → Hall road

**Defect.** A real save walked north from Gate F S08 (-241.8, 6469) and fought four aggressive Galecrest from orders 4102, 4916, 4005 and 4917 in 118 s (about 320 m). A pack then pinned the player at (-130.8, 6669.3), 5.9 m from `trail/bands[3]`. Source: `lure-walk-receipts.log` line 176 on `ralph/f03-activity-rows`. A second walk fought 4102, 4005 (twice) and 4917 at t = 7/57/76/113 s. After a won fight, pack bodies then stood on the trail and blocked the player at (-132.0, 6675.9).

**Target.** WORLD §3.1 says "a meaningful sight, encounter or decision should occur every 150–250 m". A forced ambush is one encounter beat. So consecutive road-reaching aggressive envelopes must begin at least 150 m apart, which allows at most 2 in any 300 m. ACCEPTANCE A7/F02 ask for this to be measured on the earned route. A danger floor applies at seed 0 (the authored world):
- at least one road ambush in every 600 m (A7's 120 s at the 5 m/s walk);
- Band 4 keeps at least 1 ambush and Band 5 at least 2;
- order 5001 is unchanged.

**Measure.** `tests/helpers/meadows_route_ambush_spacing.gd` runs through `tests/test_meadows_route_ambush_spacing.gd`. It is offline and deterministic.
- **Route.** `trail.bands` 3 and 4, from (0,4760) to the Hall gate (0,7560): 4088 m. This is the same spine that `meadows_earned_hall_segment.gd::departure_spine()` walks.
- **Species.** Resolved through `spawn_tables.plan_for()` for each seed, then `is_aggressive()`.
- **Envelope.** radius + wander radius (the entry's own, else combat.json's 7) + notice_range (14) + path half-width (1.8).
- **Ambush point.** The first chainage at which the road enters an envelope.

**Fix.** Config only.
- **Authored ambushes stay.** These keep reaching the road at 154 m spacing or more: 4910, 4001α, 4912, 4913, 4040, 4043, 4070, 4914, 4916, 4917, 4919, 5006 and 5001α (untouched).
- **Roadside pairs step back.** Pairs 4910, 4912–4914, 4916, 4917 and 4919 move from 5.5 m to 10 m off the centreline and gain `wander_radius` 4.0. Idle bodies therefore settle beside the trail. 4919 also slides 6 m back along the road.
- **Crowding Galecrest step back.** 4005, 4010, 4012, 4022, 4026, 4055, 4066, 4071, 4911, 4915 and 5011 move straight back from the road, 7–58 m, until their envelope clears it. They keep their species, count, radius and order.
- **On-road non-aggressive clusters get new tables.** 31 clusters roll from the new `meadows_air_trailside` and `meadows_rock_trailside` tables. These are the original tables without Galecrest and Frostclaw. Seed 0 is unchanged.
