# F02.4 — spacing of aggressive ambushes on the earned Upper Meadows → Hall road

**Defect.** A real save walked north from Gate F S08 (-241.8, 6469) and fought four aggressive Galecrest from orders 4102, 4916, 4005 and 4917 in 118 s (about 320 m). A pack then pinned the player at (-130.8, 6669.3), 5.9 m from `trail/bands[3]`. Source: `lure-walk-receipts.log` line 176 on `ralph/f03-activity-rows`. A second walk fought 4102, 4005 (twice) and 4917, then was blocked at (-132.0, 6675.9) by pack bodies standing on the trail.

**Target.**
- **Spacing.** WORLD §3.1 says "a meaningful sight, encounter or decision should occur every 150–250 m". A forced ambush is one encounter beat. So consecutive road-reaching aggressive envelopes must begin at least 150 m apart, which allows at most 2 in any 300 m. ACCEPTANCE A7/F02 ask for this to be measured on the earned route.
- **Danger floor.** It applies to seed 0 and to every rolled seed 1–200, because `roll_new_worlds` ships true. Each world must keep:
  - at least 8 road ambushes;
  - no stretch of 600 m or more without one (A7's 120 s at the 5 m/s walk), including from the start and to the Hall gate;
  - at least 1 ambush in Band 4 and 2 in Band 5;
  - order 5001 unchanged and reaching the road.

**Scope.** The spine only: `trail.bands` 3 and 4, from (0,4760) to the Hall gate (0,7560), 4088 m. This is the path `meadows_earned_hall_segment.gd::departure_spine()` walks. The optional side loops are out of scope: `wind_ridge_traverse`, `high_pasture_loop` and `watchtower_spur`.

**Measure.** `tests/helpers/meadows_route_ambush_spacing.gd` runs through `tests/test_meadows_route_ambush_spacing.gd`. It is offline and deterministic.
- **Species.** Resolved through `spawn_tables.plan_for()` for each seed, then `is_aggressive()`.
- **Envelope.** radius + wander radius (the entry's own, else combat.json `wild.wander_radius`) + catching.json `notice_range` + path half-width (paths.width/2 + shoulder).
- **Ambush point.** Every chainage at which the spine enters an envelope. If the spine leaves an envelope and later re-enters it, the re-entry counts as a new ambush.

**Fix.** Config only. No cluster, species, count, level or order was removed. Every changed entry carries a `_why_f02_route_spacing` note.
- **Spaced ambushes become anchors.** 4040, 4043, 4070 and 5006 are now anchors (no `table`), so Galecrest stands there in every world seed.
- **Roadside pairs step back.** 4910, 4912–4914, 4916, 4917 and 4919 move from 5.5 m to 10 m and gain `wander_radius` 4 (the road veto).
  - 4916 moves to the east shoulder.
  - 4919 is at 9.6 m; it now clears 4060 by 4.8 m (main: 4.3 m).
  - 4910, 4912 and 4913 still overlap 4014, 4028 and 4034. Those overlaps were already on main and are now smaller: 11.3→7.3 m, 10.8→6.4 m, 20.0→19.6 m.
- **Crowding Galecrest step back.** 4005, 4010, 4012, 4022, 4026, 4055, 4066, 4071, 4911 and 4915 move until their envelope clears the road.
  - 5011 is now 40.8 m from the road; its envelope is 36.8 m. It is not closer because it must also keep 18 m from the parallel storm_road spoke and 3 m from every other disc.
  - No moved cluster overlaps any other cluster more than it did on main.
- **On-road non-aggressive clusters get new tables.** 31 clusters roll from the new `meadows_air_trailside` and `meadows_rock_trailside` tables. These are the original tables without Galecrest and Frostclaw.
  - `_draw` rolls against total weight, so every remaining share rises.
  - In `meadows_air_trailside`, duskhush is held at `uncommon`, which gives it 1/5 of the rolls instead of 1/2.
  - In `meadows_rock_trailside` (Band 4), burrowback goes from 47% to 55%.
- **Generator.** `tools/_gen_band4_density.py` now copies these placements back in, so a rerun cannot undo them.

**Open trade-off.** Frostclaw is a rare that only appears in Band 4 in rain or fog. Almost every Band 4 rock cluster that could roll it sits on the road. Keeping it on the road would add forced ambushes in rain or fog that are not spaced. Restoring its rate needs a decision: for example, re-table some off-road Band 4 clusters to `meadows_rock`, or allow a weather-gated exception to the spacing rule.
