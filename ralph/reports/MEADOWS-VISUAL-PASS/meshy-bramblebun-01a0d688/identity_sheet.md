# Bramblebun: identity sheet (retexture pass, X04)

**The subject is the shipped mesh.** `species.json` bramblebun uses `bramblebun_redesign/models/creature_bramblebun_redesign_lod0.glb` for its model and colourway source. The mesh has 37,462 vertices, a 15-bone local quadruped rig and 6 clips. The canon reference is `bramblebun_redesign/reference/{front,side,back,top}.png`, from owner sheet 06.

## Must be preserved
- **Silhouette:**
  - a compact, heavy hare seated upright, about 1.0 m tall;
  - an oversized round head;
  - two very tall upright ears;
  - forked twig antlers branching sideways from the ear bases and the crown;
  - spiky bramble/fur tufts along the cheeks, shoulders and spine;
  - a round tail puff.
- **Proportions:** the head is about 40% of the body height. The ears roughly double the height. The antlers widen the silhouette to about 1.6 times the body width.
- **Signature features:**
  - large dark glossy eyes with a highlight;
  - a cream muzzle with a small nose;
  - a cream bib on the chest and throat;
  - pink inner ears;
  - twig antlers;
  - a moss/leaf mantle across the shoulders and down the spine;
  - cream toe tips and tail.
- **Palette identity:**
  - a warm brown coat (canon "brown rabbit"; the shipped vivid regrade already pushed it toward honey/russet);
  - cream on the chest, muzzle and tail;
  - pink inner ears;
  - bark antlers;
  - green moss accents.
  - No red and no oxblood, which is reserved for Team Tether.

## Defects to fix (R3/R7/R9 judges)
- The body is a dark, blotchy, baked brown: the median albedo value is 0.38.
- Scribbled creases and noise.
- It turns into a "brown lump" on the yellow-green meadow.
- The green moss patches share the grass hue.
- In the shipped texture the ear tips are painted green, which is not canon.
