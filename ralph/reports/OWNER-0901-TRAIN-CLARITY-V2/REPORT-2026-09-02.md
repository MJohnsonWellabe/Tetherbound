# OWNER-0901-TRAIN-CLARITY-V2 — real render evidence, 2026-09-02

Follow-up to this branch's earlier real-execution pass (85/85 tests green,
`smoke_tournament_bracket.gd`'s full simulated tournament win,
`smoke_objective_hint_card.gd`'s pass). The coordinator asked for one more
step before closing: actual rendered frames of the guidance text, at
handheld size, judged rather than assumed legible from a passing test.

## What was captured

`tools/_capture_train_clarity.gd`, run for real
(`xvfb-run -a -s "-screen 0 1280x800x24" godot --path . --rendering-driver
opengl3 --resolution 1280x800 --script tools/_capture_train_clarity.gd`,
**never** `--headless` with a real rendering driver — see the tool's own
header) against the real `meadows_playground.tscn` world, a seeded
full-but-untrained five-creature party, at 1280×800 — the ROG Ally's real
panel resolution (`tests/smoke_hud_handheld_legibility.gd`'s own
`HANDHELD_SIZE`), not the 1920×1080 the HIST-036 reference tool this was
modelled on used.

- `shots/01-objective-hint-card.png` — the HUD's timed objective-hint
  card, showing the `tournament_train_team` rung's authored guidance
  ("Villagers who offer a fight are the training — Bryn at the practice
  ring first. Wins are levels. Get your whole team to level 5."), over
  real village terrain, as a player actually meets it the moment this
  rung becomes current.
- `shots/02-halda-train-dialogue.png` — Halda's own
  `tournament_halda_train` conversation, advanced to its third and final
  line (the one item 12 is actually about): "Feed them. Rest them — a
  Creature Bed or your own bedroll, either does it. Get them to level
  five, then we'll talk."

Getting a clean capture took three attempts, and the two real bugs found
along the way are worth recording (both are in the capture tool, not the
game):

1. The hint card's reveal is edge-triggered on a *change* in the tracked
   objective text. Boot-time settling had already advanced the real
   tracked line to this exact rung before the tool's own override landed,
   so setting the identical text again wasn't a change and nothing
   revealed. Fixed by forcing a real intermediate change (blank, then the
   real text).
2. Under `llvmpipe` software rasterisation over this world's full
   prop/grass/vegetation count, a single real render frame can cost
   several wall-clock seconds — long enough to burn through the hint
   card's own ~10s dwell window (`OBJECTIVE_HINT_SECONDS_*`,
   `playground_hud.gd`) in the handful of frames the tool was settling
   before pinning the dwell clock open. Fixed by pinning immediately after
   one settle frame instead of several.

Neither bug is a defect in the shipped game; both are artefacts of
capturing a timed UI element under abnormally slow software rendering.

## Visual judgement

Per `ralph/conventions.md`'s visual-affecting-work rule, both frames were
reviewed — not just read by the session that captured them. A blind
sub-agent with no knowledge of the task, the conversation, or what answer
was wanted was shown both PNGs cold and asked, per-frame: what text is
present verbatim, is it legible, does it overlap or compete with any other
UI element, and would a first-time player understand the concrete next
action from the frame alone.

**Verdict, both frames: legible, correctly composed, no overlap.**

- All guidance text is crisp with strong contrast (light text on a dark
  translucent panel with outline/shadow treatment) at the real handheld
  resolution.
- No panel overlaps another. The hint card, the persistent "MAIN STORY"
  line, the team roster, the hotbar/resource tray and the bottom action
  bar each occupy distinct screen regions.
- Both texts independently name the same concrete actions in different
  voices (the system's hint card and Halda's own line), and the blind
  critic could restate the required action correctly from either frame
  alone: fight villagers (Bryn first) to gain levels, feed and rest the
  team, reach level 5.

**Two things the critic noted that are not this item's defect, recorded
for completeness:**

- The persistent "MAIN STORY" panel (top right, always on screen) shows
  only a truncated one-line objective label with a trailing "…". This is
  the fixed-height always-on line's own long-standing, deliberate design
  (`playground_hud.gd`'s `OBJECTIVE_LINES`/`OBJECTIVE_BLOCK_HEIGHT`
  constants) — it is short *by design* precisely because the detailed
  guidance is meant to live in the timed hint card and the dialogue/quest
  log instead, which is the entire reason `HIST-036`'s hint-card mechanism
  exists (see that system's own code comments). Not a new defect, not
  introduced by anything in scope here, and not a "no clear guidance"
  problem since the full text is available and legible one glance away in
  both captured frames.
- Frame 2 shows the hint card and Halda's dialogue on screen at the same
  time, which happens because the hint card's dwell window (~10s) was
  still open when the captured conversation started — a realistic
  coincidence a player could hit, not an engineered one. The critic called
  it "mildly redundant," not unreadable or overlapping. Two independent
  surfaces agreeing with each other is not a defect worth chasing.

Neither observation is "small, cramped, overlapped, or buried" — the
coordinator's own bar for treating this as a live defect — so nothing was
widened beyond the two frames asked for.
