READY smoke_net_f48_cut_key intermittent fix (4 Nov 18:45Z, tb/routes-f49 2d06d02e, its own commit): root cause is ENet, not the registry. ENet re-checks a peer's timeout only when a reliable command's retransmit timer expires, and those timers double without bound (Godot thirdparty/enet/protocol.c enet_protocol_check_timeouts). After the guest's hard kill the host goes idle, so the dead peer's drop lands at the first doubled expiry past the 135 s minimum: anywhere up to ~270 s, past the configured 180 s maximum and the smoke's 200 s window, depending on the round-trip time when the guest died. Fix (smallest): `scripts/net/session.gd` pings each physical ENet peer every `peer_liveness_ping_s` (1 s, `data/config/multiplayer.json`); a ping is a fresh reliable command outside the send window, so its timer re-runs the check and the 180 s maximum holds. No timeout values changed; the smoke's expectation is unchanged.
