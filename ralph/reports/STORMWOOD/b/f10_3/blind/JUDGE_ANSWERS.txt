Q5. Specific defects:
1. img_01/03/07/11: the warning is a thin ring that reads as a selection circle.
   Fix: fill the disc with a translucent hazard colour (e.g. electric white-blue or yellow). Add an inner ring that shrinks or fills over 1.2 s to show the countdown. Increase the edge thickness.
2. img_01/03/07/11: nothing in a still frame distinguishes the ring's early moments from its final ones.
   Fix: make intensity or pulse rate ramp toward impact, and add a faint descending column or crackle above the zone in the last 0.3 s.
3. img_03/11: the bolt at the far left sky edge is disconnected from the warned zone. It suggests the strike already happened somewhere else and confuses where the danger is.
   Fix: suppress off-target sky bolts while a warning is active near the player, or aim the visible bolt at the ring centre on impact.
4. img_02: the impact bolt is a flat, soft grey cylinder with no jagged/forked shape, no glow and no ground burst. It reads as a pillar or beacon, not lightning.
   Fix: use a forked, bright core bolt with bloom, plus a ground scorch/spark burst and a debris ring.
5. img_02 (flashing ON): a full-scene white/grey wash with a large exposure jump. It is harsh, and it turns the scene into a daytime overcast look.
   Fix: cap the flash amplitude and duration, and localise it (sky plus ring area) rather than lifting the whole frame.
6. img_13 (reduced): the impact is almost invisible. The only change is brighter ground veins, with no ring, no bolt shape and no scorch mark.
   Fix: keep a non-flashing impact marker, such as a static bolt silhouette, a scorch decal or a persistent brief ring burst, so the strike still reads with reduced flashing.
7. All frames: the permanently glowing gold "vein" lines on the path compete with the warning. They look like a hazard or lava crack themselves, so the ground already reads as dangerous before any warning.
   Fix: dim them substantially during Break, or use a colour that cannot be confused with a hazard.
8. Phase readability: Building and Fading look identical (06 vs 10), and Calm vs Fading also blur (05 vs 08).
   Fix: give each phase a distinct cue, for example:
   - Building: darkening, swirling cloud front and gusting foliage.
   - Fading: brightening horizon gap and thinning rain with drips.
   - Calm: no rain or mist only.
9. Day/night: no discernible night variant. If a night hour is meant to differ, the grade needs a visibly darker ambient and sky, and the warning ring should be checked for contrast at night.
