# r37: Ashfoot hut-scale planks (CPU, code-blind, 26 Oct): PARKED

**Arms:** the same `ash_in` stand, all four phases.
- **OFF:** m36 (seated + firelit + storm_view).
- **ON:** adds `cottage_interior_finish.json storm_finish.planks`. Lining boards go from 0.36 x 3.2 m to 0.18 x 1.5 m, with board variation 0.16.

**Result:** mapped **5.77 -> 5.37 (-0.40)**. Raw means are 5.53 -> 5.23, and all three judges prefer OFF. The fit is r 0.80 with anchor RMSE 0.50 and no warnings. I checked the side mapping against the pixels: A is ON, B is OFF.

**What the judges saw:** they read the original wide boards as "finer grain". The narrow boards, with their stronger board-to-board tone, read as busier slabs. At this close wide-angle stand, board width is not what reads as "stretched"; the coarse atlas grain is.

**Decision:** PARKED. The flag stays OFF. The shader uniforms stay in place with their old defaults.
