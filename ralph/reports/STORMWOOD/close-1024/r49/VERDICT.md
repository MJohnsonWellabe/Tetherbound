# r49: Crown Overlook rim lookout + storm-haze massif (CPU, code-blind, 27 Oct): NO GAIN (-0.25), first miss

**Arms:** the three deck views, calm and break.
- **OFF:** the landed SW18 vista (the deck 15 m off the Crown Overlook spot on flat ground, the white massif in scene fog).
- **ON:**
  - `deck.rim`: the deck moved 190 m along the sightline to the Glass Sink rim, then 20 m along the rim to clear a baked rim tree. It sits at (289.4, 2118.4), heading 35.2, 712 m from the sink centre: back on the level rim (about 26.8 m), front over the rim slope on long posts.
  - `massif.storm`: r46's fog-free storm haze, darker tint and rim.

**Result:** mapped **5.58 -> 5.33 (-0.25)**. The judges split: 5.6/5.3, 5.3/5.6, 5.8/5.4. Linear fit r 0.86, slope 1.86, no warnings.
- **No sense of height:** PRESENT on both arms for two judges; PRESENT -> PARTIAL for one ("a cool strip below the deck planks in the forward view hints at a drop").
- **Pale Crown:** mixed. Two judges call the ON Crown "pale lavender, nearly lost against the lavender sky" and prefer the landed white Crown's contrast. One prefers the darker ON Crown.

**Why:** the catalogue camera is level, 5.2 m behind the trainer at eye height. From the rim deck it looks out over the rail at the far sink floor, which at that distance reads as a flat plain at deck level. The slope that falls away right under the deck never enters the frame. The storm haze also sits at nearly the calm sky's value, so the Crown loses the contrast it had as a white shape.

**Decision:** first miss for the rim approach. The `deck.rim` and `massif.storm` candidates stay OFF. No retry as built.
- **New-idea threshold:** something that puts the drop *in* a level frame. Examples: a deck built out over the slope so its front rail and the canopy below share the frame, or tree crowns below eye level between deck and Crown.
- **Haze tuning:** for the Crown, darker than the sky in calm (not lavender on lavender).
