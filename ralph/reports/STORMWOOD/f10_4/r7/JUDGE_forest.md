# Code-blind visual judgement: Stormwood forest (views J/K/L, storm states r7)

I looked only at the 12 images named. Note: view_J matches forest_heart_west_calm, view_K matches forest_calm and view_L matches forest_road_calm, frame for frame.

## Part 1: forest readability

### View J (heart west): INSIDE DEEP FOREST (a weak pass)
- **Trunks:** Very large, dark, buttressed trunks. One fills the left 40% of the frame right beside the trainer, who reaches barely a quarter of its visible height. Several more stand at mid-distance and far back, overlapping each other. Their scale says "old".
- **Canopy:** Closed. Leaves cover the whole top of the frame and no sky shows overhead.
- **Open land and horizon at eye level:** No horizon. There is one tiny lavender gap at the vanishing point between far trunks. The right half of the floor, however, is an evenly lit, uniform lawn of grass blades with no trunks, roots, logs or shadow pools for a long way. That clearing is the one parkland cue.
- **Depth:** Good. There are four or more layers of trunks receding and getting smaller, and the far layers stay dark instead of opening up. Overall it reads as deep woods, but in a glade within them. If the camera turned right into that clearing it would drift toward "edge of wood".

### View K (forest): INSIDE DEEP FOREST (the strongest of the three)
- **Trunks:** Huge trunks frame both edges (left foreground and right foreground) and several more stand behind the trainer. The spacing between them is only a few trainer-widths, and they overlap in depth.
- **Canopy:** Fully closed across the top. There are no sky holes.
- **Open land and horizon at eye level:** None. Every sightline at eye level ends on a trunk. There is only a hint of violet in the far gap near the centre.
- **Depth:** Strong. Trunks recede in layers into shadow, and the mushrooms and flowers at the trunk bases give a scale ladder. The player would feel enclosed and deep in the woods.

### View L (forest road): INSIDE DEEP FOREST (a forest road; borderline toward edge of wood)
- **Trunks:** Big gnarled trunks line both sides of the road, with more receding in depth on both flanks. The right side reads as a thick wall of trunks behind the pylons.
- **Canopy:** Arches over the road like a tunnel and closes overhead.
- **Open land and horizon at eye level:** A clear slot of purple sky and a sliver of flat horizon show at the vanishing point, at the end of the road. That is the only open land, but it sits right in the centre of the frame where the eye goes.
- **Depth:** Very good. The road, the pylon line and the tunnel of trees all lead the eye far into the distance. It reads as "a road through a big forest". The bright sky at the end of the tunnel does suggest "the wood ends up ahead", so it is less "deep" than K. It would read deeper if that end-of-road gap were closed by a far bend, a trunk or fog.

**Shared note (readability, not polish):** In all three views the canopy is plain green and purple appears only in small sky slivers. It reads clearly as a dark forest in rain, but "purple storm forest" rests almost entirely on the sky, which is barely visible.

## Part 2: storm states

### Forest (forest_calm / forest_break / forest_aftermath_calm)
- **(a) Break vs Calm:** Yes, easily. Break is much darker, with a cold blue-violet grade, dense slanted rain streaks across the whole frame and a canopy almost lost in shadow. **However, no lightning is visible.** There is no flash, bolt, rim light or sudden brightening. The "lightning peak" reads as "the storm gets darker and wetter", not as a lightning strike.
- **(b) Aftermath:** It is clearly the same place: same trunks, mushrooms, flowers and trainer pose. There is no lightning and the rain is lighter, down to a handful of streaks, so it is close to none. It is also noticeably brighter and more saturated than Calm: vivid green canopy and lighter bark. That reads as "the weather cleared and the sun came back", which pushes against "sky stays purple". No sky shows in this frame to confirm that it stayed purple. I saw no scars in any of the three states here. A brown and violet creature appears at the far left in the aftermath.
- **(c) Trainer findable:** Calm yes. Aftermath yes, easily. Break yes but weakest: the trainer is a dark silhouette on dark grass, and only the white collar, the backpack and the blue shirt pick them out. You can find them because they sit centre frame, not because they have contrast.

### Forest road (forest_road_calm / _break / _aftermath_calm)
- **(a) Break vs Calm:** Yes, very clearly. Break is dark and violet with heavy wind-slanted rain, a bright violet glow at the end of the road and a golden ground crack that burns much brighter. Again, **no lightning bolt or flash is visible.** The peak reads through darkness, rain and the crack glowing, not through lightning.
- **(b) Aftermath:** It is the same place: same road, pylons, cable and trees. There is no lightning, and rain is nearly gone (one or two streaks). The sky slot at the end stays purple with clouds, which is good. **Scars that remain do not really read:** the glowing golden crack that marked the road in Calm and Break has almost vanished, leaving only a faint amber smear. The pylons' cyan lights are off. A player would read "the scar healed" rather than "the scar remains". As in the forest view, the whole frame is also much brighter and greener than Calm, which reads as the weather clearing.
- **(c) Trainer findable:** Yes in all three states. In Break the trainer is backlit by the golden crack and stands centre on the path, so they are the most readable of the three Break frames.

### Forest heart west (forest_heart_west_calm / _break / _aftermath_calm)
- **(a) Break vs Calm:** Yes. Break is darker, the canopy is nearly black, the grade is violet and the rain is dense. **No lightning is visible.**
- **(b) Aftermath:** It is the same place, with identical composition. There is no lightning and only two or three faint rain streaks, which is lighter rain, bordering on none. Again it is brighter and greener than Calm, like a clear day. I saw no scars. The small gap at the vanishing point is still faintly lavender and grey.

## Summary of issues to address
1. **No lightning is visible in any Break frame.** Break is distinguishable from Calm only through darkness, heavier rain and a violet grade. If Break is meant to be the "lightning peak", the capture shows none: no flash, no bolt, no strike light on the trunks.
2. **Aftermath looks brighter and sunnier than Calm** in all three places, with a saturated green canopy and lighter bark. The rule is that the aftermath differs only through lighter rain, no lightning and scars. As captured, it reads as "the storm cleared", with the purple sky visible only in the road view.
3. **Scars do not visibly remain.** On the road the golden crack mostly disappears in the aftermath, and the other two places show no scars in any state.
4. **Aftermath rain is close to zero** rather than "lighter rain". There are only a few streaks per frame.
5. **The trainer is weakest in forest_break.** They are findable only by position and the small white collar.
6. **Readability of place:** K is solidly deep forest. J is deep forest with a lawn-like clearing to the right. L is a forest road whose bright end-of-tunnel sky slot hints at the wood's edge.
