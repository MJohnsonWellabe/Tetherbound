## What shipped

`terrain_playground.json`'s `rises.peaks[1..4]` were sitting at their
pre-corridor coordinates — a real, previously-flagged gap (the Burrow
Warrens peak's own `_why` names it: "those four are still sitting at their
pre-corridor coordinates... a real, separate gap"). Two live spoke
`_why`/`_comment` fields were written as present-tense fact about peaks that,
until this pass, were thousands of metres from the geography they claimed to
describe:

- `mountain_trail` (Fire spoke, Band 2): "at the foot of `rises.peaks[1]`" —
  peak was at (−165,−150); the spoke's road is at (400,1800)…(673,1824).
- `high_pass` (Ice spoke, Band 4): the blocker's own comment said the pass's
  two shoulder-peaks (`peaks[3]`/`[4]`) stood "either side of the road end"
  — true at the OLD road end (49.7,−185.4), stale since OW5D moved the road
  to (698.07,5935.25) without moving the peaks. The spoke's own top-level
  `_why` still read "NOT YET BUILT... there is no relief on this bearing
  today," which is also stale by itself (`built: true`, full blocker/sign
  already exist) — the terrain was the missing half, not the props.

Fixed both, config-only:

- `peaks[1]` → (741.2, 1829.9), radius/height unchanged (58/34). Sited one
  radius beyond `mountain_trail`'s own blocker (683.32,1825.71), along the
  road's own final bearing, so the rockslide sits on the mountain's actual
  skirt.
- `peaks[3]`/`peaks[4]` → (726.8,5936.5) / (672.8,5921.9), radius/height
  unchanged (24/21, 24/19). Translated by the exact delta from the spoke's
  documented OLD road-end (49.7,−185.4) to its current blocker
  (698.07,5935.25), preserving the already-tuned saddle geometry (each
  ~28m from the blocker along its axis, ~56m apart) instead of re-deriving
  it.

Clearance checked computationally against every spine point (all three
bands), the full river course, and every named site (Old Quarry, Burrow
Warrens, Tether Relay, all three regional captains, Old Mill Crossing,
Stronghold) — nearest approach 207m, all clear by 150m+. `_rise_cone`/
`_rise_relief` (`playground_heightfield.gd`) are additive and exactly zero
outside a peak's own `radius`, so a move this size cannot disturb terrain
anywhere else (verified by reading the function, not assumed).

Updated the `_why`/`_comment` text on both spokes and on `rises` itself so
the documentation matches the geometry again, and `docs/
MEADOWS_MACRO_LAYOUT.md` §10.2's own table row for `rises.peaks[1..4]`.

## Deliberately not touched — `peaks[2]`

`peaks[2]` (60,175) is `blighted_road`'s (Dark spoke, Band 3) landform.
Left alone. Its spoke's own `_comment_sf33` describes an already-tuned
composition: two dead pylons are deliberately hidden behind the Team
Tether wall because "peaks[2]'s outer skirt falls away faster than their
tops rise," a payoff for a player who climbs the peak's west skirt and
looks over. That composition was tuned pre-corridor (it still checks pylon
radii against "world_perimeter.gd's 232m minimum," the retired ring) and
is stale in the same way the other three were — but re-siting a peak whose
skirt shape a hide/reveal relationship depends on, with no way to render
and check the result, risks trading one landmark bug for a worse one.
**Flagged, not fixed.** A session with a working Godot binary should re-site
it the same way and confirm the pylon hide/reveal still works before
shipping it.

## Method note

Every siting decision above was derived from data already live in the
repo — spoke `_why` text, blocker centres, spine polylines — the same way
the stronghold siting bug was ruled "from configs, no render." Where the
repo's own comments already gave an exact prior relationship (the high
pass shoulders), the fix preserves it by translation rather than
re-inventing it. Where no such anchor existed (`peaks[2]`), the peak was
left alone rather than guessed at.
