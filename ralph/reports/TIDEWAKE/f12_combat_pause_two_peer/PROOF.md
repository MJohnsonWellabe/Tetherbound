A draining view can therefore trail the snapshot just received by one or more revisions: the two processes are not frame-locked, especially when rendered (#44: `applied_lag_revisions` 1). The HUMAN-phase steps assert `applied_mode_matches_received`. The paused steps assert the exact `applied_matches_received` (same revision, same mode, stamina and drowning), because no revisions advance while paused.

Whether Water wilds should intercept swimmers is an open design question, and it is left unchanged here.
