BRINE TERRACE "fell below terrain" -- root cause (CI batch 23, run 36252654368)

SUMMARY
The player never left the ground. The terrain SAMPLER was wrong, not the body
or the collision. Terrain3D 1.0.2 `Terrain3DData::get_height` has a
near-vertex shortcut: within 0.01 m of a vertex it returns
`get_pixel(TYPE_HEIGHT, pos)` for the UNROUNDED position, and get_pixel floors.
A query up to 1 cm BELOW a vertex in x and/or z therefore returns the height of
the vertex one step down/left. On the 27-degree Brine terrace that is 0.4-0.7 m
too HIGH, so `water_world.ground_height_at()` reported the ground above a body
that was standing on the real collision surface, and the smoke's
BELOW_TERRAIN_M (0.6) check fired. It is intermittent because it needs a
per-frame body position to land inside that 1 cm window just below a grid
vertex on steep ground; frame timing decides whether any walk frame does.

Discriminated:
 (a) collision missing/late -- NO: a ray at the fall point hits
     /root/WaterArchipelago/Terrain; the collision matches the data vertex
     heights to 0.00 m on a 0.25/0.5 m grid at all three sites, unchanged
     across +0/+60/+600 physics frames after load.
 (b) tunnelling / region seam -- NO: body y equals the collision height at
     the fall point, vertical velocity 0.0 (walking on floor). The repro fall
     was at x=548, 36 m from the x=512 seam.
 (c) placement artefact -- NO: failures occur hundreds of metres into the
     loop (repro: waypoint 12, 618 m walked), long after placement.
 (d) sampler vs collision -- YES, see below.

FIX (product, Water-owned: scripts/world/water_world.gd)
ground_height_at() snaps a query within NEAR_VERTEX_M (0.02 m, covers the
library's 0.01 m window) onto the vertex itself before calling get_height, so
the shortcut reads the vertex it meant. Every gameplay caller of Water's
sampler (creature bodies, combat, riding, ramps, docks) gets the same fix.
No threshold changed. The smoke diagnostic `_collision_under()` is kept.
