## What improves, and what prevents final material acceptance

Candidate 2 is a meaningful improvement over this baseline. The repeated large polygon/cell patches no longer dominate every exposed face. The gray/cool rock family fits Tidewake's coastal palette better, and its darker values help grass, pale boulders, timber and the trainer separate. There is no visible new cliff silhouette displacement, detached edge or broad lighting/color change outside the cliff surfaces in these matched views. Trainer lower-body and route visibility are preserved in the useful stands.

**The replacement still reads predominantly as uniform fine-grained mottling spread over very large surfaces.** Shellwatch 007–008 and Deep Watch 013–015 are decisive: they gain surface detail but not enough organized fracture, weathering or material-scale variation to feel like finished coastal rock. Fine contrast competes with the already busy grass rather than establishing clear large, medium and small rock features. This is a material limitation as well as a geometry limitation; simply retaining the smooth terrain and adding more grain would not close it.

At distance, broad fairly even horizontal dark bands are visible across cliff faces, especially the opposite island in 004 and 013–015. They provide some variation but can read as painted stripes rather than convincing rock layers. Up close, the much finer cliff finish also makes the adjacent pale, simply shaded boulder cluster at First Shore feel less closely matched. This is a coherence concern, not a reason to restore the baseline cell texture.

The principal regression is **reduced information on fully shaded rock**, most clearly Shellwatch 009 and, more mildly, the far island in Brine Steps 006. It does not make the pictured trainer or passage unreadable. The lit blue cliff in Deep Watch 015 stays readable but is quite granular. Stills cannot establish shimmer, filtering stability or movement behavior.

## Remaining whole-frame failures, separate from the material decision

1. **Cliff form and landform identity:** smooth continuous banks, long exposed strips and similarly shaped cut passages remain. Deep Watch 013–015 and Brine Steps 004–006 need convincing large rock breaks, ledges and authored profiles; a surface replacement cannot supply the reference's structural hierarchy.
2. **Ground and shoreline integration:** sharp grass lips, triangular material joins, uniform grass coverage and the abrupt cliff/sand contact persist. Brine Steps retains the repetitive foam outline, very simple circular sandbar and sparsely dressed pier approach. Those are unchanged scene deficits, not newly introduced material defects.
3. **Scene composition and chapter identity:** broad bare terrain and weakly differentiated places remain well short of the layered, inhabited coastal reference. First Shore's timber/banner cue, clear trainer, cyan water, warm sand and distinct day/night moods remain strengths. This set contains no usable view of the actual Veilfall landmark, so it cannot reassess that hero asset.

**Bar A — identity: FAIL in the useful pictured environmental views, with an incremental material improvement.** Candidate 2 moves the rock palette and surface identity in the intended direction, but neither reference-like coastal form nor integrated shore/place composition is established.

**Bar B — finish: FAIL.** The replacement is preferable, but the material remains too uniformly granular across large simple faces, and the wider scene lacks the form hierarchy and terrain integration in the supplied gameplay comparison. Retention is not final material or chapter acceptance.
