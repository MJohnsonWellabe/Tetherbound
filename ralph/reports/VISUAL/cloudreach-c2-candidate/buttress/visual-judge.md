# Code-blind subset verdict

Reviewer `cloudreach_buttress_visual_judge` inspected the eight individual native
frames, their contact sheet, the Meadows key art, five Palworld screenshots and
Sky Aviary board. No source, conversation, prior verdict or status was provided.
This is a summary of the independent report, not full-C2 acceptance.

**Bar A: No. Bar B: Yes.** The B answer means these read as the same kind of
third-person creature adventure, not that they achieve shipping-quality parity.
The combined visual gate remains failed.

Ranked gaps:

1. Creature presentation in 09/13/32: fragmented feather treatment competes with
   body form; a distant magenta creature-like object reads as a defect; multiple
   creatures overlap and obscure the trainer/path.
2. Natural material coherence in 09/12/13/25: slab cliffs, carpet ground, flat
   leaves and discrete grass beds disagree instead of forming one landscape.
3. Inhabited height/night destinations in 25/30/31/32: limited settlement life,
   weak architectural integration with cliffs and inadequate warm practicals.

Frame findings:

- 05: strongest cozy settlement; empty lawn, sharp path edges and flat cream
  windows weaken integration. Bird merges with the house.
- 09: height and bell identity read; slab overhangs and uniform tan rock dominate.
  Bird blocks the gateway; decking repeats.
- 12: regional height reads. Flat foreground leaves, sparse lawn versus dense
  beds and broad uniform near cliff remain weak.
- 13: repeated bright vegetation beds; distant solid magenta actor and glowing
  plant compete along the route.
- 25: settlement silhouette reads, but yellow grass dominates habitation; bed
  edges and regular path look procedural.
- 30: destination reads, but pale grass floats visually above near-black ground;
  warm inhabited pockets are missing.
- 31: nearly featureless dark slope consumes the view and hides the route.
- 32: actor overlap, rectangular dark route patches, pale grass and weak distant
  landmark hierarchy.

Bounded vegetation reading: the large foreground leaves in 12/13 are subdued
sage. The whole vegetation palette is not muted: middle-distance grass remains
bright yellow-green, especially in 13. Flat leaves, fine bright grass and smooth
ground remain an incoherent material family.

Scene work: actor separation, route visibility, defect investigation, path and
grass-bed transitions, night values, settlement activity and cliff placement.
Art work: coherent foliage, cliff surfaces/forms, creature materials and
Cloudreach architecture with terraces, arches and inhabited vertical layers.
