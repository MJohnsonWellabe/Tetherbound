**B versus C:** Distinguishable by day: B’s purple shoulder areas and cream/yellow combination contrast with C’s cool white and blue. At night, distinguishable side by side, but weak at a glance: both become blue-white wolves. B’s darker shoulder patch carries most of the separation; eye colour is too small to carry variant recognition alone.

The three largest gaps from the references are:

1. **A1/A2:** Uniform neon colour destroys natural material hierarchy; the references retain meaningful dark facial anchors and controlled colour blocks. Material correction is required.
2. **B1/B2/C1/C2:** Coarse, jagged chest layers and abrupt shoulder boundaries read as assembled plates. The Cloudfang board conveys flowing, directional fur; Palworld uses cleaner sculpted transitions. This needs authored surface/mesh refinement, beyond scene lighting.
3. **All six:** Large bare ground areas and conspicuous oversized grass blades weaken the creature’s authored setting relative to both reference families. Terrain materials, vegetation scale and composition are scene-level repairs.
