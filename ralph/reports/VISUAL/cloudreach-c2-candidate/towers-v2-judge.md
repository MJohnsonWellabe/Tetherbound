# Cloudreach towers v2 — blind visual review

Reviewed only the Meadows key art, all five Palworld reference screenshots, the Cloudreach Sky Aviary board, all three supplied contact sheets, and all four native 1920×1080 frames. No source, status, provenance, or other verdicts were consulted. Frame prefixes below identify the full filenames in `visual-acceptance/shots/cloudreach-c2-towers-v2/`.

## Creature and character assessment

The blue bird has a readable eagle-like face, large wings, and a distinct blue/cream silhouette. In **29_summit_final_stronghold_detail-aviary_day.png**, it visibly stands taller than the 1.80 m trainer despite being farther from the camera: the companion size requirement is supported for this creature. Its crouched, turned pose conveys some personality. The trainer's backpack, hair, blue shirt, and boots remain legible.

The cast does not yet have the consistent, finished surface language of the Palworld examples. In **33_summit_final_stronghold_finale400_day.png**, the bird's dense teal/black/cream feather markings break its torso into many competing patches; the white wolf ahead loses most facial and body modeling in its nearly white surface. The trainer, smooth angular tree foliage, mottled bird, and pale spiky wolf read as different degrees and kinds of stylization. Palworld's creatures use stronger broad color masses and readable shaded volumes. The wolf is too distant for a confident facial-expression or exact height judgment. These stills do not establish companion scale for every inhabitant.

## Ranked concrete defects

1. **The long and near approaches read as a sparse constructed corridor, not a natural high sanctuary.** In **33**, enormous bare tan rock slabs have abrupt planar corners and almost uniform surface treatment. The narrow grass shelf meets them sharply. In **34_summit_final_stronghold_finale100_day.png** and **35_summit_final_stronghold_postfinale_day.png**, two nearly continuous rock walls occupy most of the upper frame. Broad flat grass surfaces, a straight ochre track, and isolated grass strips fill the lower frame. The strips have conspicuously straight edges and repeated elongated shapes. The reference landscapes connect exposed rock, varied plant cover, trees, paths, and distant layers into a continuous place. Break the corridor into authored rock outcrops, rooted vegetation clusters, irregular path shoulders, and overlooks. The right edge of **33** does reveal clouds below, but this is a small peripheral cue rather than the organizing composition.

2. **The Aviary silhouette is identifiable, but its supporting architecture and relationship to the cliffs are not convincingly presented.** Gold dome ribs are visible in all four frames. **34/35** show slender stone towers with slate-colored pointed roofs and open upper arches, matching useful board vocabulary. However, their tall narrow shafts read as isolated posts against the dome, and the large central shaft overlaps the dome center. The rock corridor hides nearly all of the lower building. **29** offers an open entrance and substantial stonework, but crops the tower tops and dome crown; its large exposed gold grid dominates the facade. The board's sanctuary has a clear hierarchy of dome, roofed towers, inhabited arcades, terraces, and cliff foundations. No supplied view clearly shows that hierarchy together. Adjust approach sightlines and present the base, terrace edges, and supporting cliffs; refine the facade's intermediate architectural scale where necessary. These images cannot prove that unseen terraces or foundations are absent.

3. **The art family is inconsistent, especially at the inhabitant and entrance focal points.** In **29**, the dark, intricate object left of the doorway and cyan-tipped fixtures compete with the simpler warm stone arches and gold rib structure. The dark object loses much of its shape at contact-sheet size. Small barrels and crates sit in separated placements rather than forming an evident activity area. The pale winged creature behind the trainer merges with the bright opening, while the blue bird's busy markings compete with the masonry. **33** makes the discrepancy between the bird's painted feather detail, the wolf's pale low-contrast surface, and the simplified trees especially clear. Establish a common material/value hierarchy and give inhabitants readable resting or interaction spaces. Creature finish and incompatible prop design need art/material work; moving props alone will not resolve them.

## Other visible findings

- **Long approach, 33:** the dome survives at contact-sheet size as a gold cage-like landmark at the path's end. Its small size, terrain occlusion, and the foreground bird covering much of the trainer make the destination secondary. The bird/trainer overlap is a composition or companion-position problem visible in this still; it does not demonstrate a collision-system defect.
- **Near approach, 34/35:** destination identity is stronger because the dome and roofed towers occupy the skyline, but the view feels enclosed between cliffs rather than elevated above them. The trainer is readable, and the cropped left bird communicates presence while obscuring its full silhouette.
- **Inhabitation, 29/35:** multiple creatures and entrance supplies establish occupancy. They do not yet communicate the board's rich sanctuary life through layered perches, planted galleries, or visible activity spaces. In **35**, the wolves separate into two silhouettes; the otherwise similar framing does not establish a completed narrative or environmental transition.
- **Light and palette:** the four views share daylight, olive grass, tan stone, and blue sky. Directional shadows ground the trainer and bird. In **29**, a dense black lattice of shadows competes with the foreground path and dark left prop. In **34/35**, the right cliff is a large dark mass against the bright left wall. There is value range, but little subtle surface hierarchy within those masses. The oxblood entrance banners in **34/35** introduce a danger-associated color into the sanctuary image; their narrative meaning cannot be determined from these frames.
- **Artifacts and limits:** angular rock/grass junctions and conspicuously straight ground-cover boundaries are visible. No motion, popping, traversal, UI, or performance claims can be made from these stills.

## Three largest reference gaps

1. **Connected, authored landscape:** Meadows and Palworld use varied terrain and vegetation to sustain interest from foreground to destination. **33/34/35** expose large bare planes and repeated grass strips.
2. **A sanctuary visibly built into the heights:** the Aviary board unites dome, arcades, slate-roofed towers, terraces, bridges, and cloud cliffs. **29/34/35** communicate the dome and some tower vocabulary but do not show the foundation-to-sky composition.
3. **A coherent, expressive creature-centered art family:** Palworld's inhabitants retain clean color masses and modeled forms within their environment. **29/33** show a noisy bird surface, a nearly white wolf, and competing prop/material styles.

## Acceptance bars

**A — No.** The daylight palette, stone construction, trainer, and companion support the intended direction, but the bare angular approaches and disconnected architectural staging do not yet read as a finished part of the Meadows key art's coherent nature/stone/village family. Scene work can address vegetation clustering, path edges, sightlines, shadow emphasis, and prop placement. Rock forms, architectural transitions, and incompatible prop surfaces also require mesh/material art refinement.

**B — No.** The creature-adventure premise is unmistakable, but beside the supplied Palworld screenshots these frames do not yet sustain the same visual acceptance bar: environmental coverage, creature surface clarity, and consistency of finished art remain conspicuous gaps. Scene composition and density are addressable with placement and terrain work. Bringing the cast and entrance props into one convincing art family requires authored art/material work beyond scatter or lighting adjustments. The stills cannot establish which suitable assets may already exist elsewhere.

**C2 remains unclosed.** Four daytime stills support only this limited exterior/detail and approach review. They do not establish complete interior, side/rear, night, traversal, motion, or full environmental-transition acceptance.
