## Concrete comparative conclusion

B does show more broken coverage at certain ground edges, but the breakup has large, repeated right angles and high local contrast. In 009/010 it dominates the foreground and is already evident on the contact sheet. In 005/006 and 021/022 it is narrower but still reads as a grid rather than dirt, leaf litter, gravel, or worn vegetation. The light-pool changes in 017-020 are smaller, yet they inherit the same problem. I would not accept these B differences as a finished material-transition improvement.

A's comparative win does not excuse its ground: 009 has a hard angular green/brown junction; 011-016 have detached rectangular dirt strips; 021/022 have a large scalloped patch and rectangular pavement ending. These are direct scene/material tasks. The next visible repair should have irregular, land-following transitions with a consistent scale, and preserve connected routes rather than adding dense square chips.

## Three largest remaining gaps, ranked

1. **The world does not yet read as a continuous, inhabited landscape.** In both sets, 001/002 have enormous empty sky and lawn around a small destination; 011-016 resemble cleared circular pads with isolated people, a lone tree, and detached path pieces. In 003/004 a grass clump visibly floats in the left sky and the right grass rises to roof scale. The key art and Palworld references use coherent terrain, purposeful edges, grounded vegetation and subjects along a readable journey. Scene fixes: ground the floating vegetation, repair vegetation scale/placement, connect routes, shape clearings, and provide landform and clustered mid-ground interest. The available art inventory was not inspected; these frames do not prove that new assets are required for those placement repairs.

2. **Ground and structure materials expose their construction too strongly.** A/B 011-016 show one repeated lawn material; 017-020 expose large repeating cobbles and sharply bounded geometric regions; 021/022 have oversized, outlined cobbles and abrupt surface joins. B adds the grid artifact in 005/006/009/010/017-022. Palworld's path/base references maintain surface-scale consistency and irregular grass/soil transitions. Fixable through scene/material authoring: connected wear, non-grid edge shapes, consistent texture scale, localized contact/edge detail and better material blending. The highly plain cliff planes in 007/008 also need authored silhouette and material work, potentially mesh revision; tint alone will not make those forms natural.

3. **The defining creature-adventure subject and emotional staging are missing from these views.** The trainer reads, but 001-016 show mostly a lone backpacked figure; 009/010's nearby NPC has limited readable facial appeal at the gameplay view; 011-016's person is small and largely dark. Palworld's creature and base frames contain recognizable expressive creatures that organize the scene, and the key art repeatedly stages trainer/companion relationships. These images provide no equivalent. Scene fixes: include and stage the actual companion/ambient creature subjects and their activity, then judge faces, silhouettes, scale and grounding in comparable gameplay frames. Asset-dependent work cannot be diagnosed responsibly until those bodies are visible; their absence from this package is not proof they are absent from the build.
