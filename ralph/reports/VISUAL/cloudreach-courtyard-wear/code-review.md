## Blockers

1. **Voss clearing blends dry grass over a wet-grass shoulder.** `scripts/world/cloudreach_battle_yards.gd:23-25` builds every `RootedClearingShoulder` and initial clearing cylinder with `materials.upland`; line 41 also uses upland for its entry. `scripts/world/cloudreach_environment_materials.gd:80-83` selects dry turf by `centre.y >= 700.0`. Voss's configured road is `[300,1080,5100]` (`data/config/cloudreach_scene_runtime.json`), whereas Ila and Senn are below 700. World material construction (`cloudreach_world.gd:803-804`) makes upland wet and upland_dry dry. The candidate now actually consumes those previously unused dry parameters: verge texture and #a2ad78 instead of meadow texture and #8ca867. Thus its non-soil fragments cannot match the real wet crown below. The new capture covers only Ila, so cannot validate this material boundary. Resolve by explicit substrate selection or limit the candidate to matching consumers; then include Voss's edge in the comparison.

2. **Brazier ash uses this shader over paving, not grass.** `scripts/world/cloudreach_summit_presentation.gd:418-421` assigns worn_ground to `BrazierAsh`, a radius 1.5 m cylinder, with shader radius 2.4 m. Its center is 0.20 m above the arena origin and its top is 0.215 m, over the paving courts. The candidate replaces discarded fragments with dry turf, including across these small ash discs. The shader's fixed 3 m inner feather is wider than their radius; even the center is partly turf. At angle zero, edge=1.776, giving about 16% turf at the center and 60% turf at the 1.5 m rim. There is no cobble background blend, so the patch is not a faithful continuous version of the previous ash coverage. This needs a substrate-specific treatment or preserved ash behavior, plus an actual brazier view. This is a source/material mismatch finding, not a claim that a visual reviewer has rejected its appearance.

## Geometry and coverage limitations

- Settlement crowns use the same altitude threshold (`cloudreach_world.gd:3461-3467`, `3625`) and same turf parameter factory as wear. Their inner crown is a flat y=0.11 surface. Communal wear y=0.17, doorstep y=0.18, activity wear y=0.19 and maintenance apron y=0.16 are also horizontal. The turf function samples xz only, so these small height offsets do not shift texture coordinates. Generated upward normals and roughness 0.98 match the flat crown case. No settlement parameter mismatch was found. The offset geometry can still produce distinct shadows/occlusion, and patches reaching the sloped outer crown do not inherit its normals or rock transition. Native captures remain necessary.
- The added summit edge row does not exercise `ArenaEdgeWear` as advertised. With radius=39.5, the shader discards everything beyond at most `39.5*(0.70+0.065+0.04)+1.8 = 33.5975 m`. The arena has radius 36 m and outer court radius 35 m. ArenaEdgeWear top is y=0.165; the outer court top is y=0.175 (center 0.155 plus half its 0.04 height), covering the entire surviving wear mask. This is pre-existing ineffective skirt geometry/masking, not a new shader regression. A skirt-edge capture therefore cannot certify the changed shader at the summit. The proposed stand x=136 is exactly on the 36 m east arena rim, looking inward; it is not a targeted brazier-ash or aviary-floor witness.
- `cloudreach_world.gd:4353` also assigns worn_ground to the aviary floor. `cloudreach_aviary.gd:720-758` reuses it for both the flat floor and low-poly sphere litter heaps. Dry substrate selection matches the upper summit crown, but heap normals/heights differ from it. The settlement capture subclass overrides the architecture rows rather than appending them, so its current list does not include the existing aviary views. These consumers remain unvalidated.
- The change removes square coverage everywhere this shared shader is used. It also adds two grass texture samples to surviving fragments and causes more fragments to survive; no performance measurement was made.
