# Cloudreach C2 art: code-blind verdict

**Inputs:** 37 day/matrix frames (`day/`), 37 night frames (`night/`) and 12 High Perches flight frames (`f083/`). All are Compatibility "Low" at 1280x720. I compared them with the Sky Aviary board, the Cliffs roster board, Palworld 02/04 and the Meadows key art. I read no source, reports or other verdicts. I did not open `f083/VERDICT.md`.

**Scope caveat:** every frame is Low preset. The bar says to judge visual rows on High and Medium, with Low as a readability control. So these frames can show Low readability and fail Bars A/B, but they cannot pass Bars A/B.

## Camera blocked or unreadable
- **day/18 detail-shrine (and night/18):** about 90% of the frame is a flat rock wall at point-blank range, with a staircase-pixelated shadow edge at top left. No shrine is visible. This shot fails as a "detail".
- **day/09 detail-ropebridge (and night/09):** the companion's back fills the lower 70% of the frame. The bridge deck, rails and landing are hidden. Only the rope lines and the bell gate read.
- **day/29 detail-aviary (and night/29):** a large creature (the armoured beast) fills the left 60% from about 1 m away. The Aviary is cut off at the top of the frame, so the dome crown, towers and entrance are lost.
- **day/16 route, day/20 perch-vista:** a cobble ring and a gold arc cut across the bottom 15% of the frame. The camera is sitting just outside a ring prop.
- **day/16:** a quest banner overlaps the tower, and its text is clipped by a post: "…r Windward — Lv 40 Master… Follow the …ide path". The left edge of the text runs off-screen.
- **day/14 detail-beacon:** no beacon is visible. The frame shows an open grass field, Bracken and two stone-wall stubs cut by the frame edge.
- **day/34 finale100 vs day/35 postfinale:** the two frames are near duplicates (same camera, same composition). The post-finale state change does not read.
- **f083 crown-rim-out-day/night and arrival-lip-day/night:** a black, unlit, unreadable object intrudes at the lower-left frame edge.
- **f083 departure-lookback-day:** the trainer hangs from the flyer in front of a featureless dark cliff face. The destination crown is a thin strip at the top, and the cliff takes 70% of the frame.
- **f083 crown-rim-out-day:** world-space name or prompt labels above the distant NPC are tiny and illegible at 720p. The same happens in departure-lookback (right edge) and day/19.

## Floating or placeholder objects
- **f083 crown-rim-out-day/night:** a yellow blob (a pickup or creature) hangs in mid-air over the cloud sea, past the cliff edge, with no support.
- **f083 arrival-far-day, arrival-lip-day:** white "cloud-puff" sprite clusters lie flat on the lower island tops and stone shelves. They read as decals or snow patches, not clouds.
- **f083 arrival-far, arrival-lip, rim-out, lookback:** the neighbouring perch top is a flat, untextured, saturated orange disc on a perfect cylinder. This is the same class of defect as a flat magenta slab.
- **f083 arrival-lip, arrival-far, crown-court:** the perch towers are perfect extruded cylinders with stacked plank discs, so they read as greybox. A black, untextured curved pipe or rail lies at the lip.
- **f083 arrival-landed:** a loose plank lies flat on the grass, and green gem pickups float slightly above the ground.
- **day/15 approach:** a black, untextured cylinder (a cannon or log) lies diagonally at the foot of the trees. It reads as a missing material.
- **day/24 detail-observatory:** bright green gem pickups clip through the trainer's legs. The armillary's "glass" arc is a flat white band.
- **day/12 route:** flat 2D cut-out grass cards in the right foreground show as paper planes.
- **day/05 settlement:** two long poles or ropes cross the frame diagonally from top to bottom, with no visible anchor.
- **night/34:** a flat green triangular ground polygon sticks out of the right ravine wall.

## Silhouette and strata
- **day/26, day/37, day/23, day/12:** the cliff walls are one continuous vertically smeared mass with soft vertical striations. There are no horizontal bedding planes, no ledge stacks and no moss following the shelves. The board's stacked, sharply broken crags with trees on their ledges are missing.
- **day/34, day/35, day/27, night/07, night/34:** the ravine walls are two smooth planar wedges with one uniform noisy rock texture. There is no strata, overhang or vegetation, so they read as terrain sculpt, not cliff.
- **day/01, day/04:** the "cliff" behind the gate is a rounded spiral cake of grass bands, a soft blob silhouette rather than a crag.
- **day/02, day/03, day/06, day/07:** the side masses are smooth brown slab blocks with grass tops. They are low-poly and identical in material.
- **day/18:** the rock texture is a brown marble-noise at close range, with no believable metre scale or bedding.
- **f083:** the perch pillars are cylinders and the islands are vertical extrusions. The board's irregular cliff islands with waterfalls are absent. The distant islands in arrival-far are the best silhouettes in the set, but they are flat-shaded and repeat.
- Grass covers every walkable top, but the rule fails in reverse: almost every frame is a broad lawn. Cloudreach reads like Meadows on a hill, not like high pale stone with restrained flowers.

## Horizon, depth and cloud sea
- **f083 crown-rim-out-day, departure-lookback-day:** above the cloud sea the horizon turns into a flat grey-green fog wall that fills 40% of the frame. It has no sky gradient and no distant islands, and it reads as a fog cap rather than air.
- **night/25, night/30, f083 night set:** the far plane is a hard, flat dark band (ocean or void) with a ruler-straight horizon. The cloud sea vanishes or becomes a dark slab.
- **day/01, day/09, f083 arrival-far:** the cloud sea is a flat, bright white plane with a hard intersection line against the cliffs. There are no height-aware banks between shelves and no haze separating stacked shelves.
- **day/02–08, 11–13, 21–23:** most route frames show no cloud sea and no drop, only grass to a near horizon. The high-altitude identity ("lower plateaus reveal inaccessible upper country") does not read.
- **No sun shafts and no aerial-perspective layering** anywhere in the set. Distant cliffs in day/26 and day/37 carry the same contrast as the midground. (This is a Low-preset limit in part, but Low still needs a value falloff.)

## Colour
- The grass is a saturated Meadows lime-green in nearly every day frame (01–08, 10–17, 21–23, 33–37). It overwhelms the "pale weathered stone, grass and restrained high-altitude flowers" palette.
- **day/26, day/37, day/12:** the stone reads as warm khaki or brown, not the authored grey-green pale stone. The board's stone is cool grey-blue with gold accents.
- **day/18:** the rock is marbled tan-brown, drifting towards the brown the bar warns against.
- **f083 lookback-day, crown-court:** the shadowed cliff faces are near-black olive. Warm/cool separation is muddy rather than "crisp warm stone/cool sky".
- **Oxblood check, day/37 and day/36:** the Galefoot watchtower carries dark-red or maroon banners. Oxblood is reserved for Team Tether, so this needs a palette check unless the banners are a deliberate Team Tether marker.
- Sunset or golden-hour frames are missing from the matrix (morning, noon, golden hour, weather). The pink-gold rim cannot be judged.

## Settlements
- **day/05 Galefoot:** the timber-and-stone cottages are coherent and appealing. This is the strongest set piece in the matrix. The square, however, is a flat dirt disc with a ring decal.
- **day/25 Cliffhold:** four houses and a watchtower sit on a bare lawn tabletop with no terracing, retaining walls or cliff-hugging construction. They are not "height-aware". They read as Meadows houses placed on a flat hill.
- **day/37 Galefoot approach:** the settlement reads as a cluster in front of the cliff wall, with bunting and tents. This frame is good, but it is again flat-ground siting.
- **night/25 Cliffhold:** no window is lit. The houses go dark and the settlement disappears at night.
- **night/05, night/36:** Galefoot windows are lit warm. This is good and consistent.

## Aviary
- **day/34, day/35:** the dome reads at distance, with gold ribs, a central tower and two blue-capped turrets. It sits at the end of a bland grass trench, and the trench walls hide the base, bridges, cliffs and waterfalls that make the board's silhouette. It is not presented as a hero landmark on a cliff.
- **day/33 finale400:** the dome is a tiny far glint. There is no Aviary skyline read in any wide shot from a lower plateau.
- **day/29:** the stonework is a dark brown mixed-cobble wall (not the board's pale cream stone), with one plain arch and no banners, statues or arch windows. The Aviary glass shows only ribs, with no glass tint or reflection. The court is bare grass and barrels.
- **night/29, night/34:** the dome is not lit. The board's night view, a warm-gold glowing dome and windows against dark blue, is the single most recognisable night cue, and it is absent. Only a dim lamp shows at the gate in night/34.
- No interior Great Aviary frame exists in the matrix.

## Night and route cues
- Night across the set is a blue-tinted day with a moon disc. Ground shadows stay sharp and directional (night/26, night/07, night/34), so it reads as moonlit noon, not dark-blue night.
- **night/07, night/27, night/34:** the route has no lanterns, cairns or lit markers. The dirt path is the only cue, and it nearly merges with the dark grass.
- **night/15–20 (High Roost):** the only lights are a weak torch post and the purple creature effect. The landing ring decal disappears.
- **night/25, night/29:** no contained warm windows. The bar names them explicitly.
- **day:** the route cue is a single dirt ribbon on grass, so wayfinding reads. Day frames lack guiding landmarks at the end of most routes (day/02, 06, 11, 21), apart from the bell gate in day/10 and the dome in day/34.
- **f083 night set:** perch landings carry no lit cue at all. A landing beacon or windsock is needed to read the Fly target at night.

## Scale
- The trainer (1.80 m) is correctly smaller than Bracken, Ram and Cloudfang.
- **f083 lookback-day, arrival-far, arrival-lip:** the carrying flyer's body is roughly trainer-sized, with a wingspan of about 1.5x the trainer's height. It reads as smaller than the human it carries. That breaks "creatures taller than the 1.80 m trainer" and is not plausible as a carrier.
- **day/34, day/35:** the cliff walls tower, but the Aviary at the end looks small (dome height about 10 trainer heights at distance). Its "stronghold" mass does not read against the cliffs.
- **day/26, day/37:** with no ledges, trees or people on the cliff faces, their scale is unknowable.
- **day/19:** the perch pillars are about 6–8 m with no railings at the plateau edge, against the bar's "rails and landing platforms".

## Three biggest gaps from the references
1. **Cliff identity and the cloud sea.** The board shows pale, stacked, broken crags with trees, waterfalls and cloud banks between shelves. The build shows smooth khaki slab walls, cylinder perches and one flat white cloud plane or a grey fog wall. Most frames read as Meadows lawns on a hill.
2. **The Aviary as hero landmark.** The board shows a pale stone fortress-dome on cliffs with bridges, banners and a glowing night view. In the build, the dome is seen only down a grass trench, the stone is dark brown, it is unlit at night and no wide shot shows its skyline.
3. **Light and atmosphere (Bar B).** There is no haze layering between shelves, no sun shafts, no time-of-day grade (the night is a blue-tinted day with hard shadows), no warm contained night lights at Cliffhold or the Aviary, and no golden-hour or weather frames in the matrix.

## Scene-fixable vs needs new art
**Scene-fixable (cameras, dressing, materials, lighting, config):**
- Re-aim the detail shots day/09, 14, 18 and 29, plus the f083 lookback, so their subject reads and the companion is not in the way. Move the camera off the ring props in day/16 and day/20. Make day/35 differ from day/34.
- Remove or fix: the floating yellow blob; the cloud-puff decals on islands; the orange perch disc; the black cylinder in day/15 and the black pipe in f083; loose planks; floating and clipping pickups; the triangle polygon in night/34; the flat grass cards in day/12; the diagonal poles in day/05.
- Cliff material: switch to the cool grey-green pale-stone palette with horizontal course/strata and shelf-following moss (the F26 cliff-course candidate). Dress the cliff faces with ledge trees and stones.
- Cut lime grass saturation, thin the grass on high shelves, and add restrained high-altitude flowers and exposed stone.
- Atmosphere: height fog banks between shelves, replace the grey fog wall with a sky gradient and distant islands, and a proper night grade (lower key light, softer shadows). Light Cliffhold windows. Glow the Aviary dome and windows at night. Add lanterns, cairns or beacons on night routes and Fly landings.
- Aviary presentation: open the trench or add a lower-plateau vista camera, recolour the stone towards pale cream, and add banners and tinted glass from installed families.
- Height-aware Cliffhold: terraces, retaining walls and rails from installed kit pieces. Add rails at perch edges.
- Capture the missing golden-hour, weather, fight and High/Medium frames.

**Needs new art (meshes, which are Codex/Meshy scope):**
- Cliff and island hero meshes with real stacked strata, overhangs and waterfall ledges to replace the extruded cylinders and slab walls.
- Aviary exterior massing at board fidelity: towers, bridge arches, statues, arch windows, a pale stone kit, and an interior Great Aviary.
- Perch tower meshes that are not primitive cylinders, plus landing platforms.
- The flyer mount, rescaled or remodelled so it is visibly larger than the trainer (a scale fix grows the creature).
- Moored-island anchors and ruined skyroad pieces. Neither appears anywhere in the matrix.

## Answers
- **Bar A: NO.** The creatures and the Galefoot village read as coherent Tetherbound appeal. The world falls well short of the Cloudreach board and Palworld plateau intent: the cliffs are generic, the Aviary underperforms and placeholder geometry is visible.
- **Bar B: NO.** There is no Valheim-class atmosphere, no haze layering, no shafts and no night grade, and the matrix is Low only, with no High or Medium frames.
- **Overall C2 art verdict: FAIL.** The high-perch footage has blocked or unreadable frames and placeholder or floating objects. The cliff silhouettes lack strata. Cliffhold is not height-aware and goes dark at night. The night route cues and the Aviary night glow are missing.
