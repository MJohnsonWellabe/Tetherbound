# F08#4 code-blind visual verdict: Cloudreach settlements and cliff identity (Compatibility "Low" preset)

Inputs used: the day and night frame folders, criteria.md and the four reference images. I read no source code, reports or earlier verdicts. Scale ruler: the 1.80 m trainer.

## Settlement rows

- **Day 05, Galefoot (before the finale).** This is a flat, grass-floored village square. Two cottages frame the shot and a third cottage, tents and a ring sit behind. Nothing in the frame shows that the village stands on a cliff: no ledge edge, no drop, no cloud sea and no upper country above. It could be the Meadows village. The trainer is clear and well framed. Also, the cottage on the right has its roof right at the frame edge and the companion is jammed against that wall, which looks like the two models overlap.
- **Day 36, Galefoot (after the finale).** The camera, lighting and layout are identical to 05. The only change is a line of blue and gold pennant bunting across the sky gap and a slightly different cloud pattern. It is distinguishable when the two frames sit side by side, but it is a small decal-level change: no new lights, crowds, repaired wind-road element or creature arrivals. It has the same lack of height cues as 05.
- **Day 25, Cliffhold.** This is the strongest settlement for height. The village sits on a raised shelf, with a slope dropping away on the right edge and a stone watchtower. However, past the shelf the horizon is a flat white haze band with no cloud banks below, and the path ends in a featureless grass bevel. The companion sits directly behind the trainer and visually fuses with their silhouette, but it does not cover them.
- **Day 37, Galefoot approach.** This is the best height-aware settlement frame. A pale fluted cliff wall and stacked ledges tower behind the village, with a tower, bunting and a campfire. The village is still on a flat lawn and no drop or cloud sea is visible. **Bunting is already up here.** If 37 is a pre-finale capture, then the post-finale difference in 36 is not exclusive to post-finale; this needs confirming.
- **Night 05.** The warm windows and string lights read well against a deep-blue sky. The horizon shows a flat dark sea-like plane, not lit cloud banks. It has the same flatness problem as the day frame.
- **Night 25.** Past the shelf, the horizon is a flat, uniform dark-navy plane that reads as an ocean or a void, not a cloud sea. The tower lantern is the only warm accent, so the settlement lights are weaker than the board's night panel.
- **Night 37.** The dark cliff mass behind the village with a moon above is a good silhouette. The campfire and windows give contained warm light. This is the best night settlement.

## Cliff rows

- **Day 01.** The gate approach has a real cloud sea with rock spires on the right and the gate on a cliff, which gives the best Cloudreach identity in the set. The gate's base crag is a smooth coiled "stacked-ring" lump, not weathered strata, and the companion screens the left third.
- **Day 02.** The canyon walls on both sides are smooth bevelled wedge blocks with uniform dirt texture and green caps. They read as extruded placeholder slabs, not exposed beds. No cloud sea is visible.
- **Day 03.** The reverse view shows low rounded mounds and dark angular blocks. This is generic hill terrain with no strata and no sense of height or cloud sea.
- **Day 04.** The gate detail shows the same coiled crag at close range: a rounded spiral with grass bands, which looks extruded and sculpted rather than layered. Cloud sea and spires show at the right edge.
- **Day 06.** The right wall is two stepped, blocky terraces with a uniform noise texture and a hard vertical slab seam. This is placeholder-grade. The companion occupies about 25% of the left edge.
- **Day 07.** The ravine is two flat planar walls, one dark and one sun-lit tan, with no ledges, beds or moss. The far pale spire is the only stacked silhouette. The companion covers about 20% of the left side.
- **Day 08.** The far horizon shows many repeated small stepped mesa blocks, so a stacked-tier read exists at distance. The silhouettes repeat in a tiled way and the horizon is flat haze, not cloud.
- **Day 09.** **Camera occlusion.** The companion's back and head fill roughly 60% of the frame and hide the trainer completely on the rope bridge. Behind it, the cliff on the right has the set's best overhanging layered ledges, and a cloud sea sits on both sides, but the cloud sea is a flat smeared white plane with no structure.
- **Day 10.** Distant mesas are stepped. The near cliff on the left is a smooth extruded drum with a texture seam. No cloud sea shows beyond the island, only a haze horizon.
- **Day 11.** Pale stacked spires on the left with a waterfall are the closest match to the board's pale strata. On the right is a field of identical faceted pyramid and box rocks that read as procedural low-poly placeholders.
- **Night 31 (day folder).** These are the same flat planar ravine walls as day 07, under a moon. The walls are featureless and the only silhouette is the distant spire.
- **Night 01.** The cloud sea and spires stay legible in blue moonlight, which works. The coiled crag is a dark blob with no shelf separation.
- **Night 06.** The blocky terrace wall and slab seam are still visible. The companion takes about 25% of the left edge but the trainer is clear.

## Summary checks

- **Settlements height-aware:** NO. Only 25 and 37 hint at height. 05 and 36 are flat villages with no ledges, drops or cloud sea, and none of the settlements is built into the cliff in a tiered or stacked way the board implies.
- **Cliffs distinct stacked strata:** NO. 09 and 11 (left), plus the distant mesas in 08 and 10, approach the bar. 02, 03, 04, 06, 07 and 31 are smooth extruded wedges, planar walls or coiled lumps with uniform texture.
- **Cloud sea:** structured and present in day 01, day 04 and night 01. It is present but flat or smeared in day 09. It is absent, replaced by a haze band, a dark ocean-like plane or no view of the drop, in day 02, 03, 05, 06, 07, 08, 10, 11, 25, 36 and 37, night 31 (day folder), and night 05, 06, 25 and 37. It reads as a void or flat plane in day 25 (white haze) and night 25 and night 05 (dark plane).
- **Camera occlusion:** day 09 is blocking, because the companion covers most of the frame and hides the trainer. These do not block but are distracting edge crops (companion takes 20–30% of the frame): day 01, 06 and 07, night 06 and 31. In day 25 and night 25 the companion stacks directly behind the trainer, which merges their silhouettes.
- **Post-finale 36 distinguishable from 05:** YES, but only through the bunting. It is weak, and it is undermined if 37 is pre-finale, because 37 already shows bunting.
- **Bar A:** NO. Creature appeal is fine, but the world misses the board's pale weathered strata, tiered cliff-built architecture and cloud-sea framing. The cliffs read as placeholder extrusions.
- **Bar B:** NO. On Low the light is plain directional sun. There is no layered haze between shelves, no cloud banks below, and the night horizons are flat. (High and Medium matrices were not provided, so Bar B cannot be cleared on Low anyway.)

## Overall F08#4 verdict: FAIL

Blocking defects:
1. **Cliff identity.** The canyon and terrace walls in 02, 06, 07 and 31 and the coiled gate crag in 01 and 04 read as extruded placeholder shapes, not related stacked strata beds.
2. **Settlements not height-aware.** Galefoot (05/36) sits on a flat lawn with no ledge, drop, cloud sea or tiered build. Cliffhold (25) has a shelf, but the frame ends in haze.
3. **Missing cloud sea.** Below the settlement shelves the cloud sea is a void or flat plane: 25 day shows white haze, 25 and 05 at night show a dark ocean-like plane. The bar requires structured cloud banks below the shelves.
4. **Camera occlusion in 09.** The companion fills most of the frame and hides the trainer on the rope bridge.
5. **Post-finale change too slight.** 36 differs from 05 only by bunting, which 37 may already show before the finale.
6. **Missing matrix.** Matched High and Medium frames were not supplied, and the bar judges on those presets.
