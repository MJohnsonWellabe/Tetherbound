# F40#4 code-blind verdict: Sky Aviary landmark (Low / Compatibility preset)

Inputs: day/ and night/ frames 24, 26, 27, 28, 29, 33, 34, 35; criteria.md; the Cloudreach Sky Aviary board; the Palworld plateau-landmark reference. I read no source code, reports or other verdicts. Distant regions were cropped and upscaled 3-4x to check what is there.

## Per-frame criticism

**24 observatory detail (~850 m), day.** I cannot see the Aviary at all. The frame is filled by the observatory's brown drum and gold armillary rings, and a flat beige cliff wall closes off the right horizon. The frame is labelled as ~850 m from the Aviary, but its sight line does not point toward it, so it adds nothing to F40#4. Separately, the white crescent band on the armillary reads as an unlit placeholder sliver.
**24, night.** Same composition. The sky is dark blue and the crescent band glows flat white. There is no Aviary and no warm window anywhere on the horizon.

**26 approach (~850 m), day.** This is the key distance view, and the Aviary does not read. When upscaled 4x, the summit crest (top centre, about x 560-620, y 80-130) shows only a few-pixel grey spike and two dark specks. At native size this is a sliver, with no dome, drum or turret silhouette. The tall flat beige cliff wall dominates the frame, and the badger companion takes the left third.
**26, night.** The same spike is now invisible against the navy sky. Nothing at the summit tells the player where the goal is.

**27 summit road route, day.** A narrow grass ramp runs between two dark walls, with a winged white creature on the crest. There is no Aviary in frame. The ramp crest and sky are where a dome top should appear as a pull, but nothing does. The badger is cut by the left edge.
**27, night.** The walls are near-black. The moon and a glowing pale-blue wolf creature are the only focal points, and there is no landmark glow beyond the crest.

**28 summit reverse, day.** This looks back down the route. Upscaled, the hazy right-hand distance holds a pale moored island topped by a small dark crenellated box, plus a thin pillar and a tower ghost. None of these is the Aviary. The badger blocks the right fifth of the frame and partly covers that distant island.
**28, night.** Same composition. A white cluster of small discs floats beside the pillar on the left of the island. It reads as floating fragments or an unlit cloud placeholder against a flat, dark sea plane.

**29 Aviary detail at gate, day.** The camera is blocked. The large armoured rock creature fills about 60% of the frame in the foreground, covering the gate, the drum and the lower dome. What shows is the glass dome's gold rib cage, two square stone posts that rise past the frame top, and a short crenellated wall with one arch. The stone mass is thin, wall-like pieces, not a massed drum, and no turrets or arched-window rows are visible. Shadows from the dome ribs on the cobbles are the best lighting in the set.
**29, night.** Same blocking. The dome glass is cool and transparent with no warm interior light. The stonework is lit evenly, like a daytime texture darkened, with no contained window glow.

**33 finale road (~400 m), day.** The Aviary is identifiable here. Upscaled, a gold-ribbed dome with a central spire sits in the notch at the top of the road. At native size it is only about 40 px wide and sits low behind a ridge, the two fox creatures on the path stand directly beneath it and compete with it, and no stone drum can be resolved. It reads as a glasshouse dome rather than a domed stone sanctuary.
**33, night.** The white wolf creature stands right in front of the dome and covers its lower half. One small white light shows on the spire, and the dome is grey glass with no warm glow, so it is barely legible.

**34 (~100 m), day.** Best view of the set. Framed by the canyon walls, a full glass dome with gold ribs, two blue-capped turrets, a gate wall with banners and a door all read clearly. Problems: (a) a square stone tower pierces straight up through the centre of the dome and out the top. This contradicts the board, where a lantern sits on a dome supported by the drum, and it looks like an intersection error. (b) The "drum" is a low, thin gate wall in front of the dome rather than a massed stone ring with arched windows. The glass hemisphere looks like it sits nearly on the ground. (c) The turrets are thin and short compared with the board's sturdy towers. The dome reads clearly against the sky, but the base where it meets the cloud sea is not visible from this framing.
**34, night.** Contained warm light amounts to one small amber lamp at the gate. The turret and tower windows are small, flat white-grey squares, not warm. The dome is cool blue glass with no interior glow. A blue dragonfly creature stands on the path in front of the gate.

**35 post-finale, day.** Effectively identical to 34. It inherits the same tower-through-dome intersection, the thin drum and the weak turrets. No change after the finale is visible (no banners restored, no light change).
**35, night.** Identical to 34 night, with two dragonflies now in front of the gate. Still no warm windows.

## Questions

- **DISTANCE 850 m: NO.** At most a few-pixel spike shows at the summit in 26, which is a sliver and not identifiable. In 24 and 28 the Aviary is out of frame or not present.
- **DISTANCE 400 m: NO (marginal).** In 33 day a dome is identifiable on close inspection, but at about 40 px, low behind a ridge and competing with creatures, it does not read as the hero landmark. At night it is covered by a creature and unlit.
- **CLOSE: NO.** In 34/35 the glass dome and turrets read, but there is no massed stone drum or arched-window row, and a tower intersects the dome. In 29 the camera is blocked by a creature.
- **NIGHT: NO.** There are no contained warm windows. Window panes are flat white, the dome is cool and dark, and the only warm source is one gate lamp.
- **Distinct from the cloud sea:** not demonstrated. No frame shows the Aviary against the cloud sea. Where the dome is visible, it is against sky or canyon walls.
- **Bar A: NO.** The landmark shows only part of the board's intent (dome plus turrets). It lacks the stone drum mass, the tower ring, the arcade windows and the cliff-top setting. The flat beige cliff walls and the plain canyon corridor do not match the board's stacked, weathered strata.
- **Bar B: NO.** There are no shafts through the dome, no haze layering between the shelves, no warm/cool night separation and no contained window glow. The night grade is uniformly darkened.

## Overall F40#4 verdict (Low preset only): **FAIL**

Blocking defects, in priority order:
1. **No distance read at ~850 m.** The Aviary is a sliver at the summit in 26 and absent from 24/28. It needs a silhouette scale, placement or sight line that reads from the approach. A lit lantern or spire at night would also help.
2. **Tower intersects the dome** (34/35, also visible in 33): a square stone tower pokes straight up through the centre of the glass dome.
3. **No massed stone drum with arched windows.** The dome sits behind a thin gate wall. The board's heavy stone ring of arcades, with the dome sitting on it and sturdier turrets, is missing.
4. **Night has no contained warm windows.** The windows are flat white quads and the dome has no interior glow. Only one gate lamp is warm.
5. **Camera or creature blocking.** In 29 day/night a foreground creature covers about 60% of the gate-detail frame. In 33 night the wolf covers the dome at 400 m. In 34/35 night dragonflies stand at the gate. Companions are parked at the left edge in nearly every frame (badger in 24/26/27/28/33/34/35).
6. **Not shown against the cloud sea.** No frame demonstrates the dome as distinct from the cloud layer.
7. **Floating or placeholder elements near the landmark route.** There is a flat white crescent sliver on the observatory armillary (24) and floating white disc fragments by the pillar in 28 night.
