## Remaining priorities

1. **Complete criterion coverage before a full acceptance claim.** Obtain current-build continuous evidence showing the phase cycle and normal/reduced flash behavior at real play speed, including a strike while moving through vegetation. This is an evidence gap, not a finding that unseen phases fail. Independently verify the specified time and radius.
2. **Strengthen the warning's electrical cue if first-encounter lightning identity is required.** Preserve the filled area, stable circular extent and perimeter countdown, which already work. At the production outside angle, the trainer's head hides the centre; a small centre-only lightning glyph would be unreliable. A concrete candidate is a few clearly forked electrical paths across the middle-radius ground on both sides of the trainer, replacing some generic diagonal hatching. Their characteristic zigzags need enough width and contrast to survive the shallow outside view. Keep the fill darker than those paths and leave the outer boundary continuous. The charge shape should visibly intensify toward impact in both modes without becoming a premature pillar or an additional full-area white flash. Judge whether it reads as electrical charge rather than a decorative rune or cracked floor. This is weaker semantics, not a reason to discard the functional area warning.
3. **Improve the bolt's finish after readability is secured.** The broad flat ribbon is the clearest visible quality limitation, especially `inside_normal_071` and `brush_inside_normal_071`. More convincing taper, irregularity and luminous branching could improve it without increasing whole-scene flash or obscuring the boundary.
4. **Retain the current foliage gain while testing movement.** The photographed central obstruction is resolved. Verify the boundary and player remain readable as the camera enters/leaves foliage; the surviving lower-left fern mask is a local limitation to watch, not a renewed whole-frame failure.

No full-region Bar A/Bar B or earned gameplay acceptance is claimed. The defensible result is readable sampled attack presentation, including the fresh brush stand, with remaining semantic/finish weaknesses and explicitly incomplete phase, temporal and moving-camera coverage.
