**REJECT this experiment as an improvement. Prefer the current baseline overall.** The candidate retains the boundary and countdown, but its four detached pale shapes read more like repeated rune/arrow-like marks than impending electrical charge. Darkening the interior also weakens the immediate filled-hazard read. This does not justify retaining the candidate on the proposed recognition criterion.

## What survives and what regresses

**Boundary and urgency survive.** The outer circumference remains clear and continuous apart from ordinary vegetation overlaps. The inner circle contracts between `006/036/066`; the outer perimeter whitens toward late warning. The new shapes do not create visible gaps in the actual perimeter. There is no new full-frame glare or loss of purple storm identity, and the reduced samples retain the warning.

**Electrical semantics do not improve enough.** Strongest witnesses are `inside_normal_006`, `inside_reduced_066`, and `brush_inside_normal_036`: the pale paths have thick, rounded terminations and appear as four separated emblems. They are recognizable as angular marks, but not unambiguously as electricity. At `outside_*_006/066`, perspective further collapses their distinguishing shape. This is primarily a rune/inscription ambiguity rather than convincing cracked-earth texture.

**Filled-area clarity is weaker.** At `outside_*_006` the candidate reads primarily as a rim and interior strokes. In `inside_*_006` and `brush_inside_*_066`, the broad spaces between strokes are nearly black-purple. The rim still encloses them, so this is not a total hazard-read failure; nevertheless, the baseline more clearly says that the entire enclosed ground is threatened. The candidate's foreground glyphs also compete with the contracting circle when they intersect in `*_036`.

**The sampled charge shape barely changes.** Between early and late, the motifs remain substantially the same arrangement and visual weight. The rings carry almost all urgency. These stills cannot rule out intervening motion, but they do not demonstrate a charge buildup that would overcome the static-symbol reading.

## Bounded next-step guidance

Return to the baseline for retention purposes. If another electrical variation is explored, preserve a visibly tinted whole-area fill and evaluate the shallow outside angle first. The key issue is the silhouette of the charge: connected, tapered, irregular fork paths with sharp diminishing ends would be more promising than four detached, rounded emblems. They should remain distinguishable from the countdown rings and visibly accumulate without requiring extra scene flashes. That is a direction to test, not pre-approval of another implementation.

This is a staged, no-HUD visual comparison. It neither passes the whole region nor closes the full strike/phase criterion. Exact duration/radius, real-time reaction opportunity, phase identities, moving-camera coverage and reduced-motion flash rhythm remain outside this packet's visual proof.
