# Cloudreach Cliffs: named-place approach survey, code-blind verdict

Material judged: 19 production frames, P001 to P019 (1280x720, day 10:00 and night 23:00), against the Cloudreach Sky Aviary stronghold board, the Cloudreach creature roster board, the Meadows key art (palette only) and palworld-01 to palworld-05. Rubric: visual-judge. No scores.

**Headline:** the brief says each frame contains the player's companion creature. **None of the 19 frames shows any creature.** No companion, wild creature, herd or perched flyer appears anywhere. A creature-expedition chapter whose roster board has eleven species shows zero bodies.

## 2. Does each named place read as its landmark from the approach?

- **Realm Gate Crag (P001, P002): partly.** A ruined crenellated gate tower beside a terraced dark rock mass reads as "gate + crag". But the crag is a smooth, soft stacked blob with no strata, the gate is a thin cut-out, and the setting is a flat lawn with pale unexplained blocks.
- **Galefoot Waycamp (P003): no.** No camp elements are visible. It reads as a village lane between cliffs.
- **Three Bells Bridge (P004, P005): partly.** The three bells are the clearest single landmark signal in the set. No bridge is visible, and the bells are unlit at night.
- **Windscar Beacon (P006, P007): no.** The frame structure is too small and too thin, and has no fire or glow even at night.
- **Windscar Flight Aerie (P008, P009): no.** The subject is cropped off the top and hidden by trees. One banner and two poles do not make an aerie. There are no perches or flyers.
- **Sky Shrine (P010, P011): no.** A blank grey cylinder wall with a floating black slab. The shrine itself is not visible.
- **Cliffhold (P012, P013): partly.** Tower and cottages read as "a hamlet", not a cliff settlement. It is unlit at night.
- **Old Wind Observatory (P014, P015): partly.** The squat brown cylinder with a vane rod and crescent ring reads more as a water tank or silo than an observatory. There is a stray triangle plane beside it.
- **Summit Eyrie (P016, P017): yes, weakly.** The glass dome is unmistakable and is the only board-identity match in the set. The base, setting and night lighting do not match the board.
- **Stormward Overlook (P018, P019): no.** Two small stone blocks with banners and a ring read as a gateway. There is no overlook platform and no vista, and the view past it is haze or empty sea.
- **Broken Skyroad Arch and High Roost Perches: not judged.** No frames (stand unreachable). An unreachable approach to two "sky" landmarks is itself a finding about route composition.

## 3. Strengths worth keeping

- **The painted sky and cloud layer** (P001, P004, P012, P016) is the best element here: bright, open, readable blue with good warm/cool contrast against the grass. The night sky with the moon (P019) and streaked clouds is atmospheric.
- **The trainer** reads at a glance in every frame, day and night, with distinct colour blocks (blue top, tan pack, dark trousers). The day contact shadow grounds them.
- **The aviary's glass dome with its ribbed spire** (P016, P017) is the right silhouette and should be kept and built out.
- **The Three Bells gantry** (P004) is a strong, simple, readable landmark icon.
- **Blue/gold banners** (P008, P018) match the board's banner language.
- **Tall mixed-tone grass tufts with lilac flowers** (P004, P014) are a good "restrained high-altitude flower" accent when they cluster.
- Day and night frames agree on layout. Nothing moves or pops between the pairs.

## 4. The three biggest gaps against the references

1. **No creatures (all frames).** Every Palworld reference (01 to 05) has multiple creatures in frame: flanking the player, in the mid-ground, on the path. They are the subject. The Cloudreach roster board is eleven bespoke, high-contrast species. These frames contain a trainer alone in empty land. That fails the genre, the rubric's first priority, and §5.1.
2. **No altitude or vertical world (P004, P006, P012, P018 most clearly).** The Sky Aviary board is built from vertical stacks: sheer strata cliffs, waterfalls into a cloud sea, floating islands, and bridges spanning voids. Palworld-04 layers foreground field, mid rock cliffs and a distant tower against high masses. These frames are flat grass mesas under white haze or over a flat sea, with nothing above eye level except the landmark itself. Cloudreach reads as "Meadows on a table", not "cliffs above the clouds".
3. **Landmarks are primitives or props, not places (P010, P006, P018, P003).** The board's places are dense, layered architecture seated into rock: gates, towers, arches, bridges and statues. Palworld-04's distant spire reads through layered terrain. Here the named places are a blank cylinder, a 40 px wooden frame, two blocks with banners and three far cottages, each standing alone in undressed grass at the end of a straight road with no lure, light or approach dressing.

## 5. Bar questions

**Bar A: do these frames read as belonging to the Cloudreach world on the Sky Aviary and Cloudreach roster boards? NO.**

- What carried it: the glass dome (P016), the sky palette and the blue banners.
- What sank it: no board creatures at all, no clouds-below or waterfall/floating-island world, no pale gold-trimmed stone architecture, and a night read with none of the board's warm gold light.

**Bar B: beside the Palworld frames, would someone say these are trying to be the same kind of game? NO.**

- What carried it: a stylised third-person trainer on a dirt road through tall grass under a bright sky is the right genre posture.
- What sank it: no creature appears in any frame, open field is empty, and there is no mid-ground density or life. With no subject beside the player, these frames read as an exploration-game prototype, not a creature-adventure game.

### Fixable in-scene (existing assets, placement, material, lighting)

- Spawn and stage the companion, plus wild herds and perched flyers, on every route (defect 1). This is only in-scene if the creatures already exist in the build.
- Stop grass texturing on vertical faces. Apply the existing rock material to sheer faces (defect 5). Remove or texture the pale blocks, the plane in P002 and the triangle in P014 (defects 11, 12).
- Remove the floating slab and wall-glued tree at the Sky Shrine (defect 3) and move the stand so the shrine top is visible.
- Cluster grass and flowers into drifts and clearings instead of road-parallel strips. Add stones, bushes and ground-cover breakup to the flat tiled ground (defects 7, 8, 21).
- Curve roads, add a near framing element and put an off-route lure in each approach. Soften the pixel road mask (defects 17, 18).
- Night practicals: lit windows at Cliffhold, a fire or glow at the beacon, a warm aviary interior, lanterns at the bells and a glow at the shrine. Stop grass blades self-illuminating (defect 13).
- Replace the white horizon haze with a cloud-sea layer below plateau level, day and night, using existing painted-cloud material or cloud cards (defect 14, part of defect 2).
- Reframe the Aerie stand so the tower top is in frame, clear the screening trees and remove the wedge planes (defect 10).
- Add rope and timber bridge pieces from the installed village/prop family at the bells and settlements (defects 15, 16).
- Make the beacon larger and add fire. Dress the Overlook with a platform and rail looking over a void (defect 9, Stormward Overlook line).

### Needs new art (not in the build as seen)

- The **Cloudreach creature roster** itself (Pebbik, Craghorn, Cloudfang and the others). If the absence in defect 1 is because the models do not exist, this is the largest asset gap.
- **Stacked strata cliff meshes and vertical rock masses** with pale weathered stone, plus **waterfall** and **floating-island** assets. The current blob and smeared cliff forms (P003, P001) cannot be retextured into the board's cliffs (defects 2, 6).
- **Aviary architecture kit**: pale-stone towers with blue conical roofs, arched windows, arch bridges, eagle statues and gold rib trim, to replace the dark block base (defect 4).
- **Sky Shrine** and **Old Wind Observatory** hero structures with real silhouettes. The current cylinder and silo forms cannot carry the names (defect 3, P014).
- A **height-aware settlement module** set (terraces, stilts, cliff-anchored platforms) so Cliffhold and the Waycamp stop being Meadows cottages on a lawn (defect 15).
- Better foliage textures for the trees (defect 19). This is minor next to the items above.
