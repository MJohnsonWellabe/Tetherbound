# Code-blind visual verdict: roster + cast stage captures

Standard: ART_DIRECTION.md only (§1, §5.1, §5.2, §10, §11). References: ref/ key art, Palworld frames and roster boards.
Evidence: both contact sheets for each set, all 57 `_front` roster frames, 12 `_attack` frames, 2 `_rear` frames, 5 cast line-ups, and about 12 individual cast front, rear and face frames. The creature frames are numbered by species order (see the contact-sheet order in `roster/`).

Scale check from the manifest: every species measures taller than the 1.80 m trainer. The smallest are Pipwing 1.06×, Sparkit 1.08×, Mudsnout 1.11× and Bramblebun 1.14×. The data holds no scale violation.

## 2. Largest three gaps

1. **The enemy faction does not read.** Rank is not readable (the grunt, officer and captain ranks are one body), the uniform families contradict each other, and the palette is wrong. Oxblood is almost absent, the Warden is green, and civilians carry more red than the enemy (defects 2, 3, 4, 13, 14).
2. **Creatures come from two production families, and some have broken topology.** Meadowhart's disconnected legs and pill torso, Tempestwing's pole mount, duplicate or recoloured meshes, and melted or dotted generated albedos sit next to clean hand-painted species (defects 1, 8, 9, 10, 11).
3. **The combat and face layer is missing.** The attack frames are idle poses, and many humanoid faces are blown-out, low-resolution or hair-tinted. The five-creature relationship and the story cast lose expression exactly where the player looks (defects 5, 6, 7).

## 3. Bars

**Creatures, Bar A (key-art world): partly.** The best species (Terrapup, Pipwing, Ripplet, Frostclaw, Cloudfang, Torrentoad, Sirenseal) have the warm, chunky, big-eyed, hand-painted "pal" identity of the key-art Meadows companion and sit comfortably above the trainer, as the art direction asks. A large share of the roster breaks that world. Meadowhart is broken. Tempestwing, Voltarach and Solmane use a realist or monster register with noisy albedos. The dark Stormwood set (Nightburrow, Ashtusk, Shadelet) loses its colour blocks.

**Creatures, Bar B (polished creature-adventure register): partly.** The scale language and silhouette variety are Palworld-like and the good species would sit in a Palworld field. The idle-only "attack" frames, mismatched texture finish, recoloured duplicate meshes and the one broken mesh would not pass beside the comparison frames.

**Humans, Bar A: partly.** The trainer, Kael, Sera, Lyra, Grandpa, the Innkeeper and the Farmer match the key art's warm stylized adventurer look, and the trainer's blue, white and brown blocks are strong. The key art's Team Tether uses oxblood banners. The rendered faction is black, plum and green, so the enemy side does not belong to that world.

**Humans, Bar B: no.** The cast mixes clean hand-painted heads with blurred, posterized, blown-out faces (Captain A, Officer A, Lost Traveler, Grunt C). It repeats villager bodies with hair swaps, and it has an enemy ladder where three ranks are one model. A polished creature-adventure game would not ship its antagonist roster this undifferentiated.

## 4. Do not touch (reads fine)

- **Trainer** (cast/001–003): clear 2–3 colour blocks, clean face. Keep as the scale ruler.
- **Terrapup** (roster/001, 003): best finish in the roster and the style anchor. Its attack pose still needs an animation, but not the mesh or texture.
- **Pipwing** (roster/043): excellent face and eyes. A scale bump at most.
- **Frostclaw, Cloudfang, Torrentoad, Sirenseal, Fulgocobra** mesh and texture.
- **Burrowback** armour (the named 1.30:1 exception; keep it dark).
- **Grandpa, Innkeeper, Kael, Sera** cast models.

## 5. Capture and framing problems (not asset defects)

- The `_face` frames (for example cast/033, 048, 054, 093) put the head in the lower-centre with more than half the frame empty. The chin and shoulders sit at the bottom edge, and on the Warden frame the staff head intrudes into frame. Re-aim the face camera to head height and centre it.
- For the largest species (Abyssal Guardian, Solmane, Fulgocobra, Veridian) the camera pulls back so far that the trainer is under about 60 px tall. The ruler is too small to judge fine contact, so add a closer second framing.
- Cast line-ups: lineup_05 holds a single member, and lineup_01 shows the trainer twice (the ruler plus the "trainer" subject), which wastes a slot.
- The attack frames are captured side-on in idle, so the rig may have an attack clip the capture did not sample. Verify before treating defect 7 as an asset gap.
- No subject is cut off at the frame edge in the frames I opened.
