Stormwood ENVIRONMENT frames. Hand-checked traversal stands, production gameplay camera. Stormwood has no day/night by design (a fixed purple storm); each stand is shot in three Surge storm phases: calm, building, break. The trainer (1.80 m) stands with their companion creature beside them; the companion IS expected in frame. Wild-creature spawning is paused for capture, so absence of wild creatures is not a defect.
