Stormwood PLACE frames. Stormwood has no day/night cycle by design (a fixed purple storm); frame 'times' are Surge storm phases: calm (and break for major places). Each named landmark is seen from a road point ~90 m away (or an off-road ring stand where noted). CAPTURE FIXTURE: the player's companion is deliberately parked out of shot for place frames, and wild-creature spawning is paused, so absence of creatures is NOT a defect.
