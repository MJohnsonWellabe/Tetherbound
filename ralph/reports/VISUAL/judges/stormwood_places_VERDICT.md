# Stormwood named places: visual verdict (code-blind)

Material judged: 25 frames P001–P025 (1280x720, production camera), with the Stormwood stronghold boards A and B, the Stormwood creature roster board and the Palworld frames. Glass Sink was skipped by the capture (the trainer never reached the stand), so it has no frame and is not judged. I did not count missing creatures as a defect, because LABELS.txt says they are held out on purpose. The creatures that do appear anyway (P004, P013, P014, P016, P022, P024) are judged as art.

## 2. Per place: does it read as that landmark from its approach?

- **P001/P002 The Struck Sentinel: no.** There is no struck tree or scar. A grey arch speck sits at the vanishing point.
- **P003 Ashfoot Waycamp: partly.** A lantern post and a cobble pad suggest a stop, but there are no tents, fire or camp pieces, and the Meadows cottage on the horizon dominates.
- **P004 Verge Rod Station: no.** A tiny spire at 90 m about 40 px tall, and the ravens steal the frame.
- **P005/P006 The Lantern Pools: no.** A blue toy gate is the only feature. There are no pools and no lanterns.
- **P007 Hollows Rod Station: no.** A tiny dark spire, and the frame is interchangeable with P008 and P020.
- **P008/P009 Crown Overlook: no.** Road and trees only, with no height, edge or view.
- **P010 Rodline Post: partly.** There is a settlement of sorts (cottages and a blue box), but nothing says "rod line."
- **P011/P012 The Capacitor Grove: no.** There are no capacitors and no grove identity, only a floating cyan line and a grey slab.
- **P013 The Still Grove: partly.** A true tree arcade frames the road and the wolves give it life, but "still" is not distinguished from any other grove.
- **P014/P015 The Crown Arch: no.** A blue block about 20 px tall on the horizon.
- **P016 The Crown Heartstone: no.** From 146 m no stone is visible. The Staticub is the subject.
- **P017 Lantern Hollow: partly.** There is a destination cluster (houses and a gate) with a valley between grey slabs, but no lanterns and no hollow.
- **P018/P019 The Fallen Giant: no.** No fallen trunk is visible, and in P019 the ring hides everything.
- **P020 Deepwood Rod Station: no.** A spire speck with a few purple crystals beside it, on an open plain. It is not deepwood.
- **P021 The Old Rodfolk Hall: no.** No hall is visible, only a single blue crystal at the vanishing point.
- **P022/P023 The Glass Field: partly.** Cyan glass spikes and a dead tree are the only genuinely Stormwood-specific place language in the set. The field itself is still grass and flowers, not glass-fused ground.
- **P024 Dynamo Outer Works: partly.** The giant trunk is present as a destination mass, but a Meadows cottage and a mole crowd the frame, and there are no works (platforms, pylons, occupation).
- **P025 The Stormheart Tree: partly.** Scale reads, but it is a bare cone with a black-ring slot and blocky cyan light, with no canopy, platforms or banners, and it is not seen from an approach.

## 3. Calm vs Break: distinct and legible?

The phases are distinct but not legible as named storm phases. Break is darker exposure plus denser, longer rain streaks, a more contrasty cloud layer and brighter road cracks (P002, P006, P009, P012, P015, P019, P023). That is enough to tell "worse" from "better" side by side. It is mostly a global value shift, though, which is close to the "screen tint" the contract forbids. None of the contract's art cues appear: no lightning bolt or flash in any break frame, no white-violet strike light, no copper flicker, no visible wind response in the canopy, no steam or afterglow, and no change in moss or understory value. The one phase-specific object, P019's magenta ring, is a mechanic marker that hides the trainer. Seen alone, a break frame reads as "night," not "Break." Relevant clause: §3.3.

## 4. Strengths to keep

- **Palette base.** The fixed purple storm sky with dark trunks and green canopy is coherent across all 25 frames and reads as one place with an identity distinct from Meadows' daylight.
- **The Staticub (P016)** is close to the roster board: brown fur, purple lightning streaks, blue eyes, a readable face. It is correctly larger than the trainer and grounded. It is the best asset in the set.
- **The Tanglevolt wolves (P013, P024 distant)** have blue-streaked coats that read at distance. The herd in P024 is a good lure.
- **The Voltarach (P022)** has a readable spider silhouette with glowing blue eyes against the grass.
- **The Glass Field spikes (P022/23)** are the one Stormwood-specific material read and a good lure shape.
- **Scale agreement is broadly right.** Trunks dwarf the trainer, the ravens, bear and wolves stand above 1.80 m, and the Stormheart trunk in P024/P025 is colossal.
- **The trainer reads well in calm frames.** The blue shirt, brown pack and dark legs make three clean blocks.
- **Near ground material (P003, P010, P024).** The road has a textured gravel material with scattered pebbles and verge grass. This works up close.

## 5. Three biggest gaps vs references

1. **No landmark or destination language (P001, P008, P014, P025).** Board A/B build the region around a colossal, canopied, platform-ringed tree lit with warm lanterns and blue-white lightning, "visible across the Electric Forest," and Palworld-04 puts a tower landmark in the middle distance of an ordinary travel frame. Here 13 of 25 place frames have no visible place, and the Stormheart itself (P025) is a bare textured cone with blocky cyan rectangles and no canopy, platforms, banners or lights.
2. **The biome is Meadows grassland under a purple sky, not the Electric Forest (P007, P020, P021).** The boards show dense layered conifer and broadleaf forest, cliffs, waterfalls, mossy rock and depth in several planes. Palworld-02 and -04 show dense varied ground, rock outcrops and cliffs with real material. These frames are an even flower carpet, evenly spaced trees, a flat horizon and untextured grey slabs, with Meadows cottages scattered through.
3. **The frame's focal point is a glowing gold route line (every frame, worst in P005, P006, P016).** In both the boards and Palworld the eye goes to landmarks and creatures, and routes are worn earth. Here a saturated emissive line is the brightest, most central element of every composition, so the player is told where to go and never shown why.

## 6. Bar answers

**Bar A: do these frames belong to the world of Stormwood boards A and B (and the roster board)?** **No.**

- Carried it: the purple-storm mood, and the creatures that match the roster (Staticub, Tanglevolt, Voltarach).
- Sank it: no canopied Stormheart tree (P024/25), no warm lanterns, banners, walkways or platform detail, no forest density, cliffs or waterfalls, Meadows cottages, the blue toy gate, and the gold road lines.
- **Fixable in scene:**
  - Remove or restyle the Meadows cottages and blue toy gates.
  - Cluster trees into dense groves with clearings.
  - Replace the flat plain with landform, and texture the grey slabs.
  - Tone the road cracks down to copper wear, or remove them.
  - Add black pools, moss, fungi and roots from installed families.
  - Put lanterns and warm light on the Stormheart.
  - Choose approach stands that actually frame each landmark, including P025.
  - Add real lightning flashes and afterglow to the break phase.
- **Needs new art:**
  - The Stormheart Tree itself: a canopied split trunk with platforms, walkways, banners and crystal, per the boards.
  - Stormglass arches, rod-station and rod-line scaffold structures, capacitor banks.
  - A Stormwood building family to replace the Meadows cottages.
  - Glass-fused scar ground.
  - A redone Stormraven with the board's lightning veining.

**Bar B: beside the Palworld frames, would someone say these are trying to be the same kind of game?** **No, narrowly.**

- Carried it: the creature frames P013, P016 and P024. They show a stylised trainer next to large, expressive creatures in a grassy world, which is recognisably the same genre.
- Sank it: the other roughly 20 frames are a lone trainer walking a glowing line through an empty, repetitive plain. Palworld frames are dense, busy and full of landmarks, with creatures and structures as events. These read as a walking simulator.
- **Fixable in scene:**
  - Mid-ground density and clustering.
  - Landmark placement and stand framing.
  - Removing the route glow.
  - Creature staging inside the frame, not cropped at edges.
  - Break-phase trainer readability (rim or contact light).
  - Ground variety: rocks, outcrops, pools.
- **Needs new art:**
  - The landmark structures listed under Bar A.
  - Hero-quality Stormraven and Thundertunnel materials to match the Staticub.
  - Real lightning VFX, to make the Surge phases an event rather than a darker exposure.
