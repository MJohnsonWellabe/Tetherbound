## Scoped findings

Frame names below use the full prefix `art-shape-normal_h12_` or `art-shape-reduced_h12_`; t120 means 1.20 seconds.

- **PASS — warning footprint:** Both t000 frames show a large magenta perimeter around the trainer, distinct from the gold ground markings. It stays locatable in rain and against grass. The trainer is visibly inside it, and the open centre preserves the body silhouette. Foreground portions are near the image edge, but the lateral boundary and rear arc establish the footprint adequately in this camera.
- **PASS — qualitative countdown:** Comparing t000, t030, t060, t080, and t110 in both modes, short perimeter-facing branches progressively reach toward the centre, then form a connected pattern beneath the trainer. The perimeter also becomes more assertive near t110. This communicates increasing imminence through shape, not colour alone. The foreground branch crossing the bright gold stripe is harder to follow in normal t060–t100, but the lateral branches retain the same progress information. These captures establish an early/middle/late read, not a player's ability to estimate exact remaining milliseconds in combat.
- **PASS — impact identity and placement:** Both t120/t125/t130 show a white-violet fork entering from the upper image edge and reaching the marked centre at the trainer's feet. The white ground branches and perimeter distinguish impact from the preceding magenta warning. There is an identifiable vertical lightning event, not merely an unexplained ground flash. The trainer partially occludes the central channel, but enough appears above the head and beside the legs to preserve the connection.
- **FAIL — strike shape and finish:** In both t120, t125, and t130, the upper fork consists of a few very long, ruler-straight spans with large angular elbows. The left descending branch makes an especially conspicuous wide triangular detour above the trainer; its almost even-width bright core and thin violet border read like a bent luminous strip. The right fork is thinner, which helps hierarchy, but also spans large distances with very few changes of direction. The same rigid silhouette persists across all three impact samples. It reads as lightning functionally, but lacks the irregular taper, smaller subsidiary branching, and varied segment rhythm that make the board's energy feel alive. This is a shape-art defect, not a request for more full-screen brightness or for matching painted per-pixel fidelity.
- **PASS — recovery:** At t140 the airborne bolt is gone and the ground pattern is faint; by t150–t170 the magenta/white warning pattern is gone in both modes. The surviving gold ground stripes are already present before the warning and therefore are not evidence of a stuck strike. Normal mode visibly lifts the ground and trainer more during impact than reduced mode. Reduced mode retains the footprint, progress, vertical bolt, and clearance while reducing that light response.
- **Limit — reduced-motion certification:** The supplied samples support equivalent hazard information with a quieter illumination response. They do not certify flash frequency, continuous interpolation, camera shake, comfort, sound, or behaviour between samples. Rain continues in both modes.

The scoped failure can be addressed by revising the lightning channel artwork/geometry and its visible branching hierarchy while preserving the clear target, warning progression, and restrained reduced-mode lighting. Changing the forest density or global colour grade would not repair this particular defect.

## Three largest reference gaps, ranked

1. **Authored depth and forest enclosure — both t000 and t170.** The middle distance is a broad, largely vacant flat strip under an uninterrupted horizon. The Stormheart boards layer enormous living trunks, canopy, structures, and terrain into a destination; Palworld 02 and 04 use landform and habitat masses to give travel a readable middle distance. The near trees and grass here frame empty country instead. This is principally scene composition/landform/dressing work; these frames do not establish whether the required distant assets already exist.
2. **Electrical shape quality — both t120/t125/t130.** The sparse, rigid airborne fork and broad flat ground spokes have a diagrammatic quality. Board B gives electrical channels a hierarchy of core, taper, and smaller irregular branches; Palworld 01 and 03 make effects feel like an event through shaped highlights and subject integration. The current bolt needs revised effect art, not additional scatter. Its readability pass does not close this finish gap.
3. **Ground material and effect integration — both t000, t110, and t150.** Wide gold stripes compete with the warning and expose straight-edged tonal patches across the foreground path. The surrounding dirt is blurred and the bright strips sit on it with little convincing material transition. Palworld 02/03/05 separate worn ground, grass, rock, and illuminated action while retaining surface detail. This gap calls for ground/effect material and placement work; a global saturation increase would not solve it.
