## Frame-specific defects and practical priority

1. **N120, N125, R120, R125 — bolt core reads hollow/doubled at the upper bend and fork.** This is the clearest strike-specific finish defect. Resolve the interior and edge hierarchy before adding more branches or more brightness.
2. **N060, N090, N110, N120 and their reduced counterparts — ground spokes read as flat cut strips.** Their solid fill and mechanically sharp changes of direction are disconnected from the ring's softer glow and the airborne fine line. The consistent world perspective is a strength; the material/shape language is the issue.
3. **N120, N125, R120, R125 — permanent ground energy outweighs the impact.** The conspicuous white-yellow channels continue to dominate the foreground at the supposed peak. A local contact emphasis and a calmer competing layer would make the strike the event.

These are VFX shape/material and local scene-hierarchy repairs. The frames do not show that a new creature or landmark mesh is needed to fix the scoped lightning failures.

## Whole-frame review, separate from lightning acceptance

The trainer has a readable backpack, head, blue sleeves, dark trousers and boots; those colour blocks are useful at this camera. The rear view cannot establish facial quality. The left NPC is discernible but small and embedded in dark foliage. No adequately readable creature is presented in this sequence, so creature identity, expression, relative roster scale and Palworld-level creature appeal cannot be accepted or rejected from it. That absent evidence matters for the whole-frame creature-adventure comparison; it is not a scoped lightning defect.

The largest three gaps from the references are:

1. **Authored world depth and destination.** N000 and R170 have strong near tree framing but open onto a broad, almost featureless dark horizontal mid-distance beneath a very large sky. The Stormheart boards give layered woodland and an unmistakable destination; Palworld 04 gives traversable landform and a landmark through distinct depth planes. Add meaningful middle/distant masses, route staging and a destination silhouette to this view. This is largely scene composition; a suitably designed landmark may require an asset if none exists, which this blind review cannot establish.
2. **Ground and vegetation material cohesion.** N110 and R120 juxtapose blurred soil, extremely bright yellow zigzags, flat pink/white warning strips, needlelike grass and dark schematic trunks. The Stormheart material panels retain bark, moss, living wood and energy as different materials within one place; Palworld 02/03 integrate grass, path and rock while keeping the active event dominant. Ground-emission hierarchy, path edges, vegetation clustering and lighting separation are scene/material work. Better bark/root or canopy forms may need mesh work if the visible thin, skeletal shapes are the available asset ceiling.
3. **Event and subject presentation.** N120 and R125 do depict a strike, but the outlined fine bolt over a larger luminous floor graphic reads like several effect layers sharing space rather than one concentrated natural event. Palworld 01/03 stage strong subject silhouettes and effects around a clear action centre; Stormheart board B relates electricity to its living structure. The lightning failures above are repairable with VFX art and composition. The sequence supplies no close creature subject to carry the project's defining relationship.

**Bar A — NO.** The purple storm, large trunks, natural undergrowth and white-violet electricity point toward Stormwood, and the current fixed-purple direction takes precedence over the boards' bright exterior skies. However, this view lacks the boards' layered, inhabited woodland and material integration. The open flat distance and dominant graphic floor prevent the whole image from convincingly belonging to the approved world. Scene depth, emission balance, path/material transitions and VFX integration are actionable; stronger trunk/root/landmark silhouettes may require authored asset work. No conclusion about unseen installed assets is made.

**Bar B — NO.** These frames communicate a third-person fantasy game, but beside the supplied Palworld gameplay images they do not yet carry the same polished creature-adventure register. The unfinished ground/effect relationship, weak mid-distance and absence of an expressive creature subject sink this sample. Scene and VFX repairs can address the first two. Creature-art parity remains unproven and requires an appropriate production witness; it cannot be repaired or waived by this lightning review.
