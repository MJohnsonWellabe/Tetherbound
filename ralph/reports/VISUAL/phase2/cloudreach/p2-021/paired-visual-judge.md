# P2-021 paired visual judgement

Overall paired acceptance: **FAIL**.

The trainer and creatures remain visually inconsistent with one another. In matrix `03_gate_lower_cliffs_reverse_day`, the restrained, heavily feathered white bird, the saturated blue/orange creature group, and the small, large-headed trainer do not have the unified treatment of the Palworld cast. The bird has a strong, legible silhouette and convincing size beside the trainer, but that individual success does not make the ensemble cohesive. The bright purple creature in matrix `20_high_roost_sky_shrine_perch-vista_day` also loses its body outline where it overlaps the bird and pillar. These are visible art and staging limitations; the images do not establish asset provenance.

The candidate improves distant vertical silhouettes in several matched views. It does **not** resolve the broad level grass and sparse-tree composition where layered elevated land should lead the route. The clearest improvements are in the background, while the clearest failures remain in the playable foreground and middle distance.

## Evidence and pairing

Inspected both matrix `_sheet_frame_matrix.png` overviews and every native before/after frame, including obstructed frames. No source, diffs, implementation descriptions, or other review narratives were consulted.

## Three largest reference gaps, ranked

1. **Cast cohesion and presentation.** Matrix `03_gate_lower_cliffs_reverse_day` and `20_high_roost_sky_shrine_perch-vista_day` lack the clean, mutually distinct creature bodies and consistent stylisation visible in Palworld `01`, `03` and `04`. The roster board also gives each species a clear body shape and controlled colour hierarchy. The white bird is readable, but the nearby saturated creatures and trainer do not yet read as a fully resolved ensemble. Staging and separation are scene fixes; coherent proportions, facial treatment, materials and silhouette simplification require art work beyond scatter changes.
2. **Layered land that directs attention.** Locations Realm Gate Crag approach/close, all five Sky Shrine route captures, and matrix `17` still organise the world as a broad green plane with sparse props. Palworld `02` and `04` place paths among recognisable changes in elevation and landmarks; the Aviary board makes bridges, ledges and stacked land support a destination. The candidate's distant spires help the setting but cannot carry this missing middle-ground structure. This is primarily scene composition and terrain work, with distinct architectural landings or crossings needed where the existing kit cannot communicate them.
3. **Authored ground and architectural material hierarchy.** Matrix `01`/`02`, Heartstone close and matrix `19` show abrupt islands of identical grass, large smooth ground areas, repetitive coarse stone and plain beams. Palworld `02`/`05` integrate worn paths, varied ground cover and useful props into a lived-in place; Meadows key art balances clearings with clustered planting and warmer local detail. Reworking density, clustering, material scale, path edges and lighting is scene/material work. The Aviary's distinctive arches, dome language and detailed structural transitions require suitable architectural art; their presence cannot be demonstrated by these obstructed shrine views.

## Bar decisions

**Bar A — No.** These frames do not yet read convincingly as belonging to the Meadows key art's world. Natural greens, stone landmarks, a friendly trainer and the warmer dusk palette provide some continuity. Broad level surfaces, repetitive sparse tree placement, disconnected pale spires and unresolved landmark presentation prevent the intended inviting, layered world from carrying across. Scene changes can address composition, density, palette relationships and route hierarchy. Cast cohesion and a distinctive finished architectural vocabulary also require art work.

**Bar B — No.** Beside all five Palworld screenshots, these do not meet the intended visual comparison. The companion bird and third-person trainer communicate the genre, but the world repeatedly reads as a sparse assembly around them, while Palworld's ground, creature staging and landmarks sustain the same visual language across the frame. This is not a demand for concept-art leaf detail or a judgement of interface, performance or motion. The scene-fixable and art-dependent gaps are separated above.

**Overall paired acceptance — FAIL.** There is a genuine, limited skyline improvement. Broad level grass and sparse trees still dominate the critical Sky Shrine route views, and the remaining image evidence does not demonstrate the layered elevated route or finished landmark identity required for acceptance.
