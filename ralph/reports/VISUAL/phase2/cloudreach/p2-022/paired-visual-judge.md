## Top three reference gaps, ranked

1. **Landscape composition and ground density still read as repeated construction.** Palworld's open-path and plateau frames blend grass, dirt, rocks, and authored clearings into a continuous landscape, with distinct depth layers. Candidate `06_broken_causeways_approach_day`, `11_windscar_ravine_approach_day`, `26_summit_final_stronghold_approach_day`, and `34_summit_final_stronghold_finale100_day` retain the broad bare turf, hard vegetation patches, enormous flat-textured banks, and weak distant context. Scene-fixable: varied scatter/clustering, contact detail, softened ground joins, and layered depth. Art-dependent: more convincing cliff forms/materials and cohesive vegetation assets.

2. **Aviary architecture and night mood remain below the stronghold board.** The reference composes the dome with arches, secondary towers, tiered terraces, inhabited foliage, cliff foundations, and warm nighttime spaces. Candidate row 29 and Summit Eyrie `day__close` still read as cage over drum, while both `night` views retain a cold bright lattice over a dark base. Scene-fixable: entry sightlines, tree placement, warm lighting, and stronger cliff context. Art-dependent: articulated structural kit, rib/support junctions, bay/tower hierarchy, and a convincing occupied interior. The added arches alone do not close this gap.

3. **Creature cohesion and staging still fall short of the game reference.** Palworld and the roster board preserve distinct forms, clear color groupings, and readable creature/person relationships. Candidate row 06 juxtaposes pale patchy bodies with densely shaded feathers; rows 21/32 entangle the Ribbonray and horned creature while the companion crowds the trainer; row 33 overlaps insect and wolf. Rows 09/29 still lose the trainer/objective behind wings. Scene-fixable: spacing, camera-safe companion placement, and activity staging. Art-dependent: common material/value treatment and more controlled creature surface detail. No animation conclusion is inferred.
