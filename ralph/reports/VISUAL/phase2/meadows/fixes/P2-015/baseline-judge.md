Inputs: Meadows `contact_sheet_creatures_05.jpg` and

## Ranked findings

1. `meadows__terrapup__fainted` retains the upright, open-eyed silhouette of
   `terrapup__idle`; incapacitation is not readable. This needs pose work, not
   scene dressing.
2. `meadows__veridian__resting` retains a tall upright torso and raised antlers.
   `terrapup__resting` tips sideways, but grass hides its supporting surface;
   the reviewer could not approve convincing settled weight. The comparison
   references do not contain directly comparable resting poses.
3. `trailpup__fainted` and `trailpup__resting` are too small and grass-obscured
   to distinguish support, hovering or penetration. Veridian's horizontal
   faint silhouette is clearer, but its underside remains ambiguous. Fresh,
   closer diagnostic views are needed alongside the matched normal camera.

## Verdict

- **Bar A: yes.** Sunny meadow, flowers, blue water and timber architecture
  broadly belong to the key art's welcoming world. This does not approve poses.
- **Bar B: no.** State silhouettes are inconsistent and several creatures are
  too visually subordinate to verify against the creature-focused references.
- **Scoped pose/contact: not accepted.** Terrapup faint is a clear silhouette
  failure; Veridian rest is insufficiently distinct. Contact for the obscured
  bodies cannot be certified at this tile size. No motion claim follows from
  these stills.

Scene-level separation and contact lighting may help readability. The upright
faint silhouette needs creature-pose work. The reviewer did not determine
whether appropriate pose assets are already installed.
