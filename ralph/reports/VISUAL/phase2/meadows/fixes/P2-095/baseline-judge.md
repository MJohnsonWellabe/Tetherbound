# Crafting baseline: independent visual review

Code-blind reviewer `ui_baseline_judge` inspected the original full-resolution
`meadows__system__crafting` image recovered from
`7697ca02b^:ralph/reports/VISUAL/phase2/meadows/systems/meadows__system__crafting.jpg`,
alongside the Meadows map baseline, ART_DIRECTION and the visual-judge references.
The reviewer received no source, catalog, change description or desired verdict.

The crafting screen is partly understandable. Both visible "Ironwood Haft (..."
entries truncate at the same point and have no visible distinguishing icon.
They cannot be told apart. Axe, Berry Seeds, Hoe and Knife remain identifiable.
Several orange ingredient summaries also truncate, although the selected Axe's
requirements are fully readable on the right.

The three ranked gaps were ambiguous crafting identities, map labels without
terrain/route context, and weak secondary information. In this crafting frame,
"Ingredients", "Craft: A / Enter" and "Leave: B / Esc" recede at reduced size.

**Bar A: yes, narrowly.** The leafy grove, wildflowers and warm path belong to
the key art's natural Meadows vocabulary; blue-gray panels and cyan focus match
the written interface direction. This is a limited identity judgment.

**Bar B: yes, narrowly, for trying to be the same kind of game.** The visible
stylized trainer, vegetated setting and crafting presentation suggest that genre.
This does not establish comparable finish. Palworld UI was not a design reference.

Static menus obscure most of the world and do not establish whole-chapter
acceptance, creature quality, controller behavior, hidden contents or motion.
This is a baseline defect assessment, **not a candidate before/after PASS**.
