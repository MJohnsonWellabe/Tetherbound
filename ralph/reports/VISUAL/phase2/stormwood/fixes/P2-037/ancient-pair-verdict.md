## Blind result

Fresh code-blind reviewer `stormwood_ancient_pair_judge` inspected all 28 paired
native images, two reduced sheets and nine supplied reference images.
`ancient-blind-verdict.md` is the unchanged full verdict. Neutral A is baseline;
B is the combined new candidate.

B improves warm/cool separation and makes the ascent and platform construction
readable. It also exposes repetitive orange planking, stretched bark surfaces
and empty floor area. The distant crown remains sparse and disconnected; the
interior remains a regular shaft with an undressed crown chamber. The cyan
energy feature reads as separate flat blocks. Core views additionally expose
opaque mint/lavender effects, a rail crossing the focal area and weak creature
face/charged-detail presentation. These are visible findings, not a claim that
these stills identify the runtime species, explain effect timing or prove a
combat-state defect. The candidate does not resolve the complete item.

## Limits

This is HUD-hidden debug placement in the real production scene and camera,
with phase/clock pins, healing and staged aftermath. It proves neither earned
ascent nor ordinary combat, traversal, collision safety, motion, full UI or
device performance. Weather, animation and arena effects are not synchronized;
the changing lavender wedge in a core pair cannot be attributed to the art
candidate. Raised-floor collision identity does not make a still into gameplay
proof. Fixed purple aftermath is intentional and remains preserved.
