## Remaining defects and limits

1. Hesk/Bryn/Maud share one elderly face/outfit; Fenn/Oswin share one merchant;
   Lio/Neri share one caretaker. Matching the portrait does not make these
   named characters individually recognizable. Distinct character treatment
   or art is needed; lighting/camera alone cannot differentiate identical faces.
2. Fenn's base, progress and aftermath post images in both sets are almost
   entirely filled by a near-black trunk/foreground surface. Neither Fenn nor
   the player is visible there. The dialogue shot is usable but does not repair
   the obstructed ordinary-camera stand. Staging/camera diagnosis remains open.
3. Close-range world faces/clothing have dark, mottled, soft surface definition
   beside clean text/portraits. Fenn/Lio's dark clothing merges with background
   values. Lighting/material work may help; the stills cannot establish whether
   all surface limitations are repairable that way.

Scoped Bar A: **YES** for B's movement toward the intended identity, carried
by earthy expedition clothing, readable stylized faces and consistent body/
portrait identity. Scoped Bar B: **YES** for movement toward the intended
creature-adventure register, carried by the stylized cast, creature presence
and coherent speaker presentation. These aspirational verdicts do not assert
asset parity, commercial readiness or chapter acceptance. Palworld UI was not
used as a comparison. Repeated identities and rough close-range finish remain.
