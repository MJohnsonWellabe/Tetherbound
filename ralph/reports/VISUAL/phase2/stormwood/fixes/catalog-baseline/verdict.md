# Current landmark baseline verdict

Independent fresh code-blind reviewer: `stormwood_landmark_baseline_judge`.
Inspected all four contact sheets (78 tiles), all 24 unique native originals
listed in the coverage files (13 locations and 11 routes), Meadows key art,
both Stormheart stronghold boards and all five Palworld references. No game
source, config, diffs or previous verdicts were inspected. Manifest reads
also exposed capture metadata; the reviewer did not use it as visual proof.

**Bar A: NO. Bar B: NO. No item is accepted as fixed by this baseline.**

## P2-037: Stormheart

All six required sightings show enormous dark walls, horizontal platform
bands and unclear connections. Day approach/close frame an unfinished-looking
enclosure, an oval overhead opening and an isolated small beam frame. The
Dynamo Core close, route and vista views expose strips of sky below straight
wall edges. Walk 02 shows stretched-looking bark bands, a nearly black
overhead surface and uniform grass within the footprint.

Ground contact, entrance sequence, platform support and circulation do not
read. Both boards instead connect roots, split trunk, electrical core,
attached walkways and foliage into a coherent stronghold.

These are close or obstructed views. They do not establish that an exterior
canopy or better distant silhouette is absent. Grounding and arrival
composition need scene work; the visible flat wall expanses, platform
undersides and organic-to-architectural transitions need modelling/material
work. Exterior views are required before judging the whole tree silhouette.

## P2-038: named destinations

Lantern Pools close reads as an NPC and electrical crossroads, with no
identifiable lantern-water composition. Crown Overlook close gives no clear
destination or elevated overlook. Fallen Giant close frames standing trunks
and posts, not a recognizable fallen giant. Crown Heartstone close and Walk 02
show creatures, an ordinary boulder and small props without a dominant
heartstone. Its route view is almost entirely blocked by close creature
geometry: that frame cannot establish an art defect. Old Rodfolk Hall close
shows an NPC and forest junction without an identifiable hall.

The views establish poor destination presentation, not that unseen assets
need replacement. Confirm destination sightlines and name-specific subjects
before commissioning replacements; staging and distinct arrival ground
treatments are needed.

## P2-039: ground and distance

Sentinel approach has an even sprinkle of small plants with isolated tall
trees and posts. Crown Overlook close/route/vista and Walk 01 expose busy
near-ground scatter ending before a bare middle distance. A lone boulder and
simple pale horizon mass provide little layered woodland. Crown Heartstone
close repeats low-hierarchy scatter against a large pale background wall.

At small size the foreground becomes green-white noise and trees become
repeated pillars. Cluster planting, leave quiet ground, vary tree groups and
compose middle-distance masses. Uniformly adding grass is not the remedy.
The visible abrupt transition is established; static images cannot identify
its implementation cause or prove popping.

## P2-040: charged road

The yellow electrical identity is strong and should remain. Lantern Hollow's
location close and landmark night close have equally bright crossing lines
that dominate settlement doors and people; long parallel marks resemble
traffic lanes. Glass Field close has weak road-edge/material integration.
Verge Rod Station route shows a hard horizontal cutoff and sharply clipped
bright segments. Fallen Giant vista has a useful leading route but flat
electrical markings against trunks and posts.

Preserve the gold charge while improving intersection hierarchy, bright-core
and branch balance, segment boundaries, verges and material structure. The
still establishes a cutoff artifact, not its rendering cause.

## P2-041: Crown Arch

The required close frame clearly establishes a visible art defect: a flat
navy crenellated slab with a pointed hole and four cyan rectangles, on a large
rectangular pad pierced by grass. The face has little construction/material
detail, and its upper silhouette merges with the tree behind it. Human
passage scale is plausible, but it lacks a landmark approach and setting.

Fix foundation/grass integration and backdrop composition; the slab needs
authored construction detail and material variation beyond lighting.

## P2-043: Glass Field

Walk 03 exposes a black angular slab under translucent cyan spikes. Grass
penetrates the slab and is visible through a spike. A stark white bar crosses
the lower spikes around trainer upper-body height. The close trunk and spike
also obscure the composition. The cluster's full silhouette cannot be judged,
but its slab edge, penetrations and crossing element are visible defects.
Glass Field landmark close also shows protruding dark bases at distance.

Ground the clusters, eliminate penetrations and resolve the white crossing
element. Natural glass growth needs a coherent fused/fractured ground
transition; moving the camera cannot correct those construction defects.

## Reference gap and limits

The three largest gaps are (1) the finale's unreadable living-tree
architecture, (2) foreground detail without a composed middle/distant world,
and (3) inconsistent creature/landmark finish. Scene changes can improve
grounding, visibility, grouping and focal hierarchy; characterful creature
finish and authored landmark construction also need art work.

The trainer's shirt/pack remain readable but dark legs merge with soil.
Crown Heartstone's repeated pink-purple creatures have noisy surfaces and
weak facial readability; smaller tan creatures disappear into busy ground.
The visible sizes do not establish a wrong species hierarchy or the roles
needed to compare companion/practice-creature scale.

Bar A is NO: natural palette, timber houses and electrical accents provide
partial continuity, but depth, destination identity and landmark construction
do not convincingly extend the reference world. Bar B is NO: genre intent is
visible, but creature-led identity and finished environment/material language
remain below the five gameplay references.

The other 54 frames were inspected only as sheet tiles. No readable gameplay
HUD or clearly staged fight supports UI/combat acceptance. Motion, traversal,
popping and performance are unjudged. The exact 24 native IDs are retained
in the adjacent coverage files; shared P2-038/P2-039 sightings were inspected
once.
