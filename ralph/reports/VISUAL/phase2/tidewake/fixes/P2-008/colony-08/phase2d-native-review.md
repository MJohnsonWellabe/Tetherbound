This is an **earlier dune candidate versus a later dune candidate**, not a live-production before/after. The baseline uses the original five-leaf dune fan and 57,836 allocated grass instances; the candidate uses the eight-leaf fuller clump, revised coverage and 38,760 allocated instances. Coverage, mesh and count change together, so the images do not isolate one cause. Both production dune gates stayed false.

Visual verdict for these three pairs: **partial, item still open**. Gull Rest has fuller grass in the right-hand channel, but the foreground remains an empty sand apron and the huge dark wall retains triangular sand/rock teeth. First Shore's right colony gains varied gaps and thicker individual clumps; its long gray bank, ribbon-like slope transitions and distant sparse island remain. Sluice Isle changes little at scene scale: the pale foreground and broad, smooth gray bank still dominate, with thin crest ecology and weak pump identity. Small creature pose and moving cloud differences are not attributed to the grass candidate.

This comparison does not justify enabling either gate or declaring P2-008 fixed. It contains no motion, traversal, frame-cost, chapter-matrix or regional Bars A/B proof.
