## New regressions and persistent defects

1. **More conspicuous angular surface boundaries — Gull beach, Brine Steps walk03, First Shore horizon route, after.** Pale sand against darker steep faces makes repeated triangular teeth and angular cuts highly visible. Gull's top-left cap is the clearest new visual distraction compared with its before image. The underlying form may predate the change; the defect here is its increased visibility, not an assertion about implementation.
2. **Loss of midground structure — Shellwatch jetty and both Sluice frames, after.** Removal/reduction of the large canopy masses and green surfaces leaves enormous similarly pale, smooth spans. The sand direction is correct; the scene now needs landform and ecology structure that survives at sheet size. Adding uniform grass coverage back would miss the requested direction.
3. **Grass colony shapes remain too regular — First Shore horizon route and Shellwatch jetty, after.** Thin continuous contour ribbons and broadly similar upright fan clumps look placed by one rule. Break the long bands into connected but irregular colonies, with variable clump size, dead straw, isolated pioneers, bare blowouts, and denser sheltered pockets.
4. **Regional surface uniformity — Brine Steps walk03 and First Shore horizon route, after.** Beach, steep exposed bank, broad upland, and far islands share almost one pale treatment. Distinct wet shore, open moving sand, partially stabilized grass, and sheltered scrub zones are not yet readable. The Sleeping Bear reference supports open sand and limited grass; it does not imply covering every landform with one uninterrupted tone.
5. **Persistent non-item presentation gaps — First Shore horizon route / Sluice frames and Brine Steps walk03.** The distant waterfall landmark remains a smooth gray peak with simple white streaks; the water at Brine reads as a dense bright repeating surface pattern. Both reduce the authored natural-world impression. These are not shown to be new dune regressions and should not be silently folded into P2-008's closure requirements.

No obvious new floating trainer, camera penetration, or grass-to-sand hard color seam is visible. This is an observation of these stills, not a physics certification. There is no HUD shown on which to judge interface legibility.

## Three ranked reference gaps

1. **Authored landform and material hierarchy.** Palworld 04 organizes cliffs, vegetated benches, paths, and architecture into distinct connected shapes; the key art and Water board make shore, slope, grove, and landmark legible at distance. **After Sluice pumps, Gull beach, and Brine Steps walk03** retain smooth slabs, dome-like tops, and angular caps with little intermediate structure. This is the largest visible gap and directly affects P2-008.
2. **Ecology composed into convincing colonies and transitions.** Palworld 02/05 mix open walking space with varied, irregular edges and grounded vegetation. The key art groups foliage around believable terrain/settlement features. **After Shellwatch jetty and First Shore horizon route** instead alternate huge bare spans with fairly uniform thin grass ribbons; **after Brine Steps walk03** scarcely expresses the grass stage across its large visible island. The dune target can remain sparse while acquiring richer organization.
3. **Focal character/creature and landmark presentation.** Palworld 01/03/04 give creatures strong silhouettes, readable color blocks, evident scale, and clear roles in a composed scene. The Water board gives the destination a distinct broken silhouette. **After Brine Steps walk03 and Sluice pumps route** offer small, pale or tangled creatures against an empty environment; **after First Shore horizon route** offers a simplified gray peak. The trainer is reasonably readable, but these frames do not carry comparable creature appeal or destination identity.

## Scene work versus art needed

**Scene-fixable:** reshape visible dune crests and banks into varied ridges/swales/blowouts; soften or intentionally break the faceted cap transitions; distinguish wet shore from dry dune and sheltered interior; organize existing grass into irregular colonies with graded edges; place tree/rock groups in believable sheltered pockets; preserve open sand while composing stronger midground anchors. Improve creature/background contrast and framing with existing subjects. These address most of the item-specific shortcomings without requiring a denser meadow.

**Art/material work needed for the remaining reference gap:** a broader family of dune-grass silhouettes, dry stems, and low coastal vegetation if the existing library cannot supply them; better dune-bank/erosion surface detail and blending where placement alone cannot hide the angular look; an authored waterfall/stronghold silhouette and materials to approach the board's landmark language. The distant creature evidence is insufficient to prescribe wholesale creature replacement; closer presentation and asset review would determine whether additional character/creature art is actually required. No asset availability was inferred from source files.

## Regional bars and limits

**A — No.** These eight after stills do not yet convincingly read as the same finished world as the Meadows key art. Sand, turquoise water, natural grass color, and the trainer are compatible ingredients, and a dunes region need not copy meadow greenery. What sinks this limited gate is the large-scale simplicity of surfaces and shores, weak ecological layering, and the primitive distant landmark, most clearly in **Sluice pumps, Brine Steps walk03, and First Shore horizon route**.

**B — No.** Beside the five Palworld frames, these eight after stills still read as an early environment assembly rather than a comparably realized creature-adventure world. **Shellwatch jetty and Sluice pumps route** lack authored midground structure; **Brine Steps walk03** lacks strong creature/environment presentation. This answer concerns the visible scene finish and presentation, not whether the intended genre is recognizable.

These are eight matched sightings, not a regional traversal acceptance, and the two Sluice compositions are highly redundant. Stills cannot establish motion stability, popping, wind, traversal feel, encounter behavior, or performance. No performance judgment was made. The before/after sets demonstrate a clear and useful dune direction, but P2-008 should remain partial until transitions and landform/ecology organization are visibly resolved in the same views.
