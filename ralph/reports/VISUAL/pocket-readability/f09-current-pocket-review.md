## Overall art finish versus functional lure

The shared marker language is effective: bright lamps plus a vertical reward cue announce a side destination, and pale cobbles reinforce the approach. The large silhouettes remain readable in the rainy, dark-purple environment. The mouth images provide a clear escalation from distant marker to visible reward.

Full art finish is not established by that success. All five spaces use nearly the same enormous bare-tree room, dense crossing branches, grass/flower floor and paired lantern gateway. The trunk scale dwarfs the trainer and makes the enclosure feel assembled as a wall. Changing beam colour and plant shape helps reward recognition more than place identity. Three rewards are visually similar green leafy pods. The bright main-road line and bright pocket beacons also carry much of the navigation burden. These are asset-form and composition concerns visible in the images, separate from the narrower lure criterion.
