# Meadows independent visual audit

Capture snapshot: `b2fe128ef`. Main baseline: `1ba253eb` (identifiers supplied by audit coordinator; no source/git inspected). Reviewer: independent code-blind image review. Scope: all 36 native 1920x1080 PNGs in `visual-acceptance/shots/ranked-audit-current/region/meadows`, numbered 001–036: ten environment stands across five bands in day/dusk/night, plus three selected place approaches in day/night. These are staged fixtures with HUD hidden and native CameraRig, not continuous gameplay. No engine runs or production edits.

## Separate bars

**Bar A, identity: partial, not closed.** Creatures, warm village kit, paths, leafy woods and cyan tether structures belong to the intended world. Repeated scatter, simplified landmarks and weak layered vistas prevent a convincing whole-scene match to the Meadows keyart.

**Bar B, genre/finish: direction recognizable, finish below the references; not closed.** This is visibly a creature-adventure game, but environment asset coherence, terrain transitions, landmark construction and night value hierarchy remain materially below the Palworld comparison frames. No whole chapter, motion, performance or earned-play acceptance follows from this packet.

## Ranked findings

1. **Ground and vegetation lack ecological cohesion.** Evidence: `004_meadows_env_band1_lower_meadows_1_day.png`, `010_meadows_env_band2_stone_and_root_1_day.png`, `016_meadows_env_band3_the_river_lock_1_day.png`, `022_meadows_env_band4_upper_meadows_ironwood_1_day.png`, `035_meadows_place_stronghold_approach_day.png`. Repeated narrow blades, isolated tall pale flowers and similarly sized shrubs create foreground noise over broad yellow-green/brown mottled ground. Path verges often resemble scatter exclusions rather than worn transitions. River Lock 013 has flowers broadly across the flat worked yard. Scope: all five bands. Repair class: material, vegetation assets and placement composition. Acceptance witness: same ordinary traversal views show readable grass masses, quieter spaces between flower clusters, and distinct path/verge/woodland/occupied-ground transitions; close exposed ground remains credible without vegetation hiding it. Preserve clear paths.

2. **Progression landmarks have weaker geometry and surface language than the creatures and village kit.** Evidence: `013_meadows_env_band3_the_river_lock_0_day.png` has broad regular castellated walls, a simple angular opening and repetitive masonry. `019_meadows_env_band4_upper_meadows_ironwood_0_day.png` and 020 show enormous angular canopy chunks and enlarged streaky trunk detail, conflicting with the smaller leafy trees. `028_meadows_env_band5_stronghold_approach_1_day.png` repeats wall/floor stone around a plain dark rectangular doorway. Scope: multiple progression landmarks. Repair class: geometry/assets first, material scale and composition second. Acceptance witness: approach and close views show believable wall depth and differentiated structural parts at River Lock; a colossal Ironwood sharing the foliage/bark language of its surroundings; a stronghold threshold whose form and material hierarchy communicate entrance importance. More small props alone will not repair these masses.

3. **Open vistas lack convincing middle-distance structure and distant terrain finish.** Evidence: `010_meadows_env_band2_stone_and_root_1_day.png`, `025_meadows_env_band5_stronghold_approach_0_day.png`, `026_meadows_env_band5_stronghold_approach_0_dusk.png`, `035_meadows_place_stronghold_approach_day.png`. Long gentle rolls lead into pale angular mountains; band 5's distant structure is tiny while intervening ground provides little large-scale progression. Dusk makes the mountain belt a particularly uniform beige cutout. Scope: open views across bands. Repair class: terrain geometry, distance material/lighting and composition. Acceptance witness: same stands reveal foreground route, substantial middle-distance landform/ecology and separated distant ridges; destination identifiable without label. Preserve breathing room; uniform extra scatter is insufficient.

4. **Night loses material/subject information and useful destination hierarchy.** Evidence: `003_meadows_env_band1_lower_meadows_0_night.png`, `006_meadows_env_band1_lower_meadows_1_night.png`, `015_meadows_env_band3_the_river_lock_0_night.png`, `021_meadows_env_band4_upper_meadows_ironwood_0_night.png`, `024_meadows_env_band4_upper_meadows_ironwood_1_night.png`, `027_meadows_env_band5_stronghold_approach_0_night.png`. Dark fur, trunks and fortification faces merge into near-black areas while flower edges, white markings and blue plates remain conspicuous. Village windows help locally. In 030, the trainer/wall are bright but the central doorway remains almost black. Scope: every band's night set. Repair class: lighting/value composition and material response. Acceptance witness: identical stands plus normal traversal preserve dark body volume, terrain boundaries and entrance shapes within blue nighttime character; practical light makes meaningful places readable.

## Capture limitations and additional verification

The large companion dominates right-side framing throughout. In 013–015 it visibly sits above the River Lock yard and intersects the elevated structure; 016–018 also show questionable foot-to-slope contact. This is unacceptable in the captured presentation, but staged placement prevents diagnosis of ordinary follower behavior. Verify movement and settling at those sites. Its occlusion is not proof of absent environment content.

`031_meadows_place_south_bridge_day.png` and 032 face down a steep-sided trench, not a bridge approach. They show stretched-looking cliff surfaces but do not prove a missing bridge. 033–034 establish woodland path/deer while the named relay is largely concealed. 035–036 show pylons/open route, not a clear stronghold entrance. Correct subject witnesses are needed before claims about those approaches. Coordinator reports one dynamic-location metadata skip for Meadowhart grazing ground, not a selected screenshot; three selected approaches survived deduplication.

Strengths to preserve: creature faces/color blocks, trainer readability, warm village roofs/plaster and human-scale props, clear daytime paths, leafy enclosure, cyan tether identity, distinct day/dusk/night moods.
