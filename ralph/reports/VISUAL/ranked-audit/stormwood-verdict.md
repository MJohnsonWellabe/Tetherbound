# Stormwood independent visual audit

Capture snapshot: `b2fe128ef`. Main baseline: `1ba253eb` (identifiers supplied by coordinator, no source/git inspected). Reviewed all 41 native 1920x1080 PNGs numbered 001–041 in `visual-acceptance/shots/ranked-audit-current/region/stormwood`: 36 environment images (12 stands, Calm/Building/Break) and five places. Coordinator reports engine exit 0 with no errors/skips. Staged fixture, HUD hidden; still evidence only. This region uses Surge phases, not day/night. No engine runs or production edits.

## Separate bars

**Bar A, Stormwood identity: partial motifs, substantial shortfall; not closed.** Purple storm, massive trunks, electric fauna and a split vertical Dynamo suggest the intended chapter. The broad exposed horizons, meadow-like understory, intensely luminous roads and elementary Dynamo construction do not yet establish the enclosed, wet, rooted electric forest and inhabited ancient tree in the chapter boards.

**Bar B, genre/finish: not met in the reviewed scenes.** Readable characters and paths belong to creature-adventure gameplay, but landscape composition, ground integration and hero landmark asset finish sit visibly below the Palworld reference register. This is a broad audit, not change acceptance. No whole chapter, motion, performance or earned-state closure.

## Ranked findings

1. **The forest repeatedly reads as a sparse set of large trunks on open rolling ground.** Evidence: `001_stormwood_env_cinder_verge_0_calm.png`, `004_stormwood_env_cinder_verge_1_calm.png`, `007_stormwood_env_glowmoss_hollows_0_calm.png`, `010_stormwood_env_glowmoss_hollows_1_calm.png`, `016_stormwood_env_conductor_run_1_calm.png`, `028_stormwood_env_deepwood_1_calm.png`, `038_stormwood_place_old_rodfolk_hall_calm.png`, `039_stormwood_place_glass_field_calm.png`, with the same geometry visible through phase counterparts. Broad sky/empty horizon remains visible between a few oversized trees, even at Glowmoss and Deepwood. Landforms barely differentiate these places; distant creatures/props can appear scattered across a bare field. This is the highest repeated scene-level identity loss. Repair class: terrain/forest composition and asset scale/variety. Acceptance witness: normal routes through Cinder Verge, Glowmoss and Deepwood demonstrate distinct enclosure rhythms, overlapping trunk/canopy depth, substantial midground roots/rocks/understory, and controlled destination openings. Do not merely increase uniform scatter or hide everything with fog.

2. **The Dynamo's visible construction remains an elementary giant shell rather than a finished ancient-tree destination.** Evidence: `031_stormwood_env_dynamo_0_calm.png` through 036 and `041_stormwood_place_dynamo_core_calm.png`. The distant form is a tall straight-sided split column. Close view 041 shows giant smooth/streaky bark surfaces, very regular dark ring platforms and flat pale-blue segmented core shapes. 034–036 show broad dark horizontal platform bands and little authored transition at ground level. These are actual visible surfaces; the packet does not establish the entire crown or every interior route. Repair class: hero geometry/assets, material scale and architectural composition, not lighting alone. Acceptance witness: full exterior silhouette, entrance approach and climbing interior at normal camera distance show branching/rooted mass, believable bark scale, supported walkways/rails/landings, integrated occupation and a readable energy core. Preserve height and split-core concept.

3. **Luminous road treatment dominates the region and exposes its joins.** Evidence: `007_stormwood_env_glowmoss_hollows_0_calm.png`, `013_stormwood_env_conductor_run_0_calm.png`, `025_stormwood_env_deepwood_0_calm.png`, `031_stormwood_env_dynamo_0_calm.png`, `034_stormwood_env_dynamo_1_calm.png`, `038_stormwood_place_old_rodfolk_hall_calm.png`. Thick yellow zigzags recur across nearly every usable road view. They are often the brightest, most continuous object, pulling attention away from destinations and making multiple branches equally emphatic. In 025–026, 031–033 and 034–036, abrupt straight/rectangular changes around the luminous path are plainly visible near the trainer. Repair class: ground/material transitions, luminous effect integration and value composition. Acceptance witness: same intersections and camera movement show uninterrupted terrain-attached transitions, with no obvious rectangular patch boundaries; electric/copper cues distinguish their purpose while ordinary ground, landmarks and safe places retain hierarchy. Preserve route legibility.

4. **Understory and occupied places remain too generic for Stormwood.** Evidence: `013_stormwood_env_conductor_run_0_calm.png`, `022_stormwood_env_hollow_crown_1_calm.png`, `025_stormwood_env_deepwood_0_calm.png`, `031_stormwood_env_dynamo_0_calm.png`, `037_stormwood_place_crown_heartstone_calm.png`. Similar fine grass and tall pale flowers blanket pathsides and open areas; sparse mushrooms/broad leaves exist but do not govern the forest-floor read. Small familiar cottages/well stand on broad mottled ground. Cyan spike clusters at Dynamo/Glass Field read as simple cones rather than materially convincing fused glass. Repair class: biome vegetation/prop assets, ground materials and local composition. Acceptance witness: several normal stands distinguish wet moss/root/fungus beds, worked safe-place ground and fused-glass scars by form and surface, not purple tint alone; safe places show a grounded coherent occupied area. Preserve warm windows and mushroom/fern accents already present.

5. **Break gains storm intensity partly by losing useful local values.** Evidence: compare `007_stormwood_env_glowmoss_hollows_0_calm.png` with 009, `010_stormwood_env_glowmoss_hollows_1_calm.png` with 012, `016_stormwood_env_conductor_run_1_calm.png` with 018, `028_stormwood_env_deepwood_1_calm.png` with 030, and `039_stormwood_place_glass_field_calm.png` with 040. Break's denser/slanted rain and stronger purple sky are visible, but lower-body, dark creatures, trunk and understory information collapses while road strips remain bright. Calm/Building differences in these stills are modest; no claim about temporal phase recognizability is supported. Repair class: lighting/value hierarchy and weather presentation. Acceptance witness: unlabeled continuous Calm→Building→Break→Fading traversal makes phases recognizable while preserving trainer legs, route edges and nearby dark creature forms between flashes. Existing still differences alone cannot certify wind, flash rhythm, steam or sound.

## Invalid witnesses and limits

019–021 (`stormwood_env_hollow_crown_0`) show a near-black ground/wall surface filling the view with scattered grass/flowers and a small trainer. They are invalid Crown subject/composition witnesses, not proof the Crown is absent. 037 is a forest path/Staticub view without a clear heartstone subject; 038 shows a path rather than a useful Hall approach. Correct the subject stands before diagnosing missing content. 041 is valuable for the visible Dynamo lower/core construction but crops the full top/crown.

Several frames have staged bodies close to or overlapping the camera/other bodies: 016–018 have a large blue subject across the lower foreground and overlapping wolves; 022 shows the companion airborne-looking above the grass, unlike the lower stance in 023. These demand ordinary in-world motion/settling evidence before production contact/animation diagnosis. No static attack or follower motion pass is implied.

Strengths: stable purple storm identity; visible rain and stronger Break weather; clear traversable roads; readable trainer/companion silhouettes in Calm; blue electric fauna; warm cottage openings; massive trunk scale; conceptually clear split vertical Dynamo. Preserve these while improving landscape and asset hierarchy.
