## Ranked recurring priorities

1. **FAIL — Finish the island landforms and close cliff surfaces.** Across the region, broad rounded grassy humps, continuous bare gray cliff bands and similarly shaped smooth cut channels dominate. Rock reads as uniformly mottled terrain rather than varied strata, erosion or authored coastal structure. Close stands expose severe simplification and stretching. Representative witnesses: `019_tidewake_env_shellwatch_0_day.png`, `025_tidewake_env_tidal_cradle_0_day.png`, `031_tidewake_env_salt_crown_0_day.png`, `067_tidewake_env_deep_watch_0_day.png`, and `075_tidewake_place_veilfall_white_cascade_day.png`. Prioritize distinct cliff profiles, ledges and convincing close rock treatment; this is the broadest repeated environmental deficit.

2. **FAIL — Give Veilfall its reference identity and increasing approach detail.** The landmark is visible from First Shore, which works for orientation. Its current visible form is largely a smooth cone/bell with narrow white fall streaks; the approved board establishes a tiered, broken mountain, substantial waterfalls, terraces and inhabited stronghold architecture. The approach images do not supply that missing visual complexity. Witnesses: `004_tidewake_env_first_shore_1_day.png`, `028_tidewake_env_tidal_cradle_1_day.png`, `040_tidewake_env_sluice_isle_1_day.png`, and `075_tidewake_place_veilfall_white_cascade_day.png`. Preserve long-range visibility while strengthening tiered silhouette, waterfall integration and an identifiable architectural destination. The close `043–045` stand is partly swallowed by rock/fall planes and grass; it cannot establish a complete entrance or whole mountain.

3. **FAIL — Replace the uniform ground-cover treatment with legible coastal layers and transitions.** Repeated bright thin grass strips make a busy foreground carpet over broad green surfaces, while the middle layer is sparse. Grass-to-rock lips often make repeated sharp triangular edges; sand, cliff and grass meet abruptly with little intertidal or disturbed-ground transition. Witnesses: `007_tidewake_env_reedhaven_0_day.png`, `037_tidewake_env_sluice_isle_0_day.png`, `049_tidewake_env_lantern_cove_0_day.png`, `064_tidewake_env_drowned_garden_1_day.png`, and `076_tidewake_place_drowned_garden_terraces_day.png`. Prioritize readable plant clusters, exposed working/route ground and softer, physically motivated coastal joins. Large foreground blades in `040–045` additionally restrict visibility; stills do not establish whether camera motion resolves this.

4. **WEAK / FAIL as arrival evidence — Make settlements and working places recognizable from their approaches.** Brine Steps and Shellwatch show credible timber, crates and pier cues, but similar sparse fence/pier arrangements do little to differentiate or inhabit the islands (`016_tidewake_env_brine_steps_1_day.png`, `022_tidewake_env_shellwatch_1_day.png`). Gull's isolated narrow timber tower on a white base is a destination cue without clear surrounding activity (`055_tidewake_env_gull_rest_0_day.png`). The named Reedhaven, Shellwatch and Drowned Garden approaches (`073`, `074`, `076`) do not visibly establish a hall, rescue jetty or garden. Prioritize meaningful approach composition, access and clustered working-site dressing. This is a failure of the pictured arrival read, not proof those named objects are absent elsewhere. **Retain Deep's chart canopy:** `070–072` visibly communicate a small physical work station, with the light chart and blue canopy still separable at night.

5. **WEAK — Integrate water, shore foam and current cues into the coast.** Cyan water and warm sand establish the region immediately, but broad water areas are visually sparse, foam often outlines shores as regular white dots, and currents appear as straight lanes of repeated white dashes. Witnesses: `016_tidewake_env_brine_steps_1_day.png`, `034_tidewake_env_salt_crown_1_day.png`, `061_tidewake_env_drowned_garden_0_day.png`, and their night matches `018`, `036`, `063`. Prioritize coherent shallows, wet shore contact and irregular surf/current structure while preserving readable navigation cues. These stills cannot establish flow, traversal behavior or animation quality.

## Retained strengths and narrower observations

- Cyan sea, golden beaches, painted cloud sky and clear day/dusk/night changes give Tidewake a coherent palette. Veilfall already serves as a distant bearing. Warm pier lights are restrained, and Deep's chart station has a readable purpose and physical construction.
- The recurring companion has an expressive face, eye and clear large-creature silhouette. These views do not establish the water-region creature roster. Dark body areas lose much of their detail at night, but white face/plates and the trainer usually remain identifiable; a blanket claim that night play is unreadable would overstate the evidence.
- Some sloped stands show the companion's forward paws visibly above descending ground (`028–030`, `034–036`). Treat this as a contact/pose witness needing follow-up, not a demonstrated animation-system defect. Its frequent right-side occlusion is also a capture limitation, not grounds for reducing the intended creature scale.

## Bars and evidence limits

**Bar A — identity: FAIL in the pictured environment comparisons; partial strengths retained.** The coastal palette and distant waterfall landmark register, but repeated island profiles, weak place-arrival differentiation and Veilfall's simplified visible structure fall short of the approved chapter board. This is not a verdict that unseen interiors, inhabitants or all chapter content are missing.

**Bar B — finish: FAIL in the pictured environment comparisons.** The supplied comparison scenes establish layered terrain, convincing rock breaks, clear paths and clustered places. Tidewake's visible bare cliff bands, repeated grass carpet, abrupt material joins and sparse site integration remain substantially below that finish. Individual creature-face and chart-station strengths do not close the environmental gap.

The 72 environment files represent **24 stands at three lighting states**, not 72 independent locations. Many are dominated by the nearby companion; `019–021` and `031–033` face close walls, `040–045` have heavy foreground occlusion, and `043–045` do not show a useful whole Veilfall approach. The four additional approaches give clearer terrain evidence but do not sufficiently establish their named destinations. No full interior, settlement-use, complete creature-roster, current traversal, water/mount-contact, combat, restoration-state, motion, frame-rate or device-performance acceptance follows from this set.
