# Veilfall interior — independent visual verdict

Reviewed all nine PNGs in `after-r2` individually and together at 30% size in `judge-r2-contact-sheet.png`. References: Meadows key art, Veilfall stronghold board, and all five Palworld screenshots. Read only the visual-judge skill and imagery; no source, history, author notes, manifests, or prior verdicts. This is a still-image review, including the three frames named motion; it does not establish animation quality.

## Scoped waterfall verdict: NO

The curtain is identifiable as falling water, including at small size. Blue-white descending streaks and a white base fringe carry that reading in `heart_chamber_entry.png` and `heart_chamber_crystal.png`. It does not yet read as a substantial fall integrated into this chamber.

- **Water mass:** In `heart_chamber_crystal.png` and `motion_0.png`–`motion_2.png`, the fall reads as two thin rectangular hanging sheets. Similar-width vertical streaks fill nearly the entire surface; there is little separation between heavy streams, transparent gaps, or spray. The visible central strip of stone emphasizes the two-panel construction. Make the major flow volumes and irregular edges readable before adding fine streak detail.
- **Source and lip:** The tops of both sheets meet the flat stone ceiling in a sharp cut in `heart_chamber_crystal.png` and every motion frame. No feeder opening, overhang, rock ledge, or water curling over a lip explains their origin. The Veilfall board makes water emerge from the landscape overhead. An actual visible source and lip are required here.
- **Impact:** A narrow white scalloped fringe marks the bottom in `heart_chamber_entry.png`, `heart_chamber_crystal.png`, and the motion frames, but the water appears to terminate on the same stone floor used by the player. There is no readable receiving basin, displaced water, broad foam field, or outflow connection. The two cyan pools in `sluice_crossing.png` are separated from the fall by a large stone apron, so they do not resolve the connection.
- **Mist:** The fine pale activity at the bottom of the fall is too shallow to create a mist volume or veil objects behind it in `motion_0.png`–`motion_2.png`. The board's heart chamber communicates falling water through luminous spray, softened depth, and moisture around architecture; this chamber stays uniformly crisp and dry outside the narrow fringe.
- **Distant read:** `intake_gallery.png`, `pump_hall.png`, and `sluice_crossing.png` reduce the waterfall to a bright rectangular end-wall feature behind a much more assertive crystal. It functions as a backdrop rather than the defining force of the stronghold. `heart_chamber_banner.png` shows no waterfall and supplies no contrary evidence about source or impact.

## Full-frame findings

The trainer remains identifiable against the floor. Ripplet in `intake_gallery.png` has a readable face, distinctive blue-and-white silhouette, and clear creature appeal. Its very flat bright surface treatment, however, sits apart from the shaded trainer and glossy cobbles. `sluice_crossing.png` cuts its body off at an opaque cyan water plane without a visible waterline interaction. No unsupported creature-role or size judgment is made from these views.

`intake_gallery.png` and `pump_hall.png` are dominated by long empty floor and ceiling spans carrying the same repeating masonry language. Lantern repetition does not supply the arches, galleries, bridges, and vertical space that make the reference heart chamber an authored place. `heart_chamber_banner.png` exposes a completely flat blue rectangle with a white square; `heart_chamber_crystal.png` exposes plain blue-gray cubes beneath and beside the focal crystal. These are conspicuous unfinished forms at the destination.

The warm lamps against cold water are directionally appropriate, but the near-uniform dark stone values and harsh reflective floor pattern overwhelm material hierarchy. The very large UI panels compete with the scene at sheet size; that is a local hierarchy observation, not a comparison with Palworld UI.

## Three largest gaps, ranked

1. **A water system with a visible cause and consequence.** The Veilfall board connects rock lip, heavy falling streams, spray, and receiving water. `heart_chamber_crystal.png` and all three motion frames show cut-off sheets above a narrow fringe on stone.
2. **Architecture that gives the event scale and depth.** The board's heart chamber layers bridges, arches, and galleries around water. `intake_gallery.png`, `pump_hall.png`, and `heart_chamber_entry.png` show a low rectangular masonry box and large empty apron.
3. **Consistent finished art and surface interaction.** Palworld's characters, creatures, terrain, and props share convincing shading and contact. `sluice_crossing.png` has featureless cyan pools and an abrupt creature waterline; `heart_chamber_banner.png` and `heart_chamber_crystal.png` have bare geometric focal props.

## The two bars

**A — Do these frames belong to the world of the Meadows key art? NO.** The cool water, blue banners, warm lamps, and adventure trainer provide compatible ingredients. The repetitive enclosure, disconnected fall, and bare destination props do not express the key art's natural, inhabited, materially coherent world. The Veilfall-specific board makes that shortfall especially clear.

**B — Beside the Palworld references, do these read as the same kind of game at the stated visual bar? NO.** The creature-adventure premise is recognizable, but empty repeated surfaces, flat pools, and unfinished focal forms dominate the actual image. An appealing companion cannot carry the environment to that bar.

**Scene changes:** Expose a source, connect the fall to a basin/outflow, stage spray and foam over a wider impact area, create stronger light and depth separation, reduce empty spans, and compose the crystal without concealing the water event.

**Art work required:** A waterfall treatment with irregular flow volume and convincing impact/spray; completed basin/ledge and chamber architectural forms; textured receiving water; authored banner and pedestal forms. Suitable versions of these are not evidenced in the submitted frames. Placement and palette adjustments alone cannot finish the visible flat cubes, white-square banner, or two-sheet waterfall construction.
