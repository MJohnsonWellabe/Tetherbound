# Veilfall interior waterfall — independent visual judgment

Reviewed 2026-09-27. Evidence: every PNG in `after-r3` (nine frames, listed below), the visual-judge rubric, art-direction target, Meadows key art, Veilfall stronghold board, Water Realm roster board, and all five Palworld comparison frames. No implementation, capture metadata, or other verdict file was opened. The requested art-direction document contains historical dispositions; those are not evidence for this judgment. This review judges visible results only and makes no comparison with an earlier candidate.

**Scoped interior-waterfall verdict: FAIL.** Water recognition is present, but a convincing source and receiving basin are not established. This is independent of the full-scene failures below.

## Waterfall asset

- **Source — fails integration.** In `heart_chamber_crystal.png` and `motion_0.png`–`motion_2.png`, dark horizontal rails and upright end pieces establish a lip, but the falling sheets appear hung from it against uninterrupted masonry. There is no readable opening, upstream channel, reservoir, or rock break feeding the flow. The pale strip across the top makes a rim, not a convincing source. It reads as an installed decorative curtain assembly.
- **Water read — recognizable, with a material defect.** Pale blue vertical streaks, divided streams and white bases make the intended waterfall legible even in `heart_chamber_entry.png`. At closer range, the long opaque sheets have smooth, folded, tapering edges that resemble hanging cloth or flexible ribbons. This is strongest on the narrow left strips in `heart_chamber_crystal.png` and all three motion samples. Their heavy continuous forms need more falling-water breakup and less uninterrupted curtain silhouette. The sampled images show changing streak patterns; they do not establish motion quality or flow speed.
- **Impact and receiving basin — impact cue present, basin unconvincing.** White splash shapes exist where the streams meet the bottom. However, in `heart_chamber_crystal.png` and all three motion samples, they read as a row of sharp, similarly sized frosted tufts above a dark segmented curb. Little receiving water surface or depth is readable. The assembly appears to end at the curb. A visible pool with coherent foam spreading away from impact, and a legible route for the collected water, would make the action intelligible. The distant cyan pools in `pump_hall.png` and `sluice_crossing.png` do not visibly connect to this basin.
- **Fit — palette works, construction does not yet.** Cool water and blue crystal against warm lanterns belong to the Veilfall palette. The falls remain readable behind the crystal. Yet the rectangular wall-mounted assembly and shallow tray feel like an ornament in a room, whereas the Veilfall board makes water part of the chamber's architecture and depth. A cathedral-scale chamber is a full-scene issue; the local source and basin connections are still defects within this asset's integration scope.

## Three largest reference gaps, ranked

1. **Chamber structure and authored depth:** `heart_chamber_entry.png`, `pump_hall.png`, `sluice_crossing.png`. The Veilfall board uses water-filled vertical space, arched bridges, ledges and layered stone silhouettes. These frames offer a low rectangular room with a largely empty floor and a decoration on its far wall. This is a composition and architecture gap, not a demand for painted concept-art fidelity.
2. **Water physically belongs to its setting:** `heart_chamber_crystal.png`, all motion samples, `sluice_crossing.png`. The board makes sources, drop, impact and surrounding wet stone coherent. Here the falls hang from rails, terminate in a minimally legible tray, and coexist with flat cyan pools. Source/basin geometry, material breakup, water contact and scene connections need work.
3. **Finished subject-and-prop language:** `heart_chamber_banner.png`, `heart_chamber_crystal.png`, `intake_gallery.png`. The references use shaped, materially distinct architecture and expressive subjects. Plain cubes, a square banner emblem and repeated masonry dominate this scene. Trainer and Ripplet are identifiable, but Ripplet's flat/mottled treatment does not yet share the trainer's material finish or the Palworld subjects' clean modeled volume.

## Full-scene bars

**Bar A — NO.** Blue water/crystal, stone and amber lanterns carry part of Veilfall's identity, but the box-room composition, weak water integration and unfinished banner/blocks outweigh that palette agreement. This is a chapter-specific judgment using the Veilfall board alongside the Meadows identity target.

Scene-fixable gaps include focal staging, lighting hierarchy, source/basin placement, water-surface treatment and reducing repetitive empty expanses. The visible scene also needs architectural forms, finished functional props and designed banner art that are not present in these captures. Whether suitable assets already exist elsewhere cannot be determined from images.

**Bar B — NO.** These frames communicate a third-person creature RPG, but do not yet read in the same polished creature-adventure register as the supplied Palworld gameplay. Ripplet's face, trainer silhouette and the crystal are useful subjects; unfinished blocks, flat pools, repetitive enclosure and weak environmental purpose sink the full-scene comparison.

Scene composition/material/lighting work can address part of this gap. Finished prop and architectural art, plus a more coherent creature material presentation, are also required. This is not a verdict on unseen roster members or missing gameplay, and passing this one waterfall would not close either full-scene bar.
