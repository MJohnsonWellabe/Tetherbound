# Blind visual judgement — Burrow Warrens, `final2` pass (W07-WARRENS-0904)

Judged: `shots/warrens_0904/_sheet_final.png` and the five frames in
`shots/warrens_0904/final2/`, against `docs/reference/tetherbound-meadows-keyart.png`
and `docs/reference/palworld-0*.jpg`. Compared only for movement against
`shots/warrens_0904/_sheet_before.png`, `shots/warrens_0904/before/` and the earlier
verdict `JUDGE-before.md`. Same five camera stands, different build; I was not told what
changed.

Compatibility / software GL, so I judge composition, silhouette, value structure, colour
relationships, scale and geometry, and not fine shadow or post quality. Measurements are
taken against the 1.80 m trainer 3 m ahead of the camera. Pixel statistics below are
measured on the images themselves (luma = Rec.709; "dark%" = luma < 40; "bright%" =
luma > 200).

---

## 1. Per-frame defects, ranked

### 01 — entry mouth

1. **The mouth is still a small grey rectangle and still invisible at thumbnail size.**
   It has gained a root tangle over the lintel and an earth ramp on its right, but the
   opening itself is unchanged: a straight lintel on two straight jambs, a flat frame,
   90° corners. At sheet scale this frame still reads "trainer in a yellow field in front
   of some brown rocks"; nothing announces the Meadows' one required dungeon.
2. **The trainer still stands dead centre and still occludes the lower half of the
   opening.** His head sits inside the doorway rectangle. The one thing this stand exists
   to show is behind the character, exactly as before.
3. **The green on the rock tops is still flat paint.** Large mid-green polygons lie across
   the brown boulder faces with razor edges and no blend, no scattered growth, no moss
   silhouette (clearest at x≈1050–1250, y≈370–520 and x≈150–420, y≈290–330). At full zoom
   these are unmistakably coloured facets of the rock mesh, not vegetation.
4. **The ground is still the loudest colour in the frame and it is sulphur-yellow.**
   Frame saturation 0.61 — the highest of the five and higher than any reference image
   (Palworld 0.41–0.47, key art 0.42). The key art's meadow is fresh green with warm earth
   paths; this is acid ochre next to acid green.
5. **Two unrelated rock treatments still share the shot.** Untextured flat-shaded chocolate
   facets in the near pile against pale grey speckled rock in the upper middle.
6. **A pinhole of sky and distant green terrain shows through a crack in the rock pile**
   at x≈371–389, y≈250–275, behind a grass tuft. Small, but it is a hole through what
   should be solid ground.
7. **Grass is still countable** — single blades, near-uniform height, all vertical, bare
   soil between them. Two or three new bush clumps have been added; the density is still
   far below `palworld-02`/`03`.

### 02 — first chamber, looking deeper

1. **The boar is half-buried in the floor and clips the rocks it stands among.** Only the
   head, neck and one foreleg are visible; the muzzle passes into the boulder silhouette
   in front of it and the body is cut off behind two rocks. No legs, no ground contact,
   no clear silhouette. This is the loudest artefact in the set now and it was not present
   before (the boar previously stood clear on open floor).
2. **The right foreground occluder is a blank CSG wedge.** A tan slab with a razor-straight
   top edge and one sharp vertical corner fills roughly the right fifth of the frame with a
   featureless plane, no rubble at its base, no bevel, no silhouette interest. It succeeds
   as an occluder and fails as an object.
3. **The room is still boxes.** Flat ceiling slab, square lintel beams, rectangular openings
   nested inside rectangular openings, hard 90° junctions everywhere. Brightening the image
   2.4× confirms the geometry is unchanged.
4. **The boar is still lit by something the room does not have.** Its body reads at mean
   luma 111 with a 95th percentile of 227 against a floor at 72/85 — the only near-white
   thing in the chamber, and its texture is still smeared posterised paint with hard brown
   blotches and flat green card "moss".
5. **The trainer has no contact shadow.** Floor immediately under the boots measures 89
   against 86 beside them — a 3% difference, i.e. none. He sits in front of the floor, not
   on it.
6. **57% of the frame is below luma 40** and holds no information when brightened; the
   upper third of the image is a dead black band.
7. **The floor speckle reads as confetti.** Blue, lilac and pale flecks scattered evenly
   across the sand at uniform density, plus flat dark-green moss decals with hard edges.
8. **Ceiling still carries pure-black unlit box faces** (top-left and above the right wedge)
   that read as flat cut-outs stuck on, not as geometry in shadow.

### 03 — hall, looking toward the den

1. **A creature still occupies the right quarter of the frame, cropped by the frame edge,
   and still reads as nothing.** It has been pulled up so floor now shows beneath it, which
   is an improvement, but at any size it is an unidentifiable dark mass; at thumbnail it is
   a smudge. A quarter of the composition is still spent on it.
2. **That creature's material is still a tier below the trainer's.** At zoom: posterised
   smeared paint, hard black scribbles, blown near-white blobs, glossy plastic shell plates.
   No fur, no fine surface.
3. **67% of the frame is below luma 40.** The side walls dropped from mean luma 50 to 23.
   The hall is now so dark that the granite detail is gone and both flanks are near-solid
   black shapes — the frame is a black surround with a lit rectangle in it.
4. **The glowing mushrooms do not light anything.** The cluster (mean 83, p95 188) is the
   first real emitter in the cave and a genuine bright anchor, but the green "glow" on the
   wall behind is a flat wash with a hard boundary and there is no falloff onto the floor
   beneath, no lit rim on the rock beside it. It reads as a decal, and the mushrooms
   themselves are thin flat cards seen near edge-on with visible alpha edges.
5. **The hall and the den still disagree about material and colour temperature** — cold
   near-black grey looking into warm russet-orange, along a razor doorway line.
6. **Geometry is unchanged boxes.** Brightened, the frame is a rectangular pier, a
   rectangular lintel, a rectangular ceiling wedge and a rectangular doorway.
7. **The mid-ground is still empty** — bare floor from the trainer to the door.

### 04 — the den

1. **The trainer still stands on the guardian's axis and cuts across its chest and
   forelimbs.** The head and shoulders are now clear and the animal is unoccluded above
   the trainer's head — a real gain — but the boss's body is still bisected by the
   character's backpack. Compare `palworld-01`, where nothing overlaps the boss.
2. **No effect, no arena, no framing on the guardian.** There is a warm floor pool around
   it, but no floor marking, no rim light, no dust, no particulate, nothing that says
   "encounter". It is a large animal standing in a lit room.
3. **A pure-saturated green grass clump grows indoors at its left shoulder.** Unlit, flat
   card blades, the most saturated object in the frame. The oak tree is gone; this is the
   same mistake at one-eighth the size.
4. **The wooden chest and crate at the right edge are still off-palette and still cut by
   the frame.** Saturated orange planks with black gaps, at a hue nothing else in the cave
   shares.
5. **The trainer has no contact shadow** (floor under the boots 44 vs 48 beside — none),
   while the guardian does (93 under the front paw vs 127 nearby). Two objects standing on
   the same floor under two different rules.
6. **Still no burrow evidence.** No bedding, no nest, no bones, no midden, no scratch
   marks. A few loose rocks and a spoil mound at the right are the whole vocabulary.
7. **The granite portal block still abuts the earth wall on a razor vertical edge** at
   x≈390 with no transition. The alcove inside it now has real depth, which is a fix; the
   seam is not.
8. **The right wall's strata are hard-edged flat colour bands** — olive over russet with a
   straight diagonal boundary — that read as painted-on rather than as layers of soil.
9. **Ceiling beams still carry the wall texture at a different stretch ratio.**

### 05 — looking out to daylight

1. **72% of the frame is below luma 40** — the darkest frame in the set. The two flanking
   earth banks and the entire left wall are near-solid black masses that hold nothing when
   brightened. The composition is now a black frame around a bright postcard.
2. **The two flanking banks are enormous untextured wedges.** Flat planes, straight top
   edges, sharp corners, no rubble, no debris at the base, and they occupy roughly 40% of
   the image between them. They read as level-blockout geometry, not spoil heaps.
3. **The boar is the brightest object in the frame and is self-lit.** Mean luma 108 against
   a floor at 38 — 2.9× the ground it stands on, in a cave interior. It pulls the eye away
   from the exit.
4. **The leaf litter is flat green paper.** Large hard-edged dark-green polygon cards lying
   on the floor at near-uniform scale, all fresh green rather than brown, unlit. At zoom
   they are unmistakably alpha cards.
5. **The exit is still a doorway.** A straight lintel on two jambs with a plank ramp and a
   flight of cut steps leading out — post-and-lintel and carpentry, not a dug mouth. The
   dolmen colonnade is gone, which is a large gain; the doorway is not.
6. **The exterior beyond does not match frame 01's exterior.** Here: tall oaks, lush green
   sward, wildflowers, distant hills. In 01: no trees at all, ochre scrub, boulders. The
   two ends of the same burrow open onto two different landscapes.
7. **The trainer holds the identical arms-out rest pose in all five frames**, unchanged.

## 2. R1–R8, movement on the same stands

**R1 — no light structure, therefore no value structure: PARTLY IMPROVED, by subtraction.**
A viewer would see the difference, and it is real: in 02 the floor rose from mean 48 to 75
while the ceiling fell from 37 to 32, so there is now a vertical value gradient where the
walls were previously lit identically top to bottom; 04 has a genuine warm floor pool with
corners falling off; 03 has an actual emitter (mushrooms, mean 83 / p95 188 against walls at
23); 05's daylight rose from 62 to 111. But the top of the range did not move. Frame p95:
02 73→89, 03 81→63 (**down**), 05 97→97. Pixels above luma 200 are 0.1–0.4% in every final
frame, against 14–22% in the Palworld shots and 4.2% in the key art. Contrast increased
because the darks were crushed — dark% went 34→57 (02), 30→67 (03), 26→72 (05) — not
because anything was lit. The cave is no longer uniformly dim; it is now mostly black with
one bright hole. That is closer to a cave, and it is still not a lit room.

**R2 — the cave is boxes: UNCHANGED.** Brightened 2.4–2.6×, frames 02, 03 and 05 are still
axis-aligned rectangular prisms with razor 90° corners, flat faces, square lintels and
openings that are rectangles nested inside rectangles. Nothing curves, narrows, slumps or
tapers. The darkness hides some of it at first glance; the geometry did not change. The
one exception is 04's alcove, which now has real recessed depth.

**R3 — walls read as one tiled granite bitmap, not dug earth: PARTLY IMPROVED.** New earth
materials are genuinely present and visible: the tan packed-soil wedge in 02, the brown
earth right wall in 03 and 05, the russet strata walls in 04. But the granite speckle is
still the dominant surface in 02, 03 and 05 — same mottled grey/white tile at the same texel
scale on ceiling, piers and jambs, with no corner darkening, no edge wear, no strata, no
roots in the surface, no tool marks. And the new earth surfaces are flat colour with almost
no texture, so the room now has two problems instead of one: wallpaper on boxes, and blank
planes beside it.

**R4 — no evidence a creature dug or lives here: MARGINALLY IMPROVED.** Removals carried it:
the oak tree is out of the den and the orange crate is off the left. Additions are thin —
hanging root tangles at the 01 mouth, the 02 ceiling and the 05 exit (the best new element
in the set), spoil-shaped mounds, scattered rocks, moss decals, leaf cards, one web decal.
Still absent in all five frames: bedding, nest, bones, midden, hoard, scratch marks, tracks,
side burrows, narrowing throats. The den still contains a wooden chest and a crate, and the
exit still has cut steps and a plank ramp. A viewer would still not name this a burrow
unaided.

**R5 — four palettes across five frames: IMPROVED.** This is the clearest win in the set.
Interior saturation rose from 0.16/0.18/0.13 (02/03/05) to 0.32/0.28/0.37, and the hue moved
off pure slate toward earth; 04 fell from 0.49 to 0.44 and lost its sunlit-courtyard read.
On the contact sheet the four interiors now read as one brown-grey place with warm depths,
where before 04 was visibly a different room in a different building. Frame 01 remains the
outlier at 0.61 acid yellow-green. And the composition seam is fixed: the green tree that
made 02 and 03 look like they were pointing out of the cave is gone from both.

**R6 — nothing is anchored to the ground: PARTLY IMPROVED, and now inconsistent.** The
guardian in 04 has a measurable contact darkening (93 under the paw vs 127 on nearby floor)
and the den has broad soft occlusion in the corners. The trainer does not, in any frame:
02 measures 89 under the boots vs 86 beside; 04 measures 44 vs 48. Loose rocks, crates and
mounds still have no contact shading. One object in the set is on the floor and the rest are
in front of it, which is more conspicuous than when nothing was.

**R7 — no layering, foreground accidental: IMPROVED.** Frames 02 and 05 now have deliberate
near-field occluders (the tan wedge and the two flanking banks) plus a foreground boulder
cluster in 02, and there is a real near/mid/far read in both. 05's near-field creature clip
is gone. Against that, the occluders themselves are blank untextured masses, and 03 still
has an accidental foreground — the same cropped creature owning the right quarter — and an
empty mid-ground.

**R8 — artefacts: MOSTLY IMPROVED, with two gone and two new.**
- The unsupported boulder mass in 01: **fixed.** Sky pixels in the band under it fell from
  14,959 spanning x 0–1279 to 2,564 in three isolated clusters; the mass now runs down to
  the grass.
- The creature clipped into the camera at the bottom-right of 05: **fixed and gone.**
- The pure-black unlit block faces and the black slab: **reduced**, largely because the
  whole frame is now dark; still visible in 02's ceiling and 03's corners.
- The granite/sandstone razor seam in 04: **unchanged.**
- The visibly repeating floor tile in 02: **improved** — replaced by sand with speckle,
  though the speckle now reads as evenly-scattered confetti.
- The trainer's identical arms-out pose in all five frames: **unchanged.**
- **New:** the boar half-buried and clipping the boulders in 02; the sky pinhole through
  the rock pile in 01.

## 3. The two specific questions

**(a) Does the room read as a designed burrow with a real value range and
foreground / mid / far layering?**

**Layering: yes, newly, in 02 and 05; no in 03.** Frame 02 has a foreground (the tan wedge
and near boulders), a mid-ground (the boar, the trainer, the moss floor) and a far ground
(the lit doorway and the room beyond), and it reads at thumbnail. Frame 05 is the same
shape, stronger: two near banks, a mid floor with the boar, and the bright exterior. Frame
03 has near-field content only by accident — the cropped creature — and nothing at all
between the trainer and the door.

**Value range: yes as a range, no as a structure.** The frames now span far more of the
scale than before, but only downward. p5→p95 in 05 went from 11–97 to 4–97; in 03 the top
actually fell, 81→63. Every final frame puts 0.1–0.4% of its pixels above luma 200 against
14–22% in the Palworld references. There is one bright anchor per frame — the doorway, the
daylight, the floor pool, the mushrooms — and beyond it 57–72% of the image is below luma
40 and holds nothing when brightened. That is a silhouette-and-hole structure, not a lit
space with form.

**Designed burrow: no.** Read as pure shape with the colour taken out, 02, 03 and 05 are
rectangular prisms with square openings, and 05 exits through a lintelled doorway onto cut
steps and a plank ramp. The best new elements — hanging roots at three thresholds, spoil
mounds, the earth walls, the glowing fungus — are dressing laid over a mine corridor. A
player told nothing would call this a dug tunnel because it is dark and has roots over the
door, not because its shape says an animal made it.

**(b) In 01 and 05, does any large rock mass read as unsupported or floating, with open sky
or distant horizon visible underneath it?**

**No — the defect is fixed.** In `before/01`, a continuous band of blue sky and distant
green meadow ran underneath the overhanging boulder mass across almost the full frame width
(14,959 sky pixels in rows 200–300, spanning x 0–1279); the mass hung over nothing. In
`final2/01` that band is filled: the pile descends to the grass in an unbroken run of rock,
and the residual sky in those rows is 2,564 pixels in three isolated places — x 0–37 (sky
above a low rock at the frame edge, correct), x 371–389 (a pinhole crack, listed above as a
minor artefact), and x 1061–1144, a notch between two boulders. On that last one, the dark
slab above the notch tapers to a thin tongue whose tip has sky beneath it; it reads as a
cantilevered ledge over a gap rather than as a floating mass, because its other end clearly
merges into the cliff. It is worth tidying but no viewer would call it floating.

Frame 05 contains no exterior rock mass at all — it looks out through the opening at trees
and sky, and every mass in it meets the floor.

## 4. New defects and artefacts not in the earlier frames

1. **02 — the boar is half-buried and interpenetrating the boulders.** Muzzle clipping into
   a rock, body and legs occluded, no ground contact. It previously stood clear.
2. **05 — the boar is now the brightest object in a very dark frame** (2.9× the floor
   luma), so the "creature carries its own light" problem is louder here than it was
   anywhere before.
3. **05 — flat green leaf-litter cards** on the floor: hard-edged polygons, uniform scale,
   fresh green in a cave, unlit.
4. **02 / 05 — blank untextured occluder masses.** The tan wedge and the two exit banks are
   large flat planes with razor edges and no detail, which is a new kind of emptiness where
   there used to be a different kind.
5. **01 — a sky-and-horizon pinhole** through a crack in the rock pile at x≈371–389.
6. **04 — a saturated indoor grass clump** at the guardian's shoulder, replacing the tree
   as the frame's off-palette green.
7. **03 — the mushroom glow is a hard-edged wall decal** with no falloff onto the floor or
   the rock beside it.
8. **Anchoring is now inconsistent**: the guardian has contact shading and the trainer has
   none, in the same frame.

## 5. The bar questions

The three things that most separate these frames from the references, ranked:

1. **Nothing in the cave is lit — the range was widened downward.** The references put
   14–22% of their pixels above luma 200; every final frame here puts 0.1–0.4% there, and
   03's highlight ceiling actually dropped (p95 81→63) while its dark share rose from 30%
   to 67%. `palworld-01` is a dark forest interior and still carries sparks, rim light,
   sunlit canopy and a lit boss. Frames 03 and 05 give the eye one bright rectangle and
   two thirds of a black screen.
2. **Density and surface.** `palworld-01` and `-04` dress every square metre — grass,
   flowers, rubble, ruins, trees, creatures, effects. The Warrens' new foreground masses
   (02's wedge, 05's banks) are the largest objects in those frames and are blank planes;
   the floors are sand with evenly scattered speckle and a few loose rocks; 01's meadow is
   still countable blades over bare soil against the key art's dense wildflower banks.
3. **The creatures.** Both the boar and the badger-guardian remain posterised smeared paint
   with hard black scribbles, blown white blobs and glossy plastic shell plates, beside a
   trainer whose clothing is cleanly sculpted and correctly lit. In 03 a creature owns a
   quarter of the frame and cannot be identified. The bar's creature art is bespoke,
   readable by silhouette and colour at any size, and staged as an event.

> **A. Do these frames read as belonging to the world of
> `tetherbound-meadows-keyart.png`?**

**No** — but the gap narrowed. What carries: the trainer, who reads as the board's
stylised-realism character in every frame; the exterior seen through the 05 exit, which is
the single most on-board image in the set (oaks, green sward, wildflowers, hills, blue sky —
that is the key art); and the interiors now agreeing on one earth-toned palette instead of
four. What sinks it: 01, the frame a player actually approaches the dungeon through, is
still acid ochre and countable grass under an empty rockscape with no landmark, against a
board whose every panel has a silhouette on the horizon; and the interiors are earth-toned
but almost unlit, where the board's darkest panel (night) still carries a moon, a fire and
readable silhouettes.

> **B. Shown beside `palworld-0*.jpg`, would someone say these are trying to be the same
> kind of game?**

**No.** Closer than before — 05's composition, the root-hung threshold, the fungus, the warm
den pool and the unoccluded guardian face all belong to the same genre — but three things
would give it away immediately: the frames are two thirds black with no highlights; the
biggest objects in two of five frames are untextured flat wedges; and the creature art is
visibly a tier below the character standing next to it, with one creature buried in the
floor and another unidentifiable at a quarter of frame width.

### Fixable by changing the scene (no new art required)

- **Put light back at the top of the range instead of taking it off the bottom.** Every
  frame needs at least one surface reading above luma 180 that is not a doorway: lit rock
  faces near the mushrooms, a torch or brazier throwing a falloff, a bounce off the floor
  pool in 04. Target the reference's shape (bright anchor plus readable mid-tones), not its
  overall key.
- **Un-bury the boar in 02** and give it clear floor, all four legs and a silhouette
  separated from the boulders.
- **Move the cropped creature out of 03's right quarter**, or bring it fully into frame,
  lit, and put something in that frame's empty mid-ground.
- **Take the trainer off the guardian's axis in 04** — a few degrees of camera yaw or a
  metre of lateral offset stops the backpack bisecting the boss's chest.
- **Give the trainer a contact shadow** to match the one the guardian already has, and
  extend it to loose rocks, crates and mounds.
- **Break up the blank occluders**: rubble at the base of 02's wedge and 05's banks, a
  varied top edge, scale variety, a few large boulders clustered rather than one plane.
- **Move the trainer or the camera in 01 so the mouth is not behind his head**, and pull
  the ochre out of the meadow toward the board's fresh green.
- **Retire the off-palette props**: the indoor grass clump in 04, the orange chest and
  crate at the right edge, the fresh-green leaf cards in 05 (brown litter, varied scale,
  clustered).
- **Give the fungus glow a falloff** onto the floor and the adjacent rock.
- **Close the sky pinhole in 01** and trim the cantilevered slab tip over the right-hand
  notch.
- **Give the trainer any idle other than arms-out**, and vary it between stands.
- **Make the 01 and 05 exteriors agree** — one of them has oaks and one has none.

### Needs art that is not in the build

- **Irregular cave modules.** Curved, tapering, non-orthogonal wall/ceiling/throat pieces
  and a real burrow mouth with a lip and an overhang. R2 is untouched and cannot be fixed
  by lighting, darkness or dressing; the boxes are still boxes under every exposure.
- **A dug-earth material set with depth.** The new earth surfaces are flat colour and the
  old granite tile is still the dominant interior surface. Soil, clay, root-laced and
  strata textures with crevice darkening and edge wear are needed to retire both.
- **Burrow dressing props.** Bedding nest, bone or shell midden, hanging root bundles that
  are geometry rather than silhouette decals, cave flora, claw-mark decals, side-burrow
  mouths. The den is still furnished with a chest and a crate.
- **A creature-art pass.** The boar and the badger-guardian need real surface work — fur,
  sculpted detail, non-blown highlights, materials that receive the room's light — to hold
  up beside the trainer at these distances. This remains the single clearest "buy or make"
  item, and it is unchanged from the previous verdict.
- **A rock material for 01's exterior boulders**, which are still untextured flat-shaded
  facets with painted-on green tops beside a differently-authored grey rock.
