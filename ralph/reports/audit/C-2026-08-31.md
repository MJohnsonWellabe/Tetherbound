## C1 — NPCs read as people in clothing, never silhouette cutouts

**Recorded status going in:** `MEADOWS_EXIT_CRITERION.md` says closed
2026-08-30, citing grunt torso luma 13→31.5/255 day, 15→51.7 night
(`ralph/reports/handover-T1-LIGHT-2026-08-30.md` §the additive emission
floor). **That status is stale on `main`.**

**What actually happened, in order, all on 2026-08-30:**

1. `T1-LIGHT` added an additive emission floor gated on `tint luminance <
   0.95`, fixed grunts and officers, verified in-world on Dorn
   (`ralph/reports/T1-LIGHT/shots/npc_luminance/`, day 13→31.5, night
   →16.5/255).
2. `T1-CAST` reproduced those two numbers, then found the floor's own gate
   **excludes the captain by construction** — the captain's palette multiply
   is `#ffffff` (`1.000`), so `tint luminance < 0.95` reads it as "already
   bright" and skips it. But the captain rig is painted on the **same
   near-black grunt-family atlas** the floor exists to rescue (texture median
   0.093 — the darkest human atlas in the game). Measured via
   `tools/_probe_rank_ladder.gd` (new that lane, still in the repo, headless,
   ~seconds to run):

   | rank | individual | texMedian | albedoMul | emissAdd | **effective** |
   |---|---|---|---|---|---|
   | grunt | Dorn/Pell/Kest | 0.101–0.113 | 0.863 | 0.155 | 0.242–0.253 |
   | officer | Dell/Ness | 0.102 | 0.933 | 0.168 | 0.263 |
   | captain | **Vance/Oreth/Halder/Vess** | 0.093 | 1.000 | **0.000** | **0.093** |

   The four captains the player actually fights — the rank with the most
   story weight in the faction — rendered at roughly a third of the officers
   below them. C1 was not met for that rank at the moment the exit-criterion
   doc called it closed cast-wide.
3. `T1-CAST` fixed it by moving `emission_floor` from an inferred value to a
   value the rank declares (`data/config/npc_ranks.json`), landed on `main`.
   Same probe, after:

   | rank | individual | effective |
   |---|---|---|
   | grunt | Dorn/Pell/Kest | 0.242–0.253 |
   | officer | Dell/Ness | 0.263 |
   | captain | Vance/Oreth/Halder/Vess | **0.273** |

   Confirmed in-world too, on the courtyard figure specifically
   (`ralph/reports/T1-CAST/shots/hall/H-07-courtyard.png` — opened directly,
   real grass and stonework, `capture_check` clean): face/mask 0.498 mean
   luma, torso 0.339, 0.0% clipped anywhere on the figure, sitting legibly
   between the stone wall (0.375) and floor (0.259) behind it. **This is a
   real pass** — a person in tactical uniform against architecture, not a
   silhouette.

**But a second, independent defect was found the same day, after T1-CAST's
fix, on a body T1-CAST's own probe does not look at the face of.** When
`T1-VILLAGERS` wired `officer_b` onto Warder Ness at the Sigil-gate

texture, so the fix that closed the torso defect says nothing about this one.

**Verdict: PARTIAL / STILL OPEN.** Body-surface luminance (the specific
mechanism C1 was recorded closed for) is genuinely fixed for grunt, officer

live in the shipped placement table
(`data/config/bands/band5_stronghold_approach/trainers.json`,
`stronghold_checkpoint` → `base: "officer_b"`, confirmed present on
`453107fb`).

**Evidence:**

- `ralph/reports/T1-CAST/shots/hall/H-07-courtyard.png` — the torso fix, opened and viewed for this audit.

## C2 — Rank is readable: grunt vs officer vs captain vs Warden, on sight

**Recorded status going in:** the exit-criterion doc does not mark C2 closed
or open explicitly, but the black-NPC fix above was believed to make the
ladder monotonic.

**T1-CAST's own before/after (§C1 above) shows the ladder was inverted before
its fix** (captain 0.093 vs officer 0.263 vs grunt 0.242–0.253) and **ascends
cleanly after** (0.242–0.253 / 0.263 / 0.273, rank defaults 0.273/0.296/0.317).
That fix is present on `main` (`data/config/npc_ranks.json` carries
per-rank `emission_floor`; verified by reading the file directly on
`453107fb`).

**A second, independent measurement taken the same day disagrees on the
real rendered result.** `T1-VILLAGERS` shot the actual five-body lineup
(grunt / Officer Dell / Warder Ness / captain / Warden) in the real world at
8.4m and measured mean **torso** luma directly off the rendered pixels — not
the shader's internal albedo/emission terms, the pixels a player's screen
would actually show:

| body | rank | torso luma (T1-VILLAGERS, in-world) |
|---|---|---|
| `grunt` (rank default) | grunt | 25.0 / 255 |
| `officer_a` (Officer Dell) | officer | 63.1 / 255 |
| `officer_b` (Warder Ness) | officer | 48.1 / 255 |
| `captain_a` | captain | **22.7 / 255** |
| `warden` | warden | **10.7 / 255** |

This is **not** monotonic — the captain reads darker than both officers and
barely above the grunt, and the Warden (deliberately, by design — his textured
palette is untinted) is the darkest figure in the lineup. I opened the frame
itself
(`ralph/reports/T1-VILLAGERS/shots/tether-00-rank-ladder-grunt-dell-ness-captain-warden.png`)
directly: by eye, rank is legible mainly through **silhouette** (coat length,
collar, shoulder mantle — the captain's long coat and gold trim genuinely read
apart from the grunt's short jacket) and through the faction's oxblood/purple
accent colour, not through a value ladder. The Warden's grey fur collar and
disc medallion make him read as "important," not necessarily "highest-rank
in an ascending sequence," at a glance.

**Reconciling the two measurements, not picking one:** T1-CAST's numbers are
the shader's own material terms (declared albedo multiply × the additive
floor) computed by a headless probe with no camera, no distance falloff, no
scene lighting, and no per-body texture beyond a 256×256 median sample.
T1-VILLAGERS's numbers are the same bodies, same day, rendered in the real
Meadows at a real camera distance under real `world_look.gd` lighting. Both
are real and both are checked into the repo. The gap between them says the
"effective" fix genuinely raised what the shader computes, but scene
lighting, per-body texture variance beyond the sampled median, and (for
`officer_b` specifically) the face defect from C1 are enough to erase that
gain in the frame a player actually sees. **This is exactly the class of
"config-level assertion vs. played evidence" gap the audit brief warns
about** — `tools/_probe_rank_ladder.gd` is a real, useful instrument, but it
is not evidence that the ladder reads in the game, and nothing in this
project's test suite currently checks the in-world rendered case (`smoke_art.gd`
asserts the *declared* palette was applied — see below — not the *rendered*
luminance ladder).

**Verdict: CANNOT BE CLEANLY CALLED "MET." Open, more precisely: the
shader-level fix is real and verified twice, independently; the in-world
rendered ladder was measured non-monotonic the same day, on a frame I opened
and can point to.** Whether that in-world measurement still holds on
`453107fb` specifically (as opposed to T1-VILLAGERS's own branch state) is
exactly what this audit's live re-verification (last section) checks.

**What decides it, concretely, for a future lane:** re-run
`tools/_capture_t1_villagers.gd --stage=tether` against `453107fb` and remeasure
torso luma on all five bodies with the tool's own built-in `_measure()` (Rec.
709 luma on the rendered pixels, already in the tool, no new code needed).
If the numbers still fail to ascend, the fix belongs in scene-lit rendering
(a real render-time check), not the material-probe layer T1-CAST's tool
checks.

**Silhouette-tier evidence, separately, is a real and uncontested pass.**
`ralph/reports/T1-CAST/shots/rank_variety/` (not committed in full per that
handover, but `07-captain-vance-front.png` is) shows genuinely different
body types, hairstyles and coat silhouettes across ranks — grunts in short
jackets, captains in full coats with shoulder mantles and claw gauntlets. I
opened `07-captain-vance-front.png` directly: an eyepatched, white-haired
captain in an ornate purple-accented coat with gold-clawed gauntlets is
unambiguously a different *kind* of figure from a masked grunt, independent
of the luminance question above. `tests/smoke_art.gd` (lines ~600–659, run
this session, see Tests below) formally asserts this at the data level: every
rank has a distinctly-coloured badge and a distinctly-coloured body palette
(`_fail` if any two ranks' badge or body colours are equal), and this passed.

**Closing cost:** re-verify with the capture above (a render, ~10 minutes
once the world is standing); if non-monotonic on `453107fb`, the fix is
render-side (likely the same texture/lighting interaction as the C1 face
defect) rather than a new config change — plausibly the same investigation
as C1's closing cost, not a second one. No owner decision needed.

## Cross-cutting finding: a real coverage gap in how this project tests section C

`tests/smoke_art.gd` (lines ~590–659, run this session as part of the full
suite, see Tests below) asserts that each rank's *declared* palette and badge
colour are distinct from the others — a real, useful, currently-passing

rank) would ship silently green through CI the same way this one did the
first time. **Recommendation, not actioned by this lane per the no-game-code
rule:** promote `_probe_rank_ladder.gd`'s assertion (effective luminance
strictly ascends grunt < officer < captain) into a real `tests/` file.
