  anything looks — only whether the frame is actually showing the shipping

## A cross-cutting defect this lane found by comparing across both frame sets

**The smeared/blurred cloud rendering is not a Hall-specific problem.** Both
independent blind judges, working from two disjoint frame sets shot with two
different capture tools at two different points in this session, separately
and unprompted named the same defect: every daytime outdoor frame in both
sets — the Hall approach, the village, both camps, the Relay, 20+ frames
total — shows cloud shapes with a heavy directional blur/smear inconsistent
with the crisp foreground detail below them. This lane opened frames from
both sets directly and confirms the same read in each. Because it reproduces
identically across two unrelated locations, two unrelated capture tools, and
two blind judges who never saw each other's output, this is very likely a
single global cause (a post-process, DOF, or motion-blur setting applied to
the sky dome specifically) rather than a per-location asset issue, and is
probably one of the cheaper, highest-leverage fixes available across the
whole visual estate — it is the first thing named as "most separates us from
the references" in **both** independent reports. Closing cost: likely a
single render-setting change plus one capture-and-judge round to confirm,
not a new-art item.

A second, related pattern recurs at two more locations: an over-bright,
hard-edged sun disc with no bloom falloff appears at both the Hall
(`H-06-west-keep`, implicit in the judge's read) and the Relay
(`06-relay-standing-day`, explicit). Worth checking as the same root cause
before treating it as two separate defects.
