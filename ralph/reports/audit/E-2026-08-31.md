## Corridor-scatter class of defect — verified FIXED

The brief names a specific, previously-open defect: the walk from Warden Arena
to Legendary Chamber was blocked by scattered debris until 2026-08-30, and asks
this lane to verify it stayed fixed using
`tools/_probe_hall_chamber_passage.gd`.

**Verdict: FIXED on `main` @ `453107fb`.**

Ran:
```
godot --headless --path . --script tools/_probe_hall_chamber_passage.gd
godot --headless --path . --script tests/smoke_stronghold.gd
```

The probe's own header records the prior failure state: on the tip before the
Hall merges the push travelled the full hop; after the merges it stalled
21.5 m from the chamber centre against a 16.0 m allowance (about a third of a
32 m hop), stopped short of the arena's west wall where the passage should be
— i.e., something the Hall work added was standing in the doorway.

On current `main`, the same driven push (velocity-driven `move_and_slide`,
identical to what `smoke_stronghold.gd` drives) reaches **x = −18.0, 6.0 m
from the chamber centre — well inside the 16.0 m allowance** — before stopping
against `MachineBody (parent TetherMachine)`. A static half-metre shape sweep
along the straight line confirms the only collider on the line is the
`TetherMachine`'s own body, encountered only in the final ~6.5 m nearest the
chamber (from 25.5 m along to the full 32.0 m) — i.e. the player is stopped by
the room's own centrepiece machine standing where it is supposed to stand, not
by a stray scattered prop partway down the corridor. This matches the
`legendary_chamber`'s designed content (`machine 'TetherMachine': 0.0m from
the chamber centre, bounding 16.6 x 15.0 x 12.0 m` per the smoke test's own
log) — a room-filling hero object is expected to have collision near its own
centre.

`tests/smoke_stronghold.gd` (the played-path test, not a config assertion)
independently confirms: **"stronghold smoke test passed"**, with all five
chamber-to-chamber hops inside their allowances, including
`warden_arena -> legendary_chamber: ended 6.0m from its centre (allowed
16.0)` — the exact number the probe found. `COORD-LANDING`'s handover
attributes the fix to LAND-0830J: 86 baked-scatter props were found standing
inside the Hall's five rooms blocking the route to the climax, cleared via
`vegetation.gd::clear_area()` (the same mechanism the Warrens exterior already
used); `smoke_stronghold.gd`'s own build log on this run confirms the
mechanism is live — `[stronghold] cleared 86 scattered prop(s) standing inside
the five spaces`.
