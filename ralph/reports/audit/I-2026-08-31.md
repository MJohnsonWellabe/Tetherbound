## I6 — Map: records exploration, supports the mental map, not a creature radar

**Verdict: FAIL.** (Sub-audit by parallel agent.)

**Test:** `tests/smoke_gate_a_map_cycle.gd` — real `meadows_playground.tscn`
boot, physical joypad events for party cycling, minimap heading, full-map
zoom/pan/clamp/recovery.

**Run (twice, byte-identical):**
```
  ok    real pad LB cycles five owned creatures, wraps, and skips resting
  ok    zoom keeps the view pinned to the player (clamped 529.2m ... by the honest world-edge limit)
  ok    physical Map/Back, RT/LT zoom, right-stick pan/clamp, and world recovery all work

Gate A map/cycle FAIL: minimap heading 3.142 does not match resolved travel 2.096
```
`_check_actual_travel_drives_minimap()` (test lines 89-119) holds the left
stick full-right for 70 physics frames, computes the real resolved heading
from world displacement, and compares it to `minimap._movement_yaw` — they
disagree by ~1.05 rad against a 0.12 rad tolerance.

**Not a new defect — standing, previously documented, reproduced again
here.** `ralph/reports/handover-T5-OPENING-2026-08-30.md:314-317` recorded
the identical failure with the identical numbers and states it
*"reproduces byte-identical on `origin/main`."* This audit confirms it is
still present, unowned, on `453107fb`.

**"Not a creature radar" sub-claim — independently verified TRUE.**
`scripts/ui/minimap.gd:387-396` draws a dot for `_creature_pos`, fed by
`playground_hud.gd:2050-2054` from a node literally named `AllyCreature` —
the player's own out-of-ball companion (spawned by
`combat/encounter_director.gd:894`), never nearby wild/catchable creatures.
No wild-creature radar exists. The map's objective diamond is likewise
dormant in production, for the same `set_objective()` reason as I5.

**Verdict rationale:** the exit criterion's core claim — the map "supports
the mental map" — is directly undercut by a compass that points up to
1.05 rad away from the player's real heading during ordinary movement. A
misoriented compass is worse than none.

**Closing cost:** a played segment / targeted repro, not a config tweak —
root-cause the `_movement_yaw` sample in `scripts/ui/minimap.gd:120-131`
against `playground_hud.gd`'s per-frame `update_view()` cadence (a likely
single-frame-vs-net-displacement sampling mismatch). Estimated half a day
including re-verification against this same smoke test. Needs an owning
lane — nobody currently owns it.
