### S03-31a — why the ladder still tries up to 10, but should not need to
- expected: a note event carries this observation into events.jsonl
- actual: The user asked directly why this scripts 10 numbered attempts when only 3 new catches are needed (party starts at 2, S03-39 wants 5). 10 was sized as pure headroom against two problems that are now fixed or greatly reduced: the ally-species engage-gap (fixed, operator_harness.gd::_find_entity) that used to eat several attempts before ever reaching a fight, and a shallow, guessed chip-down that left real catch odds far lower than catching.json's curve actually allows (fixed, chip_to_floor + throw_until_caught -- computed to each fight's own real floor, then thrown at until caught or out of tries).
