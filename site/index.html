Development

Honest state of it

Tetherbound is in active development for Windows. The Meadows — the first region — is playable today: a village with people in it, wild creatures to catch and train, real-time combat, gathering, crafting and building, and a story that runs from a quiet morning at your grandfather's farmhouse to a confrontation at Meadows Hall. It is one region of a larger world, still being hardened, so expect rough edges.

Currently in development for Windows.

Built with Godot 4.7. Windows and ROG Ally.

Creatures and characters — all seventeen species, the trainer, Grandpa, the Warden and the Veridian Stag — are original productions, generated from the project's own concept art.

Environment art: Quaternius (Stylized Nature MegaKit, Medieval Village MegaKit, Modular Medieval Building Pack, furniture and survival packs, CC0). Interface icons, fonts and input prompts: Kenney (CC0). Terrain textures: ambientCG (CC0). Skies: Poly Haven (CC0). Full provenance in the asset ledger.
